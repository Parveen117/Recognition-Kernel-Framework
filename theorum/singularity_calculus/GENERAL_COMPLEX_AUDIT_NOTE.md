# General Complex Audit Note

The v5 development stops the manual codimension-by-codimension expansion of
Singularity Calculus.

The generalization is valid only under the explicitly declared finite
normal-crossing assumptions:

- a finite ordered seam set;
- well-defined one-sided traces on all required strata;
- pairwise commuting jump squares;
- stratumwise exterior differentiation commuting with trace/jump when the total
  differential theorem is used.

The theory does not silently extend to nontransverse intersections, infinite seam
families, singular support accumulation, or global reconstruction.

`D_Delta^2=0` is a compatibility theorem. It is not a proof that every closed
packet is exact.

Likewise, `mathbb D^2=0` establishes a total complex, not acyclicity of that
complex.
