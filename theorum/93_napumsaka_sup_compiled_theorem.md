# The Neuter Sup Paradigms Compiled — phala and vāri; śī / śi / am / luk and the num of 7.1.72–73 load-bearing

1 November 2026. Seventh nominal class: the neuters. What distinguishes them
is a handful of substitutes — luk or am for su / am (7.1.23–24), śī for au
(7.1.19), śi for jas / śas (7.1.20, itself sarvanāmasthāna by 1.1.42) — and
the num augment of 7.1.72 (before sarvanāmasthāna) and 7.1.73 (before any
vowel-initial vibhakti for ik-final neuters). Each is shown load-bearing by
removal on phala (a) and vāri (i).
