```text

      M_Σ(sheet block of w) = ∏_{+} f0_i · ∏_{−} M_Σ(B_i)   EXACTLY
                            ≤ ρ^k f0^m

      E_Σ(L★ξ) ≤ ρ² f0^{2m} E_Σ(ξ)
```

and the worst sheet ratio `M_Σ(block)/f0^m` equals `max_i M_Σ(B_i)/f0_i`
at every `m` — the bound is the maximum of the local badnesses, never
their number.

**Proof.** `L` commutes with every sheet projector (H1), so `L★ξ = Σ_w L_w★ξ_w`
on disjoint supports and `E_Σ` is additive over them.  Mass is multiplicative
on the product (certified: `D_Σ` of a product of scalars is *not*
multiplicative in general, but the product-carrier entries are single
products of face entries and the sums factor), so (ii) is an equality.
T01 Thm 7.1 on each sheet gives `E(L_w★ξ_w) ≤ M(L_w)² E(ξ_w) ≤ ρ^{2k} f0^{2m} E(ξ_w)`,
and `k ≥ 1` on the memory sheet.  Sum. ∎

## 3. Theorem 4.1 (odd-sector covariance law — "Aghora")

Let `S = L†★L` be the cut square, a `C_Σ`-valued object with even channel
`rad(S)` and odd channel `turn(S)`.  Certified:

```text
A1  diagonal turn-free:                 turn(S)_γγ = 0         (LEAN-4 P1 shape)
A2  reversal covariance:                turn(S)^T = −turn(S),   rad(S)^T = rad(S)
A3  Leibniz across faces:               turn(S_ab) = rad(S_a)⊗turn(S_b) + turn(S_a)⊗rad(S_b)
    negative even feedback:             rad(S_ab)  = rad(S_a)⊗rad(S_b) − turn(S_a)⊗turn(S_b)
    (the feedback term is NONZERO on the instance)
A4  blindness:   every quadratic form x†★S★x is turn-free;  M_Σ and E_Σ are
    orientation-invariant  ⇒ no Hilbert-type or gauge verdict sees turn(S)
A5  orientation covariance:  flipping a face (ι→−ι) negates its odd term and
    fixes the even channel; flipping all faces negates turn(S) exactly
A6  classical shadow:  turn-free faces have turn(S) ≡ 0 and the even channel
    becomes multiplicative
```

**Reading.** The positivity square of an operator is not a number and not a
Hermitian form; on the primitive carrier it is an even/odd pair.  The even
channel is what Hilbert positivity keeps (`x†Sx`, mass, energy — all
orientation-blind).  The odd channel is a certified, nonzero, reversal-
antisymmetric, orientation-covariant object obeying a Leibniz law across
faces and feeding back *negatively* into the even channel of the product
(A3: `−turn⊗turn`, the same sign as EMK-2's odd·odd = even law).  A
definition of positivity that only sees `rad(S)` cannot express A3–A5; that
is the precise content of "change positivity or leave Hilbert space": on
`C_Σ` nothing is changed or left — the odd channel was always there, the
quadratic verdict simply projects it out.

What is **not** claimed: that the odd channel is conserved or transported by
the flow (no dynamical law yet), or any physical/arithmetic identification.
Those are the next obligations.
