## 3. Recorded, not claimed: 2.4.54's scope, and seven cells

**cakṣ before yak, ṇic, san, and before the kṛt affixes.** 2.4.54 stands under 2.4.35 *ārdhadhātuke* with no exception; yak, ṇic, san and the kṛt affixes are ārdhadhātuka (3.4.114). The verbal table reads the sūtra as pinned and gives *khyāyate, khyāpayati, cikhyāsate*; vidyut 0.4.0 keeps cakṣ there (*cakṣyate, cakṣayati, cicakṣiṣate*) while installing khyā in the ārdhadhātuka lakāras (*khyāsyate*, agreeing with the table). The 144 cells (36 karmaṇi, 72 ṇic, 36 san) are the whole increase of those three residues (36 → 72, 72 → 144, 180 → 216); they are the table's reading against the oracle's and are **not claimed either way** until adjudicated against the Kāśikā's examples under 2.4.54. The kṛt table has no 2.4.54 row, so its nine cakṣ cells (*caṣṭa, caṣṭum …*) agree with vidyut by the same omission; they count in the lane's 2,004 and are **not claimed** as derivations of the sūtra. The adjudication is one item: does 2.4.54 reach yak, ṇic, san and the kṛt affixes — if it does, the kṛt table needs the row and the oracle is in error on 153 cells; if a source restricts it, the verbal table's row is to be narrowed and re-pinned.
