1. **Recognition stability under transformation**: recognition is preserved by declared admissible transformations.

4. **Noncommuting/composed change can create residue**: composition may retain order/path information not determined by an endpoint shadow.
5. **Cocycle/holonomy memory**: an abelian cocycle can lawfully record composition memory and yields an associative lifted composition precisely when the cocycle identity holds.

## Claims deliberately not promoted

The following manuscript-level statements are not imported as theorems here without additional proof:

- universal claims that every calculus is equivalent to one specific morphic algebra;
- physical identifications of formal residues with turbulence, entropy, quantum state, or other domain objects without an adapter theorem;
- any claim that a flat clock implies the native transition itself vanishes;
- any RH conclusion from morphic algebra alone;
- self-consistency arguments based only on philosophical collapse/emptiness language.

A flat/singular clock is treated as a failure or degeneration of that presentation unless a separate theorem proves native closure.
