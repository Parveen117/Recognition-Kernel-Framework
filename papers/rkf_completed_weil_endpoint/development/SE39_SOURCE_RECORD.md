SE29 already defines a particular source-attached curvature and proves

later formal unit-memory construction does not remove that obstruction.

and proves its direct RH equivalence with explicitly admitted classical
analytic theorems. It does not claim to have established either side.

The new proof adds an entire-function adapter of the existing response,
its absolutely convergent zero-sector decomposition, the direct
source-to-RH equivalence, and the resulting finite negative-witness
existence statement if RH fails. These statements bypass the historical
five-label route; they do not identify its missing columns or K_0.

Primary analytic references were opened and inspected on 12 October 2026:

* NIST DLMF [25.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4) and
  [25.10](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10): xi normalization, reflection,
  nontrivial zero location and symmetry.
* Noam D. Elkies, Harvard Math 229 (2022),
  [zeta2.pdf](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~elkies/M229.22/zeta2.pdf)
  and [prod.pdf](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~elkies/M229.22/prod.pdf):
  completed-function growth, finite-order product theorem and logarithmic
  differentiation. His xi normalization is translated explicitly.
* Frank Hansen, [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1112.0098),
  Theorems 3.2 and 5.4, published as *The fast track to Lowner's theorem*,
  Linear Algebra and its Applications 438 (2013), 4557-4571,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2013.01.022).

The [official Clay problem page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/)
and Bombieri's [problem description](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/riemann.pdf)
were also checked. Neither establishes the requested endpoint. No
purported online RH proof is used. No priority claim for a general
Lowner, Pick or xi positivity criterion is made.

Only new SE39 files and live navigation are changed. Existing pinned
proofs, certificate results, the shared engine and its source pins are
preserved. The new rational controls concern supplied polynomial models;
their successful decoding is not evidence that xi has no off-line zeros.
