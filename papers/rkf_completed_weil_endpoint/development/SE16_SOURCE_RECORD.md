and a rational target. Those readings are not loaded or trusted by the
certificate. The final implementation uses standard-library arithmetic,
unchanged 90-digit outward interval routines, exact rational spatial
endpoints and enclosed logarithms and integer square roots.

cover the full rational spatial corridor, and the separate exterior

against trial factorization and an alternating-series oracle, complex

comparison after erasing the return. None is a formal verification
