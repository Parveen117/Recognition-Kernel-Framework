## Written proof scope

[SE47](SE47_FIVE_SOURCE_OPERATOR_REPAIR.md) proves four theorem groups:

1. An actual-source 1+4 construction W=[b,C], G=diag(1,A) with a
   declared fixed complement. When the pole-restored reference R is
   boundedly positive, the source sign, five-gap sign and generalized
   unit-threshold decision agree; their negative index and nullity agree.
2. The acquired-metric response T=G^(-1)H and contrast
   Delta=(G+H)^(-1)(G-H), their covariance under every invertible label
   change, and the comparison seam ker Delta={z:Hz=Gz}. A formal marked
   unit frame does not turn a nonzero gap into the neutral operator.
3. An augmented-source congruence and the reciprocal identity for
   K^(-1), preserving dependencies lost by independent entrywise
   subtraction. A positive finite source box gives a positive corresponding
   five-gap even when a separately boxed K is inconclusive.
4. An exact rank-one response update for one additional complementary
   observation, retaining mixed products and the fixed five-label output.

The reference positivity, bounded splitting and energy-domain hypotheses
are substantive. They are not supplied by the 5 by 5 shape. The global
noncoercive endpoint requires the separately stated inverse-half/kernel
contract. The retained internal reference solve may grow with observations.
More observations increase H and decrease K; this is not an automatic
proof of a uniform positive limit.

## Unchanged boundaries

architecture, with a declared new accepted-space choice. Identification
with the historical raw E_3 numerical packet is not asserted. Global
reference positivity, the necessary endpoint energy-domain bounds and
the unobserved infinite complement remain open.

An invariant comparison-seam kernel does not imply that the seam is
nontrivial or that K vanishes everywhere. A finite five-matrix spectrum
does not by itself enumerate infinitely many primes or zeta zeros.
No Hilbert-Polya realization is claimed. The arithmetic source already
contains prime data; the operator does not manufacture those data by
diagonalization.

Zero remains the typed Cut; Lambda is not eta. The actual RH source and
its global bound remain eta=1079/2000 with floor 1/492000. Eta zero is
proved on V_16 only; the universal source inequality and RH remain open.
