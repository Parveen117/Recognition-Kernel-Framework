# SE31: counting-operator recovery, ordered flow and retained cut memory

12 October 2026. Owner: Monty.

This follows [SE30](SE30_CONSTANT_LAMBDA_COUNTING_AND_CUT.md): a fixed
marked unit, positive counting, explicit algebraic completions, then
operators. It develops no curvature. The term zero denotes the declared
cut operation, written \(\mathsf0_{\rm cut}\). The additive-neutral
polynomial, null operator and formal-series constant coefficient retain
their separate algebraic types. Earlier pinned notation is unchanged.

The result is an exact recovery procedure for the declared polynomial
operator family. Its order bound, complete response readouts and retained
unit layers are explicit. It also connects the counting exponential to
ordered operator flows without importing an unproved bounded-generator
or physical-time assumption.

## 1. Carrier and operational contract

Work over \(R=\mathbb Q[\Lambda]\) on \(P=R[x]\), the algebraic completion
and polynomial chart already admitted in SE30. Lambda is central and
constant with respect to x. Write

\[
 p_n(x)=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(x-j\Lambda),\qquad
 Ux^n=p_n,\qquad p_0=1,
\]
\[
 Df=\sum_{n\ge1} f_n\sum_{j=1}^n
       {n\choose j}x^{n-j}\Lambda^{j-1},\qquad
 Xf=x f(x-\Lambda),\quad f=\sum_n f_n x^n.
 \tag{SE31.1}
\]

Thus \(D=U\partial_xU^{-1}\), \(X=UM_xU^{-1}\), \(Dp_n=np_{n-1}\),
\(Xp_n=p_{n+1}\), and \([D,X]=I\). These are SE30 inputs, not new
axioms. Products of operators mean composition, with the rightmost
acting first. Neither X nor D is asserted to be a bounded operator on
an unspecified completion. No dagger or positive metric is selected.

Let W_Lambda be the R-algebra generated by D and X in End_R(P).
A response to probe p_k is the **whole polynomial** A p_k, including
its coefficient data, rather than one scalar sample at a point.

**Certification rule.** The certificate is the declared observer reading,
interpreted by its target decoder. In this packet the observer reads the
response prefix and retained unit layers; Theorems 1--2 prove what that
reading determines. The order and memory labels specify its scope.
Replay, source records and negative controls document this reading;
they do not introduce an independent mathematical certification rule.
The archived example includes the actual response coefficients and the
decoded operator. A reading of this example is not a reading of the
different, still unresolved RH source.

## 2. Theorem 1: normal order and exact incremental recovery

Every A in W_Lambda has a unique finite expression

\[
 A=\sum_{j=0}^r a_j(X)D^j,\qquad a_j\in R[x].
 \tag{SE31.2}
\]

Normal ordering is governed by

\[
 D^j X^k=\sum_{v=0}^{\min(j,k)}
       {j\choose v}(k)_v X^{k-v}D^{j-v},
 \qquad (k)_v=k(k-1)\cdots(k-v+1).
 \tag{SE31.3}
\]

For the class of operators with declared D-order at most r, the
response bank \((A p_0,\ldots,A p_r)\) is injective. Put
\(b_k=U^{-1}(A p_k)\). Recover the coefficients successively by

\[
 \boxed{a_0=b_0,\qquad
 a_k=\frac{b_k-\sum_{j<k}(k)_j a_j(x)x^{k-j}}{k!}
 \quad(1\le k\le r).}
 \tag{SE31.4}
\]

Equivalently, in the original counting chart set
\(\widetilde a_k=Ua_k\) and use SE30's product bullet_Lambda:

\[
 \widetilde a_k=
 \frac{A p_k-\sum_{j<k}(k)_j
       \widetilde a_j\mathbin{\bullet_\Lambda}p_{k-j}}{k!}.
 \tag{SE31.5}
\]

At each stage the recovered operator matches every earlier response;
the next response residual is exactly \(k!\widetilde a_k\). For r at
least one, dropping the last probe from this particular prefix loses
injectivity: D^r annihilates p_0 through p_(r-1), but sends p_r to r!.
This is prefix sharpness, not a universal lower bound for arbitrary
experiments or scalar readouts.

**Proof.** Moving a D past an X using DX=XD+I gives existence of
normal form. Induction using \([D,X^k]=kX^{k-1}\) proves (SE31.3).
Conjugate a normal expression by U. Its action on x^k is
\(\sum_{j\le k}(k)_j a_j(x)x^{k-j}\). If its action vanishes on the
first r+1 powers, induction in k first yields a_0=0 and then
\(k!a_k=0\). Rational factorials are invertible, so every a_k vanishes.
This proves uniqueness, faithfulness and (SE31.4). Applying U and the
definition of bullet gives (SE31.5). Terms with j greater than k
annihilate p_k, establishing incremental matching and the missing-last-
probe witness. QED.

For example, in order two,
\(a_1=b_1-xb_0\) and
\(a_2=(b_2-2xa_1-x^2a_0)/2\).
Replacing bullet by ordinary multiplication in (SE31.5) generally
changes the answer before the cut.

This is an exact, finite termination statement **inside a supplied
order class**. Observing small or vanishing residuals for an unbounded
sequence of unknown orders supplies neither an order bound nor a
uniform stability estimate. One polynomial response can contain many
scalar coefficients. No noisy-data conditioning theorem is asserted.

## 3. Theorem 2: the cut, blindness and sufficient memory depth

Define the degree-zero operator cut by the commuting action contract

\[
 \mathsf0_{\rm cut}^{W}(A)
   =\sum_j ([\Lambda^0]a_j)(M_x)\partial_x^j,
 \qquad
 \mathsf0_{\rm cut,0}(Af)
   =\mathsf0_{\rm cut}^{W}(A)\,
       \mathsf0_{\rm cut,0}(f).
 \tag{SE31.6}
\]

This is a surjective algebra map onto the polynomial differential-
operator algebra. Its kernel is Lambda W_Lambda. On the subalgebra
whose **normal coefficients** a_j are independent of Lambda, it is
injective and preserves all operator identities. The generators still
act by the Lambda-dependent formulas (SE31.1); independence here is
about their normal coefficients, not their raw polynomial action.

More generally retain the labelled coefficient layers

\[
 \mathcal M_q(A)=U^{-1}AU\pmod{\Lambda^{q+1}}
 \quad\hbox{in }W_0[\Lambda]/(\Lambda^{q+1}).
 \tag{SE31.7}
\]

The target retains q+1 layers, with its **truncated convolution
product**. M_q preserves sums and ordered products, and its kernel
is Lambda^(q+1) W_Lambda. If every a_j has Lambda-degree at most d,
M_d determines A exactly. When q<d, the nonnull operator
Lambda^(q+1) I has invisible M_q, so that shallower bank cannot recover
the whole class. With no finite degree bound, no fixed finite q suffices.

The observations

\[
 \big(J_q U^{-1}(A p_k)\big)_{k=0}^r
 \tag{SE31.8}
\]

determine M_q(A) by the same triangular recurrence (SE31.4), now in
the quotient coefficient ring. If q is at least d, they determine A.
The order of operations in (SE31.8) keeps the unit layers and the
declared counting chart; it does not discard Lambda-dependent basis
information before the responses are expressed in that chart.

**Proof.** The conjugacy identifies W_Lambda with the free
Q[Lambda]-module on the unique normal words X^i D^j. Their product
structure constants in (SE31.3) are rational and independent of
Lambda. Coefficient extraction at degree zero therefore gives an
algebra map with precisely the stated kernel. Since U and U^-1
specialize to the identity under SE30's coefficient cut, its action
on P satisfies (SE31.6). Keeping coefficients through q is the
quotient by the two-sided ideal Lambda^(q+1)W_Lambda, proving the
product and kernel claims. The degree certificate and the explicit
invisible monomial give sufficiency and the obstruction. Factorials
remain invertible in the truncated coefficient ring, so the proof
of (SE31.4) works there without change. QED.

For \(A=D+\Lambda X\), the degree-zero cut reads only partial_x;
one additional retained layer reads X and recovers this whole family.
An invisible layer is a specified missing readout, not evidence that
the original operator vanishes. The individual layers are coefficient
readings of the existing SE30 graded-cut construction after lower
layers have been subtracted. Collectively they supply Smriti.

The count-preserving map n Lambda -> n u is **degree one** in SE30.
It is not replaced by (SE31.6), which is a degree-zero formula/operator
cut with its own target. No single untyped map is declared to do both.

## 4. Theorem 3: ordered Euler flow retains its central factor

Use central formal parameters a,b. Exponentials are coefficientwise
series in W_Lambda[[a,b]], with no convergence claim. Then

\[
 \boxed{e^{aD}e^{bX}=e^{ab}e^{bX}e^{aD},}
 \qquad
 e^{aD}e^{bX}e^{-aD}e^{-bX}=e^{ab}I.
 \tag{SE31.9}
\]

On P[[b]], e^(aD) is interpreted coefficientwise: D is locally
nilpotent on every polynomial coefficient, so each such coefficient
requires only finitely many D-powers. Acting on the unit gives

\[
 e^{bX}1=\mathcal E_\Lambda(x,b),\qquad
 e^{aD}\mathcal E_\Lambda(x,b)
       =e^{ab}\mathcal E_\Lambda(x,b).
 \tag{SE31.10}
\]

Here E_Lambda is exactly SE30's counting exponential. The operator
cut and every memory quotient M_q commute coefficientwise with the
formal products and exponentials. In particular the central factor
e^(ab) survives the cut; declaring D and X to commute would lose it.

**Proof.** Compare the coefficient of a^i b^j in (SE31.9). On the
left it is D^i X^j/(i!j!). Equation (SE31.3) rewrites it as
\(\sum_{v\le\min(i,j)}X^{j-v}D^{i-v}/
  (v!(i-v)!(j-v)!)\), exactly the right coefficient. Formal inverses
give the second identity. X^n1=p_n proves the first equation of
(SE31.10); Dp_n=np_(n-1) proves the second. The quotient maps are
algebra homomorphisms and all coefficients involve finite sums,
which licenses the stated cut compatibility. QED.

This supplies an ordered-flow attachment for the SE30 pair. It does
not equate this pair to the R,K primitives of T55: their defining
relations differ. T41's analytic bounded-generator assertions are
not used to treat these polynomial operators as bounded.

## 5. Theorem 4: exact bridge to ordinary translations

Let T_a f(x)=f(x+a). On each polynomial the following sums terminate:

\[
 T_a=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{p_n(a)}{n!}D^n,
 \qquad T_\Lambda=I+\Lambda D,
 \tag{SE31.11}
\]
\[
 \boxed{G_\Lambda:=\sum_{n\ge1}
    \frac{(-1)^{n-1}\Lambda^{n-1}}{n}D^n
    =\partial_x,\qquad T_a=e^{aG_\Lambda}.}
 \tag{SE31.12}
\]

Thus the original polynomial derivative is recovered from the unit-step
operator by a regular logarithmic expression, without division by an
erased unit. The shorthand log(I+Lambda D)/Lambda is licensed only
by the already regular expression (SE31.12).

The counting flow V_a=e^(aD) has additive composition
V_a V_b=V_(a+b), but is generally different from T_a before the cut:

\[
 V_a x^2=x^2+2ax+a^2+a\Lambda,
 \qquad T_a x^2=x^2+2ax+a^2.
 \tag{SE31.13}
\]

Their degree-zero cut members agree. The first retained unit layer
of the displayed difference is a, a recoverable response rather than
an unexplained numerical error. No universal physical reading of
that response is claimed.

**Proof.** SE30's addition formula for E_Lambda implies
\(p_k(x+a)=\sum_{n=0}^k {k\choose n}p_n(a)p_{k-n}(x)\).
The lowering law proves (SE31.11) on the basis and hence on P.
Independently the finite polynomial Taylor formula gives
T_Lambda=exp(Lambda partial_x). On the invariant subspace of x-degree
at most m, both Lambda partial_x and T_Lambda-I are nilpotent.
The finite formal log and exp identities give
log(I+Lambda D)=Lambda partial_x. Each term on the left has the
factor Lambda and its formal factor removal is (SE31.12). As P has
no Lambda torsion this yields the asserted equality, including its
regular coefficient-cut member. Local nilpotence proves translation
by exponentiation, and direct action on x^2 proves (SE31.13). QED.

## 6. Theorem 5: finite observation windows retain a boundary defect

Let P_N project onto span_R(p_0,...,p_N) in the counting basis.
Use compressed operators D_N=P_N D P_N, X_N=P_N X P_N on that
N+1 dimensional carrier. With E_N the projection onto p_N,

\[
 \boxed{[D_N,X_N]=I-(N+1)E_N.}
 \tag{SE31.14}
\]

No two finite square matrices over a characteristic-zero field can
satisfy [A,B]=I: the trace of a commutator is null while tr(I)
is the positive dimension. Thus the matrices here are **observation
windows**, not exact finite representations of the whole Weyl algebra.

There is a useful exact action certificate for a finite word w. Read
its tokens from right to left, and let H(w) be the maximum, including
the empty prefix, of the running number of X's minus the number of
D's. For inputs of counting degree at most m, the bound

\[
 N\ge m+H(w)
 \tag{SE31.15}
\]

ensures compressed word evaluation equals the uncompressed action.
A polynomial in words uses the maximum bound over its terms.
For a formal-flow coefficient only its finitely many contributing
words need this guard. A fixed window is not thereby valid for all
coefficients of a raising exponential.

**Proof.** On p_n for n<N, D_N X_N and X_N D_N have coefficients
n+1 and n. On p_N the former is null and the latter is N p_N,
giving (SE31.14). Cyclicity of finite trace proves the impossibility
claim. During a word action, an initial p_k has intermediate index
k plus the running X-minus-D count, until a D on p_0 annihilates
that path. Bound (SE31.15) prevents every surviving intermediate
state from passing above N. No projection then changes that path,
and linearity gives the assertion. QED.

The failure without the guard is already visible in w=DX on p_N:
the full action is (N+1)p_N, but the compressed action is null.
Discarding this upper boundary response would invent a false identity.
It is not the same operation as the coefficient/unit cut in Theorem 2.

## 7. Scope and next use

There are now two explicit finite certificates: a declared operator
order supplies enough counting probes; a declared Lambda-degree
supplies enough unit-memory layers. The reconstruction is algebraic
and exact. Ordered products, flow coefficients and finite windows have
their own checks, so comparison cannot silently commute generators,
erase a unit response or clip a needed intermediate state.

Attaching this to the actual RH operator would require a proved
representation/approximation of that source in the declared class,
and controlled tails and decoder stability when the order or memory
is infinite. Those facts are not consequences of the finite recovery
theorem. SE27's eta=1079/2000 and native floor 1/492000 are unchanged.
No eta-zero certificate, RH proof or new prime distribution is claimed.
