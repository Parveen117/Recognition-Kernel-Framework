## Primary analytic and numerical sources

[NIST DLMF 5.11](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11) supplies the complex digamma
expansion. The bound used here is explicitly derived from the periodic
Bernoulli integral with Re Z=513/4; no real-positive-only error rule is
applied to complex arguments. The independent control uses shift 2048,
not the evaluator's shift 128 or SE11's positive-series implementation.

[NIST DLMF 10.54](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.54) supplies the Legendre
integral for spherical Bessel functions. It gives the real derivative
bound used to transfer a rational point evaluation to a whole node
interval. The normalized recurrence is the standard spherical-Bessel
recurrence rewritten to avoid loss at small arguments. Exact integer
interval arithmetic and the all-odd Parseval identity independently
control the implementation. No new special-function identity is claimed.

[Zhu, arXiv:2608.24827v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24827v2), Sections
3--6, supplies version-specific prior art for finite-frequency
comparison and complete Legendre-tail certification. SE12 imports no
numerical sign or constant from that paper. Gauss quadrature and
Legendre normalization retain the earlier
[DLMF 3.5](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.5) and
[18.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.3) lineage. The quadrature remainder is
the Chebyshev proof already given in SE7.

The digamma, Bessel and arXiv pages were inspected on 12 October 2026;

using the exact support-zero marker cos(t), a certified finite tail
corridor and an analytic envelope beyond it. The retained matrix,
quadrature error, node error and infinite complement are all enclosed.
