## Reproducibility and novelty limits

SciPy selected the aperture parameters and proposed the cutoff trial
vector. Those calculations are not certificates and are not runtime
dependencies of either replay. The trial's stored twelve-place decimals
are exact rationals. Its scalar reading is re-enclosed directly with
an analytic quadrature error; no eigensolver precision is trusted.

Adding dormant prime 5 leaves the full source unchanged by support.
SE9 already warns that this does not make its finite-band cosine zero.
SE11 records a concrete sign change in one trial reading after adding
that cosine. The modified comparison's high-frequency minorant is
not certified, so that observation supplies no new positive source
certificate and no prime-5 crossing.

The main matrix uses 90-digit outward arithmetic at nodes, exact
50-digit-grid integer sums, and a forty-significant-digit rational
archive. These are working precisions, not asserted final accuracies
of every intermediate value. The propagated total matrix error is
below 8.077e-22 and the full-source floor is 4e-15.
