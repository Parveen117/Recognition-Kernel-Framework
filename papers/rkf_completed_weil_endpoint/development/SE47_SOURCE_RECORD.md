## Decision and overlap

The requested next step was clarified to mean the programme's existing
5 by 5 operator. SE47 therefore returns to its one-boundary plus four
accepted-coupling architecture. It does not substitute a five-number
prime catalogue or declare five ordinary eigenvalues to be zeta zeros.

T02, the thermo provenance chapter and the old endpoint manuscript
already contain the five-label Schur mechanism. SE1 states the energy
domain and tail contract. SE5 attaches the corrected source but explicitly
leaves the historical five-column realization open. SE21 supplies a
complete arithmetic source enclosure on V_16 at eta zero. SE47 combines
these actual inputs with an explicit fixed accepted space and complement.

The new accepted space is E=span(f_2,f_3,f_4,f_5), with its fixed L2-moment
projection inside the already admitted native completion X. It is not
asserted to coincide with the old raw E_3 basis. This distinction matters:
the current computation is a source-attached realization of the same
1+4 architecture, not a replay of an unavailable historical numerical
packet. The source bound itself is not improved.

The contribution is the explicit current-source attachment, retained
metric and comparison seam, source-correlated inverse reading, and exact
next-observation update, together with actual arithmetic execution.
Schur complements, congruence and block inversion are established
mechanisms; the displayed proofs make no general novelty claim for them.

coefficients, actual boundary readings, source hash, label metric G,
response Gram H and gap K. All five columns use the same source form
and complement. Their off-diagonal products are retained. A nonorthogonal
coordinate basis does not silently replace the native pairing by I.
