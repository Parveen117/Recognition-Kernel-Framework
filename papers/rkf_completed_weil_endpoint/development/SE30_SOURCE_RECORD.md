The user redirected the work to constant-lambda counting before any
curvature development and clarified that zero denotes the cut operator.
SE30 implements that order. Its first object is a nonempty string of
one marked unit. Concatenation and repeated grouping define the positive
arithmetic. The neutral class needed for later algebra is derived from
equal-count pairs and is separately typed. No geometric cut or chosen
number-line origin enters the initial counting layer.

The next operation is a declared graded cut on unit-polynomial packets.
It retains the coefficient and unit degree instead of annihilating the
count. Its uniqueness follows from the stated retained-unit and discarded-
higher-order contract. Writing "at lambda zero" in this stage means
applying this operator to a typed family; it does not mean replacing all
marked units by a numerical scalar. SE28's raw-evaluation discussion and
the new cut action consequently are different operations.

The polynomial basis is chosen by ordered selection of distinct tokens.
Its lowering operator is the unit-step difference with its formal unit
factor removed. The raising operator and transported product then follow
from the basis, and the exponential is its generating series. The
coefficient cut yields the derivative, powers and exponential. No
curvature equation selects these objects.

Classical lineage is acknowledged rather than asserted to be newly
invented. The elementary counting, group-completion and prime-factorization
arguments are given in the note. Falling factorials and the triangular
power-basis relations were checked against
[NIST DLMF 26.8.7 and 26.8.10](https://dlmf.nist.gov/26.8).
The general graded quotient construction is consistent with the
[Stacks Project, tag 062Z](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/062Z),
whose affine description is direct-sum I^d/I^(d+1). These primary reference
pages were retrieved on 12 October 2026. SE30 proves its elementary
principal-unit case directly; it imports no geometry or Hilbert-space
assumptions from the general theory.

The new results here are the explicit integration, typed construction,
cut contract and exact application within this workstream. No novelty
priority is claimed for standard counting, finite-difference, polynomial
or formal-generating-function identities in isolation. No new prime
distribution or universal physical unit is derived. The action on the
actual RH source has not changed.
