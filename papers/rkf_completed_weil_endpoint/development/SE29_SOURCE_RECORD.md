The request was to prove the missing RH conclusion after the lambda/circle
development. The audit therefore examined the actual observer contraction,
not just zeta's inclusion in an Euler family. It found an obstruction to
the particular canonical-curvature route, together with an explicit way
to retain the information that route loses. The source inequality itself
remains unproved. This result is recorded to prevent repetition of the
invalid inference, not as completion of the user's requested RH proof.

The chosen attachment A=T(I+T)^(-1) is explicit and reversible. It is
not claimed to be uniquely induced by physical thermodynamics. The
countermodels contain no RH arithmetic, so they refute a curvature-only
implication and do not refute RH. The essential curvature theorem does
use the actual SE5 source and its established threshold sequence.

Classical lineage was checked on 12 October 2026. Douglas's bounded
factorization theorem distinguishes the existence of a factor from a
specified norm bound; it supplies no missing unit bound for this source:
R. G. Douglas, *On majorization, factorization, and range inclusion of
operators on Hilbert space*, Proc. AMS 17 (1966), 413--415,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1966-0203464-1),
[primary paper mirror](https://home.agh.edu.pl/~rudol/Operat/DouglasFactorizationLemma.pdf).
The three-page primary paper was retrieved; Theorem 1 and its norm statement
were read. The source-specific contraction equivalence is already in SE5.
