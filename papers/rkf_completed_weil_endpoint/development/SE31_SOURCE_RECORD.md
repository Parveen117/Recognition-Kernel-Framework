The missing link chosen after this reading is an explicit recovery
algorithm for the SE30 operator class, together with its required order
and memory bounds. It gives a concrete case of the existing observer
factorization principle rather than claiming to invent that principle.
Normal-order coefficients become recoverable from successive counting
responses; the same recurrence descends to a retained unit-jet quotient.

The Euler connection is the ordered exponential of this actual pair,
with its noncommuting central factor. The ordinary shift is a separate
flow until the coefficient cut; a locally finite logarithmic formula
connects the two generators. Finite matrix observation windows retain
their exact top-boundary loss. None of these statements imports a
positive Hilbert metric or supplies an RH source sign.

Classical method lineage is acknowledged. The normal-order Weyl
identities, triangular polynomial-operator reconstruction, finite-
difference/Newton formulas and formal exponential identities are
standard algebraic mechanisms, not new-priority claims. The article
G. C. Rota, D. Kahaner and A. M. Odlyzko, *On the foundations of
combinatorial theory. VIII. Finite operator calculus*, Journal of
Mathematical Analysis and Applications 42 (1973), 684--760, is listed
on [Odlyzko's own publications page](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/combinatorics.html).
That primary bibliographic record was retrieved on 12 October 2026;
the complete article was not retrieved in this stage, and no specific
unread theorem is imported from it. SE30's retrieved NIST falling-
factorial references remain the earlier basis provenance. The present
note gives its needed proofs directly.
