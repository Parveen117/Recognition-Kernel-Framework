in the admitted T35 graph. Typed cuts, cut scalars, derived iota,

not converge along the refinement path.

Legendre-head approximation at each fixed frequency. These are
application-specific extensions of the existing proof, not a priority
claim for general compactness or spectral approximation theory.

discipline. SE13's index note retains the distinctions among faithful

The new calibration theorem has a fixed aperture and cap. It does
not establish cofinal aperture coverage or prove the global eta-zero
condition. SE5's essential zero threshold and SE13's escaping-packet

complete tail proof. No universal marker-selection rule is asserted.
