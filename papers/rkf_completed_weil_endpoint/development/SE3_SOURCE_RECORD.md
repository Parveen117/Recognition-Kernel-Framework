## Primary literature and priority

M. S. Adamo, K.-H. Neeb and J. Schober,
*Reflection positivity and its relation to disc, half plane and the strip*,
[arXiv:2407.21123v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.21123v1), Appendix A.3.
The original text was read, including the strip boundary graph realization
and its Fourier-derived reproducing kernel. It establishes the existing
Hardy/Szego lineage. SE3 uses its own explicitly stated Fourier measure
and derives the normalization and odd evaluation directly.

The recovery/majorization lineage is the Douglas paper already cited in
SE2. The regularized inverse and projection mechanisms are not claimed as
new general operator theory. The source-bound contribution here is their
explicit composition with the RKF native graph, actual prime/Gamma rows,
transported debt and joint observer criterion.

The paper above is not an RH source-positivity theorem, and no such
application is attributed to its authors. The [pins](SE3_SOURCE_PINS.json)
record the exact repository inputs; mathematical and external-review
status are separate.
