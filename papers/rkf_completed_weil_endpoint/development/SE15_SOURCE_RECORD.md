nullity at that positive shift follow from the full written operator
bound and enclosed constants, not from a finite spectral sample.
Large positive shifts are not themselves a new route to the eta-zero
sign; this stage makes one source-specific bound explicit.

The second addition is a localization identity for this exact source,
with all Gamma, prime and pole cross terms retained, and explicit
tail and exterior budgets. It yields a sufficient quantitative aperture
and physical local tolerance for each positive eta. The auxiliary

of negative subspaces, not an assumed native isometry of the cuts.

The weighted Cauchy--Schwarz inequality, alternating-series remainder,
Laplace transform, integral tail comparison and inertia restriction
are classical tools. No priority is claimed for those tools or general
IMS localization. The source-specific constants and reductions are
the contribution of this development note, without a literature-wide
novelty claim. Earlier DLMF, Zhu, Atiyah--Singer, Robbin--Salamon and
Birman--Schwinger references remain preserved in the source pins.
NSB1/NSB2 remain methodological lineage; their nonlinear hypotheses
are not used as unstated hypotheses of the present source.

outward intervals. A fresh read-only replay checks all 39 scalar

proof-assistant verification of the written infinite-dimensional proofs.

arithmetic budgets nor a finite collection of positive-eta results
proves a cofinal family. The source remains unproved at eta zero;
global contractivity, the historical five-label endpoint, K0 and RH
remain open. External review and remote-CI success are not claimed.
