weight uses Lambda(4)=log 2. This stage certifies radius 7/10 with an

finite Galerkin eigenvalue to the historical record.

## Primary numerical-analysis literature checked

[Zhu, arXiv:2608.24827v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24827v2), Sections
2--6, remains prior art for the frequency-envelope, Legendre and
complete-tail method. Its monotone numerical comparisons are also
relevant context. The version reports larger compact-window results;
SE10 is not a claim of a world-record aperture or a new general Weil
criterion. No numerical archive or larger-window certificate from the
paper is imported as evidence for the RKF source. The local lower-edge
argument is written in full here, rather than asserted from that paper.

[NIST DLMF 3.5](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.5), particularly Gauss--Legendre
quadrature, and [DLMF 18.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.3) supply the
classical orthogonal-polynomial normalization and quadrature facts.
The replay does not trust displayed decimal nodes: it brackets all
twenty exact roots and encloses weights using the derivative formula.
Positive weights, exactness through degree 39 and the analytic
Chebyshev error estimate have distinct roles in the proof.

[NIST DLMF 5.11](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11) supplies the shifted
digamma expansion used in the independent control. The control shifts
z=1/4+it/2 by 128 and uses exact rational complex powers through B_12.
Its remainder is bounded directly from the periodic-Bernoulli integral
by |B_12|/(12(Re z)^12); the real-positive first-neglected-term rule
is not silently applied to a complex argument. This control does not
use the Gamma-ground constant or the alternating tail of the main
assembler. The main Gamma/Bessel lineage remains in SE7's source record.

The linked primary pages were inspected on 12 October 2026. The arXiv
attribution remains version-specific to the v2 HTML already recorded
in SE7--SE9.

## Arithmetic and refinement boundary

The old outward interval class, constants, cosine, Hurwitz power sums
and dimension-aware LDL are imported without edits. Only new files
contain Gauss-20, the 32-term Gamma tail and the 160-correction Bessel
seeds for the larger range. The ninety-digit ambient guard remains.
The candidate matrix has forty significant decimal digits, with its
rounding included in the proof budget.

At z=96 the enclosed Bessel recurrence loses about forty working
digits through cancellation; the independently tested j_1 interval
width is below 4.546e-49. This is retained as an interval, not rounded
away or treated as a numerical failure of the source. The full matrix
error, including these widths, is below 1.148e-13.

SciPy computations only selected parameters. The interval replay,
analytic remainders and positive rational-candidate LDL establish the
published floor. Special-function controls, finite arithmetic proof,
written infinite-dimensional implications and external review are
separate evidence classes.
