## Primary literature actually consulted

[Xuefeng Zhu, arXiv:2608.24827v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24827v2),
*Weil positivity in compact windows: a finite reduction, certified
two-sided bounds, and a Landau--Widom decay law*, version dated
2 September 2026: Sections 2--6 describe frequency splitting, a finite
Legendre block, analytic quadrature bounds and complete tail/coupling
control. Section 6 retains a negative pole in the odd sector. This
provides the method-level prior art; SE7 claims no invention of that
general reduction. The paper's numerical archives were not retrieved
or independently reproduced, and its reported larger-window constants
are not used as inputs. The version-specific HTML is cited because
search metadata for another version reported different authorship.

[NIST DLMF 5.7.6](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.7.E6) supplies the digamma
series, [5.11](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11) the real-positive asymptotic
remainder, [25.11(iii)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.11.iii) the
Euler--Maclaurin power-sum formula, and
[10.54](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.54) the spherical-Bessel integral.
The actual conservative error estimates used here are written in
SE7 and replayed without a special-function library.

The earlier Yoshida--Suzuki small-support and finite-codimension
lineage remains as recorded in [SE6](SE6_SOURCE_RECORD.md).

## Local contribution and evidence

The local result is the quantitative radius-1/2 odd-source certificate,
its exact attachment to the native graph norm and observer, and the
resulting sign of the existing F_15. The comparison has forty Legendre
coordinates, but its infinite complement and coupling are bounded too.

The replay uses only Python's standard library: exact rational budgets,
outward-rounded interval arithmetic, rational isolation of quadrature
nodes and interval LDL positivity. Archived decimal entries are exact
rational candidates accompanied by explicit error bounds. The written
proof remains an analytic input to the computation.

Preliminary floating-point sine/Legendre probes guided the choice of
frequency split. They are not certificate evidence or lower bounds.
A split at 24 was inadequate in the probe; the proved split is 48.
Neither numerical proximity to zero nor a successful sampled matrix
would suffice without the error and infinite-tail estimates.
