\begin{abstract}
This draft records the cleaned Recognition Kernel Framework route for the completed-Weil endpoint.  The earlier proof-lab route contained several overlapping presentations: parity splitting, seam integers, UGD holonomy, accepted-block arithmetic, endpoint matrices, and classical explicit-formula language.  Here these are reorganized as one dependency chain.  The framework is used as a typed anti-blindness ledger: source restriction, blind kernels, path normalization, sector reduction and endpoint support descent are each represented by a specific theorem node.  The proof spine is the positive-eta amplitude path-equality theorem, the common five-matrix seam-integer reduction, the certified positivity of the target-local scalar \(M_3\), the one-sector terminal reduction, and the zero-cut T03 endpoint completion.  The native endpoint is the projection-defect inequality
\[
K_0=\RSigma^*(I-\Psrc)\RSigma\ge0.
\]
When the classical completed explicit formula and Weil criterion are consumed in the same declared normalization, the endpoint implies the Riemann hypothesis.  The proof-lab details are kept outside the main text in the local theorem capsules \texttt{theorum/} and reusable method notes \texttt{mp\_gold/}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The purpose of this version is not to enlarge the paper.  It is to make the route readable and inevitable.  The central claim is that the Recognition Kernel Framework is not an extra hypothesis.  It is a typed bookkeeping device needed to prevent target-relevant information from being lost during cuts, source restrictions, finite observation, normalization, sector reduction and endpoint descent.

The cleaned chain is
\[
\text{path equality}
\longrightarrow
\text{five-matrix seam integer}
\longrightarrow
M_3>0
\longrightarrow
\text{single-sector endpoint}
\longrightarrow
K_0\ge0.
\]
This replaces the older fragile presentation that attempted to close the endpoint through a separate even-source positivity assertion.  That assertion is not part of this proof spine.

The classical layer is kept separate.  The native framework constructs and closes the completed-Weil endpoint in the declared normalization.  The implication from completed-Weil positivity to the Riemann hypothesis is then the classical completed explicit formula and Weil criterion in that same normalization.

We separate three layers.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

\section{Why the framework is not over-engineering}

The framework objects are introduced only when an ordinary operator presentation loses typed information.  The minimal obstruction ledger is:

\begin{itemize}
\item source restriction is repaired by the source-restriction rank formula;
\item target-relevant blind kernels are repaired by minimal lifts or decoders;
\item normalization path ambiguity is repaired by the positive-eta amplitude theorem;
\item representation dependence is repaired by common five-matrix covariance;
\item finite accepted-block uncertainty is repaired by \(G_3>0\) and \(M_3>0\);
\item endpoint support loss is repaired by the zero-cut T03 theorem.
\end{itemize}

Thus the framework is a typed obstruction ledger, not decorative terminology.

\begin{theorem}[Source restriction and minimal lift]
Let \(S(q)P\) be a rich split representation and let \(B\) be a consumed source restriction.  Put
\[
A(q)=B S(q)P,
\qquad
S(q)=\sum_s q^sD_s.
\]
Then the all-parameter observable rank is the rank of the vertical stack of the maps \(BD_sP\), and the blind kernel is
\[
\bigcap_s\ker(BD_sP).
\]
If scalar supplemental observations \(G\) are added, the augmented map is injective exactly when
\[
\ker A\cap\ker G=\{0\}.
\]
Consequently at least \(\dim\ker A\) scalar observations are necessary to repair a full blind defect.  Target-relative repair requires only that the remaining blind kernel lie inside the target kernel.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof source]
This is the transferred source-restriction/minimal-lift theorem recorded in \texttt{mp\_gold/04\_source\_restriction\_and\_arrow\_contracts.md}.  It is consumed here as the reason that blind quotients and lifts must be part of the endpoint proof data.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Arrow contracts]
The associated arrow-contract theorem requires each proof arrow to declare its domain, codomain, lawful nullspace, source restriction, blind kernel, target relevance, decoder or lift, completion topology, margin and claim status.  This rejects false endpoint promotion whenever target-relevant information remains blind.
\end{remark}

\section{Positive-eta amplitude analyticity}

The analytic theorem is a main theorem of the paper.  It shows that the positive-eta family has no normalization-path ambiguity on the fixed five-label carrier.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-five positive-eta path equality]
Let
\[
A_{\rm chart}=AU,
\qquad
\Theta_\eta=Q_{\rm chart}G_\eta^{1/2},
\qquad
G_\eta=1\oplus(A_+ + \eta I_4),\quad \eta>0.
\]
Here \(A\) is the eta-independent raw amplitude, \(U\) is the fixed unitary accepted chart, \(Q_{\rm chart}\) is the polar orientation of \(A_{\rm chart}\), and \(G_\eta^{1/2}\) is strictly positive.  Then
\[
\operatorname{polar}(\Theta_\eta)=Q_{\rm chart}
\]
for every positive eta.  Hence, for any positive eta values \(\eta_1,\eta_2\),
\[
\text{raw eta transport}=I_5,\qquad
\text{normalized eta transport}=I_5,\qquad
\text{raw-to-normalized transport}=U^*.
\]
All positive-eta normalization/continuation rectangles are identity loops on the fixed five-label carrier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Right multiplication by a unitary is polar-covariant, so \(\operatorname{polar}(AU)=\operatorname{polar}(A)U\).  Right multiplication by a positive factor changes only the metric scale and does not change the polar orientation.  Since \(G_\eta^{1/2}>0\), the polar orientation of \(Q_{\rm chart}G_\eta^{1/2}\) is \(Q_{\rm chart}\).  The claimed transports follow immediately.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Finite rectangle]
The state-locked rectangle at \(\eta=0.010\) and \(\eta+h=0.011\) reported path transport difference \(3.0669860554696173\cdot10^{-15}\), loop identity residual \(3.1396725820594018\cdot10^{-15}\), defect-to-accepted leakage \(1.7140530928543942\cdot10^{-17}\), endpoint scale difference \(0\), and no false checks.  These values are the numerical shadow of the exact factorization above, not a separate sign proof.
\end{remark}

The theorem converts the endpoint question from a path-holonomy problem into a support/Fredholm problem at eta zero, where \(G_0=1\oplus A_+\) may lose support.

\section{Seam integer and the common five-matrix}

The sign-sensitive obstruction is represented by one rank-at-most-five matrix.

\begin{definition}[Seam charge]
Let \(K_\eta\) be the compact seam response at positive shift eta.  Define
\[
\kSigma(\eta)=\operatorname{rank}\mathbf 1_{(1,\infty)}(K_\eta).
\]
On the five-label source packet this is equivalently
\[
\BSEta=V_\eta^*S_{\eta,-}^{-1}V_\eta,\qquad \operatorname{rank}V_\eta\le5,
\]
with
\[
\kSigma(\eta)=N_+(\BSEta-I).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Common five-matrix covariance]
For a positive source operator \(S\) and five-column source map \(V\), set
\[
B=V^*S^{-1}V\in M_5(\C).
\]
If \(S^U=USU^*\) and \(V^U=UV\), then
\[
(V^U)^*(S^U)^{-1}V^U=B.
\]
A unitary change of five labels conjugates \(B\).  Therefore spectrum, threshold projection, seam charge and sign decision are representation invariant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Matrix, seam and threshold holonomy]
The native matrix, prime-torus matrix and threshold-holonomy presentation are faithful avatars of the same obstruction:
\[
\BSEta\le I
\iff
\kSigma(\eta)=0
\iff
H_{\rm threshold}(\eta)=I,
\]
where
\[
H_{\rm threshold}(\eta)=I-2\mathbf 1_{(1,\infty)}(\BSEta).
\]
Thus the prime torus is a chart of the same matrix, not a second independent proof route.
\end{corollary}

\[
M_3=q(e_3,e_3)-b_3^*G_3^{-1}b_3.

\begin{theorem}[Certified accepted block]

Therefore \(M_3>0\).  Since \(M_3\) is the Schur complement of \(G_3\) in the bordered Gram, the completed-Weil form is positive definite on \(E_3\).

After the representation-complete seam-integer reduction, the common source-sign theorem and the favorable even-boundary reduction, the even sector is retained in the bilateral carrier but is not an independent terminal gate.  The remaining terminal obstruction is the odd five-label seam packet.

\section{Zero-cut T03 endpoint completion}

The endpoint theorem fixes the target-relevant blind quotient at the zero cut before choosing a lift.

\begin{theorem}[Invariant source-Gram identity]
Let \(\RSigma\) be the total response readout, \(\Psrc\) the source projection, \(\Sminus\) the complete unshifted odd source, and \(\WSigma\) the five-label response map.  The zero-cut T03 completion gives
\[
\RSigma^*\Psrc\RSigma
=
\bigl((\Sminus)^{\dagger/2}\WSigma\bigr)^*
\bigl((\Sminus)^{\dagger/2}\WSigma\bigr).
\]
This identity follows from the six-block decomposition of the five-label Gram defect: visible compression, blind response, cross blocks, omitted prime/Gamma tails, quadrature/window/solve/rounding errors, and native/Feshbach/prime-torus chart transport.  The total directed error tends to zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The theorem does not assume literal equality of ambient source maps.  Equal Grams give a canonical partial-isometry identification of final ranges.  The endpoint sign uses the invariant Gram identity, not an orientation-dependent ambient equality.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Zero-cut projection-defect positivity]
The independently constructed total-response Gram satisfies
\[
\RSigma^*\RSigma=\operatorname{diag}(1,A_3).
\]
Together with the invariant source-Gram identity, this yields
\[
K_0=\RSigma^*(I-\Psrc)\RSigma\ge0.

The endpoint Schur equivalence transfers this projection-defect positivity to the completed-Weil endpoint sign in the declared normalization.
\end{theorem}

\section{Main theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Completed-Weil endpoint theorem]
Assume the native completed-Weil carrier is in the declared normalization and consume the transferred theorem chain: positive-eta path equality, common five-matrix seam-integer reduction, \(G_3>0\), \(M_3>0\), single-sector terminal reduction, and zero-cut T03 endpoint completion.  Then the completed-Weil endpoint sign holds on the target-faithful completed carrier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The positive-eta theorem removes normalization ambiguity on the fixed five-label carrier.  The common five-matrix theorem reduces the sign-sensitive obstruction to the representation-invariant threshold matrix \(\BSEta\) and seam charge \(\kSigma\).  The \(M_3\) certificate proves positivity of the accepted odd block.  The single-sector theorem identifies the remaining terminal packet with the odd five-label source obstruction after the seam/source/even reduction.  The zero-cut T03 theorem supplies the invariant source-Gram identity and hence
\[
K_0=\RSigma^*(I-\Psrc)\RSigma\ge0.
\]
The endpoint Schur equivalence gives the completed-Weil endpoint sign.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Classical terminal implication]
If the classical completed explicit formula identifies the native completed-Weil form with the symmetric zero-sum Weil form in the declared normalization, and if the classical Weil criterion is consumed in that same normalization, then the Riemann hypothesis follows.
\end{corollary}

\section{Objection map}

The revised paper answers the beginning objections as follows.

\begin{itemize}
\item Normalization and eta-continuation commute by positive-eta amplitude factorization.
\item The seam integer is the threshold count of one common five-matrix.
\item The prime torus is a faithful chart of that same matrix.
\item \(M_3\) is certified positive by an exact transferred interval.
\item One sector is sufficient only after the seam/source/even reduction; the even sector is not erased.
\item The endpoint source map is handled by an invariant source-Gram identity rather than orientation-dependent equality.
\item The framework is not over-engineering because each layer repairs a named source, blind-kernel, path or endpoint defect.
\end{itemize}

\section{Claim boundary}

The paper claims the transferred RKF-native endpoint chain and clearly marks the classical membrane.  It does not claim that raw UGD, the prime torus, or a diagonal Euler determinant independently proves the Riemann hypothesis.  It also does not use independent \(b_3\) boxes to prove \(M_3\), and it does not claim that the even sector is absent.
