# Native operator algebra: composition, observers, and honest quotients

Author: Monty Dabas. Development edition: 26 September 2026.
Status: written general arguments under the hypotheses below, with separate exact finite checks. These are a unification and extension of the framework's existing tools, not a priority claim for the underlying linear-algebra methods.

## 0. Dependency order and terminology

Start with the framework's established cut field

    C_Sigma = R_Sigma + iota R_Sigma,
    iota^2 = -1,   iota^dagger = -iota,
    D(a+iota b) = |a|+|b|,   N(z) = z^dagger z.

F00-E supplies the completed cut-field input. T50 supplies finite pair-groupoid path convolution and path-reversal dagger. In a labelled finite face, these operations are represented by matrix multiplication and conjugate transpose; that is a coordinate realization of the declared path algebra, not an assumption that the primitive state is a Hilbert space or spacetime.

The exact executable subfield is Q_Sigma+iota Q_Sigma. The general statements use the established characteristic-zero field C_Sigma. The number of complex channels is not the number of real channels: one cut-complex coordinate has two real components.

The word **kernel** needs its source and codomain. The kernel of a state observer is a subspace of a state module. The kernel of an algebra homomorphism is an ideal of an algebra. They are not interchangeable objects.

## N01. Finite native path algebra and two gradings

On an n-object pair groupoid, set A_n=Mat_n(C_Sigma), with

    (a star b)_ij = sum_k a_ik b_kj,
    (a^dagger)_ij = (a_ji)^dagger.

Finite distributivity proves associativity, the diagonal path identity is a unit, and reversing a concatenated path gives

    (a star b)^dagger = b^dagger star a^dagger.

Let J=J^dagger=J^-1 be an admitted cut on this representation. Define

    a_even=(a+JaJ)/2,   a_odd=(a-JaJ)/2.

Then J a_even J=a_even and J a_odd J=-a_odd. Products obey the usual parity table, since conjugation by J is multiplicative. This is the cut grading, not the scalar radial/turn split or the skew/self-dagger classification. T55's distinction is retained.

For the EMK blocks

    R=[[0,-1],[1,0]],   K=[[0,1],[1,0]],

R^2=-I, K^2=I and [R,K]=2RK. The four matrices I,K,R,RK are linearly independent over C_Sigma and span A_2. Thus the finite EMK representation generates the full two-by-two cut matrix algebra. This is an algebra identification for the declared carrier, not a derivation of a physical Hilbert space, a source coupling, or a spacetime dimension.

## N02. Exact finite algebra saturation

Let S be a finite set of n-by-n generators. Include the identity and, when a dagger algebra is intended, also every generator's dagger. Let

    A_0=span{I},
    A_(r+1)=span(A_r union {a s: a in A_r, s in S}).

Then A_r is the span of all words of length at most r. This follows by induction. If A_(r+1)=A_r, the subspace is right-invariant under all generators, hence contains every further word. It is the generated unital algebra. With the dagger generators included, reversing a word shows it is also dagger-closed.

A strict step increases dimension by at least one; dimension never exceeds n^2. Therefore the first stabilized space is reached after at most n^2-1 strict increases from A_0, with one additional equality check needed to confirm termination. Exact row reduction of vectorized matrices computes a basis and records the rank ladder.

This is the coordinate algebra generated by the supplied matrices. Abstract relations need not be faithfully represented. A relation that happens to vanish on one finite representation is not thereby a universal native identity.

## N03. Minimum future-complete state observation

Let V be a finite-dimensional state module and let C:V->Y be a linear observer. Let L:V->Z be a linear target, and let S be the declared action catalogue. If a target is absent, omit L rather than inventing one. Define a subspace of the algebraic dual by

    W_0=row(C)+row(L),
    W_(r+1)=W_r + sum_(s in S) W_r s.

Let W_infinity be its stabilized value, and let D have any independent row basis of W_infinity. Then:

1. C and L factor through D.
2. Every admitted generator has an exactly descended action s_bar satisfying D s=s_bar D.
3. Every finite word in the generators descends, and every future target L w x can be read from D x.
4. D has minimum rank among linear refinements of C that recover L and descend the action catalogue.
5. The minimum added scalar channels are dim(W_infinity)-rank(C), over the stated field.

**Proof.** The first assertion is row inclusion. For the second, W_infinity s is contained in W_infinity, so each row of D s is a unique combination of the independent rows of D. These coefficients give s_bar. Composition proves the word statement. Any competing observer E with C=B E, L=F E and E s=s_E E has row(E) containing W_0 and invariant under right multiplication by S. Induction gives W_r subset row(E) for every r. Hence rank(E)>=dim(W_infinity); D attains the bound. The chain stabilizes after at most dim(V)-dim(W_0) strict increases. QED.

Equivalent kernel formulation: ker(D) is the largest S-invariant subspace contained in ker(C) intersect ker(L). This is a module quotient. It does not declare ker(C) to be a two-sided ideal in an operator algebra.

Including daggers is an additional action contract. It may increase the required observation rank. In a subsequently admitted positive representation, invariance under both s and s^dagger makes ker(D) reducing. Forward invariance alone does not have that consequence.

**Exact controls.** For C=(1,0) and the EMK R,K, the completed observer has two complex coordinates: one extra complex channel is necessary and sufficient. For a three-component block-diagonal action mixing only components 1 and 2, an observer of component 1 completes to rank two, not three. Thus genuine information-losing, target-faithful module quotients do exist.

## N04. An algebra quotient is a stronger contract

Suppose pi:A->B is an algebra homomorphism. For a in ker(pi) and b in A,

    pi(ba)=pi(b)pi(a)=0,   pi(ab)=pi(a)pi(b)=0.

Thus ker(pi) is a two-sided ideal. If pi is dagger-preserving, the kernel is dagger-stable. Conversely, a two-sided dagger-stable ideal I defines a dagger algebra A/I with well-defined product and involution. The verification consists of expanding products and changing representatives by elements of I.

The full matrix algebra A_n is simple. Indeed, for a nonzero a in a two-sided ideal choose a_ij != 0. Matrix units satisfy

    E_pi a E_jq = a_ij E_pq.

Multiplication by the inverse scalar shows every E_pq belongs to the ideal. Hence the ideal is A_n. A nonzero homomorphism out of A_n is consequently injective. In particular, one cannot retain a nonzero faithful algebra quotient of a full matrix sector by simply deleting some of its coordinates.

This does not forbid reduced states, compressions, or subsystem measurements. Those are different maps and are not generally algebra homomorphisms. Direct-sum algebras can have proper ideals: projection C_Sigma direct-sum C_Sigma -> C_Sigma onto the first summand is a nontrivial proper quotient.

## N05. Constructive minimum repair for an algebra-valued observer

For a finite-dimensional dagger algebra A and complex-linear readouts C,L on A, form W_0=row(C)+row(L). Close W_0 under

    f -> [a -> f(s a)],
    f -> [a -> f(a s)],
    f -> f^sharp, where f^sharp(a)=conjugate(f(a^dagger)),

for the chosen algebra generators and their daggers. Each f^sharp is complex-linear, although the transformation f->f^sharp is conjugate-linear. Take the complex span at every step. In finite dimension the chain stabilizes.

The annihilator I of the final W is contained in the initial observation/target kernels and is closed under generator multiplication on both sides and under dagger. Since the generators produce A, I is a two-sided dagger ideal. Conversely, the annihilator of any such ideal contained in the initial kernels is a closed row space of this kind containing W_0. The same minimality proof as N03 therefore gives the largest admissible invisible ideal and the smallest repaired linear readout realizing an algebra quotient.

The executable implementation currently covers a **generated full matrix algebra**, and rejects the non-full case rather than silently interpreting the whole matrix space as the generated subalgebra. For one nonzero coordinate functional on A_2, closure requires all four complex algebra coordinates: three additional functionals. This differs from N03's one additional complex state coordinate. State reconstruction and algebra reconstruction have different dimensions.

## N06. The compression defect is returning memory

Let P^2=P and Q=I-P, initially just an algebraic split. Define Phi_P(a)=P a P, regarded in the corner with identity P. Inserting I=P+Q gives the exact identity

    Phi_P(ab)-Phi_P(a)Phi_P(b)=P a Q b P.

The right side is a path that leaves the retained sector and returns. Erasing it is an intervention, not an exact representation of the original composition.

In a positive dagger representation with P=P^dagger,

    Phi_P(a^dagger a)-Phi_P(a^dagger)Phi_P(a)
      =(Q a P)^dagger (Q a P).

Thus if compression is multiplicative on a dagger-closed algebra for every a, Q a P=0. Applying the same statement to a^dagger gives P a Q=0. Therefore P commutes with the algebra. Conversely a commuting P has zero defect for every a,b, so compression is a dagger homomorphism onto its corner. Positivity is used in the implication X^dagger X=0 -> X=0; it is not covertly assumed in the purely algebraic expansion.

For K swapping two states and P retaining only the first, P K P=0 but P K^2 P=P. The observed two-step return is 1, while multiplication of the reset one-step observations gives 0. This is an exact finite witness, not a gravitational-curvature observation.

## Scope and lineage

N01 consumes F00-E, T41, T48 and T50/T55. N02-N05 organize the framework's observer/minimum-repair line T24, T28, T31, T32 and the U-series into explicit finite module-versus-algebra algorithms. N06 is the existing cut-corner memory identity promoted to a typed algebra contract. Finite word saturation, invariant-subspace reconstruction, matrix-algebra simplicity and reducing-subspace compression are standard mathematical mechanisms; their priority is not claimed here.

No theorem here selects the catalogue S, the physical state preparation, or the observation C for nature. They tell us exactly what a supplied catalogue and target permit, and how much memory must be retained.
