# Singularity Calculus Vertical Freeze — v5

The finite transverse normal-crossing development is frozen at SC-18.

This is a scope decision, not a claim that all singularity mathematics is solved.
The reason is structural:

\[
\boxed{D_\Delta^2=0}
\]

already generates the compatibility law at every finite codimension. Writing
separate theorems for four, five, six, or more transverse seams would repeat the
same alternating-complex mathematics with longer subscripts.

A future Singularity Calculus theorem should therefore introduce at least one
new mathematical ingredient, for example:

- nontransverse or singular intersections;
- infinite/accumulating seam families;
- analytic convergence or regularity estimates;
- operator-domain questions;
- a domain adapter with independently testable physical observables.

The preferred next framework development is not another vertical Singularity
capsule. It is integration of the v5 stratified obstruction carrier with Morphic
Recognition and target-faithfulness.
