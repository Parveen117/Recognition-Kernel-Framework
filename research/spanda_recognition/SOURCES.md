## Textual questions

The Tamil Nataraja image tradition and the nondual Kashmir Shaiva texts
below are related research directions with different historical and doctrinal
contexts. The comparison does not treat them as a single technical system.

| Source | Relevant passage or image | Question carried into SP1 |
|---|---|---|
| [Shiva Tandava Stotram](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_shiva/shivtAND.html), traditionally attributed to Ravana | Verses 1, 2 and 11 describe rhythm, flowing water, fire and dance. Verse 12 concerns equanimity; it is not a mathematical invariance assertion. | What differs between rhythmic repetition and return of a retained state? |
| [Met Museum, Shiva as Lord of Dance](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/39328), Tamil Nadu, late 11th century | Drum, flame, protective gesture and raised foot carry creation, destruction and liberation symbolism. | Can one source configuration recur while accumulated records differ? |
| [Spanda Karika with Ksemaraja's commentary](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_vasugupta-spandakArikA-comm.htm), 1.1; e-text based on M. K. Shastri's 1925 edition | Manifestation and withdrawal are discussed with the inseparable power of consciousness. | How can an admitted carrier retain identity while its transformations act? |
| [Shiva Sutra Vimarshini III, 9-11](https://mail.sanskrit-sanscrito.com/en/shiva-sutra-vimarshini-iii-1-11-trika-scriptures-non-dual-shaivism-of-kashmir/555) | Self as dancing actor, inner self as stage, senses as spectators, in a yogic recognition context. | How does an observer inside the modeled process access its retained history? |
| [Pratyabhijnahrdaya](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/6_sastra/3_phil/saiva/pratyabu.htm), 1-5 and 10-13 | Manifestation, contraction, recognition and five activities; the commentary on 11 discusses impressions surviving withdrawal. | Can concealment of a reading be distinguished from erasure of its underlying record? |

These are research prompts. Spanda is not identified with a physical frequency,
Pratyabhijna's spiritual self-recognition is not identified with an algorithm,
and the five activities are not assigned five arbitrary matrices. The affine
law, modules and coefficient choices in SP1 are declared modern constructions.

## Mathematical lineage

- [MR-03](../../theorum/morphic_recognition/03_cocycle_lifted_path_recognition.md):
  lawful central two-cocycle memory and coordinate/coboundary discipline.
  SP1 uses transported affine increments on words, a distinct one-cocycle
  construction; it does not silently replace MR-03's extension.
- [MR-04](../../theorum/morphic_recognition/04_future_complete_linear_recognition.md):
  the exact future-row closure, finite witness horizon and minimum linear
  memory. SP1.4 reuses it after removal of known affine offsets.
- [SE41](../lambda_counting/SE41_ORDERED_RETURN_AND_MOVING_CUT_FEEDBACK.md):
  changing observer calibration retains the original return; vanishing
  relative readings need not remove the source. SP1.3 gives a separate
  affine-coordinate identity rather than a new SE41 convergence result.
- [T62](../../theorum/62_silence_vibration_sutra_flow_theorem.md):
  the earlier supplied-generator vibration specialization. SP1 treats
  discrete repeated protocols, not a new derivation of its oscillation law.
- Kenneth S. Brown, [Lectures on the Cohomology of Groups](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~kbrown/papers/cohomology_hangzhou.pdf):
  Example 3.1, cyclic resolutions and coefficient cohomology supply the standard lineage
  for the quotient `M/(I-H)M`. For invertible H it describes the first
  cohomology of the infinite cyclic action; SP1 proves the needed quotient
  statements directly and also permits noninvertible H.
- Tullia Dymarz and Xiangdong Xie,
  [Day's Fixed Point Theorem, Group Cohomology and Quasi-Isometric Rigidity](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~dymarz/day2015.1.25.pdf),
  Section 2, Lemma 2.1: the standard correspondence between affine actions, cocycles,
  coboundaries and fixed points. SP1 requires no Banach-space fixed-point
  theorem; its finite-period implication follows by algebraic averaging.

This is a targeted source and overlap check, not an exhaustive priority search.
No worldwide-first claim is made for the algebraic results.
