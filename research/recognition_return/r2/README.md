## Main constructive result

Two-cell pattern **3,2,3,2,...** means individual edge products **3,3,2,2,3,3,2,2,...**. The completed boundary response is exactly `I+KR`; half of it is the native cut `(I+KR)/2`. Couplings are finite, but the exact result still uses aperture completion. Finite computations give certified rational enclosures.

For any desired positive scalar response `x` and free positive `b`, the period `a=x+b*x^2, b` realizes it. For a whole cyclic profile, the design is `a_j=x_j/(1-x_j*x_(j+1))`, provided every adjacent target product, including the wrap, is below one. Target `(1,2/3,1/2)` yields cells `(3,1,1)`.

The theorem also distinguishes profiles that genuinely retain boundary data. All finite paired apertures remain invertible, but tail independence holds **if and only if** `sum 1/a_j` diverges. For `a_j=4(j+1)^2`, every boundary interval has width at least `1/4`; no unique aperture limit is claimed.
