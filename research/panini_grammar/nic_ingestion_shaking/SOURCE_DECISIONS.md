## H01. Scope is a meaning and participant certificate

Kāśikā 1.3.87 carries ṇeḥ and overrides actor-result A under 1.3.74.
Nigaraṇa is abhyavahāra (ingestion); calana here is kampana (shaking),
not every dictionary sense of motion. Admit one current causal ṇic,
kartari expression, original agent a, current causer/controller c,
and a declared intended principal result. Passive, Bhāva, karmavat,
second causatives and independent result-upapadas are outside this grid.
Missing semantic or participant evidence yields HOLD, not default P.

The six positive source profiles choose actor-result c. They therefore
exercise 1.3.87 instead of merely coinciding with residual 1.3.78 P.
Cal/kamp have non-conscious original agents and original intransitivity;
they do not receive their P from 1.3.88's conscious-agent alternative.
The ingestion roots have the source-qualified transitive eating frames.

## H03. Preserve the cup source tension

Nyāsa groups cal/kamp as intransitive and calls the remaining examples
transitive, including copayati. The lexical record marks cup as
intransitive. These are not silently made identical. This packet's
CUP_QUALIFIED_P is explicitly the commentary-qualified transitive
application; an unqualified dictionary slow-motion request remains HOLD.

## H04. Ad prohibition targets one P cause, not all P

The ad vārttika blocks the P obtained through 1.3.87. Actor-result
ad therefore keeps 1.3.74 A: ādayate. Kaumudī explicitly preserves
1.3.78 P when the intended result is not the current actor's:
ādayaty annaṃ baṭunā. These are two certified input frames, not a
free A/P option within one frame. The A label remains kartari and
does not admit passive ciṇvat continuations.

Nyāsa explains the exception by a restricted carried vibhāṣā; the
source does not thereby license optional P on actor-result ad. Record
that explanation and its stated outcome without manufacturing another
branch from the isolated word vibhāṣā.

## H05. Causee karman and pada are independent decisions

1.4.52 ordinarily gives the pre-causal agent karman status for the
specified ingestion and intransitive domains. The ad/khād prohibition
blocks that reassignment for ad. Both ad profiles retain the source's
instrumental causee (devadattena/baṭunā), even though their current
padas differ. An overt food object remains a separate participant.

For the qualified ingestion aś/bhuj/gṝ and original intransitive
cal/kamp cases retain the admitted causee-karman relation. Cup's
selected transitive motion frame requires the commentary sense witness;
its causee mapping is held for independent adjudication and is not
needed to establish its finite agreement with c. Do not infer the
causee's case from a printed P/A label, or turn an unresolved role
edge into an invented word variant. No full sentence inflection is
claimed by these finite-word tables.
