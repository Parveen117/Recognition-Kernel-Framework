# PG268 · Four ordered active schedules, two ci perfects and the carried pair

Monty · 12 November 2026

The two perfects *cikāya* and *cicāya* are printed together both by Kṣīrasvāmin and by Maitreya. Under 7.3.58 the optional velar belongs to the root **after** the copy. The two series persist through all nine perfect requests. The second singular allows four words, *cikayitha, ciketha, cicayitha, cicetha*, under each ordered prefix pair.

The perfects of *vap* and *vah* begin in *u* or *ū* in every selected cell. The inner preverb therefore has *y* throughout their perfects. Mādhava gives *uvaptha/uvapitha* and *uvahitha/uvoḍha* for the second singular. In *avoḍhām*, his discussion distinguishes the stage at which vṛddhi was applied from the later appearance of *o*; the latter does not trigger a second strengthening. The *ks* aorist of *dih* has the distinct *adhikṣat* series under 3.1.45.
