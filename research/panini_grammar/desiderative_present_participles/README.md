# PG185 · Active and middle present participles of five desideratives

Monty · 11 November 2026

The five disputed desideratives have a second, independently testable construction. In a present **śānac** participle the middle substitute is on the same derivation as san. Kāśikā 1.4.100 includes śānac in *āna*, and Siddhāntakaumudī 7.2.59 specifies the absence of *taṅ* and *āna* for the prohibition. Thus *vivartiṣamāṇam* ‘[I see] him wanting to turn’ has inner iṭ on the selected middle path. The active *vivṛtsantam* has aniṭ. In an agent noun the later tṛc gives a different condition, as PG184 showed.
