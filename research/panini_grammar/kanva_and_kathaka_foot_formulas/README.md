**Monty · 26 November 2026.** PG985 left open what a separately published Kāṇva mantra actually says. The Vedic Heritage Portal’s Kāṇva chapter 9, running mantra 29, gives *viṣurūpa* in the first half and *prathantām* after the five accusatives in the second. Its serial **29** and internal **3** distinguish this locus from Mādhyandina 8.30. The *vi-rūpa / prayantām* pair remains the wording of the Kāṇva commentary panel inspected in PG985. The independent presentation establishes a positive Kāṇva reading at both places. It leaves the manuscript basis of the panel and the genealogy of the disagreement open. The Kāṭhaka parallel changes both the patient and the inflected series. The explicitly expressed *garbham* can control the masculine accusative singular series. Its four forms cannot be evidence for a fifth feminine accusative. At 13.10 j–m the prose quotes the incipit, then quotes the beginning of the count and gives singular causative *prathayati* in explanation. These two Kāṭhaka loci independently distinguish the mantra's third-plural middle command from a prose explanation. No second credit accrues to the PG985 command. The present middle participle *pavamānaḥ* agrees with *induḥ* in the first clause. [Kāṭhaka Saṃhitā 13.9 and 13.10, TITUS](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/etcs/ind/aind/ved/yvs/ks/ks013.htm)

## Why the perfect can have an imperative gloss

At Mādhyandina 8.30, Mahīdhara takes unprefixed √añj in its manifestation sense. He explicitly names liṭ and 7.4.71. With actor *induḥ*, single agent, the finite request is parasmaipada perfect third singular **ānañja**. Retain the reduplicative initial long ā (7.4.70), the inserted n before the root (7.4.71, the two consonants ñ and j) and the singular perfect ending. The resulting sequence is ā-n-añj-a. This initial ā belongs to the perfect formation. No preverb is required. Uvaṭa explains the manifestation with a present, whereas Mahīdhara uses the wish/command paraphrase *vyaktīkarotu*. Credit the one perfect under the sense specified by the commentator. The Kāṭhaka parallel provides a second object construction, but the exact lexical sense of its √añj is reserved. Neither explanatory mood supplies a second ending to the mantra. [Mādhyandina 8.30, Uvaṭa and Mahīdhara](https://vishvasa.github.io/vedAH_yajuH/vAjasaneyam/mAdhyandinam/saMhitA/sarva-prastutiH/08/)
