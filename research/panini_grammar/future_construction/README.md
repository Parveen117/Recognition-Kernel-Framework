# PG16 — Meaning-to-form construction of lṛṭ and luṭ

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

PG16 constructs two future formations on eight exact root/sense entries:
bhū, gam, nī, kṛ, hṛ, pac, labh and edh. There are twelve admitted
root/pada profiles and nine person-number positions for each future:
**216 written paradigm positions**. This is a bounded mathematical and
source construction, not 216 newly executed cases or a claim that the two
future systems are complete for every Sanskrit root.

The full native operator extension is declared in [N1–N5](NATIVE_EXTENSION.md).
Semantic, morphological and phonetic operations below are its labelled,
typed arrows. Its classical reading preserves original lexical information,
affix ownership, commentary choices and derivation history. Lambda is not
identified with tense, calendar time or a scalar deformation parameter.

- [Native extension](NATIVE_EXTENSION.md): compatible cuts, pairing,

## Completion boundary

The selected forms are classical, unaccented, unprefixed, underived kartari
words in pause, with the non-doubling spelling choices of 8.4.46–47.
Four U roots have different completed principal-result frames for P and A;
their two rows do not assert free variation in one unspecified frame.
External sentence sandhi, accent, Vedic usage, prefixed exceptions,
causative/desiderative/intensive futures, bhāva/karma futures, future
participles and lṛṅ remain outside this packet.

Python grammar/navigation tests, old certificate replays and the engine
suite were **not run**, following the owner's mathematics-first instruction.
No existing runtime, historical coverage count, theorem packet, certificate
or shared-engine file is changed. Written proof, source reading and future
implementation work remain separate in the [work record](WORK_RECORD.md).
