# PG19 — Causal and voice continuations of the modal construction

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## C1. Active ṇic before the owned śap

PG13–PG15 supply C_root|i_ṇic and PG18 adds the exact śam formation.
In the causal kartari route, the new sārvadhātuka ending licenses śap.
The trigger is that distinct pit śap, so guṇa and ayādi of ṇic give

\[
 C_{root}|i_{ṇic}|a_{śap}\to C_{root}|e_{ṇic}|a_{śap}
 \to C_{root}|ay_{ṇic}|a_{śap}.
\]

No 6.4.51 lopa is triggered by this sārvadhātuka śap. Vidhiliṅ's
outer ṅit prescription does not turn śap into kit/ṅit or suppress this
guṇa at a different boundary. Earlier root vṛddhi/guṇa, mit shortening
and puk/yuk owners remain exactly those in the predecessor formation.

The resulting interface is thematic, with B=C_root·ay. E7 therefore
applies to every source-licensed pada in D6. Bhū/sthā/budh/śam remain
P-only on the selected active causal frames; Karma(q) below has A.

## C2. Causal Karma(q): lopa at yak, not at a modal augment

For causal karmaṇi, 3.1.67 inserts **yak before the sārvadhātuka tiṅ**.
Yak itself is ārdhadhātuka under 3.4.114 and kit. Its visible y is not
val, so 7.2.35 does not supply an ordinary val-iṭ there. PG15's
6.4.51 procedure therefore applies:

\[
 C_{root}|i_{ṇic}|ya_{yak}\to C_{root}|\varnothing_{ṇic}|ya_{yak}.
\]

The causal role graph and prior root effects survive. The thematic
interface has B=C_root·y and uses I_A/O_A. A later yāsuṭ/sīyuṭ
ending operation does not recreate a deleted ṇic or delete yak.

This differs from PG18's futures: there, source-sensitive iṭ determined
whether ṇic-lopa was admitted. Here yak already supplies the relevant
owned lopa environment, and the vidhiliṅ sīyuṭ is outside 6.4.62's
ārdhadhātuka-ciṇvat domain. There is **no optional future-style ṇic-ay
retention or mit-length trio** in this construction.

## C5. Meaning-to-form examples with different controllers

**Several causal bhū causees.** PG15 selects the causees as q, with
the causer unexpressed/instrumental. A 3pl loṭ gives **bhāvyantām**;
vidhiliṅ gives **bhāvyeran**. The basic intransitive Bhāva construction
instead remains bhūyatām/bhūyeta even with plural instrumental actors.

**Two addressee causees of sthā.** Causal Karma(q) has A 2du:
**sthāpyethām/sthāpyeyāthām**. In causal kartari the causer controls
the P ending instead. P-only in that active frame does not ban passive A.

**Causal cognition with different karmans.** For budh, PG15 permits
the stated choice of learners or learning-content as q. Several learners
give **bodhyantām/bodhyeran**; one third-person content gives
**bodhyatām/bodhyeta**. The same causal stem and causer can therefore
have different agreement because q, rather than the causer, is expressed.

**Causal hṛ optional role designation.** Retain precisely PG15's
(causee-karman, expressed causee) or (causee-agent, expressed base-karman)
branches. Their modal continuations use hāry, but no independent choice
of any participant is added. A modal request cannot undo that dependency.

## C6. Continuation theorem

**Theorem C6.** On D6's completed role/pada frames, C1–C4 constructs
396 causal kartari positions, 234 causal Karma positions, 72 basic
Karma positions and four basic Bhāva entries in the selected two moods.

**Proof.** C3 has 22 active pada profiles and thirteen causal A profiles.
E7 gives two nine-slot tuples for each. C4 has four nine-slot A profiles
and two Bhāva profiles restricted to 3sg. The source-derived stem guards
are independent of the later ending spelling, while the semantic controller
fixes the slot. Thus the counts are 22·18, 13·18, 4·18 and 2·2.
The role relation remains PG15's dependent one, and N3 preserves the
typed composition and all complementary outcomes. □
