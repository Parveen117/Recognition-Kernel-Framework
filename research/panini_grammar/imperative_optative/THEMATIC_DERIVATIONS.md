# PG19 — Thematic stems, trigger locality and the new ending seam

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## T1. Eight basic interfaces

Each interface retains its original root, vikaraṇa, markers and source
history. It is not produced by removing ti from an arbitrary present word.
The older laṭ-only implementations are dependencies for their **stem
arguments**, not claims that their runtime already handles these moods.

| Root | Owned stem derivation | E7 argument B | Pada |
|---|---|---|---|
| bhū | bhū ∣ a_śap → bho ∣ a → bhav ∣ a, 7.3.84/6.1.78 | bhav | P |
| pac | pac ∣ a_śap | pac | P, A |
| edh | edh ∣ a_śap | edh | A |
| labh | labh ∣ a_śap | labh | A |
| tud | tud ∣ a_śa; the apit/ṅitvat trigger blocks root guṇa | tud | P, A |
| nī | nī ∣ a_śap → ne ∣ a → nay ∣ a | nay | P, A |
| gam | gam ∣ śap; 7.3.77 cha, 6.1.73 tuk and 8.4.40 ścu | gacch | P |
| śam | śam ∣ śyan → śām ∣ ya, 7.3.74 on the exact śamādi entry | śāmy | P |

Śap, śa and śyan are not interchangeable because their visible output
contains a. Śap retains its pit witness; śa and śyan have their own
ṅitvat conditions. For gam, final m→ch under 7.3.77 gives ga∣ch;
6.1.73 attaches tuk's t to the preceding short a and late 8.4.40
reads t∣ch as c∣ch. The displayed gacch is a **final readout factor**;
it is not fed back before that late rule. Kāśikā 6.4.105 also quotes
gaccha. Śam's ā is the named śyan-conditioned length,
not a newly guessed guṇa or causal vṛddhi.

The new tiṅ in both moods remains sārvadhātuka. It therefore meets the
same kartari vikaraṇa input contract. Its outer marker changes do not
erase the independent root-facing affix witness. In particular vidhiliṅ
bhavet still has bhav, while tudet still has tud, for different reasons.

## T2. Four complete representative derivations

**Bhū P loṭ 2sg.** The frame selects actor/addressee, P, 2sg and loṭ.
The original sip becomes apit hi by 3.4.87. The root-facing śap still
licenses bhū→bho→bhav. On bhav|a|hi, 6.4.105 removes hi, giving
**bhava**. Hi-luk does not remove śap or its prior root effect.

**Bhū P vidhiliṅ 3sg.** On bhav|a|tip, the owned liṅ procedure adds
yāsuṭ and the applicable t-suṭ, confers ṅit on the ending, removes
their non-final s and tip's i, and replaces yā by iy after a.
The final path bhav|a|iy|t → bhav|a|i|t → **bhavet** uses 6.1.66/87.
Its final t is read with the chosen devoiced pause option. This is not
āśīrliṅ bhūyāt and has no ciṇvat/iṭ branch.

**Pac A loṭ versus vidhiliṅ 3du.** Loṭ's owned āte→ātām is adjacent
to the thematic a, so 7.2.81 gives pac|a|iytām → **pacetām**.
Vidhiliṅ has pac|a|īy|ātām after augment-s deletion. Its intervening
īy is not erased before a vowel; the result is **paceyātām**.
The one extra y has a precise augment/seam history.

**Pac A loṭ versus vidhiliṅ 1sg.** Loṭ gives pit āṭ plus ai,
and pac|a|ā|ai → **pacai**. Vidhiliṅ instead replaces ending iṭ
by a and has pac|a|īy|a → **paceya**. Treating the ending iṭ as
an optional root augment would destroy the ownership of this contrast.

## T3. Bounded phonetic readout, including the imperative n

Retain PG6's ordered late sound policy. For the present domain:

- In I_P's first singular, the new ni-n becomes ṇ under 8.4.1–2
  only when the actual same-pada source and permitted interveners are
  present. Thus kārayāṇi, hārayāṇi and karṣayāṇi have ṇ; the other
  thematic rows have dental n. The kṛ athematic result is karavāṇi.
- In krīṇāni, the earlier stem ṇ is not an allowed intervener from
  r to the later ni-n. The new n stays dental. Root ṇ and ending n
  are not globally normalized to one kind of nasal.
- The n of ant before t first becomes anusvāra under 8.3.24, then
  is restored as dental n by 8.4.58. Its late appearance cannot feed
  backward into 8.4.1–2. Thus kārayantu and kurvantu are dental at nt.
- Pada-final n of ran remains n; 8.4.37 excludes final-n retroflexion.
  Final s has the inherited ru/visarga pause route, 8.2.66/8.3.15.
- The selected stop-at-pause profile takes the **devoiced** 8.4.56
  alternative after the earlier 8.2.39 reading. Consequently bhavet,
  kuryāt and the other final-t words are displayed with t. The other
  licensed pause sound choice is outside this declared readout profile.

No optional consonant doubling or external sentence sandhi is counted.
The ν of E7 is precisely the first of these bounded decisions, not a
general Sanskrit transliteration function. All other readout events and
their original objects remain in the history.

## T4. Thematic continuation lemma

**Lemma T4.** T1's eleven pada profiles each admit all nine slots of
both selected moods across completed D frames, through the E7 constructors.

**Proof.** The source-fixed root/vikaraṇa arguments in T1 depend on the
actual affix and sārvadhātuka continuation, which E1 supplies. None is
changed into a different root/vikaraṇa pair by E2–E6. E7 exhausts all
ending seams and T3 exhausts this domain's added late readout. A completed
controller selects its one slot. This gives 11·2·9=198 written positions,
without a new whole-root inventory or an executable adapter. □
