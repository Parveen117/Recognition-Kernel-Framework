# PG19 — Complete bounded loṭ/vidhiliṅ register

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## P1. Reading the tables

The component order is (3sg,3du,3pl,2sg,2du,2pl,1sg,1du,1pl).
I_P/I_A and O_P/O_A are E7's fully specified nine-component thematic
constructors. Their argument is B, the owned stem without final thematic a.
For kṛ/krī only, the named tuples are the **separately derived A2–A5
tuples**, not applications of a thematic suffix table to a bare root.

Each row below specifies two complete tuples, one for each mood. A
completed D3 profile may license one mood or both; the register is their
union across the declared semantic profiles. Different role/benefit/modal
witnesses remain different native histories without multiplying the
morphological row count. T3 fixes the non-doubling, devoiced-pause profile.

There are 54·2=108 nine-slot tuples and four single-slot entries:
108·9+4=976. On the selected non-āśīs sound/morphological domain each
fixed root/formation/expression/pada/mood/slot profile has one reading.
Semantic choice, causal role witnesses and repeated spellings are not
silently counted as extra morphological forms.

**Theorem P6.** Every admitted morphological profile is specified by
P2–P5, and every listed component has the owned source-relative
derivation of E/T/A/C. Its native construction has the same accepted,
excluded and open outcomes under the typed zero-reading.

**Proof.** D7 enumerates the semantic/role inputs; T4 covers the eleven
thematic profiles and A6 the four athematic ones. C6 covers every remaining
active/voice profile. E7 specifies all thematic components, A2–A5 all
athematic components, and D4 restricts Bhāva. The displayed partitions
exhaust the input domain. N3 supplies the typed specialization identity
and the recovery of unit-start histories. □

976 is a written factorized coverage count, not 976 executions, distinct
strings, independent quotations, or a percentage of all Sanskrit grammar.
