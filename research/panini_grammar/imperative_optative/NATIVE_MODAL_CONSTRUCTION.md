## N1. Full carrier first

Use the canonical cut field C_Σ, its derived iota and scalar dagger.
Extend PG18's event index by the source-labelled modal, ending, marker,
augment, visibility, stem and sound events constructed in D/E/T/A/C:

\[
 B_{19}=C_Σ\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le19}\rangle.
\]

This is the free cut-field algebra of finite ordered residue words.
Dagger reverses their order and stars their letters. The empty word is 1.
No scalar lambda, Hilbert-space premise, positivity, probabilistic weights
or physical clock is supplied by this declaration.

Let H_old retain predecessor histories, and H_modal retain the new ones.
Each h includes its initial interpreted frame, source/lexical identity,
constituent owners, selected mood/voice/slot, branch witnesses, marker
overrides, rule-specific views, ordered events and unresolved obligations.
Define finite-support right modules

\[
 M_{19,\lambda}=\bigoplus_{h\in H_{old}\sqcup H_{modal}}e_hB_{19},
 \qquad M_{19,0}=\bigoplus_he_hC_Σ.
\]

Grades distinguish interpreted input, modal choice, expression/controller,
root formation, vikaraṇa, original/augmented ending, addressed stem query,
completed morphology, pronunciation and Accepted/Excluded/Open outcomes.
Only matching grades compose. Old coefficients embed unchanged; old
operators extend by zero on the new sector. Their identity is P_old,
not the global identity. This is an extension, not a replacement engine.

The pairing is

\[
 \langle x,y\rangle_B=\sum_h x_h^*y_h.
\]

It is sesquilinear and algebraically nondegenerate, as pairing with a
basis vector detects its coordinate. An analytic or positive metric is
not assumed. All constructions below use this declared pairing.

## N3. Exact typed zero-specialization

Let ε:B_19→C_Σ take the empty-word coefficient. No product of nonempty
free words is empty, so ε is a unital dagger algebra homomorphism.
Set

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(\sum_he_ha_h)=\sum_he_h\epsilon(a_h),\qquad
 \Pi_{0,op}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

All source, semantic and ownership data remains in h. The same ε is
the specialization on pairing values. There is no identification of
residue degree with the temporal U of D3.

**Theorem N3.** Every admitted finite PG19 operator construction satisfies

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,op}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
 \epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B
 =\langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_Σ}.
\]

The specialization preserves typed sums/products, adjoints, query cuts and
their complements. Unit-start forward grammatical paths recover exactly
the declared written grammar relation, including its accepted, excluded
and open channels. This relational claim concerns the licensed grammar
edges; adjoining an algebraic adjoint does not license its reverse edge.

**Proof.** ε((1+z_e)a)=ε(a), and dagger commutes with ε. Guards depend
only on h and the addressed q, both preserved by π_0, so their cut values
are unchanged. Coordinatewise multiplication proves the first identity;
the homomorphism/dagger properties prove the pairing identity. Finite
composition gives the general statement. Every admitted forward unit-start path
has constant coefficient 1 in its own terminal-history coordinate, and
every forward zero path has the same guarded native edge sequence. This proves
both directions of recovery on the stated relation. □

Arbitrary signed combinations may cancel under ε; no general preservation
of their support is claimed. Nothing here solves infinite grammar search,
proves an unrestricted visibility scheduler or changes a frozen certificate.

## N4. Loṭ transport is typed and partial

Retain a model-side laṅ ending context and a target-side loṭ context,
including the actual lakāra and meaning. Transport only E2's witnessed
tām/tam/ta and uttama-s operations, with their guards. The target's

The past-prefix and special laṅ-jus operations have no image under this
transport. Nor is the target's temporal or discourse meaning changed.
Within the admitted finite matching paths, each transported edge has its
source, target and owner correspondence recorded.

**Proposition N4.** The admitted transport preserves loṭ's mood and
ending types and has the source-prescribed zero-reading. It is not a
total equivalence of the laṅ and loṭ grammars.

**Proof.** E2 names every admitted image and its explicit exceptions.
None changes the actual lakāra or adds a past prefix. Matching images
compose on the recorded owner correspondence; induction proves type
preservation. N3 gives exact recovery on every such path. An omitted
operation has no licensed image, so the proposition entails none. □

## N5. The complete morphology can still forget the intended sense

Take two completed, otherwise matching pac kartari A 2sg frames.
One has σ=nimantraṇa (obligatory participation); the other has
σ=āmantraṇa (discretionary participation). No overriding sma is present.
Both satisfy D2. Their corresponding row extensions across admissible
controller frames are the **same two full tuples** I_A(pac), O_A(pac).
The fixed 2sg words are pacasva/pacethāḥ on either input. This is a
written application of the general source
rules; their literal shared examples under 3.3.161 use other roots.

Define W on these two input histories to return their lexical/pada/slot
profile and those two complete row extensions, but forget the intended-sense
record and corresponding discourse evidence. Then W(h_N)=W(h_A), while the obligation query has
different values on these two histories.

**Theorem N5.** That query does not factor through W, even when W is
given both complete mood paradigms. The obstruction already occurs in
the two-dimensional zero-history span; zero-specialization itself does
not create it.

**Proof.** A factorization would assign different values to the same
W-output. In linear form, q(e_N)=q(e_A)=e_w, whereas the obligation
cut D=diag(1,0) gives qD(e_N−e_A)=e_w≠0 although q(e_N−e_A)=0.
Thus D cannot descend to the collapsed observation. π_0 retains e_N
and e_A as distinct coordinates and N3 preserves D. □

Retaining the two-valued sense tag repairs this **particular** observer
for this query. A linear injective repair on the two-coordinate span needs
at least two output coordinates by rank; keeping the two labelled outputs
attains it and transports their declared pairing. This is not a universal
lower bound on Sanskrit memory or a pragmatic parser from isolated words.

## N6. One unaddressed weak flag cannot replace boundary queries

Consider causal C_root|i_ṇic|a_śap before a P vidhiliṅ ending.
At the q₁=(ṇic,śap) boundary, śap's pit condition makes the relevant
kit/ṅit inhibition **false**. At q₂=(vikaraṇa,liṅ ending), 3.4.103
makes that inhibition **true**. The two values belong to different
addressed questions on one retained history.

**Proposition N6.** An observer retaining h but forgetting the queried
boundary q cannot supply both values of this inhibition query through
one unaddressed Boolean.

**Proof.** Its images of (h,q₁) and (h,q₂) are the same h, but the
required outputs are false and true. A single-valued factorization is
impossible. Keeping q and its actual affix-marker witness suffices on
this displayed pair; N3 preserves them. □

The result justifies the concrete active ṇic guṇa versus outer weak
ending construction. It does not assert that an implementation cannot
reconstruct an omitted field from other adequate source-bearing data.
