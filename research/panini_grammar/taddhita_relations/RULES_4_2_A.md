# PG35 — 4.2.1–66: semantic admission, selection and elision

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These are written contracts on the proof-carrying inputs of [DOMAIN](DOMAIN.md).
`A` means the residual aṇ of 4.1.83 **after** applicable exceptions have been
resolved, not an unconditional additional choice. `S` means the qualified
śaiṣika selector; `C` the four place-name relations. A gaṇa name requires a
literal membership witness from the pinned Kāśikā. A listed member with an
additional qualification must satisfy it. An unattested extension is Open.

Every row specifies a bounded rule action. The last column supplies a source
witness/control, not a newly completed word paradigm. Source spellings and
editorial differences are preserved in [the archive](SOURCE_EXCERPTS.json).
Affix names below are original upadeśas: ठक्, ठञ्, वुन् and वुञ् are distinct.
An affix-selection row inherits optional formation versus the analytic phrase
from 4.1.82; a second affix alternative requires its own row or explicit `+`.
