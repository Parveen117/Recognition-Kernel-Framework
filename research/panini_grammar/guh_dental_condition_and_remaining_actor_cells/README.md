# PG990 · Why vah(i) and mah(i) diverge in the middle aorist

**Monty · 26 November 2026.** At 7.3.73 the Kāśikā names all four roots, including √guh. It illustrates the dental condition with middle first-dual *aduhvahi / adhukṣāvahi* under √duh and gives *adhukṣāmahi* in answer to the question why that condition is needed. The first-dual initial v participates under this source’s policy. The first-plural initial m does not. For √guh within the same named class this distinguishes *aguhvahi / aghukṣāvahi* from the no-iṭ first plural *aghukṣāmahi*. The iṭ branch remains independent in both numbers. In the *Bṛhaddhāturūpāvalī* p. 128 the first-plural line prints a kṣ-less member alongside the kṣ member. Record that print as a competing policy. Extending its kṣ-less member into the Kāśikā first-plural cell would defeat the commentary's explicit counterexample. The transmission of the extra printed member remains open. [Kāśikā 7.3.73, key 73073](https://github.com/ashtadhyayi-com/data/blob/1ad4bfdf66bf73763e4be689d37382e46288bfe4/sutraani/kashika.txt) · [Printed p. 128](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/पृष्ठम्:बृहद्धातुरूपावलिः.pdf/१५२)

**Direct prefixed control.** The same Kāśikā entry gives the pair *nyagūḍha / nyaghukṣata* for ni + √guh, middle luṅ third singular, no iṭ. The prefix meets the past augment before either middle branch. The consonant-initial ending permits deletion or retention of ksa, as in the unprefixed singular at PG989. This is one new prefixed position with two directly supported readings. The iṭ reading at that prefixed position is reserved. The earlier upa + guh embrace belongs to a different sense and construction.

The print gives the weak dual *juguhathuḥ*. Its abbreviated line does not itself attest the following active plural. The middle 2pl has three histories. The two with iṭ differ at the ending's dental/retroflex dh under the 8.3.78–79 conditions; the third keeps the no-iṭ consonant history. In the middle aorist 2pl the two iṭ readouts coexist with two no-iṭ paths through the dental-initial domain of 7.3.73. The ending's phonology and deletion of ksa must be decided independently.
