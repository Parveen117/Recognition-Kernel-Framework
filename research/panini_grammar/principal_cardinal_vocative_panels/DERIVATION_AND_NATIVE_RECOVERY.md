## Inputs, address and number

PG419 closes the selected principal-cardinal `eka` M/F/N singular cases. PG413,
PG414 and PG415 close the corresponding `dvi` dual, `tri` plural and `catur`
plural panels. Each stage explicitly leaves vocative Open. PG880–882 positively
review Kāśikā 2.3.47: address receives first-case endings in singular, dual and
plural, while 2.3.49 restricts `sambuddhi` to singular. The address operator
does not invent a new outer number. PG883 therefore admits exactly

\[
\{eka\}\times\{sg\},\quad\{dvi\}\times\{du\},\quad
\{tri,catur\}\times\{pl\}
\]

in each of M/F/N. No root, preverb, voice or derivational affix is introduced.

## Singular `eka`

Masculine `eka + su` loses the sambuddhi ending consonant by 6.1.69, yielding
`eka`. Feminine `ekā` retains PG419's `ṭāp`; 7.3.106 replaces `ā` by `e` before
sambuddhi and 6.1.69 removes the ending consonant, yielding `eke`. Neuter first
singular takes the 7.1.24 `am` replacement and the neuter-sambuddhi explanation
of 6.1.69 deletes `m`, yielding `eka`. This is not an `adḍ` history and has no
second pause reading.
