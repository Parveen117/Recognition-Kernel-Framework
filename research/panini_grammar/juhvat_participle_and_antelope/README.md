**Why the antelope has a different vowel.** PG578 left the named *juhvat* member of the prajñādi group open. Kāśikā's list at 5.4.38 couples *juhvat* with *kṛṣṇamṛge*. On Nyāsa's first parsing the black-antelope referent licenses self-sense aṇ. The ṇ marker triggers 7.2.117 on the **first** vowel of the base, ju → jau. The consonantal ending plus sounded a gives *jauhvata-* and M NOMsg *jauhvataḥ*. An ordinary person offering retains the participle on this analysis.
