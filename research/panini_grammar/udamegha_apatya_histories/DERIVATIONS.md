# PG54 — उदमेघ descent, two ष्यङ् policies and the later य् deletion

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

The adopted spelling is **उदमेघ**, supported by Kāśikā, Nyāsa and
Padamañjarī 6.3.57 and the घ-spelled discussion in Padamañjarī 4.1.78.
The latter traces the female descendant and her later offspring. The ध
spellings within the Nyāsa 4.1.78 field stay literal in the inherited
archive; they are not turned into a sound-change rule. See the source
decisions in the [README](README.md).

The paths start with supplied nominal उदक and मेघ, the named-person
use and the specified descent meanings. They do not derive those nouns
from roots or infer actual genealogy. D is the original इञ्; Y is
the ष्यङ् formation; F is चाप्; M is the later ढक्. The two
policies differ in whether Y replaces D or is newly introduced after it.
The later य् supplied by M is a separate sound occurrence from Y's य्.

## Shared preparation S1–S6

|Stage|Construction and retained condition|
|---|---|
|S1|For the supplied proper-name use, form [उदक,मेघ] as the षष्ठी compound described at 6.3.57. Inherit the 2.1.1/2.2.8 relation, compound status, ordering and internal सुप-deletion procedure from PG53. Padamañjarī explicitly identifies the person's name through resemblance.|
|S2|Apply 6.3.57 in this संज्ञा use: the specified उदक word becomes उद before the retained मेघ member, giving उदमेघ. This is the stated word substitution, not a general क-deletion rule.|
|S3|Supply the non-ऋषि technical गोत्र descent and the female request needed later. Introduce इञ् under 4.1.95 in the 4.1.92 अपत्य meaning, as the exception to general अण्. Mark final ञ् by 1.3.3 and remove it by 1.3.9, retaining D's ञित् feature and audible इ.|
|S4|The preceding अ-final base is भ before the vowel-initial तद्धित D (1.4.18). Apply 6.4.148 to that base's final अ: उदमेघ् + इ. Do not delete D's इ at this stage.|
|S5|Apply 7.2.117 to the first vowel before ञित् D: उ→औ, giving औदमेघि. The operation occurs while D is current.|
|S6|Inspect the prepared इञ् formation: the penultimate मे is long and hence गुरु by 1.4.12; the formation has at least three syllables. Together with the supplied non-ऋषि गोत्र and female conditions, this admits the selected 4.1.78 construction. The long penultimate, not an invented consonant cluster, supplies the weight condition here.|

The पुत्र wording at 6.3.57 corroborates the name and इञ् spelling;
it does not by itself prove the technical पौत्रप्रभृति गोत्र premise.
That premise is independently supplied for these 4.1.78 paths.

## H01 — आदेश policy: D is replaced and its अपत्य relation is retained

This is Kāśikā's stated ष्यङादेश interpretation, defended in Nyāsa
4.1.78. The latter explicitly uses स्थानिवद्भाव to retain the original
descent classification for the later य्-deletion query.

|Stage|Operation|
|---|---|
|A1|After S6, replace D itself by Y under 4.1.78. D becomes historical; it is not a second active suffix before Y. For the later आपत्य query, retain D's descent classification by the source-authorized 1.1.56 transfer. This is the specific semantic transfer used in the commentary, not unrestricted transfer of every sound property.|
|A2|Give Y's initial ष् and final ङ् their इत् status (1.3.6/3), remove them by 1.3.9, and retain the pronounced य (ya): औदमेघ्य.|
|A3|Introduce distinct F=चाप् under 4.1.74 in the feminine domain. Remove its initial च् and final प् markers by 1.3.7/3/9, leaving आ.|
|A4|Combine Y's अ with F's आ by 6.1.101 under 6.1.84/85: औदमेघ्या. Retain both contributors and Y's replacement provenance, then use T1–T8 below.|

This path has **18 stages**: S1–S6, A1–A4 and T1–T8. Its final
uninflected offspring stem is **औदमेघेय**. The existing ञित्-induced
initial वृद्धि is not discarded when D is replaced.

## H02 — प्रत्यय policy with the explicitly quoted अपत्य justification

Padamañjarī 4.1.78 considers a separate ष्यङ् suffix. It quotes a
reply attributed to Vāmana: **आपत्याद्विहितः ष्यङ् सोऽप्यापत्य एव**
(spacing/avagraha regularized here; the archive remains literal).
Here that explicit source policy supplies the descent classification of Y.
It is a different justification from H01's substitution transfer.

|Stage|Operation|
|---|---|
|B1|After S6, introduce Y after the current इञ् formation, retaining D as a distinct preceding affix. Select the separate-suffix analysis discussed in Padamañjarī, not the H01 replacement event.|
|B2|Mark and remove Y's ष्/ङ् as in A2. Its audible य follows D's still distinct इ.|
|B3|The base before this य-initial तद्धित is भ. Apply 6.4.148 to its final इ, the pronounced residue of D. D has zero current pronunciation but remains in the derivation record; this deletion is not D→Y substitution.|
|B4|Introduce F=चाप्, handle its markers, and combine Y-अ with F-आ as in A3/A4. The surface is again औदमेघ्या, with a different D/Y history.|
|B5|For the following 6.4.151 query, admit Y's आपत्य classification under the explicit quoted reply: Y has been prescribed from the descent formation. Record this source-policy justification separately from H01's 1.1.56 justification, then use T1–T8.|

This path has **19 stages**: S1–S6, B1–B5 and T1–T8. It reaches
the same **औदमेघेय** stem on the stated policy. Without B5's
justification, the separate-suffix objection in Nyāsa remains; C04 records
that failed query. The two policies are not silently declared equivalent
for every later operation.

## Shared continuation T1–T8

|Stage|Construction and retained condition|
|---|---|
|T1|Supply the further अपत्य relation from this female descendant. Apply 4.1.120 to the actually qualified feminine-suffix formation and introduce M=ढक्. स्त्री here is the specified feminine-affix domain, not merely any referent called female.|
|T2|At suffix prescription, 7.1.2 replaces initial ढ् by एय्. Preserve M's own following अ; its final क् is marked and removed by 1.3.3/9, retaining the कित् feature. M's audible form is therefore एय. Do not delete ढ् first under a generic initial-marker procedure.|
|T3|Before vowel-initial M, the mother's formation is भ under 1.4.18. The कित् तद्धित also supplies the 7.2.118 initial-वृद्धि context; the existing initial औ already has that form, so no new letter change or alternative reading is counted.|
|T4|Retain the feminine formation: the भस्याढे तद्धिते पुंवद्भाव discussion at 6.3.35 excludes this ढक् environment. Recognize M's prescribed suffix identity even though its current form starts with ए. Do not undo Y/F by applying the masculine-like restoration used in a different following-suffix environment.|
|T5|Query 6.4.151 at the old Y-owned य्. Its आपत्य justification is A1 or B5; it follows घ् and is still penultimate (1.1.65) in the mother's भ base while the final आ is present. The following तद्धित M has current initial ए, so the exclusion for an आ-initial suffix does not apply. Delete this old य् only. The intermediate boundary is औदमेघ् + आ + एय.|
|T6|Apply 6.4.148 to the mother's final अवर्ण vowel आ before M. For this shared भ base, retain the 6.4.22 असिद्धवत् view of the preceding य्-lopa: the final आ and the same following तद्धित condition are available in that view too. Delete that current coalesced vowel, retaining its Y/F contributor history. This is neither deletion of M's ए nor ordinary external vowel fusion.|
|T7|Read the remaining stem boundary औदमेघ् + एय as औदमेघेय. The य् belonging to M's एय survives. It is not the old Y-owned consonant that T5 removed.|
|T8|Export the uninflected stem with its selected policy, both descent relations, D/Y/F/M ownership, marker/substitution/deletion events and query views. No final सुप ending or complete nominal paradigm is newly constructed.|

The source's 6.4.150 is not an alternative justification for T5: that
rule has the ई environment, whereas M here begins with ए. The chosen
order removes the old य् while its penultimate address is explicit,
then removes the final vowel. An arbitrary reversal without its own
visibility account is not licensed by this table.

## C01 — Spelling evidence does not prescribe घ→ध→घ

The 6.3.57 fields agree on उदमेघ and औदमेघि. Padamañjarī 4.1.78
also supplies the घ-spelled female/later-descendant example. Adopt that
coherent chain. Keep Nyāsa's ध spellings as literal variants and do not
invent a consonant rule to connect them. This is a bounded editorial choice
for the construction, not manuscript collation of the entire tradition.

## C02 — The name and the lineage gates are independent

6.3.57 requires the specified संज्ञा use; its ordinary उदकगिरि
counterexample is not shortened by this rule. The later 4.1.78 additionally
requires the appropriate suffix, non-ऋषि गोत्र, weight and female
conditions. Unknown genealogy remains OPEN; the fact that a source calls
someone a पुत्र does not establish पौत्रप्रभृति by itself.

## C03 — Replacement and vowel deletion are different histories

H01 replaces the इञ् owner D by Y. H02 introduces a separate Y and
then removes D's audible इ under 6.4.148. Both yield the same feminine
surface after F, but only H01 has the D→Y replacement event. A later
query cannot choose a justification from the final string alone.

## C04 — A separate suffix without the quoted justification is insufficient

Under the objection stated in Nyāsa 4.1.78, a newly added feminine Y
does not acquire आपत्य status merely by sharing the final spelling.
Then this 6.4.151 query is BLOCKED on that policy; 6.4.150 cannot rescue
it because the following form is एय, not an ई suffix. H02's B5 is
the explicit quoted reply to that objection, not an unrecorded assumption.
If no source policy is selected, classification remains OPEN.

## C05 — ढ् replacement precedes a generic marker deletion

7.1.2 supplies an initial-consonant replacement at prescription. The
Padamañjarī field explicitly says the consonant alone is the substituend.
Thus ढ्→एय् retains M's original following अ, while final क् is
separately removed. Treating all of ढक् as an unowned एय string,
or deleting ढ् before this instruction, loses the required identities.

## C06 — Current onset and prescribed suffix identity answer different queries

M is the prescribed ढक् for the अढ limitation on पुंवद्भाव,
and its current pronounced onset is ए for the अनाति query. These
are compatible typed facts, not alternative global spellings to choose between.
The source's गार्ग्यायण example illustrates the excluded आ-initial
environment; that exclusion is not a ban on every vowel-initial suffix.

## C07 — Only the old य् and the mother's vowel are removed

The old Y-owned य् follows घ्; the new M-owned य् is part of
एय and follows ए. T5 targets the former. T6 targets the mother's
current final आ, whose Y/F contributors were retained through coalescence.
Neither instruction deletes every य् in the word or removes M's initial
ए. The query-relative 6.4.22 view retains the previous event record;
it does not restore a deleted sound to the final output.

## C08 — A common output does not erase the source-policy boundary

Both positive histories produce औदमेघेय on their respective stated
justifications. This neither proves that their intermediate histories coincide
nor settles the source's separate हास्तिशीर्ष्य problem. Their result
is a stem, not a complete inflectional paradigm. Earlier PG51–PG53 paths,
counts and proofs are inherited by reference rather than counted again.
