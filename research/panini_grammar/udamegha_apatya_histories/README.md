## Spelling and source-policy decisions

The primary edition remains ashtadhyayi-com/data at
5744762f010d677cfb43f347a42d02796cf615d6. The 6.3.57 Kāśikā,
Nyāsa and Padamañjarī fields consistently attest **उदमेघ / औदमेघि**.
Padamañjarī 4.1.78 also uses the घ-spelled female/later-descendant family.
This supports the adopted घ chain. The ध spellings in the Nyāsa
4.1.78 field remain literal variants; no घ→ध rule is inferred. This
is an editorial choice for the stated example, not a manuscript-wide verdict.

The name construction selects the षष्ठी interpretation given at 6.3.57.
Nyāsa's alternative मध्यमपदलोपी analysis is retained in the archive
but is not silently merged into the chosen path. The non-ऋषि technical
गोत्र premise for 4.1.78 is supplied independently; the पुत्र wording
used to identify the name does not establish that premise by itself.

For H02, the crucial classification is the reply **quoted and attributed to
Vāmana by Padamañjarī 4.1.78**. Its cross-reference points to the
ये च तद्धिते discussion. In this digital edition's 6.1.61 fields,
Kāśikā and Padamañjarī do not reproduce that reply, and the Nyāsa
field is empty. Therefore PG54 uses the explicit quotation at 4.1.78;
it does not claim an independently recovered direct Vāmana passage at
6.1.61. The inspected empty key is recorded without literal-field credit.

Without the quoted classification, the separate-suffix objection in Nyāsa
blocks the proposed 6.4.151 query. That rule requires the old Y's आपत्य
status. 6.4.150's ई context does not supply a substitute justification
before the current एय. The two positive policies are locally sufficient
for this stem; global agreement on all later constructions remains open.
