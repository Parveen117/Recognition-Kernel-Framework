## H05. A blessing retains root ā and two different s owners

Āśīrliṅ is ārdhadhātuka under 3.4.116. A has sīyuṭ by 3.4.102; H02's
7.2.10 decision blocks root-facing iṭ. H03 excludes unsupported kit, e or
ā-lopa. The owned boundary is **jñā ∣ sīy ∣ ending**, with separate suṭ
at the addressed t/th under 3.4.107.

Reuse PG20 E5/E6's complete A ending schedule. The first s follows root ā,
outside iṇ/ku, and remains dental. In 3sg/2sg the separate suṭ follows ī,
receives ṣ by 8.3.59 and retroflexes the following t/th under 8.4.41. In
the duals suṭ follows the ending's ā and remains s. Rule 6.1.66 removes
augment y before consonants and retains it before vowels. Therefore

\[
(jñāsīṣṭa,jñāsīyāstām,jñāsīran,jñāsīṣṭhāḥ,jñāsīyāsthām,
jñāsīdhvam,jñāsīya,jñāsīvahi,jñāsīmahi).
\]

The ī belongs to sīyuṭ, not to the root and not to śnā. Sārvadhātuka
7.2.79 does not apply; its vidhiliṅ s-lopa must not remove this first s.
At 2pl the addressed root ā is not iṇ and there is no iṭ. Neither 8.3.78
nor 8.3.79 gives ḍh: **jñāsīdhvam** is dental in this policy. Looking only
at the visible ī immediately before dh would inspect the wrong owner.

## H06. Ja copy first, then final-root-ā lopa

Liṭ tiṅ is ārdhadhātuka by 3.4.115. Rule 6.1.8 copies the root, leaving
the actual prefix outside the reduplicated constituent. On H04's selected
1.1.59 view the original vowel is available for this operation. In the
copy, 7.4.60 retains the initial j and drops copied ñ; 7.4.59 shortens copied
ā. The original root's **jñ** is unchanged:

\[
[jñā]_{copy}\mid[jñā]_{root}
\longrightarrow [ja]_{copy}\mid[jñā]_{root}.
\]

The original j is already palatal; no k→c operation is needed to obtain it.
After copy formation, 6.4.64 removes **root-owned ā**, not copied a.
The following A endings are all apit-kit, with H02's iṭ at the val slots:

| Slot | Owned ending/augment after liṭ selection | Root-lopa environment | Word |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3sg | ta→eś→e, 3.4.81 | Vowel-initial kit | jajñe |
| 3du | āte, 3.4.79 | Vowel-initial kit | jajñāte |
| 3pl | jha→irec→ire, 3.4.81 | Vowel-initial kit; i is irec's | jajñire |
| 2sg | iṭ + thās→se | iṭ; s→ṣ after its i | jajñiṣe |
| 2du | āthe, 3.4.79 | Vowel-initial kit | jajñāthe |
| 2pl | iṭ + dhve | iṭ | jajñidhve |

| 1du | iṭ + vahe | iṭ | jajñivahe |
| 1pl | iṭ + mahe | iṭ | jajñimahe |

The ā in **jajñāte/jajñāthe** belongs to the ending after root-ā lopa.
It does not demonstrate that the original root ā survived or merely merged
with it. The e in **jajñe** likewise follows lopa; it is not guṇa of
root ā+e. Keep 3sg eś and 1sg i→e as distinct histories.

At 2pl, 8.3.79 requires iṭ after iṇ. The sound before iṭ after lopa is
root ñ, not iṇ; the original ā is also outside iṇ. Iṭ's own i does not
meet that *preceding-iṭ* condition. Thus **jajñidhve** has no optional ḍh
branch. Do not import the corresponding nī-perfect option into jñā.

## H07. Aniṭ A sic and deletion before dhvam

For the selected basic kartari luṅ, 3.1.43/44 supplies cli→sic. Reuse
PG32's Kāśikā policy: the teaching i is pronunciation aid and c a marker,
not a pronounced inserted i. There is no ṇic/caṅ, śal-final ksa,
named aṅ/sic-luk premise, jan-specific ciṇ or karmakartṛ/passive licence.
H02 excludes both root-facing iṭ and the P-only 7.2.73 sak+iṭ route.

The factor is **aṭ ∣ jñā ∣ s_sic ∣ A-ending**. No present śit substitution
or P sic-vṛddhi is involved; the long ā is lexical. Root ā is neither jhal
nor short, so **8.2.26/27 do not delete sic-s before ta/thās**. The actual
ā before s also gives no ṣ trigger. The complete augmented tuple is

\[
(ajñāsta,ajñāsātām,ajñāsata,ajñāsthāḥ,ajñāsāthām,
ajñādhvam,ajñāsi,ajñāsvahi,ajñāsmahi).
\]

At 2pl **8.2.25** deletes sic-s before dhvam. Root ā remains and is
outside iṇ; there is no iṭ, so neither mandatory nor optional ḍh arises.
This is a deletion of an affix sound, not an absent sic selection. The
complementary retained-s histories in other slots remain distinct.

The A tiṅ is sārvadhātuka although preceding sic is ārdhadhātuka. Its
3pl jha→at by 7.1.5 has no thematic a interface and no added ant-n.
First-singular **i is the ending**, giving ajñāsi, not affix iṭ or īṭ.
The A endings do not supply the apṛkta-hal īṭ condition of 7.3.96.
Rule 3.4.100's P-only i-lopa does not remove A -vahi/-mahi.
