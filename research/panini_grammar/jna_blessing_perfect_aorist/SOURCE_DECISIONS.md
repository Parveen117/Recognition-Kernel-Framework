## H02. Aniṭ is not one global switch across all lakāras

At **sīyuṭ** and **sic**, 7.2.35 proposes root-facing iṭ, but **7.2.10**
blocks it for this one-vowel anudātta teaching. No independent iṭ is admitted
there. The prefix does not change the lexical root's upadeśa syllable count.

At **liṭ**, Kāśikā/Siddhāntakaumudī 7.2.13 make the listed kṛ/sṛ/bhṛ/vṛ/
stu/dru/sru/śru restriction a niyama. Jñā is not one of those roots. Its
ordinary 7.2.10 refusal therefore does not suppress **7.2.35 iṭ before
val-initial liṭ endings**. The counted A perfect has iṭ at 2sg, 2pl, 1du
and 1pl, and no added iṭ at the five vowel-initial ending slots. A 3pl's
i belongs to **irec**, while 1sg has the original ending i transformed to e.
Neither is an affix iṭ.

Rule **7.2.73**, including its sak+iṭ provision for ā-final roots, inherits
**parasmaipade**. It cannot be imported into these A aorists. Nor does the
passive/bhāva ciṇvat+iṭ option of **6.4.62** apply to the selected kartari
frames. These refusals preserve pada and voice as separate source guards.

## H03. Root ā has different addressed continuations

No new label supplies śnā or another śit affix, so PG79's 7.3.79 **jā**
substitution is absent. The base is the original **jñā**, not jānī or a
present stem reconstructed from pronunciation.

In A liṭ the original root is vowel-final, hence not saṃyogānta: its
**initial** jñ cluster does not defeat **1.2.5**. The apit liṭ endings are
kit. With a vowel-initial ārdhadhātuka ending, or with its iṭ augment,
**6.4.64** deletes the root's final ā. Establish the kit condition before
that deletion; a later consonant-final display is not a reason to retract
an already licensed affix designation.

In blessing/sic, the actual right boundary starts with s. It is not the
vowel-initial context for 6.4.64; a remote A 1sg ending i or a cannot be
queried across sīyuṭ/sic as if it were adjacent. Nyāsa/Bālamanoramā 6.4.64's
dāsīya discussion specifically distinguishes ending iṭ from its augmented
surface. Nyāsa's electronic final clause is difficult alongside its explicit
negation of vowel-initial status; preserve it literally and use the clear
sīyuṭ explanation together with independent Kāśikā/Bālamanoramā evidence.

The A kit rules of 1.2.11–13 do not cover jñā: it is neither an ik-adjacent
consonant-final root, ṛ-final, nor gam. The sthā/ghu change of 1.2.17 is also
inapplicable. Thus no ī-root substitute is imported into the A aorist.
The optional e of **6.4.68** requires kit/ṅit liṅ; 3.4.104's blessing kit
is P-specific, so that P comparison adds no e branch to this A target.
