# PG127 — The aorist remembers a vowel that has disappeared

Monty · 11 November 2026

A surface-only derivation could lengthen the a of dṛṣad after both pieces of ky have disappeared. Kāśikā at 1.1.57 supplies a close control: its derivation of avadhīt expressly uses the standing of lost a to block 7.2.7. Kaumudī's 6.4.50 uses the same type of evidence for samidhitā and guṇa. These are distinct precedents for separate grade rules.

This packet applies that reasoning to 36 new luṅ requests and 74 written word readings.
