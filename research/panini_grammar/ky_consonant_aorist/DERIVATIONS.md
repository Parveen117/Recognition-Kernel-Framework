## H07. Two long-vowel P endings

P 3sg and 2sg use ending-owned īṭ and 8.2.28 in the inherited sic schedule. The P 3sg words are asamidhyīt/asamidhīt and adṛṣadyīt/adṛṣadīt. These are two forms for each corresponding request, with no optional grade from the already contracted host. Other P slots use the short-i/sic portions of the pinned schedule, e.g. adṛṣadyiṣṭām/adṛṣadiṣṭām in 3du.

## H08. A endings and the exceptional 2pl

A 3sg gives asamidhyiṣṭa/asamidhiṣṭa. In 2pl 8.2.25 deletes sic-s before the ending. Retained y puts iṭ after iṇ and allows asamidhyidhvam/asamidhyiḍhvam. Deleted y leaves dh before iṭ and produces asamidhidhvam. The dṛṣad profile gives the same 2+1 branch structure.

## H09. Inverse controls

A readout adṛṣadīt identifies the P 3sg key and the deleted-y schedule within this packet. An input identifying only dṛṣad, without the semantic or pada certificate, admits either P or A. A request for long nominal ā based only on the final spelling has no witness. Distinguish it from the real long ī of the ending in adṛṣadīt.
