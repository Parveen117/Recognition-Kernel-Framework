# PG227 · Radical accent and actual ciṇ in the activity and patient aorists

Monty · 12 November 2026

The two *sam* entries give **asami** in the impersonal aorist. The passive of *nam* gives **anāmi**. Both are m-final, but the original lexical accents differ. Under 7.3.34 the two udātta *sam* entries retain short *a*. The anudātta teaching of *nam* leaves the ordinary strengthening available.

| Third singular, patient or activity | Morphological decision |
| --- | --- |
| *amāhi*, from class-one *mah* ‘honour’ and *māh* ‘measure’ | Short radical *a* strengthens in the first, inherited *ā* survives in the second. |
| *amahi*, from curādi *maha* ‘honour’ | Its vowel-final teaching and prior ṇic history give a short vowel after loss of ṇic. |
| *astyāyi*, both *styai* roots | The ā-final input acquires *yuk* under 7.3.33, before the pronounced *i* of *ciṇ*. |
| *aśāmi*, curādi *śam* ‘consider’ | The selected lexical reading is long. The separately printed *aśami/aśāmi* under 6.4.93 has its own *mit* context. |

Only third singular has actual *ciṇ* and deletion of the following ending *ta*. In the other patient-agreement positions, a curādi primary can have an ordinary *sic* route or a route treated like *ciṇ*. For curādi *maha*, the latter gives *amahiṣātām*, beside ordinary *amahayiṣātām*. At second plural the inherited rule for *dh/ḍh* permits **amahidhvam** alongside **amahiḍhvam** after h. The comparison archive prints only the retroflex spelling on this particular branch.

The six patient and six impersonal senses have **60 aorist positions**. Three were already counted for the rod in PG221, leaving **57 new positions and 87 readings**. All archive readings at these 60 licensed positions are represented. The one additional written form is identified above.
