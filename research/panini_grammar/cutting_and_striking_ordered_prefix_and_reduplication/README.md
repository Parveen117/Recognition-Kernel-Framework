# PG274 · The cutting and striking roots across augment and reduplication

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Kāśikā gives *praṇiṣyati/pariṇiṣyati* for cutting or ending and *praṇihanti/pariṇihanti* for striking. The first word involves two consonant changes. Rule 8.4.17 supplies the inner *ṇ* in both roots, while 8.3.65 separately supplies *ṣ* in *ṣyati*. The Mādhavīya quotes the ordered present and the augmented imperfect together, *praṇiṣyati, praṇyaṣyad* in the edition. The pausal imperfect below has *-t*.

| Selected third singular | Present | Imperfect | Perfect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ending, *pra + ni* | praṇiṣyati | praṇyaṣyat | praṇisasau |
| Striking, *pra + ni* | praṇihanti | praṇyahan | praṇijaghāna |

Mādhava explicitly gives *abhisasau* with dental *s* in the perfect of the cutting root. He invokes the restriction on the copy in 8.3.64. He also gives both augmented aorists *abhyaṣāt, abhyaṣāsīt*. The two paths therefore remain visible in *praṇyaṣāt / praṇyaṣāsīt*. A past augment and a reduplicative syllable have different effects on the root consonant.

For striking, the lexical commentary directly contrasts bare *hanti, hataḥ, ghnanti*. With the two preverbs the inner prefix stays the same through those changes. In the independently cited single-prefix *praghnanti*, 8.4.22 excludes a change in the root nasal since it lacks a preceding short *a*. Under 8.4.23 the commentary gives both *prahaṇvaḥ* and *prahanvaḥ* in the direct single-prefix first dual. Those examples belong to that specific root and prefix environment.

The three third-person numbers add **24 positions** for cutting (present, imperfect, perfect, aorist) and **18** for striking (present, imperfect, perfect). The two aorists of cutting yield **48 readings across 42 positions**. The two roots still have additional persons, moods and tense histories to cover.
