## Neuter: three ending replacements and the plural augment

On the positive neuter input do not attach ṭāp. Retain the same a-final
bhinna kta stem with its original root/suffix n owners. 7.1.24 replaces
su/am by am after neuter a, giving bhinnam under 6.1.107; the general
7.1.23 luk account must not displace that a-specific prescription. Dual
au becomes śī by 7.1.19; after ś-marker lopa the a+i seam gives bhinne.
These are separate gender histories from feminine dual bhinne.

7.1.20 replaces nominative/accusative plural jas/śas by śi. Its 1.1.42
sarvanāmasthāna status licenses 7.1.72 num in the vowel-final neuter stem;
1.1.47 puts the m-it augment after the final vowel. After marker lopa the
owned view is bhi-n_root | n_kta-a | n_num | i_ending. The resulting
n-final aṅga has its a upadhā lengthened by 6.4.8, giving bhinnāni.
The third dental n is neither root n nor kta n; do not erase their lineage
because the visible word contains similar letters.

The same plural route applies to vocative plural: 2.3.49 defines sambuddhi
as vocative **singular**, so 6.4.8's asambuddhi condition does not suppress
plural lengthening. Vocative singular gives bhinna under the pinned nominal
ending-lopa interface; no unnecessary final m is retained there.

Oblique singular/dual/plural use PG349's ordinary a-stem operations on the
same a-final stem, while retaining neuter agreement. At neuter ablative singular select devoiced pause
bhinnāt, as in PG349. The PG216 interface also records voiced styānād;

bhinnāya, bhinnasya, bhinnayoḥ and bhinnānām may coincide with masculine
words but are new gendered keys. Locative plural uses e before su and then

plural nuṭ n is different from nominative plural num n. No retroflex n
