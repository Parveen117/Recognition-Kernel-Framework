These are neither extra positions nor the same consonant history as √chid.

The aniṭ future path gives *kṣotsya-*; luṭ gives *kṣottā-*. Perfect reduplication is *cu-kṣud*, with strong P *cukṣoda/cukṣoditha* and weak *cukṣud-*. A retains only its sic-kit path, beginning *akṣutta, akṣutsātām*. P blessing begins *kṣudyāt/kṣudyād*; A begins *kṣutsīṣṭa*. Present-system śnam does not enter the two blessing paths.
