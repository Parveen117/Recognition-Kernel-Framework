# PG848 · Urvaśī’s two predictions and the future of unprefixed śī

In Mādhyandina Śatapatha 11.5.1.11 Urvaśī tells Purūravas to come on the night completing the year. In the first prediction Purūravas is the sleeper and addressee. The accusative ekāṃ rātrim measures the night spent near the speaker. Sāyaṇa glosses ante with antike, nikaṭe and explicitly parses śayitāse as śīṅ in luṭ. In the second prediction the child is the subject. In the joined text bhaviteti resolves to bhavitā + iti. Sāyaṇa parses bhavitā as a second luṭ and explains the prospect of the child's birth. One speech thus supplies independent second-person middle and third-person evidence, with different roots, subjects and original endings. PG16 owns bhū in luṭ. PG847 counted the feminine night ordinal. Both are reused.

Urvaśī's promise itself has no such counterfactual reading. Prefixed intensive/comparative ati-śī, causative śāyaya, bhāva, passive and desiderative requests do not enter these keys.
