# PG158 · Four witnessed aorists and the nominal vowel before ṇic

Monty · 11 November 2026

The 3.1.21 nominals now have ten complete lakāras in both of the selected result frames. The caṅ aorist takes the copied nominal factor, after the prefix and independent aṭ. Ṇic-lopa before caṅ does not undo the nominal's earlier ṭi-lopa. In particular the ordinary sanvat operations of 7.4.93–94 require the relevant absence of aglopa. The Kaumudī's hali and kali instruction says expressly why these roots do not take the long sanvat pattern.

For hal the copy's h takes the palatal treatment of 7.4.62 and the final copy consonant is j under 8.4.54. For kal, k gives c; the c in the kṛta copy has the same place change, and 7.4.66 supplies a in the copy of ṛ. In tūsta the copied vowel is short u. Agenda such as the oath's two readings belongs before these sound decisions. The lexical prohibition on a sanvat lengthening cannot choose which food is eaten.
