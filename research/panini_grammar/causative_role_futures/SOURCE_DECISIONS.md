## H02. Yak/ciṇ prohibition does not prohibit ciṇvat

Kāśikā 3.1.87 lists yak, A, ciṇ and ciṇvat work as distinct
consequences of karmavat transfer. The ṇi-vārttika at 3.1.89 blocks
yak and actual ciṇ. Kaumudī explicitly states that ciṇvat-iṭ still
applies and gives kāriṣyate; Bālamanoramā explains eligibility through
the originally taught vowel of ṇic. The complete continuation passage
is stored under **3.1.88** in this source archive: its discussion
explicitly moves to 3.1.89. Do not attach that sentence to tap's
special rule merely from the file key/header.

Accordingly, transfer the established 6.4.62 aṅga-work relation only
on **UPA_SIC_ROLE_IDENTITY_A**. The actual expression remains kartari,
but the admitted karmavat work context supplies the relevant karma-like
query. The other five profiles lack that work-transfer witness and
have only ordinary causal continuation. Merely being A, containing
ṇic, or having the named root dṛś is insufficient.
