## H08. Keep prefixes outside the aṅga augment and perfect copy

After H01/H02 admission, construct the basic finite verb with its actual pada and context. Retain the prefix array as separate constituents throughout; do not concatenate them into an apparent new root before selecting markers, vikaraṇa or aṭ. Shared endings reuse the pinned PG16 future construction, PG31 secondary endings, PG6/7 present construction and PG68–70 kṣi work.

For laṅ, 6.4.71 and 1.1.46 place aṭ at the beginning of the selected verbal aṅga, **after pra and ni**. At the ni/aṭ junction 6.1.77 replaces i with y. Thus the owned sequence is pra | ni | aṭ | gad | śap | t, with the permitted prefix ṇ operation, yielding **praṇyagadat**. The a is still aṭ, not a prefix vowel newly assigned to gad. The optional cases give **praṇyapacat/pranyapacat** and **praṇyaviśata/pranyaviśata**. This sandhi does not undo the already established ni + viś relation for A.

For liṭ, 6.1.8 duplicates the root's eligible part, not the prefix sequence: pra | ni | ca | kār | a, pra | ni | ci | kṣāy | a, pra | ni | ca | khād | a. Original teaching and copy have separate addresses. The prefix guard still queries original k/kh, even though the first visible sound after ni is now c.

## H09. Present and imperfect aṅgas

Gad, pac and khād take **śap** by 3.1.68. Viś takes **śa** by 3.1.77; its apit/ṅitvat character prevents root-upadhā guṇa under 1.1.5, so the present is viś-, not veś-. Attach the P or A thematic endings selected in H02. At A vowel junctions use the inherited e/ete/ethe/e and first-person āvahe/āmahe construction; laṅ has etām/ethām and āvahi/āmahi. Prefix ṇ does not modify this ending selection.

Kṛ takes **u** by 3.1.79. The pinned PG6 construction gives strong kar-o and weak kur-u under 6.4.110; weak u-lopa before v/m is mandatory by 6.4.108. Hence **pranikaroti/pranikurutaḥ/pranikurvaḥ** and laṅ **pranyakarot/pranyakurutām/pranyakurva**. Laṅ first singular has strong o→av before am: **pranyakaravam**. The retained 1.1.58/8.2.79 query blocks invented kur-vowel lengthening.

Kṣi takes **śnu** by 3.1.73. Its pinned PG12/68 affix construction gives strong no, weak nu, ṇ by the root ṣ, u→v before the third-plural vowel and optional 6.4.107 u-lopa at first dual/plural. Thus **pranikṣiṇvaḥ/pranikṣiṇuvaḥ** and **pranyakṣiṇma/pranyakṣiṇuma** retain exactly their two u histories. Neither ni nor aṭ changes śnu's cluster query.

Before s, ṣ→k by 8.2.41 for viś/piṣ; sya s becomes ṣ after the eligible iṇ/ku by 8.3.57/59. Before tās, ṣ+t becomes ṣṭ by 8.4.41. Thus viś gives **veṣṭā** and **vekṣyate**, while piṣ gives **peṣṭā** and **pekṣyati**. The 8.4.18 query sees original ś in viś and original ṣ in piṣ despite these transformations. Kṛ's **kartā/kariṣyati** shows why a single invariant aniṭ Boolean is insufficient.

Luṭ third-person ḍā/rau/ras and the remaining tās/as-series endings reuse PG16: A and P share the three third-person readings but differ in the other six slots. In particular viś A has **praṇiveṣṭāhe/praniveṣṭāhe** at first singular and **praṇiveṣṭādhve/praniveṣṭādhve** at second plural. These are ordinary kartari A futures, not ciṇvat or passive alternatives.

For khād 1010051, 7.4.59 shortens the **copy** ā, 7.4.60 retains its initial consonant, 7.4.62 palatalizes kh and 8.4.54 supplies unaspirated c: **ca | khād**.
