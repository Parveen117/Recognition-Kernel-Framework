# PG71 — Pra + ni: complete selected words

Monty · 7 November 2026

All requests satisfy H01–H07: ordered pra + ni, actual kriyāyoga, basic kartari, the identified lexical teaching and the declared context. These are source-qualified constructions, not attestations of every complete word. P/A are grammatical padas; viś A still expresses the agent. Optional ni readings list retroflex before dental. The ordinary pause profile selects final car and omits optional consonant doubling; it does not exhaust pronunciation variants.
