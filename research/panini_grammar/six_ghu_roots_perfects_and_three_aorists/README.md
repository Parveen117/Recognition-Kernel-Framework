# PG259 · All six ghu roots and the three aorists of the suckling verb

Monty · 12 November 2026

The sucking root **dheṭ** has three active aorists. Kṣīrasvāmin and Maitreya independently give **adadhat**, **adhāt** and **adhāsīt**. Rule 3.1.49 permits *caṅ*. When *sic* is used, 2.4.78 permits its loss. Each of the nine positions therefore has three selected words for this root. The same *adhāt* also occurs with the placing root.

| Third singular after *pra + ni* | Perfect | Aorist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *dheṭ*, suckle | praṇidadhau | praṇyadadhat / praṇyadhāt / praṇyadhāsīt |
| *dāṇ*, give | praṇidadau | praṇyadāt |
| *deṅ*, protect | praṇidigye | praṇyadita |
| *do*, cut | praṇidadau | praṇyadāt |

The protecting root supplies a separate perfect. The Kāśikā at 7.4.9 and Maitreya explain that the *digi* substitution supplies the perfect in place of ordinary reduplication. Kṣīrasvāmin gives **avadigye** as a full prefixed example. The *pra + ni* middle second plural has both **praṇidigyiḍhve** and **praṇidigyidhve** in the selected written policy.

PG257–259 now contain **720 of 720** positions for these four roots. Together with PG253–255, all **six designated ghu roots** fill **1,440 positions** for these two ordered preverb pairs. The two class-three roots each have both semantically distinguished padas. The four others each have the pada licensed for the selected context. The last packet adds **144 positions and 188 readings**.
