# PG278 · Perfect persons and a printed weak-stem difference

Monty · 13 November 2026

The Laghusiddhāntakaumudī under 7.3.55 explicitly supplies *jaghanitha/jaghantha, jaghnathuḥ, jaghna, jaghāna/jaghana, jaghniva, jaghnima*. The two ordered prefix histories retain those alternatives without changing the inherited rule for inner *ni*.

| Perfect cell | Striking, pra + ni | Cutting, pra + ni |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2sg | praṇijaghanitha / praṇijaghantha | praṇisasātha / praṇisasitha |
| 1sg | praṇijaghana / praṇijaghāna | praṇisasau |
| 1du | praṇijaghniva | praṇisasiva |

The first-person *han* option belongs to 7.1.91, where ṇal is optionally ṇit, and to its vowel consequence under 7.2.116. It must not be transferred to other persons. The cutting-root *thal* alternatives retain separate iṭ and non-iṭ paths: *sasitha* has lost the final root ā under 6.4.64. The dental-s reduplicated base follows the traditional perfect witness retained in PG274, rather than the present's *ṣy-*.

One edition difference remains explicit. The Mādhavīya XML entry 662 prints **जघ्निथ** beside **जघन्थ**. The Laghusiddhāntakaumudī and the selected bare archive instead have **जघनिथ**. This packet selects the latter for the iṭ-bearing path and retains the former as a source difference; it does not call the isolated printed string an extra grammatical option. That is **one source observation**, with no extra position credit.
