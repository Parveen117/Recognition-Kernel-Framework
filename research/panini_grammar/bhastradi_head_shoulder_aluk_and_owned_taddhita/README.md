# PG692 · Head and shoulder loads, inner aluk and owned ṣṭhan

Monty · 24 November 2026

Nyāsa and Tattvabodhinī give a locative-compound
analysis for the head-load pair. Kāśikā explicitly
lists both head and shoulder doublets, and the
dictionary gives the matching three-gender carriers.
The primary digital gaṇa has *śīrṣa* in the place
where Kāśikā lists *śīrṣabhāra*. Each membership
witness remains at its own address.

**192 new nominal positions and 396 readings** cover
the two masculine load nouns and the masculine,
feminine and neuter carrier adjectives. Each pair
of accepted compounds is a reading choice within
one requested case key. The inner locative e stays
visible through deletion of the later supporting
instrumental. The final case suffix belongs to
the resulting noun or adjective.

Census **52,323 finite positions and 8,276 nominal
positions/9,741 readings**. The separate S5 word
assignments remain Open.
