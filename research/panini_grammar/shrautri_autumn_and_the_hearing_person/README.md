# PG951 · Autumn and the hearing person

Böhtlingk and Roth, entry 101998, gives precise addresses for the adjectival use left open at PG950. The witnesses differ in both gender and the relation expressed. The cases here are written from those passages. [PG950](../shrautra_ear_and_collection/README.md) · [Pinned dictionary file](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/776f75662b804497939e179f2ae1468b83ad55a7/v02/pwg/pwg.txt)

**Feminine in VS 13.57.** The mantra joins the end of *śarát*, the descriptor *śrautrī*, and the beginning of *anuṣṭup*. At the right-hand boundary ī appears as y before a. Recovering a separate feminine nominative requires the controller as well as the phonology. Mahīdhara's gloss takes autumn as arising from hearing and in turn anuṣṭubh as arising from autumn. Thus *śrautrī* agrees with *śarad*, while the following *śāradī* agrees with *anuṣṭubh*. The succession gives two different source relations. The base *śrotra* is also used directly at the end, as the object of *gṛhṇāmi*. That neuter object does not control the feminine. On the relational aṇ analysis the vṛddhi belongs to the inner taddhita and the feminine ī to ṅīp after aṇ (4.1.15). The interpretive expression of origin licenses the semantic input here. Mahīdhara's explicit citation of 4.3.120 occurs on the preceding *sauva* and cannot be transferred as his explicit rule citation on *śrautrī*. [VS 13.57 and commentary, revision 369793](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=369793) · [Feminine suffix contract](../independent_written_closure/RULES_D2.md)

**Masculine in Śatapatha 14.5.5.8.** In the account of the directions, *śrautraḥ* characterizes the inner person, alongside *prātiśrutka-*. The paired description in Bṛhadāraṇyaka 2.5.6 supplies the expressed head *puruṣaḥ*. It licenses M NOM singular. Śaṅkara's gloss on the latter passage associates the directions with hearing and explains the companion adjective in terms of the perception of a returning sound. This does not give a collective nominal reading. The two related texts support one position. [Śatapatha, Mādhyandina 14](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/1_veda/2_bra/satapath/sb_14_u.htm) · [Bṛhadāraṇyaka with commentary](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/1_veda/4_upa/brupsb_u.htm)

**What the third dictionary address actually reads.** In Bṛhadāraṇyaka 3.9.13, Śṛṅgeri's mantra index prints *śrautraḥ* in the answer concerning the hearing person. GRETIL's base text prints *śrautaḥ*, without the second r, at that same point. The latter file has *śrautraḥ* at 2.5.6, so a blanket correction in either direction would destroy a source distinction. Śṛṅgeri's reading supplies another use of the existing masculine key. Record GRETIL's spelling at the verse address. Its derivational relationship to *śrautra-* and the history or priority of the variants remain Open. It supplies no extra inflectional alternative on the *śrautra-* paradigm.
