# PG254 · Shared third-person futures and the two benedictive grades

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Kāśikā contrasts **deyāt, dheyāt** in the active blessing with **dāsīṣṭa, dhāsīṣṭa** in the middle. The change to *e* in 6.4.67 depends on the following affix’s marker. The printed Mādhavīya on p. 394 also spells the placing root’s middle form **dhāsīṣṭa**. Its electronic entry 741 reads **dhasīṣṭa** with short radical *a*. The scan resolves the reading of that edition; the electronic reading has its own record.

| With *pra + ni* | Giving, active | Giving, middle | Placing, active | Placing, middle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Future *luṭ*, 3sg | praṇidātā | praṇidātā | praṇidhātā | praṇidhātā |
| Future *luṭ*, 2sg | praṇidātāsi | praṇidātāse | praṇidhātāsi | praṇidhātāse |
| Future *lṛṭ*, 3sg | praṇidāsyati | praṇidāsyate | praṇidhāsyati | praṇidhāsyate |
| Blessing, 3sg | praṇideyāt | praṇidāsīṣṭa | praṇidheyāt | praṇidhāsīṣṭa |

In *luṭ* the three third persons coincide across padas. Consequently 12 of the 36 same-root and same-prefix comparisons coincide, while the other 24 show distinct endings. *Lṛṅ* places *aṭ* after the inner preverb, giving active *praṇyadāsyat* and middle *praṇyadhāsyata* for the selected roots.

These four lakāras add **288 written positions**, bringing the eight ordered profiles to **576 of 720**.
