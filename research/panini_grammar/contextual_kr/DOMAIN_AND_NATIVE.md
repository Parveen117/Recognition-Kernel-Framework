## N2. Source-policy histories and ordered construction

\[

\]

where h′ appends the source-labelled event, recipient and stage. Alternatives have separate target coordinates; false or unknown guards retain the appropriate complementary histories. Ordered composition supplies grammatical evolution. Elapsed time would need a separately admitted clock adapter.

\[
|H_{luṅ,K}|=9,\qquad |H_{luṅ,B}|=2+2+7=11.
\]

At dhvam, both policies retain the shared sic-selection history, 8.2.25 s-lopa and 8.3.78 ḍh; 2.4.79 has no 2pl branch.

These are explicit obstructions to those two lossy observers, not claims that every language-analysis task is impossible.
