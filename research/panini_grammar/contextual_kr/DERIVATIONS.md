Rule 3.1.79 supplies u in place of śap. This u lacks ś and has its own ārdhadhātuka classification; it is not the following tiṅ. PG19 gives the full ending construction: loṭ's first-person āṭ is pit and keeps strong kar, with u→av before the augment. Its intervening vowel blocks the direct u/v-or-m lopa query. Thus karavāvahai/karavāmahai do not share laṭ's kurvahe/kurmahe path.

In ordinary A liṅ, 7.2.79 removes the appropriate non-final s, leaving the actual ī-initial material. The u then becomes v before a vowel: kurvīta. The P y-initial u-lopa of 6.4.109 does not apply to this A ī-initial context, and one cannot skip īy to inspect a later v/m. Laṅ has the non-ṭit endings and its own aṭ; it does not borrow laṭ's e substitutions. Its 2pl dh stays dental.

Prefix the admitted adhi/vi to the first three interfaces. In laṅ place the aṭ after that prefix and perform the stated i/a yaṇ junction. For example, **adhi|a|kuruta → adhyakuruta**, **vi|a|kurmahi → vyakurmahi**. This changes neither u's owner nor the agent-controlled agreement.
