# PG126 — A pair of words can hide three derivations

Monty · 11 November 2026

For the two stop-final nominal witnesses of [PG125](../ky_consonant_hosts/README.md), P blessing has two ways to reach the single-y spelling. One deletes denominative y in the morphology. The other retains both y sounds and licenses reduction after the consonant. The double-y spelling still survives. This is a finite example of why an inverse Sanskrit reader needs both ordinary words and retained derivational history.
