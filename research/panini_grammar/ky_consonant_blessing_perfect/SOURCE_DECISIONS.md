## H01. Same nominal and semantic evidence

Reuse the two explicitly stop-final 6.4.50 witnesses and the selected self-wish/P and agent-imitation/A relations of PG125. The outer operators may change, while the nominal reference and subject agreement remain part of the request. No new Dhātupāṭha entry is introduced.

## H02. Keep apart the two y owners

6.4.50 acts on consonant-following ky before an ārdhadhātuka operation. P blessing brings yāsuṭ, whose initial y is outside val. Thus 6.1.66 does not force the first y to disappear before the second. Under 8.4.64 the first y of a two-y group immediately after a consonant can optionally disappear before the following yam. Keep both underlying y IDs for this schedule.

## H03. Scope of the sound readout

The chosen normalized Sanskrit spelling retains the two underlying sounds or reduces their adjacent pair according to 8.4.64. Extra recitational doubling under neighboring rules lies outside this projection. Kāśikā illustrates the wider phonetic domain using śayyā and āditya; those examples establish the rule conditions, without attesting the new blessing words.

## H04. Follow the sound before iṭ in A

8.3.79 makes the dh/ḍh alternative available when the iṭ is preceded by iṇ. The retained ky-y meets it. On deletion the immediately preceding sounds are dh and d, both outside iṇ. The visible i of iṭ is present on each schedule and cannot decide between these outcomes by itself.
