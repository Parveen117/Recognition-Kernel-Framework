## Text and roles

The same clause pair occurs at Śaunaka 11.3.32e–49e. GRETIL supplies both
Whitney's continuous address and the split-hymn address 4.1–18. The
speaker explicitly says *aham* in each preceding c clause. A pronoun
referring to the instrument or support and accusative *enam* occur before
each verb. The following f clause names the dish, *odanaḥ*. The pada
transcription at 36 separates the two verbal words: the pra belongs to
*prāśiṣam* and the next verbal word is bare *ajīgamam*. The following
*saṃ bhavati* in g belongs to a distinct predication and verb. Thus this
source series establishes a null preverb for the attested gam token.
No inference from the orthographical distance of an otherwise eligible
preverb is needed. This also leaves PG678's separated sam–gam analysis
of 6.74.1 intact.

   It supplies no second reading of the caṅ word.

The instruments vary in number, which supplies controls on agreement:
head gives singular *tena*, eyes give dual *tābhyām*, teeth give plural

The locative *satye* belongs to the absolutive in d; the instrumental

## Two lexical policies, one word

**W, causal motion in the food context.** Whitney interprets the verb
alongside eating as causing the rice-dish to pass down. The base theme that
would move becomes causal karman by 1.4.52, while the speaker initiates
the event under 3.1.26. The selected organ or support is the means under
1.4.42. The unexpressed karman and means get accusative and instrumental
by 2.3.2 and 2.3.18 respectively. Tiṅ expresses the speaker. This is an
inanimate motion theme, with no eater introduced as inner agent.

**T, Trivedi's svārtha interpretation.** Trivedi explicitly gives a
svārtha-ṇic formation with luṅ and caṅ, glossed as reaching or obtaining
the dish. Here the accusative is directly the object of attainment.
The analysis supplies the same finite speaker, word and means. It has no
independent caused eater or event-controller argument. Preserve this
source-labelled analysis alongside W; the lexical-semantic permission
for a general svārtha route outside the passage is Open. The scan for
this packet does not establish Sāyaṇa's view on the odana clause.

Both use the pinned gam factor. Luṅ and caṅ are selected on the one
admitted ṇic formation. The caṅ route has owned ṇic deletion, jī reduplication
and retained root *gam*. The augment aṭ stays to the left of the copy.
The P1sg secondary schedule gives *ajīgam + a + m → ajīgamam*, with the
ending morph's m addressed separately from the thematic a. The root has no
