## H07. Causal caṅ preserves short copy i before kṣ

For causal kartari P/A, 3.1.48 replaces cli by caṅ. It is vowel-initial and ṅit, with pronounced a, so no val-iṭ is added. Kāśikā 6.1.11 and 7.4.1 specify **ṇic-lopa → root-portion upadhā shortening → doubling**. Rule 6.4.51 removes ṇic-i from kṣāy|i; 7.4.1 shortens ā to a in the root portion, yielding **kṣay**.

H02's conjunct-initial application of 7.4.93, with 7.4.79, gives **ci**. The root a in kṣay is light before single y; loss of ṇic does not count as the forbidden non-ṇic ag-lopa. But ci stands before original kṣ: **1.4.11 makes its short i guru**, blocking 7.4.94. The outcome is **ci·kṣay·a_caṅ**, not cī·kṣay·a.

No actual san affix, desire meaning or san-root ī is introduced. Original kṣ remains; copy reduction does not erase it. The 7.4.95–97 named exceptions fail their lexical guards. One short-copy result is counted.

Reuse PG66 H02's thematic P/A secondary endings after the pre-caṅ factor cikṣay.
