Monty · 9 November 2026

These are manually written rule inferences, not generated outputs or a claim
that all whole paradigms are independently attested. The three basic kartari A
profiles retain their [meaning/case policy](SOURCE_DECISIONS.md), supplied event
and agreement frames, and [owned operations](DERIVATIONS.md). Slot order is
third, second, first person, each singular/dual/plural.

SAM_RECIPIENT_DAN_A and SAM_PRA_RECIPIENT_DAN_A require the source-qualified
aśiṣṭavyavahāra usage and an expressed, connected third-case phrase with
recipient/fourth-case meaning. UPA_MARRIAGE_YAM_A selects the pāṇigrahaṇa
domain. These labels do not enlarge either source condition.

Selected sam spelling retains padānta anusvāra before consonants. Root-internal
nasals have their own obligatory sound operations. Only yam's luṅ cells have
two readings: kit first, non-kit second. They are not free nasal-spelling options.
