## Source policy and occurrence addresses

Siddhāntakaumudī in the field indexed by **2.4.79** attributes the
absence of उ-conditioned light-upadhā guṇa to Ātreya and others under
`संज्ञापूर्वको विधिरनित्यः`, and then records the contrary position.
It prints ऋणोति / अर्णोति and तृणोति / तर्णोति. Bālamanoramā explicitly
extends the disagreement from क्षिणु to ऋणु and onwards. This is a
commentarial, lexically qualified **matabheda**, not an optionality word
in the numbered text of 3.1.79 or 7.3.86, nor a universal permission to
omit guṇa. Call the selected branches **N** (no root guṇa) and **G**
(root guṇa). Each is retained with its authority; their union is the
packet's admitted source family, not one author's unqualified doctrine.

| Occurrence | Owner and role | Pronunciation after marker processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| u₀ in ऋणुँ / तृणुँ / घृणुँ | Nasal teaching vowel, retained as root-associated it metadata | Absent by 1.3.2 and 1.3.9 |
| u₁ prescribed by 3.1.79 | Separate vikaraṇa affix, neither tiṅ nor śit | Present as u before subsequent operations |

PG12's admitted root shapes ṛṇ / tṛṇ / ghṛṇ are reused. The lexical
note on ṛṇ expressly calls its ṇ a derived dental-series outcome; this
packet retains that note and the normalized PG12 identity, without
inventing a new irreversible normalization of the whole Dhātupāṭha.
The G branch's actual rṇ cluster is present irrespective of whether the
root's underlying dental-to-retroflex history is separately expanded.

## C01 — No unmarked root-u alternative from these entries

The exact upadeśas have nasal उँ. Both source policies apply 1.3.2/9
and supply u₁ by 3.1.79. A different tradition retaining a pronounced
root-final u would need its own pinned teaching/marker prescription and
affix account. Neither a flat string nor a generator's option list
provides that missing authority. Such an additional model remains OPEN,
not an alternative silently included in these 120 readings.

## C02 — Keep the two grade targets and their nimittas distinct

Reject a universal guard `root_guṇa_requires_pit_tiṅ` for the selected
G source family: अर्णुतः and तर्णुते are explicit counterexamples.
Also reject an unconditional `u_affix_is_ṅit_because_apit` designation.
Conversely, the G policy does not authorize *tarṇote in A 3sg: u₁'s
own grade still encounters the apit sārvadhātuka tiṅ.

## C03 — No unrestricted product of options

Reject G-plus-u-lopa in the four m/v cells of each root. Accept G-plus-
yaṇ before the vowel endings in H04. Do not prohibit a pronounced rṇv
sequence without identifying which event is requested. The deletion
guard is branch-local, evaluated after the actual root grade.

## C04 — Preserve the actual affix identity at the vowel seam

Do not borrow śnu's 6.4.77 / 6.4.87 behavior for u₁ or change the
root owner to obtain uvaṅ. P jhi and A jha also have different
substitution paths; H06's P -vanti and A -vate cannot be collapsed
into one ending rule. A blanket nasal class setting would lose both
distinctions.

## C05 — Literal attestation, extension and admitted context stay separate

Kaumudī supplies the ṛṇ and tṛṇ words identified above; Laghu-kaumudī
adds the explicit A pair. Its field literally says **अप्रत्यये** in
the preceding kṣiṇ discussion; preserve that text and use Kaumudī's
unambiguous **उप्रत्ययनिमित्तो** for the affix interpretation.
Bālamanoramā literally has **अर्मोतीति**; do not count it as the exact
word अर्णोति. The latter has its own Kaumudī witness.

The selected ghṛṇ passage gives the perfect **जघर्ण / जघृणे**, not
an exact present word. Its present tables use Bālamanoramā's stated
extension, the identified lexical row and the written rules. PG12's
missing independent literal present-word witness for 1080007 remains
open. None of these tables decides the contextual P/A license for a
sentence, other meanings, prefixes, Vedic readings, or the other lakāras.
