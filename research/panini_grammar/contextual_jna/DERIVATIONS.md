## H06. Śit substitution and the three śnā-vowel states

For the four present-system labels, 3.1.81 supplies śnā in kartari. Marker
ś is removed under 1.3.8/9 but its śit designation is retained. Rule 7.3.79
replaces **jñā by jā** before that śit affix; the long substitute and the
original whole-root history are preserved. It is not a newly appended n:
the n of jān belongs to śnā. No pvādi shortening is imported into this
explicit jñā substitution, under either of PG60's selected endpoint policies.

Write the owned interface **jā ∣ n-ā ∣ ending**. The outer recipient query
is separate from the root's śit query:

| Actual outer condition | Śnā-owned ā | Display interface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sārvadhātuka kit/ṅit, consonant-initial | ī, 6.4.113 (non-ghu) | jānī + consonant |
| Sārvadhātuka kit/ṅit, vowel-initial | lopa, 6.4.112 | jān + vowel |
| Loṭ first person, pit by 3.4.92 | retained ā | jānā + ending āṭ/ai |

Resolve A jha to **at**, 7.1.5 for the non-short-a aṅga, before the vowel
query. Thus laṭ 3pl is **jānate**, not a thematic *jānante* or a stem with
ī left before at. Resolve the liṅ augment before the same query. The root's
ā survives every one of these operations: root substitution and vikaraṇa
vowel change have different recipients.

## H07. Complete present and imperfect endings

All A laṭ endings are apit sārvadhātuka and hence ṅitvat under 1.2.4.
The ṭit transformations 3.4.79/80 produce e/se; A duals here keep āte/āthe
without the short-a-conditioned iy operation. With the original nine slots
ordered 3sg,3du,3pl,2sg,2du,2pl,1sg,1du,1pl, the owned result is

\[
L_A=(jānīte,jānāte,jānate,jānīṣe,jānāthe,jānīdhve,
jāne,jānīvahe,jānīmahe).
\]

The ending s after ī becomes ṣ under 8.3.59. A laṭ 1sg has ending i→e
first; śnā's ā disappears before that vowel, leaving jāne. In loṭ and
laṅ the same printed sounds may have different ownership histories.

Laṅ uses its ṅit ending series. Rule 3.4.100's i-lopa is P-only, as its
Kāśikā explicitly shows with retained A -vahi/-mahi; it does not delete
these A ending vowels. The final ṅ marker of mahiṅ has its separate
1.3.3/9 history. Initial aṭ is 6.4.71/1.1.46. The complete unprefixed tuple is

\[
G_A=(ajānīta,ajānātām,ajānata,ajānīthāḥ,ajānāthām,
ajānīdhvam,ajāni,ajānīvahi,ajānīmahi).
\]

In particular **ajāni** retains the A 1sg ending i; it is not the thematic
-e contraction and has no affix iṭ. No śap is introduced after śnā, and
no luṅ-specific sic/īṭ history is assigned to the initial a or final i.

## H08. Ordinary loṭ and vidhiliṅ

Reuse PG21 E4/E5 at the non-short-a śnā interface, with its source-qualified
ending/augment schedule. For loṭ, 3.4.90–93 distinguish the ordinary A
ending substitutions from first-person āṭ and pitness. The complete tuple is

\[
I_A=(jānītām,jānātām,jānatām,jānīṣva,jānāthām,jānīdhvam,
jānai,jānāvahai,jānāmahai).
\]

The last three keep śnā's ā before the pit ending: ā+ai gives ai by
6.1.88 and ā+ā coalesces by 6.1.101. This is the **ending āṭ**, not an
initial past augment. Tātaṅ's P-only alternatives do not enter these A
tables; the counted loṭ meaning is ordinary vidhyādi.

For vidhiliṅ, retain sīyuṭ and suṭ owners and 7.2.79's sārvadhātuka
non-final s-lopa. The resolved augment is īy; śnā's ā is removed before
this vowel, with the root ā unchanged. Augment y disappears before the
appropriate consonant by 6.1.66 and remains before a vowel. Therefore

\[
O_A=(jānīta,jānīyātām,jānīran,jānīthāḥ,jānīyāthām,
jānīdhvam,jānīya,jānīvahi,jānīmahi).
\]

The ī of **jānīte** is śnā's substituted vowel, whereas **jānīta** in
vidhiliṅ has augment-owned ī after śnā-vowel lopa. Equal-looking jānī
cannot merge those histories. Dental dh is retained in all these A 2pl
slots; no āśīrliṅ/liṭ/luṅ-specific retroflexion is imported. The ending
ran's final n is protected by 8.4.37; prati's earlier r also has blocking j
before śnā's n.

## H09. Future families and prefix/augment junctions

Rule 3.1.33 supplies sya for lṛṭ/lṛṅ and tās for luṭ, superseding śnā.
These are ārdhadhātuka and non-śit. H05's 7.2.10 decision withholds iṭ;
the lexical root remains **jñā**, without present jā or śnā's n. Thus the
owned factors are **B = jñāsy** (sya's thematic a left to the ending
contract) and **H = jñāt** (tās's remaining ending work left addressed).
Root ā does not trigger ṣatva of s: **jñāsya**, not an iṭ-bearing iṣya.
