## N2. Ordered construction and a limited observation obstruction

For an admitted elementary event define
\(U_d(e_hb)=e_{h'}(1+z_d)b\), where h′ appends its source, premises, recipient
and stage. HOLD/EXCLUDED transitions have coefficient 1 and no word output.
Alternatives, if a wider source policy admits them, occupy distinct labelled
coordinates; the present five-profile spelling policy has one path per key.
Evolution is finite ordered composition. Elapsed time would require a
separate clock adapter; a grammatical past/future certificate is not one.

Root jñā, its śit-conditioned jā substitute, śnā's n/ā, the outer ending,
sīyuṭ, initial aṭ and ending āṭ remain different constituents. The recipient
view first resolves the ending/augment and then queries śnā's vowel. The
future view instead has non-śit sya/tās and retains original jñā, with its
own 7.2.10 iṭ refusal. A single cached present stem loses this distinction.

For a concrete bounded impossibility result, define Q to retain lexical
ID, the sam prefix, laṭ, third singular and a genitive dependent presentation,
but discard that dependent's intended role and the ādhyāna certificate.
Consider (i) a completed intransitive means-of-engagement frame with sam,
non-ādhyāna certified, and (ii) the source's mātuḥ remembrance frame.
Their Q values agree. The first is admitted to A by 1.3.45, applying H03's
prefixed general rule; the second is excluded from that A route by H04.
The first is a rule-based control, not an additional attested or counted
profile. No function of Q alone decides both correctly: equal inputs to a
function cannot have different outputs. Retaining role and meaning resolves
this two-request obstruction. It is not a universal minimal-memory theorem.

every new z and its dagger to zero. Typed coefficient maps on inputs and
outputs preserve lexical/source identity, semantic roles, prefixes, meaning
and fruit certificates, rule/affix owners, slot, status and ordered history.

the same tagged successor. The same statement holds for complementary

give the written identity on this declared domain:

\[
\pi_{0,out}\operatorname{Construct}_{79,\lambda}
=\operatorname{Construct}_{79,0}\pi_{0,in}.
\]

\[
\epsilon(\langle x,y\rangle_{B_{79}})
=\langle\pi_0x,\pi_0y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.

each event. Unit-start admitted histories recover the 315 written readings

specialization, which retains the role and meaning. The finite enumeration,
source integrity checks and deferred grammar execution are separate claims.
