# PG79 — Complete manually written jñā tables

Monty · 8 November 2026

All five panels have A endings and the meaning/role/prefix certificates of
H01. Each cell is one position, reading and labelled path. Laṭ, laṅ,
ordinary loṭ, vidhiliṅ, lṛṭ, luṭ and lṛṅ have their own contextual
certificates. The selected sam spelling retains anusvāra before j;
accent, consonant doubling and alternative parasavarṇa are not counted.
These are rule-based written words, not 315 independently attested quotations.
