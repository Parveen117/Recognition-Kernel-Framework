## N2. Which choice does the shared ending conceal?

Fix kṛp, luṭ and 3sg. The same third-singular ḍā and surface
cikḷpsitā occur in both padas. Holding the pada fixed and asking
whether cikalpiṣitā is another reading gives different answers:
yes on A, no on P. Consequently a function of the shared word
and its ḍā alone cannot recover this alternative set. The PG118
branch and source ending class decide it on this finite domain.

output maps retain discrete guards, branch IDs, history and statuses.
All licensed choices are functions of those retained data. Each arrow
commutes since ε((1+z_e)b)=ε(b), also for unit complementary arrows.
Induction over the ordered inner and outer paths gives equality of
π₀,out Construct119,λ and Construct119,0 π₀,in on the admitted domain
and complementary statuses. The history pairing specializes termwise,
ε(Σ b_h* c_h)=Σ ε(b_h)* ε(c_h), preserving the declared tensor types.
No event-isometry, clock or physical identification is assumed.
