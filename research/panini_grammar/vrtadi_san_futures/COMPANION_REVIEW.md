belongs to sya/tāsi on the completed san-dhātu. Retain the original
finite ending in all nine slots and record ḍā, rau or ras separately.

counterfactual premise to the outer desire.

cikalpiṣitā and cikḷpsitā while P has only cikḷpsitā. For vṛt and
vṛdh, the P collision persists across all 27 new paired requests and
every corresponding A pair differs. The state and tables record these
as independent lexical requests.
