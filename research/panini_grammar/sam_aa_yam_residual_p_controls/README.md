# PG287 · *Sam/ā + yam* residual-active controls

Monty · 13 November 2026

After prescribing middle endings for an actor-directed principal result, Kāśikā 1.3.75 repeats the condition and directly contrasts **saṃyacchati, udyacchati, āyacchati** when that actor-result condition is absent. Rule 1.3.78 assigns active endings to this residual agent domain.

This packet writes the two still-open controls **SAM_YAM_OTHER_RESULT_P** and **AA_YAM_OTHER_RESULT_P**. They do not invent a particular object: the licensed distinction is exactly that the principal result is not certified for the agent. PG106's grain-collection A profile and PG107's cloth-attachment A profile retain their own objects and actor-result certificates.

The *sam* schedule preserves two different boundaries: *saṃ-yacchati* without a past augment, but *sam-a-yacchat* with internal *aṭ*. In the *ā* schedule, present *ā-yacchati* and imperfect *ā-a-yacchat* have the same written vowel after coalescence while retaining different histories.

Each profile has 90 positions and 94 selected readings. The selected alternatives are the same archive-governed four cells as PG286: *tāt* beside the ordinary imperative in 3sg/2sg, and two perfect words in 2sg/1sg.

**180 finite positions, 188 selected readings, zero nominal positions and zero new source observations. Cumulative finite total: 50,840.**
