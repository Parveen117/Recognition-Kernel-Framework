2. Establish R admission for that frame; preserve Open when unsupported.
3. Select the actual sya and its qualified O/C relation. Branch C-short/

4. Carry origin-sensitive iṭ and ṇic-lopa views, including grah's distinct
   i/ī paths. Do not replace an owned state with suffix-stripped text.
5. Add aṭ/āṭ and construct secondary A endings from their original members.
6. Apply the declared phonetic observation and retain the derivation result.
