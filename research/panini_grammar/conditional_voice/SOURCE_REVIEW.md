The Nyāsa 6.4.62 digital field has the sequence `अचायिष्यते` followed
by `लुङः`; that label/spelling is not used to turn actual sya into sic
or to impose a present/future ending on the conditional. The adopted
construction follows 3.1.33 and Kāśikā's explicit conditional examples.
The raw field stays unchanged. Nyāsa's reference to dṛś's am rule as
6.1.57 is also retained literally; the operative pinned rule is 6.1.58,
as PG18 already recorded. Other source typos are not alternate word forms.

Kāśikā 6.4.72 discusses ordering and aṅga identity in other passive
examples. Here edh is vowel-initial in both relevant views and has no
new samprasāraṇa operation. This packet does not settle every possible
competition between initial augmentation and aṅga reconstruction.

For ṇic-lopa, retain PG18's expressly adopted 6.4.22 reading and
origin-sensitive query. No source-policy rival is silently combined into
a new free morphological option. For actual C, i_cv is joint with the
transport and excludes a second ordinary augment. The basic/causal
formation boundary survives even where the resulting word is identical.
