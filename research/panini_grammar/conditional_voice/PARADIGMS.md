# PG33 — Complete bounded conditional-voice register

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

## P1. Explicit word constructor

In 3sg/3du/3pl/2sg/2du/2pl/1sg/1du/1pl order define

\[
R_A(C)=(Cata,Cetām,Canta,Cathāḥ,Cethām,Cadhvam,Ce,Cāvahi,Cāmahi).
\]

Juxtaposition means exact concatenation. C is the complete augmented
factor already derived in CONSTRUCTION, excluding its final thematic a.
Every Karma row below specifies this tuple's sole argument, so every
word in all nine slots is specified. These are written symbolic paradigms,
not output from an executed morphology generator. For Bhāva use only
R_B(C)=Cata. The other eight slots are excluded, not omitted work.

O/C denote the ordinary/ciṇvat source branches. Short/long on C are
the dependent mit alternatives. Branch rows do not create new position
keys. Every row requires D's conditional-admission and role evidence.

## P5. Accounting boundary

|Sector|Profiles|Positions|Branch rows|Readings|

Thus 49 nine-slot branch rows plus seven single-slot rows give
**448 readings**. Cumulative positions: 11,293+229=**11,522**.
Equal spelling across Basic/Causative or Kartari/Karma/Bhāva does not
identify their position keys. Repeated source examples, role witnesses
and conditional contexts do not create extra morphological positions.
These counts are neither distinct strings nor executed grammar cases.
