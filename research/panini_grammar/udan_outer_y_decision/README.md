### What the witness decides

Both dictionary forms still have the n of the thirst base. The y-less *udanitā* separates morphological deletion from the later 8.4.64 reduction of two adjacent y sounds: no second y is present in this diagnostic ending. The source provides positive evidence for a y-less outer formation on this exact lexicalized input. The dictionary does not label the two written -tā forms by lakāra or itself cite 6.4.50. This packet adopts the already source-defined optional ky-lopa of 6.4.50 to explain the pair. Its application to the other five outer environments is a grammatical inference on the same affix and host. Under the ordinary non-thirst purpose the vowel-preceded y of udakīya is retained.
