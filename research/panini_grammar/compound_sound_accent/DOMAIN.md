## D2. Evidence-bearing input

Retain PG37's relation, semantic case, denotation, register, selected source
policy, quantity/time structure, compound tree and addressed affix tokens.
Extend its history with the following views:

|View|Required information|
|---|---|
|Original lexical input|Root identity, upadeśa vowel and accent, markers, lexical versus technical designation, qualified gaṇa membership|
|Compound tree|Actual class, first/last member, external referent, nested structure, member's original case and inner taddhita/luk history|
|Repeated material|Trigger, first/second one-vowel unit, अभ्यास address, whole अभ्यस्त community and source-specific exclusions|
|Segment operations|Original and active value, target address, affix/root/augment owner, ordered replacements and lopa records|
|Joint replacement|Both source addresses, one output segment, membership views and rule-relative visibility before/after coalescence|
|Vedic context|Text/register, ऋक्पाद versus grammatical पद, मन्त्र versus other छन्दस्, witnessed optionality and editorial इति|
|Accent|Original udātta/anudātta/svarita, current accent owner, deletion/substitution history, source policy and competing prescriptions|

Every decisive gate is T, F or ?. **? remains Open.** Missing accent,
compound class, semantic relation, original marker or textual policy cannot
be inferred merely from unaccented surface spelling. A rejected accent
route does not delete all accents; a residual suffix route requires resolved
exclusions. A source gloss is linguistic evidence, not a claim about the
empirical truth of its traditional explanation.

## D4. Alternatives and output

Grammatical choice, dependent source clause and textual-policy disagreement
remain different types. At 6.1.196 four alternate accent placements are
source-authorized; at 6.1.200 and 6.2.51 two accents are simultaneous.
At 6.2.28 three placements occur across two different interpretations of
6.2.26; they are not three unconstrained options under one fixed policy.

Outputs retain admission evidence, selected clause, owned operations,
active and historical segments, accent claims, source policy and unresolved
realization obligations. Status is
`BOUNDED_WRITTEN_CONTRACT_NOT_EXECUTED`. The JSON register transcribes
the authored specifications; it is not an executable predicate compiler.
The denominator is the pinned edition's 3,983 catalogue entries, not a
claim that all editions enumerate the text identically.
