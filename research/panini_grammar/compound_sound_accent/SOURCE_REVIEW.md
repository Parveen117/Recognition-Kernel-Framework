## S3. Remaining source and realization work

Literal archives are not a normalized complete lexicon or gaṇapāṭha.
Many lexical/vārttika extensions are bounded by source attestation and remain
unconstructed individually. Original accent evidence, recensional choice,
register and exact derivation can remain Open. Full sound/accent interaction
with 6.2.55 onward and later chapters, feminine formation, nominal inflection,
runtime compilation and independent verification remain pending.

Earlier unclosed 4.1 families and recorded 4.4/5.1/5.2/5.3 source policies
are not silently resolved by this packet. The broader project claim remains
a source-relative grammar construction, with separate status for proof,
written contract, executed computation and physical or AI application.
