# PG38 — 6.2.1–54: compound and gati accent

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Read with [Domain](DOMAIN.md), [Construction](CONSTRUCTION.md) and
[Source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md). “Preserve first accent” means retain
the first member's established udātta/svarita, not invent a new first-syllable
accent. Source-policy alternatives stay distinct. K is Kāśikā.

The source's alternative first-member accents are retained as input facts.
Preservation therefore need not produce one surface accent for every lexical
entry. Such alternatives are source-conditioned, not permission to choose
arbitrary stress or to infer Vedic accent from present-day pronunciation.
