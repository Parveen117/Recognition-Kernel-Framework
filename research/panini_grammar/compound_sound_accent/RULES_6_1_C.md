At 158 priority includes पर, नित्य, अन्तरङ्ग, अपवाद and सतिशिष्ट
relations where the source licenses them. It is not a universal rule that
the last-numbered or last-executed accent wins. K expressly protects the
सार्वधातुक accent against a later विकरण construction, and gives नञ्
priority over both direct and case-triggered accent. The operation applies
at accent construction time, without waiting for a late surface पद label.
