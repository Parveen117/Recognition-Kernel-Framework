# PG51 — Compound-node and vowel-owner controls

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These eight written controls decide supplied queries. They are not executed
tests, eight new sūtras or eight additional agreement positions.

## C01 — Only the ष्यङ्-owned य् is replaced

Kāśikā and Nyāsa 6.1.13 explicitly restrict the operation to ष्यङ्
even when the surrounding stem contains other यण् sounds. Nyāsa's
वाराहीपुत्र example makes the distinction concrete: the initial व् is
not a second target. The ष्यङ्-owned य् is the target in H01/H02;
the larger ष्यङन्त constituent establishes its environment.

Replacing every यण् in the first member, replacing its whole last syllable,
or choosing another same-letter sound without its owner is BLOCKED. Missing
suffix identity leaves the query OPEN. The specific rule does not supply
a general य→इ operation on arbitrary words ending in या.

## C02 — The complete immediate उत्तरपद must be पुत्र or पति

|Supplied right constituent at the queried node|6.1.13 result|
|---|---|
|Exactly पुत्र or exactly पति, with the other premises satisfied|ADMITTED|
|पुत्रकुल, although it begins with पुत्र|BLOCKED at this outer node|
|परमपुत्र, although it ends with पुत्र|BLOCKED at this node|
|कुल|BLOCKED|
|An unparsed sequence containing पुत्र|OPEN until its constituent structure is supplied|

Kāśikā states both the तदादि and तदन्त exclusions. Padamañjarī
explains why H03's completed inner पुत्र compound can nevertheless occur
inside a larger कुल compound. H04's outer failure does not refute H03:
the two queries address different compound nodes and histories.

## C03 — A बहुव्रीहि containing पति is not this तत्पुरुष

The source gives the village-description case **कारीषगन्ध्या पतिरस्य
ग्रामस्य**, with citation **कारीषगन्ध्यापतिरयं ग्रामः**. Its relevant
compound is a बहुव्रीहि, so 6.1.13 is BLOCKED there even though the
letters पति follow the feminine stem. H02 instead supplies the actual
षष्ठी तत्पुरुष relation to the husband.

The citation is a source control, not a new full derivation of the village
sentence or its external sandhi. Unknown compound type is OPEN; it is not
resolved by checking adjacency alone.

## C04 — A different य suffix cannot impersonate ष्यङ्

Kāśikā's **इभ्यापुत्रः** and **क्षत्रियापुत्रः** exclude a broad
surface-ending rule. Their visible या does not provide the prescribed
ष्यङ् owner. On these supplied contrary suffix facts, 6.1.13 is BLOCKED.

This control concerns the named operation, not the validity of those words.
Their complete suffix derivations are not newly reconstructed. A parser that
has only a printed form must return OPEN about suffix identity rather than
guessing the positive ष्यङ् premise.

## C05 — Feminine prominence is scoped within the expanded first member

The commentaries use **न स्त्रीप्रत्यये चानुपसर्जने** to qualify the
usual suffix-based तदादि restriction. With the feminine bearer still
principal in **परमकारीषगन्ध्या**, the expanded first member can qualify;
the source gives **परमकारीषगन्धीपुत्रः / परमकारीषगन्धीपतिः**.
An अति compound in which that feminine bearer is subordinate does not
receive the same extension. That contrast is supplied semantic structure,
not a rule that every परम string passes and every अति string fails.

The ordinary subordinate-first designation at the outer षष्ठी तत्पुरुष
is a different query. If it automatically defeated the स्त्रीप्रत्यय
qualification, the source's own positive genitive compounds would fail.
Keep the inner constituent's prominence and the outer case relation separate.

Missing inner structure or prominence leaves the extended-host query OPEN.
The pinned Kāśikā and the material in the Nyāsa field differ in their
displayed अति stem and shortening discussion; [source notes](SOURCE_NOTES.md)
preserve that issue. This control decides the shared 6.1.13 exclusion and
does not claim to settle the full अति construction or its exact output.

## C06 — Replacement, vowel coalescence and lengthening are three events

For the admitted first-member ending, the addressed current sequence is
**ध् + य् + आ → ध् + इ + आ → ध् + इ → ध् + ई**.
The first event is 6.1.13/1.1.45, the second 6.1.108/84, and the third
6.3.139. The middle event replaces both vowel positions by one; it does
not erase the entire feminine suffix's grammatical history.

The 6.1.85 general contributor contract is retained only within its stated
limits, including its restriction for sound-conditioned operations. Historical
contribution is not permission to apply every later rule to a vanished sound.
Nor may an arbitrary later external vowel substitute for the internal आ
environment, which Kāśikā 6.1.108 distinguishes.

In H02 the first-member vowel becomes ई while पति's own इ remains इ.
A proposal to lengthen the final vowel of the whole compound, or every इ
in the displayed string, is BLOCKED as a wrong target.

## C07 — This source does not admit an optional short गन्धि output

Kāśikā 6.3.61 explicitly excludes the कारीषगन्धीपुत्र example through
व्यवस्थितविभाषा. Its 6.3.139 field supplies both the long कौमुदगन्धी
examples and the local priority/non-recurrence explanation. Thus an extra
short branch is not added to H01/H02 merely by reading “optional shortening”
without its qualification.

This local resolution is not a theorem that all long vowels survive every
compound operation, or that no rule ever applies again. It is also not the
separate 1.2.48 shortening question in the अति compound. General scheduling
and other source-policy cases remain OPEN.

## C08 — Internal सुप-luk does not delete the new external ending

S4 removes the compound constituents' own internal case affixes under 2.4.71.
The later nominative singular ending belongs to the whole compound and is
introduced only after its stem is formed. Applying S4's recorded deletion
to that new ending is BLOCKED as a different-owner event.

The retained case relation is not a pronounced suffix. The general
1.1.62/63 contracts distinguish qualified प्रत्ययलक्षण from लुमत्
exceptions for the अङ्ग; the packet does not infer that every property of
every deleted case ending remains operative. Its starting case records avoid
pretending that a full genitive allomorph was derived and then stripped by
an unspecified string operation.

The final visarga is admitted at the declared अवसान. In a different
following-sound context, 8.3.15 must be queried with that actual context;
the pause form is not an unconditional external-sandhi output.
