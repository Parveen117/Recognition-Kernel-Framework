# PG51 — Addressed ष्यङ् inside a qualifying compound

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

The starting point is the admitted feminine stem **कौमुदगन्ध्या**, with
its actual ष्यङ् history retained. PG51 constructs its पुत्र and पति
compounds and their singular citation forms. It also writes the two different
compound groupings explicitly distinguished by Padamañjarī 6.1.13.

## Supplied stem and construction boundary

Kāśikā 4.1.78 names कौमुदगन्ध्या and कारीषगन्ध्या. Its 6.1.13 and
6.3.139 fields explain the relevant descent formation, ष्यङ् and चाप्.
For this packet, supply the stem with that qualified provenance: the admitted
non-ऋषि गोत्र/feminine frame, the ष्यङ् owner Y, its current य् sound,
and the final आ with its feminine-suffix history. Use the replacement
account as the fixed input policy; do not reconstruct a second added-affix
account merely from the same printed stem.

The earlier उपमान compound, 5.4.137 गन्ध→गन्धि formation, descent
suffix admission, initial grade and feminine formation are source-attested
input history, not newly completed derivations here. Their general lexical
and semantic recognition remains outside this stage. In particular, a string
ending in गन्ध्या does not by itself prove a ष्यङ् owner.

Supply the indicated genitive relation and समर्थता. The explanatory
विग्रह **कौमुदगन्ध्यायाः पुत्रः / पतिः** expresses that relation.
The operative input is a stem-plus-case record, not an instruction to remove
letters from a finished inflected phrase. Its internal सुप affixes are owned
separately from the later external citation ending.

## Shared compound preparation — five stages

Let X be that admitted कौमुदगन्ध्या record and let U be exactly पुत्र
or exactly पति. These are the two immediate constituents of the compound
node C; U is not merely a prefix or suffix found inside a larger constituent.

|Stage|Operation or admitted condition|Retained result|
|---|---|---|
|S1|Supply the connected genitive relation under 2.3.50 and समर्थता under 2.1.1; admit the case-labelled constituents under 4.1.1/2|X with its genitive सुप owner, and U with its separate सुप owner|
|S2|Apply the qualified षष्ठी compound prescription 2.2.8 within 2.1.4/22|C is a तत्पुरुष, with X and U as its two immediate constituents|
|S3|Identify the prescribed subordinate constituent and put it first under 1.2.43 and 2.2.30|Ordered pair C = [X, U]; U supplies the compound's principal meaning in this admitted frame|
|S4|Give the compound its प्रातिपदिक status under 1.2.46 and remove its internal सुप affixes by 2.4.71|Current display X + U; case owners and their relation remain in history, not in pronunciation|
|S5|Evaluate 6.1.13 on C|ADMITTED: the left constituent carries the qualified ष्यङ्, the right constituent is exactly पुत्र or पति, and this node is the required तत्पुरुष|

Neither the whole compound's genitive relation nor its linear spelling
replaces S5's structural query. The स्त्रीप्रत्यय qualification used for
an expanded first member has its own scope, explained in C05; it must not be
confused with the outer compound's ordinary subordinate-first designation.

## H01 — कौमुदगन्धीपुत्रः with owned संप्रसारण

Take S1–S5 with U = पुत्र. The five shared stages and the following seven
stages give one **twelve-stage written history** from the supplied stem.

|Stage|Operation|Current result and retained address|
|---|---|---|
|P1|6.1.13, interpreted through 1.1.45, replaces Y's य् by इ|At the end of X, ध् + य् + आ becomes ध् + इ + आ; other sounds and owners are unchanged|
|P2|6.1.108 under 6.1.84 gives the earlier vowel as one replacement of this इ + आ pair|कौमुदगन्धि + पुत्र; retain both vowel contributors, including the current final आ's earlier history|
|P3|6.3.139 lengthens the संप्रसारण vowel at the end of this पूर्वपद before its उत्तरपद|कौमुदगन्धी + पुत्र = **कौमुदगन्धीपुत्र**|
|P4|Resolve the proposed 6.3.61 shortening using its source-qualified व्यवस्थितविभाषा|No short counterpart is admitted for this example; the long ई remains|
|P5|For the supplied masculine singular citation use, introduce external सुँ under 2.3.46 and 4.1.1/2; remove its nasalized उ marker under 1.3.2/9|कौमुदगन्धीपुत्र + स्; this external ending was not part of S4's internal सुप-luk|
|P6|On the सुबन्त पद of 1.4.14, 8.2.66 replaces final स् by रुँ; project its marker by 1.3.2/9|Final र् retained for the next phonetic query|
|P7|At the declared अवसान, apply 8.3.15|**कौमुदगन्धीपुत्रः**, unaccented singular citation form|

P1 targets one owned य्. P2 replaces two current vowels by one, rather than
silently deleting the feminine suffix from the grammatical record. P3 then
changes that vowel's quantity. These are three different events; a direct
text substitution गन्ध्या→गन्धी would lose their conditions and witnesses.

Kāśikā 6.1.108 explains why the internal following vowel matters. A later
vowel outside that admitted domain is not interchangeable with the current
आ. The source's treatment also prevents simply undoing this construction
with the general 6.1.77 यण् rule. Accent and a full declined paradigm are
not supplied by this one external citation ending.

## H02 — कौमुदगन्धीपतिः with a distinct external ending

Take S1–S5 with U = पति. The five shared stages and these seven stages
give the second **twelve-stage written history**.

|Stage|Operation|Current result|
|---|---|---|
|Q1|Replace only Y's य् by इ under 6.1.13/1.1.45|कौमुदगन्ध् + इ + आ + पति|
|Q2|Apply 6.1.108/84 to the internal इ + आ pair|कौमुदगन्धि + पति|
|Q3|Apply 6.3.139 to that final संप्रसारण vowel|**कौमुदगन्धीपति**, the compound stem|
|Q4|Use the same qualified 6.3.61 exclusion|Keep the first constituent's ई long|
|Q5|Introduce external nominative singular सुँ and remove its nasalized marker under 2.3.46, 4.1.1/2 and 1.3.2/9|कौमुदगन्धीपति + स्|
|Q6|Use the सुबन्त designation and 8.2.66 रु replacement, projecting its marker|The compound's final स् becomes र्|
|Q7|Apply 8.3.15 at अवसान|**कौमुदगन्धीपतिः**|

The final इ of पति is not the संप्रसारण vowel. Q3 targets the first
constituent's terminal vowel, so it does not lengthen पति into पती. The
new compound ending also differs from the internal case affix deleted at S4.

## H03 — A completed पुत्र compound becomes the first member

For the nested comparison use the source's exact **कारीषगन्ध्या** example,
with the same supplied ष्यङ्/feminine qualifications. Padamañjarī 6.1.13
explicitly allows the long-vowel result when the पुत्र compound is formed
first and its कुल relation is then composed. These are **five macro-stages**;
the first uses the already specified shared/phonological construction, not
a newly counted second set of agreement positions.

|Stage|Construction|Result|
|---|---|---|
|N1|Form the admitted [कारीषगन्ध्या, पुत्र] तत्पुरुष using S1–S5 and P1–P4 with that source-named first member|**कारीषगन्धीपुत्र** as an inner compound stem|
|N2|Supply its genitive relation to कुल and the required समर्थता|Two outer constituents: the completed inner compound and कुल|
|N3|Form the outer षष्ठी तत्पुरुष and remove its own internal सुप affixes|[[कारीषगन्धीपुत्र], कुल]|
|N4|Evaluate a proposed new 6.1.13 event at the outer node|BLOCKED: its उत्तरपद is कुल, not पुत्र or पति; no new संप्रसारण event occurs there|
|N5|Retain the already completed inner event and read the outer stem|**कारीषगन्धीपुत्रकुल**|

The long ई was licensed at the inner node; it is not licensed afresh by
seeing पुत्र inside the outer first member. An outer failed query does not
roll back that completed inner history. The source's citation कुलम् is
preserved in the archive; its neuter case ending is not newly derived here.

## H04 — पुत्रकुल is already the complete second member

Supply the other composition: first पुत्रस्य कुलम्, then the indicated
relation of कारीषगन्ध्या to that entire पुत्रकुल constituent. This
five-stage history follows the commentary's contrasting grouping. It does
not assert that the two different groupings express every semantic relation
identically.

|Stage|Construction|Result|
|---|---|---|
|M1|Form [पुत्र, कुल] as the supplied षष्ठी तत्पुरुष, with internal सुप-luk|**पुत्रकुल** is a completed compound stem|
|M2|Supply the source's genitive relation and समर्थता for the outer composition|कारीषगन्ध्या and the entire पुत्रकुल are the two outer constituents|
|M3|Form that outer षष्ठी तत्पुरुष with its own case-affix deletion|[कारीषगन्ध्या, [पुत्रकुल]]|
|M4|Evaluate 6.1.13 on the outer node|BLOCKED: पुत्र is only the beginning of its उत्तरपद, not the entire उत्तरपद|
|M5|Read the resulting outer stem on this supplied construction|**कारीषगन्ध्यापुत्रकुल**; no संप्रसारण-origin इ exists here for 6.3.139 to lengthen|

Before the relevant vowel change, forgetting brackets gives the same ordered
leaf sequence कारीषगन्ध्या, पुत्र, कुल in H03 and H04. The rule query
cannot therefore be implemented as a test for the next printed substring.
The two outcomes depend on the actual compound node and its retained history.

These four histories start from declared lexical/semantic inputs. They do
not complete a general compound parser, all source-policy variants, accent,
full nominal paradigms or runtime execution.
