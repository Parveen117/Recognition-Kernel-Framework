Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Continue the declared full carrier of

## D1. Domain and addressed compound query

A new grammar history retains a compound node C, its two immediate
constituents, their relations and prominence within their own scopes, the
supplied समर्थता, the suffix owner Y, and current versioned sound addresses.
Internal and external सुप owners are distinct. Completed inner operations
and any pending outer construction have distinct addresses and event order.

On that supplied domain, define the bounded 6.1.13 query at (C,Y):

|Required evidence|Admitted value|
|---|---|
|Compound kind at C|The specified तत्पुरुष|
|Left constituent of C|The qualified ष्यङ्-bearing formation, including the source-authorized feminine-host extension where supplied|
|Right constituent of C|Exactly पुत्र or exactly पति as the complete immediate उत्तरपद|
|Target sound|The current य् belonging to Y itself|
|Semantic and lexical admission|The declared related meanings and actual suffix provenance, not a substring inference|

All required premises affirmative gives ADMITTED. A resolved failed premise
gives BLOCKED for this query; otherwise unresolved required evidence gives
OPEN. These three records preserve the reasons and do not erase the input.
The expanded first-member prominence clause is local to that member, while
the genitive compound's subordinate-first prescription has its outer scope.

After admission the sound operations have separately typed targets:
Y's य्→इ; the current internal इ+आ pair→one इ; then that terminal
संप्रसारण vowel→ई before the actual उत्तरपद. The feminine contributor
history survives coalescence. Neither the other sounds of X nor पति's
own final इ are targets of that lengthening.

## D2. Leaf order is insufficient on the exhibited domain

Take the two supplied histories H03/H04. In hL the admitted inner node
has constituents [कारीषगन्ध्या, पुत्र], with कुल as its pending outer
continuation. In hR, पुत्रकुल is already the right constituent of the
outer node [कारीषगन्ध्या, पुत्रकुल]. Query the ष्यङ् at those respective
nodes before applying the proposed vowel operation. Fix the other necessary
premises as supplied in the histories.

Let q retain only the ordered lexical leaves, discarding compound nodes,
the designated query node and completed/pending history. Then

\[
q(h_L)=q(h_R)=(\text{कारीषगन्ध्या},\text{पुत्र},\text{कुल}),
\qquad G(h_L)=\mathrm{TRUE},\quad G(h_R)=\mathrm{FALSE}.
\]

**Bounded obstruction.** No single-valued decision function on q's output
can recover G on both records.

**Proof.** A proposed function f would have to assign both TRUE and FALSE
to the same q-value. This is impossible. Retaining the compound node and
its immediate constituents separates the exhibited pair and permits the
source-qualified decisions stated in H03/H04. □

This is a failure of this particular information-discarding observation,
not proof that all surface information is useless or that one bit of tree
memory suffices for Sanskrit grammar. Unknown parsing still gives OPEN.
No assertion of semantic equivalence, universal parsing correctness or
unrestricted reassociation of compounds is made.

## N1. Full-family extension and retained pairing

Under the unchanged PG50 coefficient and pairing assumptions, put

\[
B_{51}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le51}\rangle,
\qquad
M_{51,\lambda}=(M_{50,\lambda}\otimes_{B_{50}}B_{51})
\oplus\bigoplus_{h\in H_{51}}e_hB_{51}.
\]

The history summand has finite-support vectors. Sending the new event letters
to zero retracts the algebra inclusion. Extend the inherited pairing by
scalars and make the new summand orthogonal to it with pairing
\(\sum_h x_h^*y_h\). Retain the inherited nondegeneracy assumptions,
dagger, tensor types and cut-field structure; no positive Hilbert metric is
introduced as a primitive.

The supplied ADMITTED/BLOCKED/OPEN classification gives coefficient-independent
coordinate cuts \(P^T,P^F,P^?\). Each affirmative event e takes an admitted
source coordinate to its addressed successor by

\[
U_e(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a.
\]

Blocked and unresolved decisions have their own retained record channels.
A zero on the successful-mutation channel does not delete those records.
Compound formation, internal case deletion, sound replacement, coalescence,
quantity change and external-ending realization remain different events.
Evolution is ordered composition on histories; a stage number is not an
independently calibrated physical time.

## N2. Typed zero-reading compatibility

Let ε take the empty-word coefficient and let π₀ apply ε coefficientwise
while retaining compound structure, source policy, segment ownership and
decision/event history. For the fixed finite legal paths and decisions of
this packet, with their respective input/output types,

\[
\pi_{0,\mathrm{out}}S_{51,\lambda}
=S_{51,0}\pi_{0,\mathrm{in}},
\qquad
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_{B_{51}}
=\langle\pi_0x,\pi_0y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

**Proof.** Nonnegative free-word degree makes ε a unital dagger
homomorphism. Every supplied coordinate cut is coefficient-independent and
every event factor has ε-value one. Consequently the typed cuts, finite
ordered compositions and declared finite sums commute with π₀. Dagger
compatibility gives the pairing identity. The same reasoning extends the
inherited PG50 part by scalars under its unchanged assumptions. □

For a unit-start path this recovers its declared history and form at zero.
Signed combinations may cancel, so support preservation for arbitrary sums
is not claimed. D2's obstruction survives zero-reading because π₀ retains
the two different histories; π₀ is not q. Flattening after specialization
would discard the same necessary distinction again.

## N3. Open obligations

The construction does not supply a general semantic parser, all lexical
admissions, complete accent/inflection, a universal source-policy resolver,
termination/confluence or a runtime implementation. The next inner
परम/अति histories must settle their own prominence and shortening views.
A future coefficient-dependent admission procedure needs a new compatibility
proof. The bounded results establish neither a physical quantum gate nor a
universal hallucination-free AI system. Canonical operator development retains
its single home in operator_foundation/ on main; no runtime is changed here.
