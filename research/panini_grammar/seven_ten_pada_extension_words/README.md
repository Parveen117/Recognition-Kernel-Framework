# PG236 · Seven ten-pāda extension examples and their stated roles

Monty · 12 November 2026

The 1943 ten-pāda Uṇādi gives **seven words** under *anyebhyo ’pi dṛśyante*, 10.16. Its editor cites five-pāda 4.115 in that edition’s numbering. The project’s corresponding primary entry is 4.105. The source assigns a derivational role to each word.

| Printed word | Meaning in this passage | Derivational role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| petvam | a drink | object |
| metvam | a thing to be measured | object |
| pyātvam | sea or moon, from a root for increasing | agent |
| lūniḥ | cutting, applied to grain crops | activity |
| kṛśaḥ | thin | object |
| bhṛśam | exceedingly | agent or object in the source’s two explanations |
| accham | a seat | object |

The notes on pp. 434–435 preserve *pītvam* for *petvam*, the variant *dhyānaḥ* next to *pyātvam*, and *prasannam* in place of the gloss ‘seat’. Ujjvaladatta’s five-pāda *dhyātva* denotes thought. Its existing record cannot substitute for the main ten-pāda *pyātvam* denoting the sea or moon.

The immediately preceding 10.15 has **ten** affix labels. Its prose explicitly prints *ḍha* and short **aṭac**. At 10.16 the author invokes the affix series collectively. None of the seven examples comes with a new direct assignment to one of the four open primary labels. The register therefore stays at **99/103**.

Three previously uncounted nominal heads add **72 positions and 75 readings**. The inflections of *petva* and *lūni* were already tabulated at PG190. Their new senses here add two source observations and zero repeated case positions.
