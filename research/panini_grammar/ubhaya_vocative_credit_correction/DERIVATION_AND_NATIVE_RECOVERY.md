# PG898 · Keyed credit correction and native recovery

PG822's register marks M/F/N singular and plural vocatives `new`; PG824's
register marks the three dual vocatives `new`. These nine grammatical keys are
therefore already active before PG885. The audit key is
`(lemma, sense, gender, case, number)`, not spelling alone.

Six PG885 M/N words exactly repeat their earlier active readings. The feminine
key spellings differ, but PG822 positively replaces the ā-stem with `ubhayī`,
and PG824 conditionally transports that ī-stem on the permissive dual branch.
Thus `ubhaye/ubhaye/ubhayāḥ` are excluded only from that selected feminine
construction; `ubhayi/ubhayyau/ubhayyaḥ` retain their earlier owners. The
restrictive dual-free policy and permissive ekaśeṣa policy remain accepted
alternatives rather than being collapsed.

## Full PG14 carrier and typed zero-specialization

Reuse PG14's `B⁺ = C⟨z_e,z_e*⟩`, right history module
`M⁺_λ = ⊕_h e_h B⁺`, dagger, paired finite-support histories and empty-word
augmentation. Grade every row by exact source stage, lemma, sense, gender,
case, number, word, construction branch and prior-credit owner.

Let `K` form the grammatical key, `F` select the first active owner, `R`
retain the admitted reading history and `C` emit ledger credit. On a first
owner `C` emits coefficient one; on a later identical key it emits coefficient
zero while carrying the entire later history as an audit-labelled complement.
For a feminine reading incompatible with the selected ī-stem, `R` transports
the labelled source-block rather than deleting the record. Then

```text
π₀,Credit C_λ R_λ F_λ K_λ
  = C₀ R₀ F₀ K₀ π₀,AuditedRows.
```

For each admitted history edge use
`T_e,λ(e_h b)=e_k(1+z_e)b`; transport of a duplicate or blocked label has
coefficient one on its audit-history grade but zero on the separate credit
projection. Since the empty-word augmentation sends `(1+z_e)b` to `b`, the
elementary zero square commutes. Linearity, ordered composition, adjoints,
cuts, complements and paired evaluation extend this to the full carrier.

Typed zero-specialization therefore preserves PG822, PG824 and PG885 as three
distinct historical records while recovering one credit per active key. It
does not confuse zero *credit* with absence of a history, grammatical lopa, or
an elapsed-time parameter. The nine-position/nine-reading correction is
accounting recovery, not a new Sanskrit construction.

PG335's correction and PG340/346, PG349–352, PG355–360 and PG357–363 closures
remain unchanged. Open source readings and Claude-deferred execution retain
their separate registers.
