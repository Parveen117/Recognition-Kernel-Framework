# PG223 · Pitch, case and referent of the stellar and descriptive feminine words

Monty · 12 November 2026

The star names in Atharvaveda Śaunaka 19.7 have explicit accent: **róhiṇī** at 19.7.2a, **citrā́** at 19.7.3a and **revátī** at 19.7.5c. In the Ṛgveda, the Zürich source records the same **revátī** twice as the feminine nominative singular of the adjective *revánt-* ‘rich’. The asterism and the descriptive adjective can therefore coincide in both letters and transmitted pitch across these editions. The Nyāsa still distinguishes the two suffix histories at 4.1.41.

| Ṛgvedic selection | Number of words | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Feminine of *revánt-* | 13 | *revatīḥ* without pitch differences has three analyses in the corpus: nominative, accusative and vocative plural. Only the accusative agrees with the ordinary Classical ending in those three cases. |
| Adjective/noun *róhiṇī-* for a reddish cow or flame | 3 | Instrumental singular *róhiṇyā* and two locative plurals *róhiṇīṣu*. The source gloss is retained on each row. |
| Śaunaka star-name comparison, separately | 3 | The hymn names the constellations. Monier-Williams additionally records the possibility of final accent in the asterism name *Rohiṇī*. |

Ten of the sixteen Ṛgvedic feminine words match their corresponding ordinary unaccented case forms. The six differences are the two nominative plurals and four vocative plurals of *revánt-*. The *e* of the vocative singular is initially accented in **révati** at 10.86.13a; 5.51.14b has unaccented **revati**. The lexical interpretation and the location of a vocative within its own verse both remain available to the reader.

These are **nineteen source observations**. No finite or nominal position is added a second time.
