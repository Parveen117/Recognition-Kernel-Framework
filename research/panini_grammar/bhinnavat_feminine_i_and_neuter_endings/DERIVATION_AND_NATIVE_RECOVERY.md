## Feminine ṅīp and nadī inflection

final p undergo marker lopa (1.3.8/3/9); ī survives as the feminine suffix.
The result is bhinnavatī. The earlier ugit marker is not the observed ī;
there is no masculine strong num just because the stem once ended in vat.
Ṅīp is not a sarvanāmasthāna case ending. Long feminine ī supplies nadī
under 1.4.3.

Use the pinned ordinary long-ī interface. Nominative singular ending lopa
leaves bhinnavatī. Before ordinary vowel-initial endings, 6.1.77 yaṇ gives

7.1.23 luk; the a-final special am replacement is unavailable since the

present, and no a+i vowel merger is needed. Thus the feminine nominative
singular and neuter dual nom/acc/voc are homographs of different typed
histories. Surface ī alone cannot recover gender, suffix or number.
