# PG45 — Dependent णमुल् and क्त्वा constructions, 3.4.39–64

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These 26 bounded contracts preserve the named relation, shared-action frame,
compound choice and operation owner. Source witnesses do not count as newly
executed paradigms. See [source decisions](SOURCE_NOTES_A.md).

|3.4.39|Hand as instrument|Hand-denoting instrumental उपपद with causative वर्तय् or ग्रह्|Prescribe णमुल्|हस्त is meaning-based, admitting कर/पाणि; वर्ति is causative. The repeated verb must satisfy46|K: हस्तवर्तं वर्तयति; पाणिग्राहं गृह्णाति|
|3.4.40|स्व as instrument|Instrumental उपपद denotes the source's स्व senses with पुष्|Prescribe णमुल्|स्व includes self, one's own, kin or wealth in the stated relation; do not replace the required instrument by any object|K: स्वपोषम्, आत्मपोषम्, पितृपोषम्, धनपोषं पुष्णाति|

|3.4.42|Named kind of binding|बन्ध् construction conventionally names a binding configuration|Prescribe णमुल्|संज्ञा qualifies the complete binding expression; arbitrary resemblance is insufficient|K: क्रौञ्चबन्धम्, मयूरिकाबन्धम्, अट्टालिकाबन्धम्|

|3.4.45|Actor or object simile|उपपद supplies the compared actor or object in an उपमान relation|Prescribe णमुल्|च includes actor as well as object; literal identity without comparison is outside this clause|K: घृतनिधायं निहितः; अजकनाशं नष्टः|
|3.4.46|Following verb in कषादि clauses|A णमुल् construction governed by34–45 needs its accompanying verb|Require the accompanying verb to use the same prescribed root|This is a construction constraint, not permission to substitute an arbitrary semantically related verb; do not extend it mechanically to later47 onward|K/SK: निमूलकाषं कषति and the preceding root-matched witnesses|
|3.4.47|Accompaniment while eating|उप + दंश् with third-case उपपद in the linked action frame|Prescribe णमुल्|मूलक is object of उपदंश् but instrument of भुज्;2.2.21 makes compounding optional. |3.4.48|Injury and a shared object|Injury-denoting root and following verb share their object; instrumental उपपद supplied|Prescribe णमुल्|Same object is decisive: beating a thief while driving different cattle does not qualify|K: दण्डोपघातं गाः कालयति; चोरं…उपहत्य…गाः control|
|3.4.49|उप-prefixed pressure, restraint or pulling|उप + पीड्/रुध्/कर्ष् with third- or seventh-case उपपद|Prescribe णमुल्|उप belongs to each root; select कर्षति, not कृषति. Original case survives as addressed evidence|K: पार्श्वोपपीडम्; व्रजोपरोधम्; पाण्युपकर्षम्|
|3.4.50|Close contact|Third- or seventh-case उपपद in a construction expressing intimate proximity|Prescribe णमुल्|समासत्ति is actual closeness in the intended action frame, not any association of nouns|K: केशग्राहं युध्यन्ते; केशेषु/केशैर्ग्राहम्|
|3.4.51|Measure of length|Third- or seventh-case उपपद expresses आयाम/दैर्घ्य in the construction|Prescribe णमुल्|प्रमाण here is length, not general epistemic evidence or unrestricted quantity|K: द्व्यङ्गुलोत्कर्षं खण्डिकां छिनत्ति; द्व्यङ्गुलेनोत्कर्षम्|
|3.4.52|Haste from a source|Ablative/source उपपद and the source's haste reading|Prescribe णमुल्|परीप्सा is urgency that neglects intervening preliminaries; ordinary departure alone is insufficient|K: शय्योत्थायं धावति; आसनादुत्थाय गच्छति control|
|3.4.53|Hasty seizure of an object|Second-case उपपद and inherited urgency reading|Prescribe णमुल्|Do not drop परीप्सा: source depicts seizing whatever stick/clod is available, rather than deliberate preparation|K: यष्टिग्राहं/लोष्टग्राहं युध्यन्ते|
|3.4.54|Movement of an अध्रुव body member|Second-case स्वाङ्ग उपपद with the source's अध्रुव classification|Prescribe णमुल्|Historical body-member classification is supplied textual evidence; शिरस् is the explicit negative control here|K/SK: भ्रूविक्षेपं कथयति; शिर उत्क्षिप्य control|
|3.4.55|Whole-member distress|Second-case स्वाङ्ग उपपद and comprehensive pressure/distress of that member|Prescribe णमुल्|Extends to ध्रुव members excluded by54; general movement without the distress reading does not suffice|K: उरःपेषं/शिरःपेषं युध्यन्ते|
|3.4.56|Exhaustive coverage or repeated engagement|विश्/पत्/पद्/स्कन्द् with second-case उपपद and व्याप्ति or आसेवा|Prescribe णमुल् and retain the optional compound route|Compound itself expresses the chosen scope; in the uncombined route repeat the noun for exhaustive entities or the action word for repetition, not both indiscriminately|K/SK: गेहानुप्रवेशम्; गेहंगेहमनुप्रवेशम्; गेहमनुप्रवेशमनुप्रवेशम्|
|3.4.57|Interval between repeated actions|अस् in अस्यति formation or तृष् interrupts an action, with a second-case time measure|Prescribe णमुल्|Require both temporal measure and interruption; a distance measure or another uninterrupted action does not qualify|K: द्व्यहात्यासं/द्व्यहतर्षं गाः पाययति; योजनमत्यस्य control|
|3.4.58|Naming in an account|नामन् in second-case relation with आ + दिश् or ग्रह्|Prescribe णमुल्|The trigger is the named उपपद relation, not every act of pointing or grasping|K: नामादेशमाचष्टे; नामग्राहमाचष्टे|
|3.4.59|Report at an unintended volume|Indeclinable उपपद with कृ and pleasant news quietly or unpleasant news loudly reported contrary to expectation|Admit क्त्वा or णमुल्|The explicit क्त्वा prescription supports optional compounding under2.2.22; compounded branch has ल्यप्. |3.4.60|Completion with तिर्यक्|तिर्यक् उपपद and कृ expressing completion|Admit क्त्वा or णमुल्|Literal transverse arrangement without completion is excluded; compound choice retains its own ल्यप् versus क्त्वा realization|K: तिर्यक्कृत्य/तिर्यक् कृत्वा/तिर्यक्कारं गतः|
|3.4.61|Body-part तस् formation|Actual तस्-affixed स्वाङ्ग expression with कृ or भू|Admit either क्त्वा or णमुल् for either root|No यथासंख्य pairing: both roots take both suffixes. |3.4.62|Transformative ना/धा formation|उपपद bears ना or a धा-meaning affix and expresses च्वि-type change with कृ/भू|Admit क्त्वा or णमुल्|Require genuine affix identity and transformation; पृथक्/हिरुक् or mere separately handled objects do not satisfy both gates|K: नानाकृत्य/नानाकारम्; द्विधाभूय/द्विधाभावम्|
