## Action, relation and branch ownership

**3.4.39–45:** the same visible noun can occupy a different role in different
clauses. हस्त and स्व supply instrument meanings; बन्ध् can have a locus or
conventional-name condition; 43 specifies **जीव–नश् and पुरुष–वह्** in that
order. Those are two dependent clauses, not four freely paired choices. In
contrast, **3.4.61 expressly rejects यथासंख्य**: both कृ and भू take both
क्त्वा and णमुल्. The distinction is part of the construction.

**3.4.46** confines its repeated-root requirement to the कषादि group beginning
at 34. It does not make every later णमुल् expression repeat its own root:
मूलकोपदंशं भुङ्क्ते in 47 is an explicit different-root construction.
There मूलक is object relative to उपदंश् and instrument relative to भुज्.
**48** separately requires the two actions to share an object. A token's role
must therefore retain its action address.

**3.4.47 onward:** 2.2.21 supplies optional compounding in its admitted
scope. 2.2.22 makes the corresponding क्त्वा compound optional where that
specific prescription applies. In the compound route, ल्यप् is the resulting
replacement; the uncombined route retains क्त्वा. These are dependent
construction branches, not independent choices of spaces and suffixes.

**3.4.54–55:** अध्रुव is the source's historical body-member classification;
the clauses do not infer medical facts from a word. The special comprehensive
distress reading in 55 admits ध्रुव members such as शिरस् that are excluded
from 54's ordinary movement clause.

**3.4.56:** distinguish exhaustive relation to multiple entities from repeated
engagement in the action. In the compound route the source treats the selected
meaning as already expressed. In the uncombined route, the corresponding noun
or action expression repeats. Do not add both repetitions mechanically.
Kāśikā's स्कन्द् witness uses अवस्कन्द, whereas Kaumudī displays अनुस्कन्द;
the source examples remain literal, and the root-level clause is not reduced
to one normalized spelling.

**3.4.59–64:** the intended reporting manner, completion, actual तस् affix,
transformative ना/धा formation, silence and compliance are separate cuts.
The homonymous तस् root does not satisfy 61's प्रत्यय condition. 63 retains
भू specifically and removes the inherited कृ from its special branch.

**3.4.65–66:** तुमुन् is admitted with the listed governing expressions even
when 3.3.10's purpose relation is absent. Kaumudī confines अर्थ to अस्ति,
admitting existence synonyms without expanding every named root arbitrarily.
66 requires the sufficiency/capacity sense; abundance and prohibitory अलम्
are distinct controls.

## Roles denoted by क्त and lexical formations

**3.4.71:** Nyāsa interprets आदिकर्म as an initial **action moment**, not an
initial object. That moment may be complete while the overall artifact is not;
its discussion of प्रकृतः कटः explicitly treats the object expression through
the material's intended result. The actor/object case marking depends on which
role क्त expresses in the selected branch.

**3.4.72 and 76:** actor, object, event and locus meanings must stay separate.
The listed prefixed roots in 72 can be transitive. 76 adds locus meaning and
retains previously applicable readings; it does not grant actor क्त to every
consumption root. Kāśikā, Nyāsa and Kaumudī explain भुक्ता ब्राह्मणाः through
a possessive अच् analysis. Source witnesses are not authorization to combine
all valency and case assignments freely.

The Nyāsa field at 3.4.76 also contains discussion leading into the following
लकार section. That trailing material is archived literally; the operative
76 clause uses its role discussion, without moving the later text into a new
authored rule.

**3.4.73–75:** lexical recipient and source-role stipulations precede the
general उणादि role exclusion. गोघ्न is a source-defined designation for an
eligible honored guest even when no actual event of killing occurs. Its
conventional lexical use is not an inference about the conduct of a person.

## Vedic endings and designation views

**3.4.88** makes अपित् status of the prescribed हि optional. It does not make
हि itself optional. **94–98** retain the source of each augment or vowel:
95's आ→ऐ does not target आट्, and 96's अन्यत्र excludes the **whole 95 dual
environment**, not merely the already-replaced vowel. This prevents an extra
change of final ए in मन्त्रयैते/मन्त्रयैथे. 97 and 98 retain their separate
P-ending इ and first-person स् conditions.

**3.4.111–112:** Kāśikā indexes the जुस्/other-ending alternatives by
Śākaṭāyana versus other teachers; Kaumudī presents the resulting forms as
optional. Preserve the teacher-policy origin. Genuine लङ् is required;
लङ्वत् transfer to लोट् is insufficient, as यान्तु/वान्तु demonstrate.

**3.4.117** is now an authored designation clause, after being a control in
earlier packets. Its scope is the Vedic धातु-affix domain, not only the
nearest लिङ्. Kāśikā's **उपस्थेयाम** requires सार्वधातुक behavior for
सलोप and आर्धधातुक behavior for एत्व in the same retained derivation.
Nyāsa explains the cited operations; Kaumudī explicitly describes both
designations. Use source-qualified consumer views rather than one exclusive
global Boolean. No general permission to combine every incompatible effect
is inferred from the word उभयथा.

## Feminine formation and unresolved source policy

**4.1.7:** the वन् marker family must retain affix and root history.
Kaumudī explicitly includes ङ्वनिप्, क्वनिप् and वनिप्. Nyāsa says the
हश्-root exclusion is usage-qualified, and admits **अवावरी** as well as
शर्वरी; Kaumudī instead displays **अवावा** for the former case. Preserve
these policies separately. The field does not support a universally reliable
last-letter-only decision or a silent harmonization of those examples.

**4.1.8–9:** the target is the processed compound-final पाद् stem. ऋचि in
9 specifies **what is denoted**, not the textual register in which the word
appears. **10** uses the technical षट् designation; it is not restricted to
the number six. Its history remains available after न् deletion.

**4.1.11–13:** मन्-ending nominals and अन्-ending बहुव्रीहि have the stated
ङीप् restrictions and optional डाप्. The उपधा-deletion branch in control
4.1.28 and the coupled ङीप्/र् option for qualified वन् बहुव्रीहि are separate
conditions. One citation of अन्यतरस्याम् is not permission to choose every
feminine affix independently.

**4.1.14** begins the अनुपसर्जन authority. Kaumudī gives its endpoint as
4.1.77 inclusive. It does not retroactively alter all earlier feminine
prescriptions. The empty Nyāsa field supplies no additional support.

Complete realization, lexical membership, external meaning inference and
