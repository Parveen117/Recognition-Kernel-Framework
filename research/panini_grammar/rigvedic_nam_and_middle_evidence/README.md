# PG222 · Forty-seven Ṛgvedic words of nam across voice, tense and derivation

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Zürich/VedaWeb Ṛgveda has **47 words** under the root identifier *nam_4841*. Of these, **41 are finite**, four are participles and two are infinitives. The row register retains the source’s voice, mood, person, number, secondary verb and accented word.

Three active perfect third singulars have **nānāma**, with a long vowel in the copy, at 1.48.8a, 2.33.12b and 4.25.2a. The Classical PG218 table selects **nanāma**. Long *ā* survives accent removal, so the difference remains after projecting to ordinary spelling. The other seven primary-active words with a directly corresponding present, imperative or imperfect slot match their selected Classical words. Both perfect spellings also have an independent lexical record in Monier-Williams.
