## S3. The two-s 3sg schedule is a real source decision

On bhū∣yās∣s_suṭ∣t, the adopted Bālamanoramā/Tattvabodhinī
account uses the final-conjunct treatment of **both** s occurrences.
It expressly rejects a superficially plausible explanation that first
deletes yāsuṭ-s in the jhal context and only then suṭ-s at the end,
because of the stated 8.2.23/asiddha issue. This packet adopts that
local schedule and preserves the other discussion as source context.

In bhūyāstām/bhūyāstam/bhūyāsta, the remaining s is suṭ's.
The analogous surface with a lone retained yāsuṭ-s is not the same
owned path. E3 distinguishes the two. No global confluence or universal
Tripādī scheduling theorem is inferred from the finite examples.
