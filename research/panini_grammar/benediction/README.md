# PG20 — Āśīrliṅ and blessing loṭ: source-sensitive benediction

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

PG20 continues PG19 from intended āśīs to complete finite verbal
constructions. A blessing can select liṅ or loṭ, but that shared meaning
does not make their affix classes or stem procedures identical. The new
construction derives the P kit override, the conditional A kit rules,
retained/canceled s occurrences, ordinary versus ciṇvat iṭ, and optional
tātaṅ with its own stem consequences.

The bounded register has **1,057 morphological positions and 1,292
word-branch readings**. These are written factorizations, not executions,
distinct strings or newly discovered forms. In particular, the 488 ordinary
blessing-loṭ positions reuse PG19's morphology under the newly admitted
meaning, and T115/T117 already describe 26 of the āśīrliṅ positions.

## Boundary

The domain is classical, unprefixed, unaccented, single-causative and uses
the inherited non-doubling/devoiced-pause policy. It is a relation on
supplied intended meanings and role evidence, not automatic semantic
interpretation. The larger athematic, san/yaṅ, nested-causative, Vedic,
prohibitive and remaining liṅ domains are not inferred from these tables.

These are written source-relative proofs. Grammar tests, navigation/engine
suites and certificate replay remain deferred under the owner's instruction.
No new shared runtime, proof-assistant formalization or whole-grammar
completion percentage is asserted.
