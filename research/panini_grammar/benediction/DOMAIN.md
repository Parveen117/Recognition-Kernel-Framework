# PG20 — The āśīs frame and its grammatical branches

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## D1. Start on the native history carrier

Use N1's full residue-bearing carrier before taking its zero-reading.
An input retains exact lexical identity and sense, basic or single-ṇic
formation, interpreted event/roles, expression, active-pada evidence,
expressed controller/person/number, intended meaning, source policy and
unresolved obligations. A desired event can have specified participants
without being asserted to have occurred.

Kāśikā 3.3.173 defines āśīs as desiring the attainment of an intended,
not-yet-attained good. This is not a statement that the desired event is
physically impossible or guaranteed to occur. Its examples contrast a
wish for long life with an assertion of existing long life. The supplied
āśīs witness qualifies the intended root event.

On a completed āśīs frame set

\[
 L_{20}(F)=\{\mathrm{āśīrliṅ},\mathrm{blessing\ loṭ}\}.
\]

An unresolved meaning remains Open. No tense, probability or physical
clock is manufactured from the number of grammatical events.

## D2. The same meaning has two different affix regimes

| Choice | Finite-ending class | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| liṅ in āśīs | ārdhadhātuka, 3.4.116 | overrides 3.4.113; no present-system śap/śa/śyan/śnā/u or yak before this tiṅ |
| loṭ in āśīs | sārvadhātuka, 3.4.113 | retains PG19's stem/voice procedures; 7.1.35 adds an optional P tu/hi substitute |

Kāśikā 3.4.116 expressly denies simultaneous sārvadhātuka status in
the first row. P kit in 3.4.104 does not itself license yak, nor make
all A endings kit. Blessing loṭ does not acquire ārdhadhātuka status
merely because it expresses the same āśīs.

The controller is unchanged: basic kartari expresses its actor, causal
kartari its causer, Karma(q) its source-qualified karman, and Bhāva the
process with default **3sg**. A hearer is not automatically the expressed
participant. For Karma/Bhāva, 1.3.13 selects A independently of the root's
kartari pada. PG14/PG15's dependent causal karman selector is retained.

## D4. Causal and basic voice inputs

The causal sector is PG19's exact thirteen single-ṇic frames:
bhū, kṛ, nī, hṛ, pac, gam, sthā, dā, pā, vid, budh, kṛṣ and śam.
Its kartari domain has thirteen P and nine A profiles (twenty-two total),
with bhū/sthā/budh/śam P-only on the admitted contexts. Preserve the
benefit-conveying upapada and PG14's F/U/E conditions; an abstract label
“causative” does not determine pada by itself.

All thirteen causal Karma(q) frames have A. Basic Karma(q) is restricted
to kṛ, hṛ, pac and goal-taking gam; basic Bhāva to bhū and standing sthā.
These are PG19's selected role domains. C constructs their new āśīrliṅ
morphology, including gam's conditional kit choice and the eligible
ciṇvat branches. The ordinary kartari pada is not transplanted into them.

The **blessing-loṭ** domain is precisely PG19's full morphological input
register, now with āśīs: fifteen basic kartari profiles, twenty-two causal
kartari profiles, thirteen causal Karma profiles, four basic Karma profiles
and two Bhāva 3sg inputs. The seven newly added basic roots in D3 are
āśīrliṅ-only in PG20; their full blessing-loṭ paradigms are not silently
counted as inherited work.

## D5. Admitted choices, exclusions and obligations

The semantic choice of lakāra, an eligible tātaṅ substitute, gam's A
kit option, ciṇvat, the mit length condition and the dh/ḍh choice belong
to different addressed rules. They form the dependent relation in N4,
not an unconstrained Cartesian product of toggles.

Unaccented internal morphology, the selected devoiced pause reading and
the inherited non-doubling policy are fixed. Exclude Vedic leṭ and
special forms, prohibitive māṅ, unreviewed idioms, san/yaṅ, nested ṇic,
karmakartṛ, reciprocal uses, mixed-person coordination, 1.4.106 shifts,
external sentence sandhi and a general semantic parser. An input with
missing lexical/pada/controller evidence is Open, not an extra paradigm.

**Proposition D5.** On these completed frames the selected grammatical
relation is finite and nonempty, and the controller is independent of
the later morphological options.

**Proof.** D1 supplies two positive lakāra choices, restricted to the
explicit lexical lanes of D3–D4. The inherited role construction fixes
the expressed participant and available pada profiles. E/B/C/L give a
finite nonempty continuation for every listed profile. None of their
local choices changes the input event or expressed q. Missing premises
remain in their complementary Open channels. □
