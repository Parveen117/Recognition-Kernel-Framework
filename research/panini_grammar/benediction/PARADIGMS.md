# PG20 — Complete finite register and branch accounting

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## P1. What the counts mean

A **position** fixes root/sense, formation, expression, qualified pada,
selected mood and person/number, while factoring out repeated semantic
or role witnesses that do not change the morphological profile. A
**reading** additionally selects one of the admitted morphological/sound
branches. Neither count is a number of distinct printable strings.

B_P(R) and B_A(Z,δ) are the complete nine-component constructors in
E4/E5, in order (3sg,3du,3pl,2sg,2du,2pl,1sg,1du,1pl). A set-valued
δ changes only the 2pl component. It does **not** double all nine slots
or create two copies of the same first/third-person word.

**Theorem P7.** The register exhausts the declared D-domain relation,
including its dependent morphological and sound alternatives.

**Proof.** B5, C8 and L6 cover disjoint formation/expression/mood
profiles. Their positions sum to 1,057; their readings sum to 1,292.
Equivalently the āśīrliṅ relation has 81 nine-slot branch paradigms,
four Bhāva single readings and 29 extra 2pl readings: 729+4+29=762.
Blessing loṭ adds 488+42=530. These are unions over locally licensed
choices, not multiplication by an option at every slot. Every factor
has E/B/C/L's owned derivation; N3–N4 gives exact typed recovery of
the accepted, excluded and open channels. □
