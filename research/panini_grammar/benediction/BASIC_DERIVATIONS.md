# PG20 — Seventeen basic roots and the kit/ṅit distinction

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## B1. P starts at the root, not its present stem

D2's ārdhadhātuka ending does not license the present vikaraṇa.
In particular bhū does not first become bhava, gam does not first
become gaccha, and krī does not first acquire śnā. The yāsuṭ-initial
ending takes no ordinary iṭ by E1, including after the seṭ roots.
P's special kit condition normally blocks the ordinary guṇa/vṛddhi
query under 1.1.5; B3 states the source-specific jāgṛ exception.

The table's complete paradigms are B_P(R), not only its displayed 3sg
examples. Nī's and krī's long ī are not shortened by a non-existent
present-stem rule. The 7.4.28 riṅ prescription for kṛ/hṛ is expressly
short i: Kāśikā explains why the older rīṅ/length reading is excluded.
Its y-initial condition is false in the A sīyuṭ path below.

For dā/sthā/pā, 6.4.67 is the liṅ-specific e prescription in the
ghu/mā/sthā/gā/pā/jahāti/sā domain, overriding the relevant 6.4.66
ī route. There is no y-ādi guṇa operation on de∣yās: y is a consonant.
Kāśikā explicitly contrasts this kit domain with A dāsīṣṭa/dhāsīṣṭa.

## B2. Full A interfaces from their own sources

The seven A inputs from PG19 are aniṭ pac, labh, tud, nī, kṛ and
krī together with seṭ edh; the additional A inputs are aniṭ hṛ/dā.
Thus there are **eight aniṭ and one seṭ** A profiles. The lexical
records and 7.2.10/35, not the P no-iṭ argument, decide this partition.

| Root | Ordinary iṭ | A kit source | Owned Z factor | δ at 2pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pac | none | none | pakṣ | dh |
| edh | i_val | none; i is not jhal | edhiṣ | dh |
| labh | none | none | laps | dh |
| tud | none | 1.2.11 | tuts | dh |
| nī | none | none | neṣ | ḍh |
| kṛ | none | 1.2.12 | kṛṣ | ḍh |
| krī | none | none | kreṣ | ḍh |
| hṛ | none | 1.2.12 | hṛṣ | ḍh |
| dā | none | none | dās | dh |

For nī/krī, vowel-final ī is outside 1.2.11's consonant-final
condition and 1.2.12's ṛ condition. Ordinary 7.3.84 therefore gives
ne/kre before sīyuṭ; 8.3.59 supplies neṣ/kreṣ. For kṛ/hṛ, 1.2.12
does apply before the jhal-initial aniṭ ending: no guṇa, no P riṅ,
and kṛṣ/hṛṣ result. Tud's ik-adjacent final consonant satisfies
1.2.11, preserving u rather than manufacturing *tots-*.

Pac's c takes 8.2.30 k before s; 8.3.59 then gives pakṣ. Labh's
bh and tud's d take the appropriate car sounds before s under 8.4.55,
giving laps and tuts. These are late output factors, never early inputs
to the class/kit query. Edh has the independently owned i_val, protected
by 1.1.6; its root-final dh is one stop, not an iṇ-final h.

All nine complete A paradigms are B_A(Z,δ). No optional second-plural
branch arises on these inputs: the four direct iṇ-final aniṭ roots
have mandatory ḍh; the other five have dental dh. In particular k's
role in **pakṣ** does not make it an iṇ sound for 8.3.78.

## B3. Two source witnesses against merging kit and ṅit

**Vac.** In P āśīrliṅ, 3.4.104 supplies kit, so 6.1.15 converts
the v of vac to its samprasāraṇa u. The resulting u/a sequence has
6.1.108's pūrvarūpa: uc. Before yāsuṭ the c is not before jhal,
so this is **ucyāt**, not a pakṣ-like junction. Kāśikā 3.4.104
directly contrasts ordinary vidhiliṅ **vacyāt**, where the prescribed
ending is ṅit and that kit-only samprasāraṇa is unavailable.

**Jāgṛ.** Generic 1.1.5 would block ordinary guṇa before either kit
or ṅit. But 7.3.85 gives jāgṛ its special guṇa outside its listed
vi/ciṇ/ṇal/ṅit exclusions. Kit is not one of those exclusions.
The 3.4.104 override removes the formerly applicable ṅit prescription,
so **jāgaryāt** is derived. Leaving both flags active would wrongly
retain the ṅit exclusion and lose that source-attested result.
Kāśikā/Tattvabodhinī contrast **jāgṛyāt** in ordinary vidhiliṅ.

These contrasts are literal source witnesses. This packet constructs
the complete āśīrliṅ paradigms, while the mentioned vidhiliṅ words
are controlled comparisons, not a claim of two newly completed extra
vidhiliṅ paradigms. N5 gives the precise obstruction for an observer
that retains only the Boolean kit-or-ṅit.

from the affix class and local markers, not an interchangeable spelling.

basic kartari āśīrliṅ paradigms, giving **216 positions/readings** on
the selected sound profile.

root operations. B2 enumerates all nine A factors and the complete local
