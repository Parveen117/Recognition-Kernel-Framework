# PG239 · Five ordered-ni aorists, perfects and two epic witnesses

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Pracetas **praṇipetuḥ**, ‘bowed down’, in *Viṣṇupurāṇa* 1.14.46. The same active perfect plural stands before *ca* in the critical *Mahābhārata* 3.153.30e and appears there in sandhi as **praṇipetuś ca**. Both passages instantiate one person–number position of *pra + ni + pat*.

| Aorist 3sg | With *pra + ni* | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *gad*, *nad* | praṇyagadīt / praṇyagādīt; praṇyanadīt / praṇyanādīt | *Sic* permits both radical grades here. |
| *pat* | praṇyapaptat | The lṛ-marked root takes *aṅ*, then the *p* augment of 7.4.19. |
| *pad* | praṇyapādi | The agentive middle takes *ciṇ* in this specific third singular. Compare dual *praṇyapatsātām*. |
| *śam* | praṇyaśamat | *Aṅ* supplies the non-present formation, giving short radical *a*. |

These perfects and aorists add **162 new positions and 196 readings**. The ten profiles now have **900/900** positions. Ninety of those had already been counted for *pra + ni + gad* at PG71–73. PG237–239 together add the other **810**. The Kāśikā’s examples, the two literary plurals and the remaining written constructions are identified separately.
