# PG691 · Three 24-position written case fibres

Use **bharaṭ** for the selected potter, **bharaṭik**
for the masculine conveyance stem, and **bharaṭik**
for its separate feminine ī-stem. The table has
surface tails for singular, dual, and plural. The
factored bodies omit the final affix vowel before
the case templates handle the ordinary coalescence,
yaṇ and ending operations. It does not follow that
the vowel has been deleted from its source history.
The written ending models are pinned at PG251
and PG375. At the masculine ablative singular the
two permitted ending displays belong to one key.
