# PG691 · Bharaṭa short aṭac and the owned ṣṭhan derivative

Monty · 24 November 2026

Śabdakalpadruma gives the potter *bharaṭa* under Uṇādi
4.104 and names short **aṭac**. Vācaspatyam supplies
*aṭan* for its own analysis. The long **āṭac** field's
additional-base reading at 4.105 remains Open.

Kāśikā 4.4.16 gives *bharaṭikaḥ* and *bharaṭikī*
for the qualified instrumental conveyance relation.
The actual gaṇa is bhastrādi. Its ṣṭhan yields ika,
and its original ṣ licenses the feminine ṅīṣ.
The written case fibres add **72 nominal positions
and 74 readings** across the base and the two
derivatives. The other lexical feminine display
*bharāṭakī* retains its own source address and Open
derivation.

Census **52,323 finite positions and 8,084 nominal
positions/9,345 readings**. Uṇādi individual-field
coverage remains 99/103.
