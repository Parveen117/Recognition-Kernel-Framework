## S2. Scope and source controls

3.1.60–65 explain the inherited ta gate and neighbouring kartari/
karmakartṛ domains. They are archived as controls, not all counted as
newly implemented rules. The selected lexical domain excludes tap's
special anutāpa prohibition. Likewise the mandatory prefixed-labh num
policy under 7.1.69 is retained as a control; this register is unprefixed.

The bhū vuk passages at 6.4.88 have different general priority accounts:
Kaumudī/Bālamanoramā stress nityatva, while Nyāsa invokes an inherited
u-final condition in its discussion. Both are retained. This packet
only uses the narrower distinction between an actual ciṇ affix and a
vowel-initial luṅ tiṅ substitute. Kaumudī's abhāvi is a positive
word witness; PG32's abhūvan/abhūvam belong to their different sic-luk
ending path. No general reconciliation of those priority accounts is claimed.

Kāśikā/Nyāsa 6.4.104 discuss degree forms to show why target-instance
and visibility matter even when the rule number is the same. Those are
source-supplied controls; no new degree paradigm or finite position is counted.
The digital Nyāsa field repeats an akāritamām token in its final example
pair. Preserve that repetition rather than treating it as a new branch.

Bālamanoramā/Tattvabodhinī 3.1.66 print differing damaged non-ta
control strings (abhāviṣāt / abhāviṣām in the digital fields). They
are not adopted as new paradigm cells. The ta restriction is established
by the sūtra's inherited condition and the explicit primary explanations.

Output is unaccented; lexical upadeśa accent information is retained for
7.3.34. Omitting accent marks in the displayed words does not justify
discarding that source property from the implementation state.
