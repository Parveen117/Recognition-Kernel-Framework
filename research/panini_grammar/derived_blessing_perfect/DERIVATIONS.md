## H01. The new requests preserve the event and its controller

The ciri/jiri histories retain both lexical vowels and the reported classical non-Mantra permission. There is no prefix. Basic means the admitted hiṃsā event. Causal adds one ṇic and retains causer, causee and base patient. San adds one desiderative formation and requires the same actor for desire and desired action. Neither construction contains the other.

For the new blessings choose the liṅ member of 3.3.173: a wish for an unattained causal or desire event. Causal P/A follows PG64–66's principal-result frame: result elsewhere versus result to the causer, with the named exceptions excluded. San retains kartari P. The actor of desire controls san agreement; wishing that desire should arise is not identical to wishing the injury accomplished.

For liṭ choose the inherited non-Mantra, non-today parokṣa request under 3.2.115. First-person slots require the inherited suitable indirect-knowledge account. With san, the past event is the desire's occurrence; the desired action need not have been completed. Karmaṇi expresses the affected base patient, including in the causal and transitive desiderative frames. It supplies all nine patient-agreement slots, not an actor-based substitution of pronouns into an unrelated event.

The original request and its primary liṭ have formation, role, context and agreement identities. A later auxiliary retains them but has its own ending and pada. There is no Bhāva-only or karmakartari grid in this packet.
