# PG67 — Complete derived blessings and periphrastic perfect words

Monty · 7 November 2026

Every cell retains the ciri/jiri source permission, ordinary unaccented non-Mantra register, hiṃsā sense and no prefix. The [derivations](DERIVATIONS.md) supply its controller, context and recipient histories.

P1–P2 have three distinct blessing requests per agreement slot. In each perfect panel P3–P14, all three auxiliary columns belong to **one request position** per slot. Column labels give the auxiliary's pada; the panel gives the request's role/pada.

Within kṛ/bhū, anusvāra precedes the homorganic nasal alternative. P kṛ 1sg groups the short-root and long-root options. Passive bhū 2pl groups dental dh and retroflex ḍh, with both nasal readouts in each. Passive as 3sg/1sg prints āse before āhe; the three correlated H/I/S source policies remain explicit in the register, including their coincident words.
