# PG50 — The enabling टि deletion in औडुलोम्या

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Continue PG49's promised **उडुलोमन् → औडुलोम्या** construction. The
primary replacement path follows the explicit आनुपूर्व्य argument in
Nyāsa and Padamañjarī 4.1.78: the इञ्-conditioned टि deletion first
creates the heavy penultimate required to admit ष्यङ्. A separately
declared added-affix path retains that same prerequisite; it does not
install ष्यङ् on an ineligible earlier host.

Both outputs are unaccented feminine stems. Full accent and declined
paradigms, general recognition of the lexical name and descent relation,
and an unrestricted scheduler remain outside this construction.

## Supplied input and exact target

Supply **उडुलोमन्** as the name used by the commentaries, with its final
लोमन् member addressed. Supply the relevant अपत्य relation, the formal
गोत्र restriction of 4.1.162, the non-ऋषि condition and feminine reference
required by 4.1.78. These are admitted inputs, not meanings recovered from
the visible letters. The compound/name formation itself is not reconstructed.

Kāśikā 4.1.96 lists **लोमन्** in the बाह्वादि group. Nyāsa and
Padamañjarī 4.1.78 explain why the descent relation here concerns the
whole name ending in that member, rather than isolated “hair”. That
explanation licenses this example; it is not a universal suffix-admission
rule for every string ending in लोमन्.

The adopted spelling uses **ड**, supported by Kāśikā 6.4.144's
औडुलोमिः and the Nyāsa/Padamañjarī discussion. Kāśikā 4.1.96's final
example is literally औङुलोमिः in this edition. The archive preserves
that reading; no ड/ङ sound-change rule is invented to reconcile it.

Keep the following owned records distinct:

|Owner|Object and retained evidence|
|---|---|
|B|The admitted base उडुलोमन् and its addressed लोमन् member|
|I|इञ् under 4.1.96, its ञित् designation and descent meaning|
|T|The base's terminal **अन्** designated टि under 1.1.64|
|Y|A replacement version of I in R01, but a separate added suffix in A01|
|F|The feminine चाप् suffix and its own marker history|

T belongs to the भ host, not to the whole base-plus-I string. It is
**अन्**, beginning with the last vowel of B, not merely न् and not the
following इ of I. Consequently 6.4.144 and the later 6.4.148 operation
in A01 have different targets, causes and events.

## R01 — Replacement after its enabling टि deletion

Take S1–S7, then retain the replacement interpretation of 4.1.78. The
seven shared stages and six stages below form one **thirteen-stage** history.

|Stage|Operation or decision|Addressed result|
|---|---|---|
|R1|Replace **I itself** by ष्यङ् under the fresh S7 admission|The same suffix owner receives a new version; the base and the whole derivative are not replaced|
|R2|Project ष्/ङ् under 1.3.6/3/9|औडुलोम् + य = औडुलोम्य; preserve the earlier ञित्-caused grade and T-lopa history|
|R3|Retain the commentary's completed order|The later य does not retroactively cancel S6 or restore its deleted अन्. No second preservation mutation is inserted on that former target|
|R4|Introduce चाप् under 4.1.74 and the 4.1.3 feminine domain; project च्/प् by 1.3.7/3/9|औडुलोम्य + आ; F remains a separate owner|
|R5|Coalesce the final अ of य and F's आ under 6.1.84/85/101|One final आ, with the two contributing vowel addresses retained|
|R6|Read the current unaccented stem|**औडुलोम्या**|

R3 records the bounded source resolution by **आनुपूर्व्य**. It does not
claim that every old deletion is invisible to every later consumer, or that
no grammatical operation can ever use an authorized historical view. The
commentaries' claim here is that ष्यङ् is unavailable until the deletion
has already supplied its necessary heavy penultimate. A general theory of
later 6.4.168 queries or असिद्धवत् scheduling requires its own construction.

The ष्यङ् name and markers are kept in their standard presentation, so
4.1.74 supplies चाप्. The commentary's broader redesign of marker economy
and its accent discussion are not silently counted as completed here.

## A01 — Added suffix after the same enabling deletion

Choose the source's alternative प्रत्ययपक्ष while retaining the explicit
4.1.78 admission conditions. S1–S7 must still occur first. They and the
seven stages below form one **fourteen-stage** history.

|Stage|Operation or retained evidence|Addressed result|
|---|---|---|
|A1|Introduce a **new Y = ष्यङ्** after the admitted इञ्-ending formation|औडुलोमि + ष्यङ्; I and Y are distinct owners|
|A2|Project Y's standard markers|औडुलोमि + य; I's इ is still active|
|A3|Apply 6.4.148 to the terminal इ of the preceding भ formation before Y, a तद्धित under 4.1.76|औडुलोम् + य; record this I-vowel deletion separately from S6's base-अन् deletion|
|A4|Retain the consumer-qualified intervention account stated in Nyāsa/Padamañjarī 4.1.78|The source's 1.1.57 view can retain I's intervention for the stated preceding-operation comparison; it does not restore a pronounced इ or an active base-अन्|
|A5|Add चाप् under the retained standard ष्यङ् designation and project its markers|औडुलोम्य + आ|
|A6|Apply the same 6.1.84/85/101 coalescence|One current आ with both contributors retained|
|A7|Read the current unaccented stem|**औडुलोम्या**, with a distinct addition/deletion history|

In the commentary's **local intervention argument**, the added Y is
separated from the earlier base by I, and the stated स्थानिवद्भाव of
I's deletion preserves that separation for the relevant 6.4.168 comparison.
That is different from replacing I by Y in R01. The view is source- and
consumer-qualified; an arbitrary old vowel cannot be used as an intervener.

In the complete path displayed here, however, S6 has already happened
before Y is admitted. A4 therefore does **not** claim to cause that earlier
deletion a second time, or to issue a new active preservation decision on a
nonexistent अन् target. The retained local argument and the actual chosen
event order are recorded separately. This prevents the alternative account
from becoming a spurious license to attach ष्यङ् prematurely.

## Diagnostic continuation — forcing ष्यङ् too early

The following is a rejected proposal, not an alternative accepted output.
It deliberately overrides S3's failed admission to expose the commentator's
objection.

|Stage|Forced proposal|Why the branch is rejected|
|---|---|---|
|X1|Replace I by ष्यङ् before S6|The required heavy penultimate has not been obtained; this is the first failed gate|
|X2|Treat the resulting य as the immediate तद्धित after intact औडुलोमन्|The proposed record now has the environment used in the commentary's 6.4.168 objection|
|X3|Preserve अन् by 6.4.168 in the supplied non-भाव/कर्म frame|This prevents the otherwise requested 6.4.144 deletion, leaving the extra material|
|X4|Continue the standard feminine आ realization solely to diagnose the proposal|**औडुलोमन्या**, the unwanted form encoded in Padamañjarī's औडुलोमन्येति comparison|

The resolution is not to force 6.4.144 through a legitimately admitted
preservation rule. It is to reject X1 and follow the actual order: obtain
टि-lopa under इञ्, then re-evaluate the prerequisite, then admit ष्यङ्.
No new न→ण operation is needed in this example. The diagnostic continuation
is not a third registered successful history, a full declined form, or an
executed counterexample.

The two histories share a final spelling but retain different suffix owners
and deletion witnesses. Neither supplies universal lexical recognition,
source-policy selection, rule confluence, accent/declension completion or
runtime-verification credit.
