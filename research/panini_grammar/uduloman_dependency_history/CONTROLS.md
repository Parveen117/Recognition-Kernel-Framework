# PG50 — Controls for enabling deletion and later suffix views

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These six units decide bounded queries on supplied records. They do not add
full agreement positions. Source citation forms are distinguished from the
unaccented stem under construction, and a diagnostic bad proposal is not
counted as an accepted Sanskrit output.

## C01 — टि names the complete final अन्

**Target:** the deletion span of 6.4.144 on the addressed न्-final भ host.

By 1.1.64, टि begins at the last vowel of that host and includes the
following sounds. For **औडुलोमन्**, that span is **अन्**: the अ belonging
to the final म syllable together with न्. The consonant म् remains.
Therefore the admitted 6.4.144 event gives **औडुलोम् + इ**, whose stem
readout is **औडुलोमि**.

|Proposed target|Outcome for this 6.4.144 query|
|---|---|
|The host's final अन्|APPLIED on the admitted भ/taddhita domain|
|Only its न्|BLOCKED as the wrong span for this operation|
|The following suffix's इ or the whole इञ्|BLOCKED as the wrong owner|

A later proposal that happens to erase a remaining vowel is a different
event and cannot validate a misrecorded न्-only application of 6.4.144.
Nor is this operation a lengthening of a vowel. Kāśikā 6.4.144 explicitly
names औडुलोमिः as its citation witness; full inflection of that form is
outside this control.

## C02 — The relevant heavy syllable must actually be penultimate

**Target:** गुरूपोत्तम admission under 4.1.78.

In the following graded displays, initial उ→औ has already occurred. That
initial grade is not the change that enables feminine ष्यङ् admission.

|Current preparation|Vowel sequence|Penultimate syllable|4.1.78 weight gate|
|---|---|---|---|
|औडुलोमन् + इ, before टि-lopa|औ उ ओ अ इ|म, with short अ and no following consonant cluster|FALSE|
|औडुलोम् + इ, after टि-lopa|औ उ ओ इ|लो, with long ओ|TRUE|

Rules 1.4.10/11/12 distinguish short/lighter, cluster-conditioned heavy and
long/heavy syllables. Here deletion removes an intervening syllabic position;
it does not lengthen the old penultimate अ into ओ. The already present ओ
now occupies the required position. The initially heavy औ is irrelevant to
the particular penultimate predicate.

Nyāsa and Padamañjarī 4.1.78 expressly require टि-lopa before ष्यङ् can
be admitted in this word. A forced early ष्यङ् proposal is therefore
BLOCKED at admission, even if a later hypothetical continuation can be
printed. The other lexical, गोत्र and feminine premises remain necessary.

is obtained. That continuation does not repair its earlier false 4.1.78
weight gate and is not a second accepted feminine result.

## C04 — Added ष्यङ् has a separate intervening इ witness

**Target:** the adjacency view for a source-qualified later preservation
query, not the cause of the already completed initial टि-lopa.

The added-affix account keeps the original इञ् owner and adds a distinct
ष्यङ् owner. Its later 6.4.148 event removes the intervening इ from current
pronunciation. Nyāsa 4.1.78 expressly invokes 1.1.57 to retain that
intervention for the relevant preceding operation; Padamañjarī agrees in
this comparison. A preservation proposal that treats the added य as directly
adjacent in that authorized view is BLOCKED.

Both legal word paths still acquire the heavy penultimate through the earlier
6.4.144 deletion before admitting ष्यङ्. The later deleted-इ witness is
not a substitute cause for that earlier deletion and does not license adding
ष्यङ् on the initially light-penultimate host.
Nor is it a new preservation event on the active stem: its original अन्
target is already absent at this later stage.

In the replacement account, इञ् itself is replaced. It has no separately
added ष्यङ् with an intervening इ deleted by 6.4.148. Borrowing that other
account's witness is BLOCKED as a wrong-event justification. Unknown policy
or event identity gives OPEN rather than a guessed intervention.

## C05 — Retained history is not an unrestricted restoration instruction

**Target:** a proposed historical view or retroactive rewriting after the
word has passed through its source-attested ordered preparation.

The replacement account is justified locally by Nyāsa/Padamañjarī's
**आनुपूर्व्य** argument: टि-lopa, acquired गुरूपोत्तमत्व, then ष्यङ्.
The final sound string must not silently regain the removed अन् merely
because an earlier record still contains it. That record remains evidence
of the actual deletion, its target and its conditioning suffix.

This does not establish a universal rule that later consumers can never
inspect earlier states. Kāśikā 6.4.22 requires the specified domain and
conditioning relation for an असिद्धवत् view; equal word identity or
suffix ownership alone does not establish that relation. A general request
to treat a prior deletion as invisible needs its own source-qualified
consumer, cause and scope, rather than a global rollback of the current word.

The present example follows the explicit commentary ordering. The separate
1.1.57 intervention argument in C04 is retained on its stated domain.
A broader claim about every possible late-168/असिद्धवत् schedule remains
OPEN; it is not promoted to a general non-reapplication theorem.

## C06 — लोमन् membership does not infer every compound's admission

**Target:** the lexical and semantic premises of 4.1.96 and 4.1.78.

Kāśikā 4.1.96 lists **लोमन्** and describes the qualified बाह्वादि
prescription. Nyāsa/Padamañjarī 4.1.78 explain why the present descent
example concerns the compound ending in that member: the bare लोमन्
does not supply the intended progenitor relation. The admitted record here
is the source's **उडुलोमन्** example with its stated relation, not an
unrestricted suffix-matching rule for every word ending in लोमन्.

Even after that lexical admission, 4.1.78 separately requires its non-ऋषि,
गोत्र, feminine and weight premises. The गोत्र term retains 4.1.162's
defined scope. A missing required relation or lexical witness yields OPEN;
a resolved contrary condition blocks that particular prescription. Spelling
alone cannot establish the absent semantics.

The chosen ड-family spelling follows the explicit source comparison in
[Source notes](SOURCE_NOTES.md). No ङ→ड sound operation is invented to
normalize the variant in Kāśikā 4.1.96.
