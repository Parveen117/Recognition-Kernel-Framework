syllable is penultimate. It does not lengthen म or newly lengthen ओ.

## D2. Fresh admission after the enabling event

Let h₀ be the supplied pre-टि-lopa candidate record, h₁ its result after
the admitted deletion L, and h₂ the replacement-policy result of Y = ष्यङ्
admission. Fix the other required conditions as affirmative on this domain.
Then the source-supported heavy-penultimate predicate has

\[
G(h_0)=\mathrm{FALSE},\qquad G(h_1)=\mathrm{TRUE}.
\]

Consequently the successful mutation path Y∘L is defined at h₀, while
L∘Y is not a legal successful mutation path there: Y is not yet admitted.
This is an order witness on the specified finite domain, not a general
priority rule inferred from sūtra numbers. In particular it does not say
that 6.4.144 must precede 4.1.78 in every possible input.

An admission record contains the current revision, derivative owner, source
policy and the actual syllable witness. The earlier FALSE result cannot be
reused at h₁; a later TRUE result cannot be borrowed to justify Y at h₀.
The added-affix policy has its own admitted recipient and distinct suffix
owner. It may share the prior enabling deletion without identifying its
later इ-lopa with the replacement-policy event.

The retained deleted sounds are history. They do not automatically recreate
an active अन् for a later preservation rule. A proposed historical query
requires its own source clause and cause/view justification. The present
construction follows the explicit आनुपूर्व्य account in the 4.1.78
commentaries; it does not prove an unrestricted non-reapplication axiom or
collapse 6.4.22, 1.1.56 and 1.1.57 into one memory mechanism.

## N1. Full-carrier extension and successful/blocked channels

Under the unchanged PG49 assumptions, use

\[
B_{50}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le50}\rangle,
\qquad
M_{50,\lambda}=(M_{49,\lambda}\otimes_{B_{49}}B_{50})
\oplus\bigoplus_{h\in H_{50}}e_hB_{50}.
\]

Sending the new letters to zero retracts the algebra inclusion. Extend the
old pairing by scalars and take the finite-support new summand orthogonal
to it with pairing \(\sum_hx_h^*y_h\). Retain the inherited nondegeneracy
assumptions, tensor typing and dagger. History coordinates now include the
two owner-relative queries, before/after syllable witnesses, deleted segment
versions and the policy of the subsequent suffix operation.

The coefficient-independent coordinate cuts \(P^T,P^F,P^?\) preserve the
three admission outcomes. A successful event acts on its admitted source
coordinate by \(U_e(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a\). Define successful-mutation
operators by restricting to affirmative admission and extending by zero off
their declared source coordinates. BLOCKED and OPEN decisions have separate
retained history channels; the zero in a successful channel is not deletion
of the rejected candidate's record.

For the particular replacement records of D2, let L and Y denote these
successful-mutation operators. Then

\[
YL(e_{h_0})=e_{h_2}(1+z_Y)(1+z_L),\qquad
LY(e_{h_0})=0.
\]

The first expression is nonzero because its empty-word coefficient is one
over the inherited nonzero cut field. The second follows from failed Y
admission at h₀. The blocked-decision history remains available in its
complementary channel. This establishes only the stated order distinction;
it is not a universal grammar commutator theorem or a physical quantum gate.

## N2. Typed zero-reading and pairing

Let ε read the empty-word coefficient, and let π₀ apply ε coefficientwise
while retaining all histories, source policies and decision/view coordinates.
On the fixed finite domain of legal paths and decision records:

\[
\pi_{0,\mathrm{out}}S_{50,\lambda}
=S_{50,0}\pi_{0,\mathrm{in}},\qquad
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_{B_{50}}
=\langle\pi_0x,\pi_0y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

**Proof.** Nonnegative word degree makes ε a unital dagger homomorphism.
The supplied admission cuts are coefficient-independent. Each admitted event
factor has ε-value one, so cuts, ordered finite compositions and declared
finite sums commute with the correctly typed coefficient-reading maps.
Dagger compatibility gives the pairing identity. In particular the D2 order
witness specializes to eₕ₂ versus zero on the successful channel, while
the separate blocked-decision coordinate is retained. □

A unit-start path recovers its precise declared history. Signed combinations
can cancel, so universal support preservation is not asserted. A future
coefficient-dependent admission procedure needs its own compatibility proof.
Evolution is ordered composition; revision number is not physical time.
