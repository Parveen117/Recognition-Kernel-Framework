## Wider grammatical obligations

- Extend semantic pada licensing, upasarga contexts and voice conditions
  beyond the explicit profiles and caller-supplied licenses.
- Develop the other lakāras and tense/mood systems, including perfect,
  aorist, future and conditional derivations. Vid/brū's present ending
  substitutes do not implement the perfect system.
- Extend reduplication to other root shapes, san/yaṅ/causative and other
  derivational formations, with their separate marker and strength rules.
- Expand nominal, compound, kṛt/taddhita, accent and Vedic domains under
  their own evidence and coverage registers.
- Develop broader rule-relative visibility and conflict resolution.
  PG11's local overrides and two lih permissions are finite reviewed
  relations, not a general grammar scheduler or asiddha theorem.
- Establish soundness and completeness obligations beyond finite examples
  and agreement with another implementation.

## Native framework boundary

The grammar continues to use a bounded symbolic adapter. Full lifting to
the native operator family, typed zero-specialization compatibility and
quantum-calculator integration remain **OPEN**. This stage neither changes
the canonical operator engine nor proves an unrestricted Sanskrit oracle.

PG1–PG10 source pins, code and reports remain frozen. Continue with
changed-claim validation and required navigation checks; do not replay
untouched certificates merely to enlarge a test count. Hosted CI and main
merge remain deferred under the existing review-branch workflow.
