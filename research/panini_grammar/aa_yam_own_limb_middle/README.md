# PG289 · *Ā + yam* with one's own hand has its own middle route

Monty · 13 November 2026

Kāśikā 1.3.28 gives middle endings to intransitive *ā + yam* and adds a separate vārttika for one's own limb: **āyacchate pāṇim**. It explicitly says that *svāṅga* here means one's own limb, not the technical saṃjñā. The transitive rope example **āyacchati kūpād rajjum** remains active.

The profile **AA_YAM_OWN_HAND_A** therefore keeps an explicit agent and that agent's own hand as object. Its middle entitlement comes from the own-limb route, not from PG107's cloth/actor-result route and not merely from the prefix *ā*. Equal words across those profiles do not merge their conditions.
