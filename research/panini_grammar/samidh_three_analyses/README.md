# PG143 — Three Sanskrit analyses of the same comparison standard

Monty · 11 November 2026

Nyāsa under 6.4.50 explicitly distinguishes three readings of *samidh*. The previously written samidh tables cover the first and third senses. The second gives their existing P forms a further interpretation, with the actor and patient bound to the same participant. This adds a genuine semantic preimage across 90 existing positions; the published finite-position and spelling totals receive zero additional credit.

| Intended relation | Formation | Present 3sg |
| --- | --- | --- |
| x wants kindling for x | kyac, 3.1.8 | samidhyati |
| x treats x like kindling | kyac, 3.1.10 | samidhyati |
| x behaves like kindling | kyaṅ, 3.1.11 | samidhyate |

The distinction is sharpest when an outer construction loses even the P/A distinction in its spelling. In the luṭ 3sg pair, either **samidhitā** or **samidhyitā** has all three analyses. In the perfect an A form of kṛ distinguishes the third analysis, while the shared as auxiliary can preserve all three.
