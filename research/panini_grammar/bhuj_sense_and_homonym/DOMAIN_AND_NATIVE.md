## N2. Two failures of a spelling-only observer

First, lexical spelling plus `not protection` is insufficient for
admission. Both the eating and curvature requests satisfy that loose
description, but the former has rudhādi ID and A while the latter has
tudādi ID and P. No function of those two erased fields alone can
produce both required decisions. The lexical witness must precede the cut.

Second, word observation is non-injective even after admission. For
each A label/slot the three declared senses have identical words but
distinct semantic histories. Moreover at each luṭ third-person slot
all five requests share the same word, retaining two lexical IDs and
two original padas. Word equality therefore cannot reconstruct sense,
lexical identity or ending provenance on this bounded domain.

There are 450 keys, one selected word per key. Two P perfect 1sg
keys each retain an extra coincident ṇit-work history, yielding
452 paths. These fibre statements are finite construction results,
not universal lower bounds for arbitrary language or memory systems.
