# PG92 — Five bhuj requests through all ten labels

Monty · 9 November 2026

These are manually written rule inferences, not individual textual
attestations. The three A profiles have distinct declared dominant senses;
their identical words do not create three lexical roots. The two P profiles
also retain different lexical identities. Use ordinary non-blessing loṭ,
unaccented IAST, no optional doubling and selected devoicing at pause.
All profiles are unprefixed. The past aṭ is therefore directly before bhuj.
