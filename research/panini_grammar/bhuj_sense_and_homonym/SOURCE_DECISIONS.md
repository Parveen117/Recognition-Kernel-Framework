## H02. Non-protection is wider than eating

Kāśikā 1.3.66 gives rudhādi bhuṅkte/bhuñjāte/bhuñjate and contrasts
protective bhunakti. Kaumudī and Bālamanoramā explicitly extend the
non-protection sense to upabhoga/experience; Tattvabodhinī additionally
names ātmasātkaraṇa, taking into one's possession. Their examples
distinguish eating food, undergoing suffering, enjoying the earth and
making the earth one's own. Nothing here licenses swallowing the earth
as the explanation of those latter usages.

Count three disjoint *declared dominant-sense requests*: eating;
non-eating experience/enjoyment excluding protection and appropriation;
and appropriation excluding protection. All are unprefixed, transitive,
agent-controlled kartari with their declared objects and AGENT principal
result. Their A selection is direct 1.3.66; 1.3.72 is not its cause.
The fourth rudhādi request is protection, with OTHER principal result,
selected P. These semantic certificates are supplied inputs, not a
completed natural-language sense classifier.

## H03. The negative guard also selects the correct homonym

Kāśikā, Nyāsa, Padamañjarī and Bhāṣya explain why the protection
exclusion identifies the root which can express protection in the
first place. It does not select tudādi bhuj merely because bending
is not protection. Tattvabodhinī explicitly retains P for that root;
Bālamanoramā gives unprefixed bhujati vāsaḥ, becoming crooked.

The fifth request takes that unprefixed intransitive curvature sense,
without karman, and P under 1.3.78. Its active controller is the
grammatical kartṛ; this makes no claim that cloth has intention.
Transitive vi+bhuj examples are read as homonym evidence but are not
additional table credit. Missing lexical identity is HOLD, not an
automatic A choice. The ordinary 1.3.66 admission predicate is
`lexical_id=1070017 AND sense is admitted non-protection`.

exhaustive scope declaration.
