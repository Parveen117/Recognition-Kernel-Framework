# Source decisions

## H1. Three selected lexical acts

3.1.19 assigns kyac to namas in worship, varivas in attendance/service and citraṅ in wonder. The first two use the ordinary active P entitlement; the source expressly marks the third for A. These three forms do not require the self-directed desire relation of 3.1.8.

## H2. Case of the worshipped participant

Kāśikā gives namasyati devān and varivasyati gurūn. 2.3.16 assigns dative with a qualifying independent namas expression. The Bhāṣya at 3.1.19 raises the apparent conflict and distinguishes the word namasyati from independently functioning namas. Preserve the free object and the historical nominal component in different positions of the input tree.

## H3. Citraṅ retains its lexical marker

Kyac gives ī at the nominal boundary under 7.4.33. Tattvabodhinī at 3.1.19 separates the reason for kyac (ī) from the ṅ indication for the A endings. Replacing the suffix with kyaṅ merely because the word ends in -te would give the wrong internal history.

## H4. Two interpretations of the wonder predicate

Kaumudī gives the reading of experiencing wonder and reports another reading of causing wonder. Keep two explicitly attributed semantic paths behind the single citrīyate spelling. Its form does not insert a separate ṇic on the reported causative-semantic reading.

## H5. Namas and varivas end in original s

The source prescribes no 3.1.11 s-lopa on these kyac formations. In the four śap-series labels the s-y factor remains. Before ārdhadhātuka, 6.4.50 permits dropping only the ky-initial y following that original s. The protected former a prevents spurious preceding grade under the PG127 1.1.57 analysis.
