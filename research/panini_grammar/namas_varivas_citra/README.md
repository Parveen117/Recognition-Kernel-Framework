# PG144 — An incorporated namas changes the case of the worshipped participant

Monty · 11 November 2026

The Mahābhāṣya asks why the gods are accusative in namasyati devān, given the dative construction with independent namas. Its answer makes the independently meaningful expression and the incorporated constituent different grammatical inputs. This packet joins that case decision to the ten-lakāra morphology of namas, varivas and lexically marked citraṅ. Three profiles add 270 positions and 568 word readings.
