# PG955 · Counting ears and counting collections

Two collections may each contain many ears. The request “of the two collections” selects GEN dual *śrautrayoḥ*. The request “of the ears in one collection” can refer to many individual bodily ears and select GEN plural *śrautrāṇām*. Both stems sound identical after aṇ, but the entity counted is decided before case inflection. The bodily reading belongs to the qualified prajñādi self-sense at 5.4.38. The collective belongs to 4.2.37–38. PG950 supplies the lexical admissions, its citation control, and seven further case requests. PG953 distinguishes the unextended hearing faculty. PG951's adjectival agreement and P43's learning derivative have separate owners. **Three different sources of ṇ.** In INS singular, ṭā takes ina (7.1.12), and stem a plus i gives e. In each direct plural the neuter substitution śi licenses num (7.1.20, 7.1.72), with length before the nasal. GEN plural inserts nuṭ before ām (7.1.54), and the stem vowel lengthens (6.4.3). In all three histories the final r of the stem's tr cluster precedes the suffixal nasal across vowels. The 8.4.1–2 condition holds. The r earlier in the initial cluster need not cross the medial dental stop. Thus the same retroflex nasal in *-eṇa*, *-āṇi* and *-āṇām* has three owners. For LOC plural e licenses the retroflex sibilant in *-eṣu*. For singular ablative the ending āt reaches pause with the old voiced/voiceless alternatives retained.
