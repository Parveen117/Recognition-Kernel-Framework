## P4. Position and reading proof

**Theorem P4.** On D's exact lexical/meaning/source/sound domain,
PG22 supplies **486 positions and 558 word-branch readings**:

| Profile | Positions | Base readings | Extra sound/ending readings | Total readings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I, ordinary loṭ | 162 | 162 | 21 | 183 |
| O, vidhiliṅ | 162 | 162 | 0 | 162 |
| B, blessing loṭ | 162 | 162 | 21 + 30 | 213 |
| **Total** | **486** | **486** | **72** | **558** |

**Proof.** Twelve P and six A profiles have nine slots each, so
18·9=162 per mood and 486 overall. C5 lists 6+6+6+3=21 ordinary
positions with one additional sound reading; all others have one.
P2/S5 supplies precisely one O reading per slot. B retains the 183
ordinary readings and adds P3's two singular copies of nine single-word
sets and three two-word sets: 2(9+3·2)=30. Thus its count is 213,
and 183+162+213=558. All displayed optional words differ within their
position; controller and meaning histories distinguish shared spellings
across positions. S/H/C exclude any further option on this domain. □

The count is relative to the selected non-doubling and pause policies.
It is not a count of every Sanskrit pronunciation, an independent corpus
attestation count, a count of distinct strings, or a whole-grammar
completion percentage. Ordinary B words overlap I, earlier laṭ packets
are dependencies, and Claude's selected cell totals are not added here.
