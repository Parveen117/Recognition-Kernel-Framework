## N3. Typed zero-law and finite recovery

Let ε:B_22→C_Σ select the empty-word coefficient. No product of
nonempty free words is empty, so ε is a unital dagger homomorphism.
Define the separately typed maps

\[
\pi_{0,M}(\sum_h e_ha_h)=\sum_h e_h\epsilon(a_h),\qquad
\Pi_{0,op}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

**Theorem N3.** On the admitted domains,

\[
\pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,op}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B=
\langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_Σ}.
\]

Cuts, complements, finite sums/products and adjoints specialize
compatibly. The forward unit-start construction recovers exactly P's
source-relative Accepted relation, retaining the Excluded/Open channels.

**Proof.** ε preserves coefficients, multiplication and dagger, and
ε(1+z_e)=1. Guards inspect histories that the state specialization
does not erase. Induction over each finite composable event sequence
therefore gives the two identities and both directions of path recovery.
Each terminal history has its own coordinate with constant coefficient 1;
different options cannot cancel under these forward unit-start inputs. □

The last support claim does not apply to arbitrary signed superpositions,
whose coefficients can cancel. It does not reverse grammatical time by
using adjoints or prove an unrestricted priority/confluence procedure.

## N5. A chosen ending changes the sound fibre

Fix an admitted rudh P 3sg B frame. Consider the two ending choices
o=ordinary tu and t=tātaṅ before their dependent stem/readout paths.
C6 gives the three distinct terminal histories with words
(ruṇaddhu, runddhāt, rundhāt). Each path receives its ordered native
event product; N3 gives the following zero incidence matrix in these bases:

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The ordinary branch has strong ruṇa- and a false collapse cut. The
tāt branch has weak run- and a true cut, then two retained choices.
An adapter that copies the ordinary false flag into the new tāt branch
would instead give

\[
A_{stale}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad A-A_{stale}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

**Theorem N5.** On this block the stale-flag adapter misses exactly
one terminal history. A has rank two but is not a pairing isometry:
A†A=diag(1,2). Its sound fibres must be constructed after the actual
ending/marker/stem choice, and N3 preserves those dependent fibres.

**Proof.** The third row is precisely the newly eligible collapse path.
The two columns of A have disjoint, nonempty supports, hence are linearly
independent; direct coordinate pairing gives diag(1,2). Re-evaluating
C5 on weak runddhāt admits both rows of the second column. All native
products have constant coefficient 1, giving exactly A at zero. □

This matrix is a finite grammatical incidence operator, not a probability
transition or a claim that branching should preserve a norm. Keep/delete
choices are distinct histories even though other slots may share a word.
Bhid/chid give the analogous two-output tāt fibre; P4 accounts for all
three roots and both eligible persons without multiplying the option
independently across ineligible slots.
