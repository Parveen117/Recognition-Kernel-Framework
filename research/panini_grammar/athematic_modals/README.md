# PG22 — Adādi, juhotyādi and rudhādi modal constructions

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

PG22 constructs complete loṭ, vidhiliṅ and blessing-loṭ continuations
for twelve exact roots in the three athematic families opened by PG7–PG9.
Its eighteen qualified pada profiles give **486 morphological positions
and 558 word-branch readings** under the declared classical sound policy.
The construction includes hi→dhi, dehi/dhehi/edhi, owned abhyāsa-lopa,
weak śnam and ghu continuations, tātaṅ and optional 8.4.65 collapse.

The derivation retains the full native history before its zero-reading.
In particular edhi needs a source-qualified query of the earlier s even
after e has appeared; and tātaṅ can create a consonant-collapse site
that the ordinary strong third singular did not have.
