### What the Nepal catalogue actually preserves

At NAK 1/974, reel A 385/4, the catalogue transcribes the end of a unit on **folio 90v5–6**. Its description *loḍvilasita* and its ordinal “seventh” refer to the seventh section of the tiṅanta division. The very same line identifies the **twentieth sarga of the poem**. The following prose announces *lṛṅvilasita*; the next dialogue addresses Kākutstha. Its location is therefore the XX/XXI transition. The word “seventh” is unusable as a label for the mood of the competing separate command in **poem VII.103**, VII.101 in the alternative numbering. The other excerpt on 20r has a dual Rāghava reference in a different episode. No reading of VII.103 is assigned to this codex. A photograph or cited collation identifying that verse, and evidence for the separately transmitted command's mood, remain outstanding. ### Fire as protector in XXI.2

Fire is the first-person subject and Sītā is the person protected. Read the other verb as past conditional **abhaviṣyat**, which has final d before the following vowel. Mallinātha's discussion of the negative supposition explains a failure of protection and consequent failure of recognition as pure.
