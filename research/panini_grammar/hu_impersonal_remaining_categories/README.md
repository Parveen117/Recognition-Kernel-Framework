**Future, conditional and benedictive.** Rule 6.4.62 expressly covers both bhāva and karman. The root is vowel-final in upadeśa. Before tās, sya or the benedictive sīyuṭ, the ordinary branch keeps its aniṭ history and yields ho-. On the optional branch, ciṇ-like strengthening and inserted iṭ are coupled: hu → hau → hāv before i. The three suffixes yield hāv-i-tā, hāv-i-ṣya-te and hāv-i-ṣī-ṣṭa. The conditional prefixes aṭ to the respective future base and selects secondary 3sg *ta*. Taking the lengthened grade from one route and omitting its iṭ would break the source's joint condition. The luṭ *tā* is the finite third-singular replacement, with no priest or agent noun selected by its spelling. [Rule 6.4.62](../anga_affix_abhyasa/RULES_6_4_A.md)

**Aorist.** Here the original ending *ta* and the expressed action jointly license cli → ciṇ by 3.1.66. The ṇit affix licenses vṛddhi, and 6.1.78 before its pronounced i gives hāv. Rule 6.4.104 deletes the following ending. Thus a + hāv + i + Ø gives *ahāvi*. The final i belongs to ciṇ. This slot cannot borrow the optional sic/iṭ pair from another ending.
