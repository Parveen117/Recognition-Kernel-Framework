## H02. Principal intent is not actual payment or generic self-benefit

Kāśikā 1.3.73 explicitly retains kartṛ-abhiprāya. Its
wealth-seeking speaker rejects a nyāya with the thought of
obtaining wealth by that rejection. Nyāsa confirms that intended
purpose. Bālamanoramā explains the verbal rejection; the finite
word does not by itself establish whether the hoped-for result
was later obtained.

Kāśikā/Nyāsa 1.3.72 distinguish the principal purpose of the
selected action from incidental wages or fees. The classification
is not “money is always secondary”: 1.3.73 expressly uses the
wealth aim as the principal intent of this rejection. Retain the
intended-result relation and its current controller, rather than
substituting an observed reward balance or any benefit whatsoever.

Future, conditional and blessing contexts preserve this predicate
and intent description without asserting realization. Unknown
recipient or principal-purpose status stays HOLD. A supplied
other-directed ordinary frame is required for P; failed evidence
for A alone is insufficient.

## H03. Present/future interfaces reuse the exact vad source

PG81 establishes vad+a under śap, ordinary present-system
endings, and seṭ iṭ before sya/tāsi in both padas. Their
root-facing affixes are not kit, so 6.1.15 yajādi root
saṃprasāraṇa does not apply. The new apa prefix does not
change that affix query. Kram's A-specific iṭ prohibition and
the ṇyanta rules of preceding stages are inapplicable.

For the new P singular coordinates, reuse the already pinned
full thematic/future finite ending contracts, on this independently
established vad stem. No old PG81 singular request is retroactively
added or credited; these are new apa/result-intent keys.

## H04. Kit/root change differs from copy-only saṃprasāraṇa

P blessing has kit liṅ under 3.4.103–104 and a non-val
boundary: no iṭ, root vad→ud by 6.1.15/108. A blessing
instead takes ordinary iṭ before sīyuṭ and keeps vad.
No A2pl ḍh option exists because root d immediately before
iṭ is outside the required iṇ class.

In liṭ, all A and six P dual/plural endings are apit/kit
under 1.2.5. PG82's explicit source order gives root vad→ud
before copying, then u|ud→ūd. P singulars instead keep the
root and apply 6.1.17 to the copy: u|vad/vād. Kaumudī and
Bālamanoramā's vad subsection in the 3.1.54 field explicitly
prints uvāda, uvaditha and first-singular uvāda/uvada. These
formerly uncounted controls now support the corresponding newly
admitted apa-profile singular requests.

P3sg has ṇal vṛddhi; P1sg permits both 7.1.91 ṇit choices;
P2sg thal has iṭ and short root a. Kit/root and copy-only
branches are tied to their exact ending slots, never free
alternatives for a weak cell. The container 3.1.54 does not
license aṅ for vad or import the adjacent śvi options.

## H05. Sic grade remains pada-specific despite shared iṭ

PG82's literal 7.2.3–4 sources establish mandatory vād in
P sic, including before iṭ; vad's named mention blocks the
ordinary light-a option of 7.2.7. A has no P condition and
keeps vad. Both have iṭ, so iṭ alone cannot choose the grade.
Neither ṅit outer luṅ nor apit tiṅ makes the intervening
sic a kit trigger for root saṃprasāraṇa.

The new P3sg/2sg requests have apṛkta hal endings and receive
7.3.96 īṭ. Rule 8.2.28 removes sic-s, and affix i+ī
coalesces; this length is distinct from the root's ā. P1sg
retains sic with am. A2pl has its separate 8.2.25 sic-lopa
before dhvam, with dental dh because root d precedes iṭ.
No caṅ, optional aṅ, ksa or ciṇvat branch is admitted.
