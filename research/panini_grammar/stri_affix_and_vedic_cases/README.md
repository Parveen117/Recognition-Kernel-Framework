# PG203 · The r of strī, its two accusatives and its Ṛgvedic cases

Monty · 12 November 2026

The project’s five-pāda rule **4.165** reads *styāyater ḍaṭ* and gives **strī** ‘woman’. The second digital transcription has the same r-less suffix. The Mahābhāṣya verse preserved under Pāṇini 1.2.64 and 4.1.3 explicitly names **ḍraṭ**, with r. Dayānanda’s electronic text numbers this rule 4.166, and Satyavrata’s printed text numbers it 4.167. The Tattvabodhinī also distinguishes the numbered sūtra from the wording of the explanatory verse.

The two spellings matter to the derivation. With *ḍraṭ*, the suffix supplies the **r** of *strī*. The initial marker licenses the deletion of the root’s final vowel portion. Ujjvaladatta also invokes deletion of y before the following consonant (*vali lopaḥ*). The final ṭ identifies the suffix for the feminine *ṅīp* rule. These steps account for the r-bearing result. The abbreviated *ḍaṭ* reading in the two digital editions gives the same lexical word but leaves the source of its r open. The two Dhātupāṭha entries whose normalized root is *styai* are recorded with their own citation spellings. The unmarked verbal citation alone does not choose between them.

### Inflection of the lexical stem

The **24 case–number positions have 26 written readings**, all agreeing with the separate archived *strī* paradigm. Pāṇini 6.4.79 gives the *iy* form before vowel-initial endings. At 6.4.80 the substitution is optional in exactly two positions here, accusative singular *striyam / strīm* and accusative plural *striyaḥ / strīḥ*. The Kāśikā explains why genitive plural has *strīṇām*. The exception in 1.4.4 keeps the short vocative *stri*. The Nyāsa on 6.4.79 explicitly restricts this instruction to the word *strī*.

In the Zürich/VedaWeb Ṛgveda, **11 occurrences under the selected dictionary identifier cover six of the 24 positions**. All five accusatives use the expanded form. The three singular tokens have *stríyam* and the two plurals have *stríyaḥ*. Neither contracted accusative occurs in this selection. The same accented *stríyaḥ* appears as accusative plural beside *satī́ḥ* at 1.164.16a and as nominative plural beside *yā́ḥ* at 7.55.8c. The adjacent words supply independent agreement evidence for the two readings.

The four open affix assignments at **4.105** retain their status. The ḍaṭ field examined here has its own numbered rule.

Token forms and morphology follow Casaretto, Coenen, Fischer, Halfmann, Kölligan, Korobzow and Reinöhl, [Zürich/VedaWeb Ṛgveda version 3](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21527084), CC BY 4.0.
