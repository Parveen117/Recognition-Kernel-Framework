# PG28 — Exact exceptions and newly admitted contextual fields

Author: Monty. 3 November 2026.

## D1. Full-history input before a word or mood

Start with N1's native carrier, pairing and T/F/? cuts. Retain the
lexical ID, selected sense, event and actor identities, expression role,
qualified pada, clause address, discourse frame, person/number, intended
modality, original/current constituents, markers, deleted owners, source
policy and ordered history. Actor equality is not equality of person labels.

The finite morphological domain is classical, unprefixed, unaccented,
basic kartari with the inherited non-doubling and devoiced-pause policy.
I is ordinary non-āśīs loṭ, O admitted non-āśīs liṅ, and B supplied
āśīs with loṭ. Each uses the previously constructed ending interface.
The contextual selector domain C has its own explicit restrictions below;
it does not transport blessing tātaṅ into a wish merely because both
are described informally as desires.

The primary bhuj row retains its composite पालनाभ्यवहारयोः and P

third *selected* sense above. A is licensed by 1.3.66, not by a free
P/A switch or an inferred principal-fruit condition.

Upabhoga is one new sense family. Its objects or possible everyday
paraphrases do not each create another profile. Tattvabodhinī's further
ātmasātkaraṇa discussion is retained as source context, not separately
counted. The tudādi homonym bhuj in crookedness and the tudādi tṛh
are excluded. An unresolved composite meaning remains Open. Crossed
protection/A or eating/P requests have no license on this selected domain.

## D3. A specific extension of the tṛh source schedule

PG8 already specifies the t/th contact with an early ṣṭutva view for
8.3.13. Preserve that exact route for tu, tām/tam/ta and selected tāt.
It never established a universal permission for every later ṣṭutva
result to feed every earlier rule.

For tṛh + ordinary hi, select the separately evidenced
**Prakriyāsarvasva hi route**: hi→dhi, h→ḍh, ṣṭutva/jaś and
optional 8.4.65 collapse. This yields Bālamanoramā 7.3.92's two
readings, tṛṇḍḍhi/tṛṇḍhi. E3 gives the exact local query record.
The selection is a bounded source/context contract, not a global
adjudication of all ḍh-lopa chronologies. A broad lift at hi would
lose the retained uncontracted branch and is not the selected route.

## D4. What is newly counted

The three inherited PG8 profiles receive 81 new I/O/B positions.
The new upabhoga/A profile receives nine laṭ positions and 27 I/O/B
positions. Total: **117 new positions**, consisting of **108 modal +9
present positions**, with **123 finite-word readings**. The same printed
bhuj form can realize eating or upabhoga; sense is part of the inherited
position identity key, so their semantic inputs are retained separately.

The nine contextual selectors 3.3.152–160 operate on typed requests.
They do not inflate this register by counting every lexical example,
sentence, controller pair or repeated use of a modal template as a new
position. The separately derived bhoktum exemplar is nonfinite and is
not one of these 117 finite positions.

## D5. Contextual-selector domain

C1–C5 cover supplied affirmative uta/api, wish disclosure, sufficient
capacity assessment and future cause/consequence frames. C6–C8 cover
desire-verb embedding, same-actor infinitival/liṅ alternatives and
present-tense modality on the desire verb itself. Inputs supply their
semantic classification; word matching does not infer it.

Restrict these constructions to non-counterfactual, non-Vedic frames
with no independently overriding unreviewed construction. Kriyātipatti
requires the separately identified lṛṅ route under 3.3.139/140 and is
outside this new continuation theorem. Unknown evidence remains Open.
Non-disclosure and other unlisted cases receive NoLicenseFromThisRule,
not a claim that no Sanskrit expression of them exists.

Complete context selection, lexical admission and morphological realization
are distinct gates. An O/F selector can construct a lṛṭ request while
its word realization still requires the appropriate earlier/future root
adapter. No whole-sentence, all-lakāra or universal semantic parser is
claimed by completing these finite selectors.
