# PG803 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 5.2.122 makes Vedic `vini` bahula: `tejasvin` is positive while `varcasvān` shows that matup can remain. The supplement licenses six named bases with long final vowel before `vini`, then adds `marman` and universally admitted `āmaya`. These are bounded displayed paths, not an unrestricted long-vowel theorem.

The remaining supplements have independent owners: `ārakan` after `śṛṅga/vṛnda`, `inac` after `phala/barhis`, optional `ālu` beside the three other hṛdaya possession routes, intolerance `āluj/elu/ūlu`, and `tan` after `parvan/marut`. `vātūla` has two relations—wind-group and inability to bear wind—despite one spelling. `arthī` denotes absence of wealth; ordinary possession remains `arthavān`.
