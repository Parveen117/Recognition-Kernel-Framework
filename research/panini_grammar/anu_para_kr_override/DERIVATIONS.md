## H06. Present and modal affix owners remain separate

Reuse PG71 H09 and PG72 H06: tanādi u is a separate vikaraṇa. The
strong interface is kar-o, while 6.4.110 supplies weak kur-u. Weak
u-lopa before v/m is mandatory by 6.4.108, giving kurvaḥ/kurmaḥ;
the inherited 1.1.58/8.2.79 query blocks an invented long kur vowel.

Loṭ 2sg hi-luk gives kuru. Its first-person ending āṭ produces
karavāṇi/karavāva/karavāma; the n of ni in 1sg is ending-owned and
receives ṇatva from root r. It is not the n in prefix anu. Vidhiliṅ
has kur-u plus yāsuṭ and separate 6.4.109 u-lopa, hence kuryā-.
Laṅ first singular retains the strong karavam continuation.

## H07. Futures and blessing do not reuse the present stem

Without present u, root guṇa gives kar. Before tās the root is aniṭ,
but sya has the specific 7.2.70 iṭ exception: kartā versus kariṣyati.
Lṛṅ retains that actual sya+iṭ interface and adds initial aṭ with its
own secondary endings. The long ī of an aorist singular is not this iṭ.

P blessing takes short riṅ under 7.4.28, producing kri before the
owned yāsuṭ/suṭ construction. Preserve kriyāsuḥ at 3pl, distinct
from ordinary vidhiliṅ kuryuḥ. No future iṭ or present vikaraṇa is
transported into this blessing interface.

## H08. Perfect copy and aorist vṛddhi have distinct causes

Liṭ keeps ca as the root-part copy, with strong/weak original kṛ grades
and the P perfect endings. The 7.2.13 refusal excludes the ordinary
val-initial perfect iṭ here. First-singular 7.1.91 retains both
cakara and cakāra as distinct ṇit-work histories. Apit weak forms
retain cakratuḥ/cakruḥ and cakṛva/cakṛma under their own vowel seams.

Luṅ has aniṭ sic and root vṛddhi kār. Root r licenses sic ṣ; the
t contacts retroflex where required. Sic remains in all nine slots:
r does not meet 8.2.26's left-jhal condition, and there is no preceding
iṭ for 8.2.28. Only the apṛkta 3sg/2sg endings take īṭ by 7.3.96.
Reuse the complete kārṣīt/kārṣṭām/kārṣuḥ continuation from PG73.

## H09. New prefix seams and explicit removal of ni metadata

Without initial aṭ, anu/parā stays before the root or perfect copy.
With initial aṭ in laṅ/lṛṅ/luṅ, anu|a gives **anva** by 6.1.77;
the actual prefix vowel is u, not PG71's ni-owned i. Parā|a gives
**parā** by 6.1.101. Both keep the initial augment in the history.

The prefixes are outside the liṭ copy. Prefix anu n is not a target
of the removed pra/ni ṇatva query, and prefix parā contains no such n.
The full table spells the new words manually. Static suffix comparisons
check only the declared root/affix/ending reuse, after new semantic and
pada admission; they are not grammar execution.
