These manually written rule inferences use [the source decisions](SOURCE_DECISIONS.md)
and [owned derivations](DERIVATIONS.md). They are not individually attested
whole paradigms or executable outputs. Slash-separated words are readings
at one position, not new positions. Present-system order is **r / l**;
future/conditional order is **r+i / r+ī / l+i / l+ī**. The source/prefix,
meaning, role and event certificates remain part of each request.

Both profiles are basic kartari A, with all nine person/number slots. The
unaccented pausal policy retains sam-anusvāra before g. Where initial aṭ
intervenes in laṅ/lṛṅ, sam's m instead remains before that vowel. Alternative
nasal spellings and nominal/derived examples receive no new position credit.
