## N2. Labelled options and a meaning-erasure obstruction

An admitted elementary event acts by
\(U_d(e_hb)=e_{h'}(1+z_d)b\), with h′ appending source, guard, recipient,
choice and stage. Complementary transitions have coefficient 1. Optional
events form labelled sums of their admitted successors; these coefficients
are not probabilities. Evolution is finite ordered composition, with any
elapsed-time interpretation requiring a separate clock adapter.

The present paths have one binary choice L={r retained, root r→l} after
the owned gir/śa construction. Future paths have an earlier iṭ-length
choice I={i,ī}, followed by the same root choice L. Both I outputs are
vowel-initial verbal-affix interfaces, so each admits both L outputs.

two. This proves the finite branch product in the stated source order, not
