## H05. Tudādi śa makes the present gir interface

Rule 3.1.77 selects **śa**, the apavāda to śap, for this tudādi root.
Its ś is an owned marker and gives sārvadhātuka status; unlike śap, it has
no p marker. The apit śa is ṅit-like under 1.2.4. Consequently 1.1.5
withholds 7.3.84 guṇa at the root-facing interface, even where a more remote
tiṅ has a different marker property.

Rule **7.1.100** gives i for root-final ṝ; **1.1.51** gives its r
accompaniment. Thus gṝ|śa becomes **gir|a**, with i/r root-side and the
thematic a affix-owned. This root i is not an iṭ augment. It is short:
the root r is before a vowel, not in 8.2.76/77's relevant word-final or
following-consonant setting. No lexical-marker lopa or nasal augment is
needed for this unmarked root.

Keep gir through earlier morphology, then H03's late option gives the r/l
readout factors **gir / gil**. All 72 present-system positions have those
two histories. This is one śa construction with optional root l, not the
śap/śyan choice of kram and not two newly identified roots.

## H06. Future guṇa and optional iṭ length

For lṛṭ/lṛṅ select sya and for luṭ select tās by 3.1.33. Their
ārdhadhātuka status is distinct from the outer finite tiṅ. Root-facing śa
is absent, and the future affixes have no kit/ṅit condition blocking guṇa.
Rule 7.3.84 gives gṝ→gar with r accompaniment. Retain the explicit
traditional **i-/u-substitution versus guṇa/vṛddhi precedence** statement
in Kāśikā 7.1.102 and Kaumudī 7.1.100; do not first materialize gir and
then treat its newly consonant-final spelling as the original guṇa input.

All **54** future/conditional positions take **iṭ** under 7.2.35. Neither
the lexical P tag nor kram's separate 7.2.36 restriction supplies an aniṭ branch.
Rule **7.2.38** applies to original ṝ-final roots as well as vṛṅ/vṛñ and
optionally lengthens this iṭ. Its inherited non-liṭ condition holds for
the three labels here; the liṅ and P-sic restrictions of 7.2.39–40 do not
apply to this sya/tās request. The current query retains original ṝ-final
identity, not merely the later gar/gal spelling.

The owned interfaces are **gar|i/ī|sya** and **gar|i/ī|tās**. Affix-owned
i/ī remains protected under 1.1.6, separate from root guṇa. The initial s
of sya becomes ṣ after either length by 8.3.59. The teaching i of tāsi
is the pronunciation aid of the pinned PG16 Bālamanoramā/Vāmana policy,
not another iṭ. H03 subsequently supplies r/l in both iṭ-length branches.

## H08. Prefix/aṭ seams and the ordered sound policy

The only actual prefixes here are ava and sam, outside the verbal aṅga.
In laṅ/lṛṅ, consonant-initial gṝ takes **aṭ** under 6.4.71, not the
vowel-initial-root āṭ. Reuse PG76/PG81's explicit Bālamanoramā 1.3.17

| Profile | Nonaugmented start | Laṅ/lṛṅ start | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AVA_NIGARANA_A | avagir-/avagar- | avāgir-/avāgar- | ava+aṭ; a+a→ā, 6.1.101 |
| SAM_PRATIJNANA_A | saṃgir-/saṃgar- | samagir-/samagar- | m before g versus m before the vowel aṭ |

The selected 8.3.23/8.4.59 policy retains anusvāra before g; aṭ puts a
vowel after sam, so its m remains m. Both r/l and i/ī branches share this
addressed prefix history. No prefix is inferred from a single surface letter.

Retain inherited pausal visarga and ordered ant→anusvāra→dental-n before
stops. A dental n created late is not fed back into earlier ṇatva; final n
of ran retains 8.4.37 protection. Choosing l at the root is a later scoped
operation, not a licence to rerun all earlier sound or morphology rules on
a globally rewritten word. Accent and other nasal spellings remain outside
the selected reading policy.
