## H6. The eleven selected stems

The seven bases have 2, 1, 2, 2, 2, 1 and 1 selected stem readouts respectively: ojas → ojāya/ojasya; apsaras → apsarāya; payas → payāya/payasya; yaśas → yaśāya/yaśasya; vidvas → vidvāya/vidvasya; haṃsa → haṃsāya; sārasa → sārasāya. The two ojas readings come from competing authorities, unlike the licensed choice within the payas reading.

## H7. Present versus imperfect

Laṭ A 3sg ojas gives ojāyate on the Kāśikā route and ojasyate on the alternate Bhāṣya route. In laṅ 3sg 6.4.72 and 6.1.90 give aujāyata/aujasyata for ojas and āpsarāyata for apsaras. Aṭ precedes the full form of the other consonant-initial hosts.

## H8. The whole nine-slot contrast

In loṭ 2sg use payāyasva or payasyasva. In vidhi-liṅ 3sg use payāyeta or payasyeta. Choices in the nominal preparation precede person, number and lakāra. A source filter on a single ojas policy only removes the corresponding alternative, without changing any endings.

## H9. The same spelling can carry several citations

Ojāyate occurs under the selected Kāśikā/Kaumudī and one Bhāṣya discussion. Keep the coverage unit as one formation path with multiple supporting sources. Treat the two commentarial words ojāyate and ojasyate as evidence for different policies, not free variation within every policy.
