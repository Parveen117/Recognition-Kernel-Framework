# PG42 — Construction: qualified designations and dependent choices

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Use [Domain](DOMAIN.md), the thirteen rule tables and the
[source decisions](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) together. This constructs a bounded written
relation. It does not execute the grammar or infer missing external context.

## C1. Admission and event ownership

For a request, retain the original word/affix tokens, participants, action and
compound tree. Select a source policy. Evaluate the row's positive conditions
and decisive exclusions using supplied evidence. Each admitted clause produces
its own history, containing its evidence, addressed effect and unresolved
downstream obligations. A false condition rejects the candidate; an unknown
condition leaves it Open.

Classify effects before composing them: authority interpretation, designation,
query transfer, role assignment, compound selection, exponent selection or
interpretive boundary. The five 1.2.53–57 clauses constrain interpretation and
do not manufacture sound-changing events. Transferred कित्/ङित् status attaches
to an affix; it does not insert an audible क्/ङ्. लुक्, लुप् and phonetic loss
retain different cause labels.

## C2. Designations are qualified observations

1.4.1 does not make every label globally exclusive. A short vowel before a
cluster is गुरु for the admitted weight query, while its short duration remains.
An admitted प्रादि can be निपात, उपसर्ग and गति by explicit coexistence. A द्विगु
can also be तत्पुरुष. A technical label records its authority and consumer;
1.1.32's optional सर्वनाम effect for जस cannot leak into अकच् selection.

For feminine stems, first resolve 1.4.3/4 eligibility, then ending-specific
5/6 options, and only then the residual घि route of 7 with its exclusions.
Do not independently combine नदी and घि on the same selected clause. The
1.4.15–20 पद/भ rules likewise preserve the original affix, current target
boundary and the specifically attested Vedic coexistence in 20.

1.1.68 distinguishes mention of a form from mention of a technical class.
Explicit semantic readings such as color at 2.1.69 or crow-class at 2.1.42
remain admitted by their own source clauses. This is not general synonym
expansion or a ban on all semantic classes.

## C3. Original forms and copying

1.1.4 requires a common आर्धधातुक cause for root-part loss and proposed इक्
गुण/वृद्धि. It does not block every later strengthening after any deletion.
1.1.59 exposes the original vowel form only while constructing the licensed
copy; afterwards the active substitute remains. The general 1.1.56/57 views,
1.1.58 exceptions and tripādī visibility remain distinct inherited controls.

Likewise, 1.2.44's fixed-case उपसर्जन allows its qualified shortening effects
without moving that member to the front. A surface-only representation would
lose precisely the case and ownership evidence needed for this distinction.

## C4. Number and retention

1.2.58–63 distinguish number of referents from the admitted grammatical number.
They preserve kind-reference, speaker grouping, Vedic register and star identity.
A star name used for a person does not inherit the star construction.

In 1.2.64–73, record the suppressed expressions and their referents before
reading the retained exponent. Same-form words must share the relevant case
function; identical syncretic endings alone are insufficient. The difference-only
condition in 65/67/69 excludes extra lexical distinctions. वृद्ध in 65 is the
lineage term, not the first-vowel designation of 1.1.73.

At 69 the neuter retention is admitted and **singular treatment** is optional
under K/Kaumudī; 70/71 do not inherit singular treatment. At 68 brother/sister
and son/daughter are paired separately. Retention never deletes participant
provenance from the carrier.

## C6. Kāraka and case

Assign the role to the admitted participant of the admitted action; then consult
the relevant case consumer. A source of fear, desired object of a restrained
animal, concealed-from observer and disciplined teacher are four different
अपादान predicates. A pleased experiencer, creditor and prior requesting actor
are different सम्प्रदान predicates. 1.4.38 and 46–48 specifically change the
role that a more general rule would otherwise assign.

1.4.51's secondary object is restricted by its source-listed constructions and
the absence of a separately specified role. It does not turn every omitted
argument into कर्म. The 1.4.43 and 44 alternatives remain separate role/case
clauses, not contradictory simultaneous endings on one token.

## C7. Particle, compound and phonetic choices

A particle's spelling is insufficient. Resolve its relation to the action and
its exact sense before choosing गति/उपसर्ग/KP. KP may block prefix-based sound
and accent effects, while retaining निपात where licensed. Its case consumers
include 2.3.8–11, which are archived **controls without new coverage credit**.
KP does not imply one universal accusative route.

For concealment तिरस् + कृ + क्त्वा, the adopted source policy is dependent:

by cross-multiplying the two choices: K at 8.3.42 requires गति, and Kaumudī at

explicit open source issue. These witnesses do not add completed paradigm cells.

2.1.1 demands semantic fitness for पद operations without incorrectly blocking

have their own order; its specific option displaces the ordinary 2.2.11 block.

## C8. Accent and utterance views

1.2.29–40 distinguish accent quality, duration, एकश्रुति and the lowered
pre-high-accent realization. The initial half-mora in K's स्वरित analysis is
not half of every long/pluta vowel. Ritual/recitation and special सुब्रह्मण्या
clauses require their own context.

2.1.2 joins a preceding सुप् member to the following vocative **for accent**.
Its own boundary can still count in 8.1.55; it does not become a shared sound
domain for षत्व/णत्व. 8.1.72's later-query invisibility does not erase the
vocative's own accent. The non-mocking and mocking 1.4.106 person patterns are
separate admitted speech acts, not arbitrary person substitution.
