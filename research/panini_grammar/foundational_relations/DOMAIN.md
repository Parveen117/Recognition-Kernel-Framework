not prove a negative condition, authorize a residual P ending, or make a
compound semantically fit. A source witness supplies a bounded example, not a

## D3. Interpretation versus derivation

The 1.2.51–52 derivational analysis of लुप् and the 1.2.53–55 conventional-name
critique are recorded as different source analyses. Under the latter, a name's
gender/number is supplied by usage; no speculative affix is added merely to
delete it. 1.2.56–57 likewise constrain semantic admission. They neither erase
the operational उपसर्जन definitions nor supply a universal meaning oracle.

Historical examples involving social roles, biological origin or divination are
grammatical source data. This packet does not infer a living person's social
category or establish those examples as scientific facts.
