# PG261 · Four ā-final adādi roots and Śākaṭāyana’s imperfect plural

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Kāśikā gives both ordered prefix pairs for four ā-final adādi roots: *yā*, *vā*, *drā* and *psā*. Kṣīrasvāmin independently gives all four *pra + ni* presents. He allows both flight and sleep in his explanation of *drā*, while placing *drai* ‘sleep’ in a separate class-one entry.

| Root | *Pra + ni*, present 3sg | *Pra + ni*, imperfect 3pl | *Pra + ni*, imperative 3pl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *yā* | praṇiyāti | praṇyayān / praṇyayuḥ | praṇiyāntu |
| *vā* | praṇivāti | praṇyavān / praṇyavuḥ | praṇivāntu |
| *drā* | praṇidrāti | praṇyadrān / praṇyadruḥ | praṇidrāntu |
| *psā* | praṇipsāti | praṇyapsān / praṇyapsuḥ | praṇipsāntu |

Śākaṭāyana’s imperfect plural with *jus* is explicitly attributed in 3.4.111. Kṣīrasvāmin gives both *ayān* and *ayuḥ*. The Mādhavīya explains why the option does not carry over to the imperative: the rule expressly names *laṅ*. In the first-singular imperative *praṇidrāṇi*, the second *ṇ* comes from the *r* in the root. The first belongs to *ni* under 8.4.17.

The eight profiles add **288 positions with 312 readings** across the present, imperative, imperfect and ordinary optative. Their prefix order, differing root consonants and source-specific plurals remain recoverable from the tables.
