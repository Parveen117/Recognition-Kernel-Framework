## N1. Input and carriers

Extend the inherited free unital dagger carrier on typed histories. A source point stores nominal ID, actor, semantic role and relation witness; a request adds lakāra, pada and agreement. Basis arrows carry source, complement, ordered local operations and the resulting word. The option of analytic expression is outside the finite-word codomain.

## N3. Compatibility and pairing

Identical words can have several preimages; there is no event-isometry assertion or physical clock identification.
