The masculine bhavat entry prints bhavān/bhavantau/bhavantaḥ and sends the
remaining cells to dhīmat. The feminine entry prints
bhavatī/bhavatyau/bhavatyaḥ and sends the remainder to nadī. The neuter entry
prints bhavat/bhavatī/bhavanti and sends the remainder to jagat.

The corresponding pattern address rows are:

dhīman / dhīmantau / dhīmantaḥ
nadi / nadyau / nadyaḥ
jagat / jagatī / jaganti

The section's closing note requires a third-person predicate with bhavat and
gives bhavān gacchati and bhavatī vadati as examples.
