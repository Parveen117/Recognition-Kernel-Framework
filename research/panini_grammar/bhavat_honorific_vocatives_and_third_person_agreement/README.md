# PG897 · *Bhavat* honorific vocatives and third-person agreement

Monty · 25 November 2026

Śabdamañjarī positively assigns honorific `bhavat` to three genders and sends
its masculine, feminine and neuter remainders to the fully printed `dhīmat`,
`nadī` and `jagat` paradigms. Their address rows therefore supply
`bhavan/bhavantau/bhavantaḥ`, `bhavati/bhavatyau/bhavatyaḥ`, and
`bhavat/bhavatī/bhavanti`. The same source explicitly requires third-person,
not second-person, predicates with this honorific and gives two examples.
