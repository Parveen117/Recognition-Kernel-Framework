## N4. One unaddressed semantic coordinate is insufficient

Suppose the observer is refined by the single row
g=(1,0,1,0), recording the total genitive-sector coefficient. Let
q=(p;g), a rank-three map. On an isolated history it distinguishes the
two case classes, but it still cannot transport G on the linear carrier.

Indeed x=e₁−e₂−e₃+e₄ satisfies qx=0, whereas
pGx=(1,−1)≠0. A global genitive coefficient loses which word sector
carried it. This is a linear observer obstruction; it does not assert
that a nonlinear classifier of individual words needs four outputs.

**Theorem.** Among linear refinements D of p for which G descends,
minimum rank on this four-history sector is four. Exactly two additional
addressed scalar channels suffice and are necessary.

**Proof.** If p=RD and DG=G_DD, then pG=RG_DD. Hence row(D)
contains row(p) and row(pG). Their four rows span C_Σ⁴: pG separates
e₁/e₃, and subtracting those rows from p separates e₂/e₄. Therefore
rank D≥4. The explicit refinement with rows

\[
(1,1,0,0),\quad(0,0,1,1),\quad(1,0,0,0),\quad(0,0,1,0)
\]

has rank four, preserves p and transports G by conjugation on its image.
It attains the bound, exactly the finite module criterion of canonical
N03. It is a state/module repair, not a two-sided algebra quotient. □

The numerical minimum belongs to this declared four-history sector
and action catalogue. It does not reconstruct every semantic role,
source policy or Sanskrit phrase, and it is not a newly claimed general
linear-algebra theorem.

\[

\[
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B=
\langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
