# PG832 · What the makers do in four Ṛgvedic clauses

PG192 left four suffix-accented kartṛ tokens without a governed object in the verse. Sāyaṇa identifies participants in all four. His explanations distinguish the agent of a predicate, the comparison introduced by na, and a dependent supplied in explanatory prose. These distinctions let the syntax advance while preserving the recorded cases and accent.

| Śākala passage | Form in the source | Sāyaṇa’s analysis and its grammatical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [RV 1.55.8c–d](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=196550) | kartṛbhiḥ, instrumental plural, beside the long nominative plural avatāsaḥ | Sāyaṇa explains the covering of Indra’s bodies by deeds of strength which bring about the slaying of Vṛtra and others. In his separate illustration, living beings gathering to draw water surround wells. The wells are the term of comparison. Vṛtra and the slaying belong to the gloss, and the instrumental has no overt object of its own in the pāda. |
| [RV 1.139.7f](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=402092) | Saṃhitā kartarī, padapāṭha kartari, Zürich kartári + | The commentator takes the maker as Agni, the addressee. His gloss supplies **sarvasya** with “producer” and identifies the locative with “in you, Agni”. Within the verse, **tām** resumes the cow, Aryaman milks her, and the final verb veda again has tām as its object. The genitive belongs to the gloss. The long final ī in the connected line, short i in the separated word and the corpus editor’s plus are three separately recoverable facts. The reason for the plus remains open. |
| [RV 6.19.1d](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=315349) | sukṛtaḥ kartṛbhir bhūt | Indra is the subject. The makers are the sacrificers (*yajamānaiḥ*), and sukṛtaḥ bhūt is explained as being well served or attended. The people performing that service occupy an instrumental slot in the clause. The preceding purpose dative vīryāya belongs to the growth predicate on this reading. |
| [RV 7.62.1d](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=201042) | kratvā kṛtaḥ sukṛtaḥ kartṛbhir bhūt | The subject is the Sun. Sāyaṇa supplies Prajāpati with the first kṛtaḥ. For the second, the makers are composers of praise and the Sun is made keen by praise. His gloss explicitly separates the two acts and their agents. The identical closing three words in 6.19.1 have a different subject and a different explanation of the makers. |

The selected nominal input is the unprefixed √kṛ agent noun under PG192’s suffix-accented lexical entry. Its government question is distinct from its own instrumental plural or locative singular. The neighbouring vi + √duh belongs to the milking clause in 1.139.7. Sāyaṇa explicitly allows two analyses of *duhre*, a perfect without reduplication in this Vedic use and a present with deletion of t. Both analyses are retained at this locus. Neither assigns the cow to kartari. The precise derivation of every segment of duhre and the historical choice between the two analyses are open. This packet adds four source decisions and one clause construction with four verse readouts. The two explanations of one verb are commentary observations. No new morphological position or reading is credited.
