## S3. What remains outside the added words

Causal caṅ has an established selector and partial constituent derivation, with the 7.4.93/94 interpretation held explicitly. Wider ṛkṣi admission still requires a compatible register, teaching form and final-i role within the same source policy. The existing reported jiri branch does not by itself settle that combination. Broad Vedic/accent paradigms, optional phonetic doubling, other meanings, prefixes, karmakartari and additional perfect/passive aorist branches require their own domains.
