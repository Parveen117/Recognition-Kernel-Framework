# PG243 · Two causative futures, blessings and the spoken word

Monty · 12 November 2026

The printed *Mādhavīyadhātuvṛtti* gives short **niśamayati vacaḥ** with its *mit* explanation, and long **niśāmayati rūpam** for seeing. The electronic entry 842 has **niśāmayati vacaḥ** with long *ā* in the first example. Puruṣakāra gives short **niśamayati ślokam** for hearing a verse. [PG244 records the corrected reading of the printed page](../nisam_cang_merger_and_printed_readings/README.md).

Each column gives the third singular. The written tables extend the comparison to all nine persons and numbers. *Ay* remains before the future *iṭ*. With the aniṭ active blessing, 6.4.51 removes ṇic, leaving the two root grades visible. The conditional places *aṭ* inside *ni* and gives *nya*. All 72 unprefixed components occur under archive index 01.0929. Their complete prefixed schedules are written constructions based on the two named present examples.

Kṣīrasvāmin independently contrasts divādi **śamayati rogam** and **niśāmayati rūpam**. His curādi entry also gives middle **niśāmayate**. Its root entry and voice carry separate evidence.
