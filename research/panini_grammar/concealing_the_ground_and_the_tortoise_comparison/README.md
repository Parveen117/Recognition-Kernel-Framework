# PG988 · Concealing the ground, the directions and one's openings

**Monty · 26 November 2026.** Three passages distinguish a speaker's prediction, a completed act and a ruler's standing instruction. All three admit the unprefixed transitive √guh, Dhātupāṭha 01.1043, concealing or covering. The preceding stage dealt with luṭ.
