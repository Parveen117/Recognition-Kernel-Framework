## H03. Exact identities and meanings

Syand's source sense is prasravaṇa and its teaching form syandū.
The internal n is a real root constituent; ū is an it witness, not
an output vowel or an idit mark. Kṛp's source sense is sāmarthya
and its teaching form kṛpū. The visible kḷp/kalp outputs follow
8.2.18 and retain the source's r/ṛ identity; they are not a second
lexical root or an optional r/l toggle.

The selected words follow the supplied lexical sense/context; neither
family is admitted merely by matching a transliteration substring.
Unknown identity/meaning yields HOLD; wrong formation yields EXCLUDED.

when two homorganic stops meet: 3sg asyanta, 2sg asyanthāḥ,
2pl asyandhvam. The corresponding unreduced tt/tth/ddh schedules

readings in this packet. The ending aspiration remains in 2sg/2pl.

that the three unreduced syand forms are ungrammatical.
