## H07. A seṭ sic paths

With iṭ, the actual sic interface is vowel-initial and does not
satisfy 1.2.11's jhal condition. Syand retains n and its root a;
kṛp takes ordinary ṛ-guṇa to karp, then 8.2.18 r→l gives kalp.
Apply PG116's seṭ A tuple after syand/kalp, with initial aṭ.

Thus 3sg is asyandiṣṭa/akalpiṣṭa; 2pl is asyandidhvam/
akalpidhvam. The pre-iṭ root-final d/p is outside iṇ, so no
optional retroflex ḍh is admitted by 8.3.79. Sic-s is affix-owned
and takes ṣ after iṭ except where the pinned dhvam deletion acts.
Neither root's ūdit mark makes that iṭ long.

## H08. A aniṭ sic paths

Kṛp has ik-upadhā and the actual jhal-initial A sic interface;
1.2.11 makes sic kit, so guṇa is blocked. Retain kṛp until its
owned 8.2.18 change to kḷp. Syand has no ik-upadhā premise and
does not acquire that kit assignment. Its n is retained. The late
sic-lopa before consonantal endings is asiddha to the earlier n-upadhā
query; it does not reopen the P-style n-lopa, as the selected source
expressly explains.

Use the owned aniṭ A endings with sic-s. Before t/th the 8.2.26
sic-s deletion applies after root d/p; before dhvam use 8.2.25.
Where s remains, syand d→t by 8.4.55; kṛp p remains p. No
ṣ is inferred after these dental/labial root consonants. Before dh,
kṛp p→b by 8.4.53. Apply H04's selected homorganic reduction
to syand's three stop junctions only.

## H09. Joint relation and observation limits

For each lexical input the admitted relation is exactly
P→{aṅ, no iṭ} and A→{sic+short iṭ, sic without iṭ}, before
selected local sound normalization. Kṛp's ṛ/ḷ versus ar/al grade
is dependent on that interface; syand's n-loss is dependent on the P
aṅ path. Neither is an independent root-stem menu.

Perfect auxiliaries, passive ciṇvat, future sya and finite tāsi have
no owner in this grid. The old future/conditional packets are exact
references only. Source readings and sound normalization remain explicit
so an implementer can expand a broader phonetic policy later without
pretending the extra words are extra morphological requests.
