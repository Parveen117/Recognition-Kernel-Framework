## H07. Prefix-relative augmentation and dependent ṇatva

All nine roots are consonant-initial after the specified root work.
Laṅ/lṛṅ/caṅ-luṅ therefore have ordinary aṭ, not āṭ. For ā+yam
and ā+yas, prefix ā+aṭ gives ā. Pari+aṭ gives parya; the others
have unprefixed a-. Thus āyāmayata, āyāsayata and paryamohayata
retain prefix and augment as separate owners despite fused readouts.

allowed intervening sounds of 8.4.2. It is not a free n/ṇ pair.

path: rocayāni, nartayāni. No retroflexion is copied from a
root's mere possession of r. All remaining P loṭ 1sg forms have
