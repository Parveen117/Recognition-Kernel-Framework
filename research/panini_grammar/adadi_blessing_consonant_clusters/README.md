# PG262 · Root-initial clusters and two benedictive grades

Monty · 12 November 2026

Initial **dr** and **ps** license two benedictive grades under 6.4.68. The single-consonant initials of *yā* and *vā* retain *ā*. The Kāśikā gives **yāyāt** as a limiting example. The Mādhavīya identifies *yāyāt* and its dual *yāyāstām* specifically as blessings.

| With *pra + ni* | Simple future 3sg | Ordinary optative 3sg | Benedictive 3sg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *yā* | praṇiyāsyati | praṇiyāyāt | praṇiyāyāt |
| *vā* | praṇivāsyati | praṇivāyāt | praṇivāyāt |
| *drā* | praṇidrāsyati | praṇidrāyāt | praṇidrāyāt / praṇidreyāt |
| *psā* | praṇipsāsyati | praṇipsāyāt | praṇipsāyāt / praṇipseyāt |

The ordinary optative and blessing share a surface in two singular slots for each profile, the third and second persons. The dual separates the moods, as in *praṇiyāyātām* and *praṇiyāyāstām*. Across the eight profiles, 16 of the 72 person-matched comparisons share a reading and 56 have disjoint readings.

The two futures, conditional and benedictive add **288 positions and 324 readings**. The extra 36 readings belong to the two cluster-initial roots’ nine benedictive positions under each prefix pair. The eight profiles now fill **576 of 720**.
