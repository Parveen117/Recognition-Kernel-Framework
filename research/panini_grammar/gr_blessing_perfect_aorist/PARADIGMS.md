# PG84 — Gṝ: blessing, perfect and aorist

Monty · 8 November 2026

These explicit written rule inferences use [the source decisions](SOURCE_DECISIONS.md)
and [owned derivations](DERIVATIONS.md), not a morphology generator. The
profile, meaning, roles and prefix certificates are exactly PG83's. Each
slash-separated word is a reading at the same position.

Blessing order: aniṭ r, short-iṭ r, short-iṭ l. Perfect order: r, l.
Aorist order: aniṭ r, short-iṭ r, long-iṭ r, short-iṭ l, long-iṭ l.
At 2pl, each seṭ branch expands in **dh / ḍh** order; aniṭ blessing and
aorist have mandatory ḍh. Perfect vowel-initial endings have no iṭ; they
still admit r/l. No nasal-spelling alternatives receive extra credit.
