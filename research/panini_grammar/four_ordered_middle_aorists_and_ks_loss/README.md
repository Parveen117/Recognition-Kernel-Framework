The five remaining positions preserve *kṣ* throughout the selected aorist table, including *praṇyadhikṣi* in the first singular. The four coincident cells recur after *pari + ni*, yielding eight matches overall. The two tense requests remain distinct wherever the surface coincides.

Maitreya gives the middle perfects *cikye / cicye* for *ci*. Their optional consonant selection combines with the two spellings of the perfect second-plural ending, giving *cikyiḍhve, cikyidhve, cicyiḍhve, cicyidhve*. Kṣīrasvāmin gives *ūpe* for *vap* and the Mādhavīya gives *ūhe* for *vah*. Thus the complete third singulars include *praṇyūpe* and *praṇyūhe*. Mādhava also gives *avoḍha, avakṣātām, avakṣata* in the middle aorist of carrying.
