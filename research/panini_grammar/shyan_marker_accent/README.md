## Resolved local obligations

Padamañjarī's 4.1.78 argument requires ञ् to have its इत्
designation when the replacement is made: removing that premise to escape
the initial-U accent also loses the stated वृद्धि license. This is
different from requiring every grade event to occur before replacement.

For ङीप्, the current Y-owned अ cannot be combined with an old
अण् identity to manufacture the sound-dependent guard. For the इञ्
उपसंख्यान's ङीष्, Y's current अ ending does not meet the retained
इ requirement. Standard 4.1.74 admits चाप्, and its explicit commentary
gives the चित् accent priority over the पित् proposal. 8.2.5 carries
U through the addressed vowel coalescence.

A hypothetical replacement-plus-टाप् gives the different initial-U
diagnostic, despite equal unaccented letters. The added-affix marker-redesign
discussion can recover the same local accent with टाप् under its own
assumptions. That conditional comparison is not an optional standard branch
or a proof that every downstream marker distinction may be removed.
