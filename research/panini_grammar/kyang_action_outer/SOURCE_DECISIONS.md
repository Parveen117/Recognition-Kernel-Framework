# Source decisions

## H1. Extend the same event

Rumination, ascetic practice, expulsion, production and own experience use the same PG145 nominal IDs across lakāras. Preserve the source and semantic guard before evaluating the outer action. No change in tense converts someone else’s experience into the subject’s own.

## H2. Kyaṅ does not force A on tapas

The explicit exception in Nyāsa and Bālamanoramā concerns the kyaṅ-final derivative of tapas. There is no present-only restriction in the selected statement. Consequently the ordinary P perfect auxiliaries and P blessing/aorist endings belong to tapas. Its original s remains a valid consonant before ky.

## H3. Keep or drop y only on the original consonant

For tapasya the ordinary 6.4.50 retained/deleted-y pair yields tapasy- or tapas- before the outer recipient. The other 15 stems have ā before y, so their remaining factor ends in āy. Outer iṭ and the ending-owned īṭ on the two P aorist singulars follow the previously written schedules.

## H4. A 2pl is evaluated separately

For the 15 A profiles, the y before iṭ admits the established dental/retroflex choice under 8.3.79 in luṅ and āśīrliṅ 2pl. Tapas remains P, so these A endings are outside that profile. In liṭ the primary word carries the named event and the auxiliary carries its local finite ending.

## H5. Old vowel memory at the P sic junction

On the y-deleted tapas path the visible short a near final s could seem eligible for 7.2.7. The a of the grammatical derivative has been removed under 6.4.48; the explicit avadhīt reasoning at Kāśikā 1.1.57 supplies the preceding-grade control. Use the corresponding protected pattern in PG127/144. Frequency or direct lexical attestation for each extended form is not claimed.
