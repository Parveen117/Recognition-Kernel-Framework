# PG265 · Travelling forms in the Ṛgveda and the two wheels of the wedding chariot

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Zürich annotation has **27 gender-labelled tokens** for the travel root identified as *yA_7091*. Twenty-one are present active participles. Nineteen have the expected written word after comparing without the pitch marks. At 1.94.12b one further word, *avayātā́m*, includes *ava*. At 1.117.12a the nominative masculine dual is *yā́ntā*, with Vedic *-ā* where the regular table gives *yāntau*. Kāśikā 7.1.39 treats Vedic replacements of case endings.

In the wedding verse the genitive is compatible with the shorter feminine *yātī*. The table also licenses *yāntyāḥ*, but the source has the form without *n*. Conversely, even spelling **and pitch** coincide in the two occurrences of *yātáḥ* above. Their inflection and participial category differ. The 27 observations include three *ta*-participles, two present middles and one perfect active in addition to the 21 present actives. Source words and verse context are preserved alongside the regular comparison in the concordance. No new finite or nominal position is counted here.
