# PG790 · Derivation and native recovery

## Extended surplus

Rule 5.2.46 inherits the 5.2.45 relation “this is extra in it” and its `ḍa` suffix. A pure numeral ending in `-śat`, or `viṃśati`, is admitted. The marker causes removal of the final `ṭi` portion. Thus `triṃśat` gives `triṃśam` in relation to `śatam`; compounds retain their preceding numeral, giving `ekatriṃśam`, `ekacatvāriṃśam` and `ekaviṃśam`. The base `viṃśati` gives `viṃśam`.

The rule does not treat every compound ending in the same sounds as a numeral. `gotriṃśat` and `goviṃśati` are explicitly blocked. PG789's same-denomination and target-total guards are inherited: the surface suffix does not itself prove the required surplus relation.

## Value and exchange

Rule 5.2.47 selects `mayat` when an integral numeral greater than one states how many equal portions of one commodity value one portion of another. In the first direction, two, three or four portions of barley are the `nimāna`, the measure of value, of one portion of `udaśvit`; the neuter qualifier is `dvimayam`, `trimayam` or `caturmayam`. In the reciprocal expression the barley is `nimeya`, what is received or measured, and the masculine plural readings are `dvimayāḥ`, `trimayāḥ`, `caturmayāḥ`.

Four independent guards remain visible. The ratio is `x:1`, not `x:2` or `x:3`; `x` exceeds one; it is integral rather than fractional; and it concerns value/exchange, not merely a cooking or absorption ratio. Two different grains named together do not establish a ratio of equal portions. These failed admissions are outside this construction, not exclusions from the language.

## Recovery on the full carrier

Reuse PG14's full operator/history carrier `CΣ`, derived involution and `B=CΣ⟨zₑ,zₑ*⟩`. Type inputs as numeral and lexical class `I`, semantic relation `Q`, suffix/deletion history `H`, commodity and direction `D`, nominal address `W`, and source policy `S`. Each admitted edge acts by `Tₑ(eₕb)=eₕ′(1+zₑ)b`, with its adjoint. The projections `πI, πQ, πH, πD, πW, πS` retain types and ordered histories while applying `ε(zₑ)=0`; therefore `πout Tₑ=Tₑ,0 πin` at every typed edge, and composition and pairing commute with specialization.

The proof separates same-looking outputs. `triṃśam` retains the pure-number and surplus-target history, so a lexical `gotriṃśat` has zero incidence. The two exchange directions retain opposite commodity roles even when they share the same numeral and suffix. A generic twofold cooking relation and a valid two-to-one barter relation cannot merge after zero specialization. PG789's target guards and PG37's unexecuted contracts are reused without duplicate credit.
