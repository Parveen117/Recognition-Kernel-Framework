# PG217 · Four aorist constructions of the twelve lexical entries

Monty · 12 November 2026

Two roots that share the future *samiṣyati* have different aorists. The class-one *ṣam* gives **asamīt** with *sic*. The class-four *samī* of change or ripening is a *puṣādi* member and takes *aṅ*, giving **asamat**. The Dhātupāṭha records the latter as a variant of *masī* in the Kaumudī tradition.

| Lexical input | Third singular | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *mah* and *māh* of class one | *amahīt*, *amāhīt* in P | Short and long radical vowels keep their lexical quantity. The h-final exclusion applies before iṭ-sic. |
| Two class-one *naṭ* entries | *anaṭīt / anāṭīt* | 7.2.7 admits the short and strengthened grades throughout both active paradigms. |
| Both *styai* entries; *nam* | *astyāsīt*; *anaṃsīt* | 7.2.73 supplies *sak* and *iṭ* to ā-final roots and to the named *nam* before active *sic*. The Kāśikā prints all three third-person forms of *nam*. |
| Curādi *maha*, *naṭ*, *śam* | *amamahat*, *anīnaṭat*, *aśīśamata* | The first has early vowel loss, which excludes the i-copy and length of 7.4.93–94. The latter two have long ī in the copy. The printed Bālamanoramā names *anīnaṭat* at the dance entry. |
