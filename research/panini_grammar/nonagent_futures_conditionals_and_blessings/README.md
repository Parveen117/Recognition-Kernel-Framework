# PG228 · Activity and patient futures, conditionals and benedictives

Monty · 12 November 2026

With the two *styai* roots in an impersonal future, **styāsyate** and **styāyiṣyate** belong to the same third-singular request. The ordinary route has neither *yuk* nor *iṭ*. Rule 6.4.62 permits the alternative to take *yuk* on the preceding ā-final stem and *iṭ* on the future suffix. The periphrastic future correspondingly has **styātā / styāyitā**.

| Role-conditioned reading | Future | Benedictive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *styai*, activity (each root) | styāsyate / styāyiṣyate | styāsīṣṭa / styāyiṣīṣṭa |
| Curādi *maha*, patient | mahayiṣyate / mahiṣyate | mahayiṣīṣṭa / mahiṣīṣṭa |
| Curādi *naṭ*, patient | nāṭayiṣyate / nāṭiṣyate | nāṭayiṣīṣṭa / nāṭiṣīṣṭa |
| *nam*, patient | naṃsyate | naṃsīṣṭa |

The future suffix keeps its identity on both routes. The deletion of a following *ta* in an actual *ciṇ* aorist does not transfer to these endings. For the curādi entries the ordinary route preserves *ay* and the *ciṇvat* route exposes the previously determined radical factor.
