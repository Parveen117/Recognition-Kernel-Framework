## H08. Caṅ factors and finite agreement

The complete pre-caṅ factors are **jīgṛdh / jagardh** and
**vavañc**. Compose them with PG95/PG66's thematic secondary maps:

\[
P=(at,atām,an,aḥ,atam,ata,am,āva,āma),\quad
A=(ata,etām,anta,athāḥ,ethām,adhvam,e,āvahi,āmahi).
\]

Caṅ a intervenes before tiṅ. Therefore the thematic plural
is retained instead of direct-abhyasta 7.1.4/3.4.109. At A2pl
caṅ a, outside iṇ, licenses no ḍh option. Root dh in gṛdh
and ending dh in dhvam are different occurrences; both remain.
