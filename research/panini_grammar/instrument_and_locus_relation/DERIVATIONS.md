# Derivation and scope

## Three meanings of “two outputs”

Under 1.4.43 the effective means of div can be karman or karaṇa. The same dice and same event support accusative or instrumental (2.3.2/18). Under 1.4.44 a temporary hire for a specified wage permits karaṇa or sampradāna; with 2.3.18/13 that gives instrumental or dative for the compensation. These alternatives are licensed in the same specified frame. A permanent purchase is outside the temporary-hire certificate.

Under 1.4.48 residence and fasting are different senses. The four specified prefixes in the residence use make the place karman. The vārttika excludes the fasting use, leaving the ordinary place as adhikaraṇa and hence locative. Kāśikā’s continuous grāma upavasati is parsed here as the locative grāme before initial u. The table retains both the source spelling and the separated case form.

At 1.4.47 Kāśikā and Kaumudī describe a vyavasthita-vibhāṣā, with distributed usage. The locative pāpe ’bhiniveśaḥ involves an explicitly named deverbal noun; assigning both it and the finite grāmam abhiniviśate to the same nine-slot verb grid would lose that difference. No arbitrary cross-substitution of the two cases is claimed.

## Means and support depend on the event

1.4.42 specifies the most effective means as construed for the event. 1.4.45 designates the support which sustains the actor or object as the locus of the action. 1.4.46 changes the grammatical designation of that support under the selected adhi verbs. This preserves its underlying relation while changing the case route. In the hiring quotation, the price qualifies the participial parikrīta; the following imperative anubrūhi has its own event and argument structure.
