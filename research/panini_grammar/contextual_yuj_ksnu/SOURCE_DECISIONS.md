## H01. Two roots with different lexical histories

Select rudhādi **yuj 1070007**, teaching **yujir**, sense **yoga**,
lexical U/aniṭ, and adādi **kṣṇu 1020032**, **tejana** (sharpening),
lexical P/seṭ. All counted requests are basic, transitive kartari:
the agent is controller and a karman is supplied. Kṣṇu's object is
the thing sharpened, as in the source's śastra example.

Kāśikā explicitly calls yuj **svaritet**. Retain that marker-accent
witness separately from the lexical anudāttopadeśa/iṭ information.
The nasalized teaching i and final r disappear under 1.3.2/3/9;
their history does not disappear. The unaccented source spelling alone
cannot reconstruct the commentary's accent assertion. The selected
edition numbers yuj 1444, whereas Kāśikā 1.3.64 prints 1445. Preserve
both, identifying the root by gaṇa, teaching and meaning.

The homonyms **1040074 yuj samādhau** and **1100338 yuj saṃyamane**
are archived as excluded lexical controls. Their class, marker or
causal options do not enter this joining-root construction. Kṣṇu's
final u is root material; no śnu augment is invented from its spelling.

## H02. A special A license with a use-sensitive boundary

For pra/upa+yuj, 1.3.64 grants A when the use is **not ritual-vessel
arrangement**, including a supplied event whose principal result is
not directed to the agent. Kāśikā explains that 1.3.72 already supplies
A for agent-directed principal fruit; the new rule addresses the other
case. Therefore a ritual-vessel witness defeats this special license,
not every possible A license for yuj.

Our counted PRA_YUJ_VESSEL_P has both ritual-vessel use and principal
fruit directed elsewhere, so residual **1.3.78 P** is justified. An
agent-directed ritual event remains a zero-credit **1.3.72 A** control,
not an alleged universal P consequence of the vessel predicate. Merely
having a vessel object is insufficient: the witness concerns the
specified ritual arrangement/use. Missing use or result evidence is HOLD.

Principal fruit means the action's intended main result, not incidental
wages or benefit. Preserve Kāśikā 1.3.72's distinction in the input.
The ritual profile describes a classical prose event; no mantra, Vedic
accent or ritual sentence paradigm is counted.

## H03. The chosen vārttika expands prefixes, not meanings

Adopt the Kāśikā/Kaumudī **svarādyanta** policy: a prefix beginning
or ending in a vowel qualifies, retaining the same non-ritual use
condition. The counted extensions are **ud** (vowel-initial) and **ni**
(vowel-final). Their examples udyuṅkte and niyuṅkte are literal.
Pra/upa remain the sūtra's direct cases, not additional optional paths.

Bhāṣya's digitized 1.3.64 preserves first a svarādi formulation and
then another speaker's svarādyanta formulation. The archive keeps both;
the packet selects the latter account, rather than counting them as
free alternatives in every request. Sam, nis/nir and dus/dur lack
the chosen vowel-edge property. Only **sam+yuj** is given a counted
residual P profile, with principal fruit elsewhere. Sam+yuj with
agent-directed fruit is again a zero-credit 1.3.72 A control.

Prefix identity and its event relation are retained under 1.4.58/59/80.
Their status is not inferred from the first sounds after later aṭ
sandhi. Other qualifying prefixes and stacks are outside this finite
inventory, not prohibited Sanskrit constructions.

## H04. Sam+kṣṇu does not inherit an intransitive condition

Kāśikā, Nyāsa, Kaumudī, Bālamanoramā and Tattvabodhinī 1.3.65
explicitly allow **saṃkṣṇute śastram**. Separate placement from the
1.3.29 list avoids inheriting its akarmaka requirement. The selected
sam+kṣṇu profile is therefore **A with a karman**, even though the
root is lexical P and the supplied principal fruit is elsewhere.

This is a root/prefix rule without a seven-label restriction. The
same established license is retained across the written present and
future requests. It neither makes bare kṣṇu A nor changes another
sam-prefixed root's pada. The comparison with sam+yuj deliberately
keeps prefix and transitivity equal while the root-specific license differs.
