# PG31 — Complete conditional word register

Author: Monty. 3 November 2026.

The nine columns are 3sg, 3du, 3pl; 2sg, 2du, 2pl; 1sg, 1du, 1pl.
Every word is the selected unaccented pause reading of E's owned
construction. The register is written evidence, not executed engine output.
iṭ is the **root-facing affix augment**, not initial aṭ/āṭ or A1sg's
ending. C is E's augmented factor, excluding the final thematic a.
Exact identities, senses and qualified pada conditions are in S.

No source claims to print all 477 words together. The tables are the
consequences of E6's written construction, with exactly its selected
source/phonetic boundaries. They are not corpus attestations, empirical
accuracy percentages or a new PASS report.
