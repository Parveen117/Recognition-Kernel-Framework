## N2. The eligibility cut must live after the exception decision

Let H_pre be completed frames with preliminary context candidates;
let H_eff be their resolved C-normal forms. The deterministic normalizer
u:H_pre→H_eff retains its source history as part of the target.
Lift it by

\[
 Ue_h=e_{u(h)}(1+z_{u,h}).
\]

On H_eff let Q_κ select the effective liṅ certificate. On H_pre the
correct cut is its pullback Q_{κ∘u}. Direct calculation on basis units
gives the typed intertwining relation

\[
 Q_\kappa U=UQ_{\kappa\circ u}.
\]

A cut Q_seen that merely records that an earlier candidate included O
cannot replace that pullback. Choose h with 145's preliminary {O,F}
and 146's completed kiṃ kila exception. Then Q_seen e_h=e_h but
Q_κ Ue_h=0. Hence

\[
 Q_\kappa Ue_h\ne UQ_{seen}e_h.
\]

The distinction persists at zero, since ε(1+z)=1. This is a concrete
obstruction to premature counterfactual admission. It is not a claim
that every pair of grammar operators universally fails to commute.
The repair is to retain stage/source evidence and query the effective
certificate after normalization.

## N3. Two additional channels are necessary on a three-history sector

Fix consistent past K histories, with all unrelated fields held within
their admitted types:

- h₁: 147's O context, effective κ, Pre152;
- h₂: 152's affirmative O context, effective κ, Admitted152Plus;
- h₃: 145's preliminary O candidate superseded by 146, effective F only.

The coarse observer p=(1,1,1) records only “an O candidate was seen”.
A2's **final R-admissibility cut** and **final O-admissibility cut** are

\[
 G_R=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0),\qquad
 G_O=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,0).
\]

An observer D that retains p and transports both cuts must contain the
rows p, pG_R and pG_O. Indeed if p=vD and DG=G'D then pG=vG'D
belongs to row(D). But

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}p\\pG_R\\pG_O\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1\\1&1&0\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

has determinant −1 over C_Σ. Therefore rank(D)≥3. The displayed
three-row observer attains the bound and transports both diagonal cuts
by conjugation. **Two extra scalar channels beyond p are necessary and
sufficient on this sector.** They distinguish effective eligibility and
the past optionality band.

This is a local module-observer result in the sense of canonical N03.
It is not a universal Sanskrit memory dimension, an algebra quotient
claim, or an assertion that the transformed observer preserves the pairing.
Keeping only a printed current mood or a preliminary match loses an
operation-relevant distinction.

\[
