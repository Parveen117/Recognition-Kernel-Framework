# PG1001 · Which manuscripts Trivedī cites for the short question and active request

Monty · 27 November 2026

The first volume's Critical Notice, pp. iii–v, identifies four of the sigla encountered in the second volume at Bhaṭṭi XXI.1–3. Trivedī's Vol. II explanatory notes, p. 86, independently state his interpretation of the question and his preference between the two readings of the request. Both volumes are first editions. The identification is at the level of the editor's witness descriptions. Manuscript shelfmarks and full diplomatic readings need the exemplars.

| Siglum | Trivedī's identified witness | What Vol. II actually attributes |
|---|---|---|
| E₃ | The third Elphinstone College manuscript, received through Peterson, dated Saṃvat 1894. The notice describes its Devanāgarī text as complete and identifies its accompanying Jayamaṅgalā. | Printed p. 289 reports the *pra* middle request among its readings at XXI.3. Its poem-text apparatus report and the reading quoted from Jayamaṅgalā are distinct documentary layers. |

Vol. II p. 86 heads its Stanza 1 note with the short-initial **aśaṅkiṣyathāḥ** and explicitly identifies the conditional. The note explains the conjunction of *katham*, censure and non-performance. This is a complete form in the editor's explanation. It supplies a precise parallel for the abbreviated report at p. 288. Only that report can be attributed to T₃ or T₄ at the present evidence level.

His Stanza 3 note preserves Jayamaṅgalā's interpretation with **durmatiḥ** and an explicit counterfactual possibility of being otherwise. It rejects Jayamaṅgalā's decomposition of *tatra* and chooses the temporal interpretation. It also judges Mallinātha's reading preferable to the one retained in the text. The editor's preference and printed verse have different source status. The wording does not identify D's analysis with the Kaumudī policy of PG999, and it licenses no new finite credit for the existing short form or active request.
