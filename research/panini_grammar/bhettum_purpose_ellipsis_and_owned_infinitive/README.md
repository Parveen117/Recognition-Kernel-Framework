# PG333 · Bhettum: the explicit purpose alternative and an owned infinitive

Monty · 17 November 2026

The full Siddhāntakaumudī 1.3.28 field explicitly offers supplying a
tumun-ending word such as **bhettum**. PG330 had retained only the short
Tattvabodhinī cross-reference as Open. This stage completes **two supplied
purpose analyses and one written nonfinite word derivation**, under their
explicit semantic/control conditions. No finite or nominal positions,
readings or source observations are added. Totals remain **51,333 finite**
and **2,917 nominal positions / 3,465 nominal readings**.

The selected reconstruction binds the expressed object to **BHID_PURPOSE**,
not directly to **HAN_MATRIX**. It is an alternative to the supplied-prāpya
reading, not an instruction to put both unprinted words into a verse.
The full analytical expansions in [the records](ANALYSES.md) are our written
reconstructions; the source explicitly supplies the alternative and the
word, not those entire reconstructed sentences or authorial intent.

The nonfinite construction is **bhid + tumun → bhid|tum → bhed|tum →
bhet|tum → bhettum**. Kāśikā names bhid among the aniṭ roots and directly
prints the final **भेत्तुम्** at 8.4.55. The root's short penultimate i
gets the addressed 7.3.86 guṇa; its present śnam stem is not imported.
The two t sounds retain different root/suffix owners.
