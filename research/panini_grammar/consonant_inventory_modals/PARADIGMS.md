## P3. Vac and ordinary class-2 vid

Here ⊥ means **source-excluded on A1's selected view**, not an unknown
spelling or a word to be printed.

| Root | Ordinary I tuple |

The root-stop pairs of vettu/vittām/viddhi follow a vowel and do not
meet the prior-hal requirement of 8.4.65. They are not shortened to

ordinary kṛ word in every I slot. Thus I has **332 admitted positions**

\[
332 + 34\text{ collapse readings} +18\text{ auxiliary readings}=384.

## P4. All vidhiliṅ slots

For any admitted P weak stem W, define

\[
 O_P(W)=(Wyāt,Wyātām,Wyuḥ,Wyāḥ,Wyātam,Wyāta,Wyām,Wyāva,Wyāma).
\]

For any admitted A weak stem W, define

\[
 O_A(W)=(Wīta,Wīyātām,Wīran,Wīthāḥ,Wīyāthām,Wīdhvam,Wīya,Wīvahi,Wīmahi).
\]

Use every W/pada binding in P1/P2. For vac use W=vac; for class-2
vid use W=vid. Examples: riñcyāt/riñcīta; kṣundyāt/kṣundīta;
kṛntyāt; indhīta; class-7 vindīta; class-2 vidyāt; niṃsīta;
vṛjīta versus vṛñjīta; and vacyuḥ under A1's selected anti-family
policy. There is no vid auxiliary here, no vac kit-saṃprasāraṇa,
and no root/ending stop-collapse site.

O has **333 admitted positions /333 readings**.

## P5. Blessing-loṭ

B retains P1–P3's ordinary readings on its own āśīs inputs, including
vid's ordinary auxiliary alternatives. Vac 3pl remains Excluded.
At P 3sg and 2sg add the weak tāt readings:

- Every P profile of P2: **Tāt**, using all its T alternatives.
- Vac: **vaktāt**; class-2 vid: **vittāt**.
- Vid additionally: **Join(vidām,kurutāt)**, two readings at each target.

The fifteen P profiles supply thirty base tāt readings. The five
stop-collapse P profiles supply ten more. Vid's auxiliary adds another
four. Thus B has **332 admitted positions /428 readings**:

\[
384+30+10+4=428.
\]

For kṣud, B 3sg has kṣuṇattu/kṣunttāt/kṣuntāt and B 2sg has
kṣunddhi/kṣundhi/kṣunttāt/kṣuntāt. For vid, each target singular
has six readings: its simple ordinary word, vittāt, two ordinary
auxiliary Join readings and two auxiliary-tāt Join readings. These
are ending-dependent sums, not cross-products of all options.

**Theorem P6.** Conditional on D's complete inputs and declared source
views, P gives exactly 997 admitted positions with 1,145 source-choice-
labelled readings, and two Excluded dispositions.

**Proof.** D fixes 37 profiles and three nine-slot request grids. A1
removes exactly the two listed slots. M partitions every remaining
stem/ending context; P1–P4 bind every profile. S4 exhausts the 34
ordinary collapse sites in each loṭ meaning and the ten extra tāt
readings. A2–A4 contribute exactly the ordinary and tāt auxiliary
readings shown. No O branch or other sound choice remains on this
profile. Summing gives 997+68+36+44=1,145. □

The count is not globally distinct spellings: class-7 vic/vij share
some readouts, several T/D outputs share words, and I/B ordinary words
overlap. Exact root/sense/controller and construction histories remain
separate. N specializes these same histories; no runtime replay is claimed.
