Nyāsa 6.4.16 explicitly places the early root lengthening before
duplication. Śru's r is not a khay consonant retained by 7.4.61, and
smṛ's m is not one either. Smṛ's root ū is a late Tripādī result:
do not feed it back into an earlier copy or apply copy-a→i to its u.
Bālamanoramā 1.3.57 records the ṝ→ur→ūr sequence. Dṛś is not
vowel-final, so 6.4.16 does not lengthen its root ṛ. Its root-final
ś and san-owned s are separate recipients even where the output is kṣ.
No ordinary śṛṇu, smar, paśya or present jā substitution replaces
these retained inner formations.

## H09. Prefix/augment seams and temporal scope

Initial aṭ 6.4.71 is added at the outer derived root before its copy
in laṅ/lṛṅ. All four completed roots begin with a consonant, so the
vowel-initial-root āṭ route is not applicable. It produces ajijñāsa-,
aśuśrūṣa-, asusmūrṣa- and adidṛkṣa- before the selected endings.

| Prefix | Augmented seam | Ownership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| none | a + completed root | a belongs to aṭ before the copy |
| anu | anu + aṭ → anva- by 6.1.77 | prefix u→v; augment remains a |
| prati | prati + aṭ → pratya- by 6.1.77 | prefix i→y; augment remains a |

Thus āśuśrūṣati/āśuśrūṣat differ by ending and retained augment
history even though the prefix/augment surface begins with the same ā.
The PG31 lṛṅ context certificate remains required; a desired future
action alone does not certify an unfulfilled counterfactual desire.
The other inherited tense/mood contexts likewise qualify the outer event.
