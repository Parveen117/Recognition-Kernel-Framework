### Prefix and literal control

At sam before v, 8.3.23 and 8.4.58–59 allow anusvāra or a nasalized v followed by the original v. In the reading field **ṽ denotes a nasalized semivowel**. Thus saṃvastrayati and saṽvastrayati have independently represented junctions. The source prints the anusvāra spelling. The marked notation represents the rule-derived sound. Before the aṭ vowel only sam remains. In Bhaṭṭikāvya 17.54 *samavastrayan* is actually written, verifying the selected laṅ P 3pl form. Its source token supplies morphology; no beneficiary of the narrated act is inferred solely from that spelling. [Literary witness](https://poorvam.com/pore/corpus/bhattikavyam/chapter/17/verse/54)
