# PG211 · The two gaurādi lists and six conditions on the feminine

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Gaṇapāṭha lists **two** groups called *gaurādi*. Group 48 belongs to the feminine rule 4.1.41. Group 231 belongs to the accent rule 6.2.194. Their names alone cannot identify the governing operation.

At 4.1.41, the digital Gaṇapāṭha prints **153 entries or directives**. The Kāśikā prints **117**. There are **81 distinct exact matches**, consisting of **75 individual entries and six directives**. The Gaṇapāṭha has 71 additional distinct strings and the Kāśikā has 36. The repeated *āgrahāyaṇī* occupies two positions in the former and one in the latter. *Naṭa* occurs in both, while the additional *nāṭa* occurs in this Kāśikā list. The row-level comparison preserves every occurrence and its exact counterpart. A source-only spelling does not establish a new lexical identity by itself.

| Joint condition | Application in the Nyāsa and related commentaries |
| --- | --- |
| *eṣaṇaḥ karaṇe* | An instrument of seeking gives *eṣaṇī* with ṅīṣ. A locus can have the independent ṅīp route. |
| *sumaṅgalāt saṃjñāyām* | The special application concerns a name. The Nyāsa gives ordinary *sumaṅgalā* outside that condition. |
| *rohiṇī nakṣatre* | The designation of the asterism is admitted. *Rohitā* is the Nyāsa’s counterexample. |
| *revatī nakṣatre* | For a nonstellar referent one analysis gives *revatā*. Another, with *matup*, gives *revatī* with ṅīp. Its accent history distinguishes it from the stellar ṅīṣ formation. |
| *kaṭāc chroṇivacane* | *Kaṭī* denotes the hip; the Nyāsa gives *kaṭā* outside the specified meaning. |
| *pippalyādayaś ca* | The Nyāsa attributes the examples that follow to the vṛttikāra. The Bālamanoramā explains the open membership of *gaurādi*. |

The word *nartakī* illustrates the other limb of 4.1.41: the occupational affix *ṣvun* bears the marker ṣ, which licenses the feminine. This differs from taking *naṭa* as a listed input. A gloss such as ‘female performer’, or the final ī by itself, cannot recover those two derivations.
