## H07. Perfect word boundaries and counts

For each primary factor, retain ṃ/ñ before kṛ, ṃ/m before
bhū and m before as. Each kṛ-P first-singular length reading
has both nasal variants. Bhī A has two primary root choices:
90 words; vi+smi A has 45; each P paradigm has 47. Thus
perfect gives **229 readings/paths on 36 keys**. Prefix vi
belongs to the primary; it does not prefix the auxiliary.

## H09. Aṭ lies before the whole copied constituent

Unprefixed bhī yields **abībhapata / abībhiṣata** in A,
and **abībhayat** in P. With vi, aṭ follows the prefix and
precedes the copy; vi+a becomes vya. The smi words begin
**vyasiṣmapata** A and **vyasiṣmayat** P. No new prefix is
assigned to the root after the copy has been built.

All 36 aorist keys have aṭ and ṇic-lopa and lack outer iṭ.
Bhī A gives eighteen words on nine keys; the other profiles
are singleton, totaling **45 readings/paths**. The eighteen smi
paths have short copy i and root ṣ, in distinct owned occurrences.
