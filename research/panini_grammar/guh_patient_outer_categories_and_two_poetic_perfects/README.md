## Two perfects with different participants

In Raghuvaṃśa 14.49 *jugūha* has Lakṣmaṇa as actor. The relative *yat* identifies what he kept from Sītā; the following clause describes the future grief that her throbbing right eye disclosed to her. Mallinātha glosses the concealing verb with *pratisaṃhṛtavān* and explains that eye as the right one. The eye is instrumental in the disclosure and is no argument of the perfect of concealing. In 18.47 Lakṣmī is the actor of *upajugūha*, the young ruler is its object and the shade of the parasol is the guise of the embrace. Mallinātha explains the reluctance to embrace him directly. The available GRETIL pāda index has a short line at 18.47a; the continuous verse also has the *niveśa* member. Its d-pāda supports the same perfect. Retain the two readings of the a-pāda as documentary evidence of the indexing discrepancy, without inferring a manuscript variant. Neither perfect is passive. Bare P 3sg is already in PG989 and the upa-embracing P 3sg in PG991. These verses add direct contexts with zero additional finite credits.
