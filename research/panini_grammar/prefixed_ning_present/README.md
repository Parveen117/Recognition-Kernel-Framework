## Derivation and reverse reading

The Kāśikā prints four of the present anchors. Siddhāntakaumudī explicitly supplies *vipucchayate* and interprets vyasana as movement in different or opposed directions. The ud and pari controls express raising the tail and swinging it around. On bhāṇḍa, sam selects collection into a heap. On cīvara, sam allows acquiring the robes and wearing them.

For the sam prefixes, 8.3.23 gives anusvāra before the consonant. Under 8.4.58–59 the optional homorganic sound at the boundary gives m before bh and ñ before c. The lexical witness uses anusvāra. Either written junction supports both robe events. The named actions, source sections and six positive or negative probes appear in [RELATION.json](RELATION.json). Empty results describe only this selected construction. They impose no general ban on a prefix and noun occurring together in another construction.

The finite lifted carrier pairs the typed cuts for prefix, nominal, action, affix, pada, ending and prefix junction. Projecting each admitted pair (action, realization) to the complete word therefore gives exactly its directly written reading set. In reverse, *sañcīvarayate* retains two possible actions. Changing from *utpucchayate* to *paripucchayate* changes the indicated action. No event is inferred from the nasal realization.
