# PG894 · Directional *pūrva* vocative panels

Monty · 25 November 2026

head “Eastern”, prints its masculine address row, states that feminine
`pūrvā` follows `sarvā`, and prints the neuter address row. The masculine
plural positively retains two source alternatives, `pūrve / pūrvāḥ`; they
are not collapsed into one policy or treated as a typo.

This adds **9 nominal positions/10 readings**, one morphology construction
at ten interfaces, one address construction at ten interfaces, four source
observations and three source-policy decisions. The selected words are
`pūrva / pūrvau / pūrve~pūrvāḥ`, `pūrve / pūrve / pūrvāḥ`, and
`pūrva / pūrve / pūrvāṇi`. Neuter singular uses ordinary `am` plus m-lopa.
