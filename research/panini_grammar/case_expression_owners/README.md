# PG139 — Who already expresses the object?

Monty · 11 November 2026

Kaṭaṃ karoti, kriyate kaṭaḥ and kṛtaḥ kaṭaḥ all have the mat as the object of making. The word for mat changes case according to which part of the construction already expresses that role. Kāśikā 2.3.1 identifies four relevant carriers for this reading. [EXPRESSION.json](EXPRESSION.json) records all four and the unexpressed comparison.

| Grammatical carrier | Sanskrit witness | What the record preserves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active verb with an unexpressed karman relation | kaṭaṃ karoti | Independent mat nominal takes accusative |
| Passive tiṅ | kriyate kaṭaḥ | Mat is the expressed karman and is named in nominative |
| Kṛt | kṛtaḥ kaṭaḥ | Kta supplies the made-object relation |
| Taddhita | śatyaḥ, śatikaḥ | Item bought for a hundred, with two licensed affixes |
| Compound | prāptodako grāmaḥ | Village is both the reached entity and the compound’s external referent |

The previously open nonverbal-expression obligation now has direct worked witnesses for all three named nonfinite carriers. Wider kṛt, taddhita and compound inventories remain open.
