# PG116 — Pada chooses the owned cli replacement

Monty · 10 November 2026

## H06. P takes aṅ, not causal caṅ

After luṅ and cli, 3.1.55 supplies aṅ in the selected P dyutādi
scope. The ṅ marker is retained in history after lopa; 1.1.5 excludes
ordinary guṇa at that interface. This is aṅ, without initial c,
without ṇic-lopa and without reduplication. PG115's causal copies
cannot be reused merely because a root name coincides.

For the nasal-upadhā, non-idit roots 6.4.24 removes n before
ṅit aṅ: sraṃs→sras, dhvaṃs→dhvas, bhraṃs→bhras,
bhraṃś→bhraś and srambh→srabh. The nominalized archive anusvāra
spellings retain their original n owner for that query. A nasal in
a root is not automatically an idit marker. Other roots retain their
short basic factors, including vṛt/vṛdh/śṛdh and dyut/śvit/mid.

## H07. P thematic secondary endings

In 3sg,3du,3pl,2sg,2du,2pl,1sg,1du,1pl order, apply the pinned
secondary thematic tuple to the aṅ-facing factor:

(at, atām, an, aḥ, atam, ata, am, āva, āma).

All have initial aṭ 6.4.71 before the unprefixed root. The aṅ
a and the augment a remain different owners. Thus adyutat, aśvitat,
amidat, asvidat/akṣvidat, asrasat, asrabhat and avṛtat extend
to complete paradigms. No outer iṭ, sic, P sic-vṛddhi or copy is
present on these P paths. First-person vowel junctions and final-n
protection reuse the exact inherited tuple.

## H08. A retains seṭ sic and the ordinary root grade

The P condition of 3.1.55 fails in A; cli→sic by 3.1.44 remains.
All selected source entries here are seṭ. Ordinary short iṭ makes
the actual interface vowel-initial, so 1.2.11's jhal-conditioned
kit assignment does not apply. Light-upadhā guṇa gives dyot, śvet,
med, sved/kṣved, roc, ghoṭ, roṭ, loṭ, loṭh, śobh, kṣobh,
tobh, vart, vardh and śardh. Nabh retains a; the nasal-upadhā
sraṃs/dhvaṃs/bhraṃs/bhraṃś/srambh retain their nasal and a.

P-only sic-vṛddhi is not available in A. Neither basic A nor the
presence of iṭ licenses a caṅ copy. No long iṭ is inferred from
root vowels or teaching markers.

Reuse the exact PG85 seṭ-sic A tuple after the graded root:

(iṣṭa, iṣātām, iṣata, iṣṭhāḥ, iṣāthām, idhvam,
iṣi, iṣvahi, iṣmahi).

Initial aṭ gives adyotiṣṭa, amediṣṭa, asvediṣṭa/akṣvediṣṭa,
asraṃsiṣṭa, asrambhiṣṭa, avartiṣṭa and the other full rows.
Sic-s takes ṣ after its iṭ; before dhvam 8.2.25 deletes it.
Every surviving pre-iṭ root here ends outside iṇ, so 8.3.79
adds no retroflex-ḍh alternative. Root-r inside vart is not the
immediately preceding sound; root-t is. All twenty A 2pl cells
retain dental dh. This is not the root-r-final car interface of PG85.

## H09. Alternative readings preserve source and affix owners

SVID's P factors svid/kṣvid and A factors sved/kṣved retain
1010845/1010846 as path source identities. BHRAMS similarly retains
sras-like nasal deletion in P but the nasal in A for its s/ś pair.
Its exact factors are P bhras/bhraś, A bhraṃs/bhraṃś.
These alternatives affect every slot, with no extra request credit.

Internal n before s/ś is written as ṃ under the selected nasal
policy; before bh it is m. This is owned local phonology, not an
additional grammatical branch. Late nasal/ending conventions remain
pinned; no free spelling variants are expanded.

The data records formation='basic', cli replacement, marker/nasal
history, iṭ and guṇa separately on each path. P/A is chosen before
those dependent operations; their outputs are not assembled from
independent menus of aṅ/sic, iṭ and root grade.
