## H01. New finite target is luṅ only

dyutādi group in luṅ. This is a genuine pada option in the same
qualified lexical/semantic frame, unlike PG115's result-owned causal
A/P distinction. The finite register retains pada as part of its request
key. The selected target has twenty lexical families, two padas and
nine slots: 360 positions. No other lakāra is newly counted here.

The domain is basic, unprefixed, unaccented kartari luṅ with the
inherited aorist context certificate. No ṇic, san, passive, Bhāva,
independent upapada or karmavyatihāra is supplied. Wrong formation or
unknown lexical/meaning evidence yields EXCLUDED or HOLD respectively.

## H03. Vṛh and the two terminal roots are not silently filled

The electronic lexical list inserts vṛh 1010852 between luṭh and
śubh, but the selected Kaumudī passage does not enumerate it there
and its record lacks the dyutādi tag. This packet does not infer group
membership merely from numeric adjacency; it remains a zero-credit
collation question.

Syand 1010865 and kṛp 1010866 are known terminal members, but their
additional iṭ and sound branches require their own complete aorist
analysis. They are explicitly outside this 360-key target. The selected
grid's closure is not a claim to close the whole dyutādi group.

## H04. Reuse existing sya/tāsi coverage without fresh credit

PG17 already writes the vṛt/vṛdh/śṛdh/syand/kṛp future relations;
PG31's counterfactual packet writes their conditionals. Pin those files
and retain their branch conditions. No new future or conditional
positions are added by citing 1.3.92–93 here. San remains a separate
formation frontier; lexical icchā or a printed future does not close it.

Current ruc luṅ is the basic-root domain of 1.3.91. PG115's ruc
causative is a different formation and result-qualified request. Do not
propagate the basic optional P through ṇic or overwrite either register.

## H05. Keep source examples and complete-table inference distinct

Kaumudī 1.3.91 and 7.3.82 give explicit P/A pairs for several
members and the shared group sequence. The twenty complete nine-slot
P and A paradigms are written extensions through the owned ending
contracts, not forty verbatim classical tables. Malformed phrase joins
and alternate-reference spellings remain literal in SOURCE_EXCERPTS.

The two accepted alternate lexical readings are identified explicitly
in H02. No optional consonant doubling, arbitrary nasal spelling,
accent, prefix or extra dictionary sense is generated from the archive.
Independent source collation and runtime grammar validation remain open.

Srambh has a lexical note preferring, or in a quoted account requiring, vi.
The selected Kaumudī field explicitly writes unprefixed asrabhat/asrambhiṣṭa.
This packet follows that explicit word scope and does not generalize the
selection to every source account of srambh.
