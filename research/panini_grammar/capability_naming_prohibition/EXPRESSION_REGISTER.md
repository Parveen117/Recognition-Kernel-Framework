## V1. Thirty-six new finite positions

Every tuple is ordered **3sg, 3du, 3pl, 2sg, 2du, 2pl, 1sg, 1du,
1pl**. Words are shown at pause; mā/mā sma are governing expressions,
not removable prefixes inside the verb. The exact qualified P inputs
and prohibition semantics of D/P travel with every entry.

| Root / lakāra | Complete nine-slot tuple |
|---|---|
| kṛ U | kārṣīt, kārṣṭām, kārṣuḥ, kārṣīḥ, kārṣṭam, kārṣṭa, kārṣam, kārṣva, kārṣma |
| hṛ U | hārṣīt, hārṣṭām, hārṣuḥ, hārṣīḥ, hārṣṭam, hārṣṭa, hārṣam, hārṣva, hārṣma |
| kṛ J | karot, kurutām, kurvan, karoḥ, kurutam, kuruta, karavam, kurva, kurma |
| hṛ J | harat, haratām, haran, haraḥ, haratam, harata, haram, harāva, harāma |

U is licensed with the addressed māṅ both without and with S. J is
licensed only with S. For example, mā bhavān kārṣīt and mā sma
karot instantiate different admitted paths, while mā sma kārṣīt
reuses the same U word construction with the retained additional context.
Kāśikā directly witnesses the kṛ/hṛ third-singular words; Kaumudī
also witnesses kārṣīḥ. The full tuples are written rule consequences,
not thirty-six separately quoted traditional examples.

The register adds **36 positions /36 selected readings**. It does not
claim a full A aorist, all luṅ formations, all laṅ roots, all prohibitive
usages or a new independently executed result.
