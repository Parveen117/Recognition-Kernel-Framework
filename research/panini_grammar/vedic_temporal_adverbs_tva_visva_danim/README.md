# PG912 · *Tvadānīm* and *viśvadā́nīm* as Vedic adverbs of time

Maitrāyaṇī Saṃhitā 4.2.2 places *tvadānīm* twice in one contrast. The analytic source reads *prati tvadānīṃ tiṣṭhati na tvadānīm*. The following *yadā … yadā* clauses explain the contrast by whether a crop prospers. The first occurrence modifies the affirmative predicate (‘at one time’). The second modifies its negation with the predicate understood (‘at another time, not’). The first has anusvāra before t, the second final m in the analytic text. Both belong to one indeclinable. They give direct contextual support for Whitney §503b’s assignment of the derivative to indefinite *tva*. No person, possessor, neuter ending or inflected second-person pronoun is required. [Fushimi, 2015; TITUS edition by Jost Gippert, 5 May 2016; GRETIL analytic MS 4.2.2](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/1_veda/1_sam/maitrs_au.htm) · [Whitney §503b](https://en.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=13777079)

Ṛgveda 6.52.5 supplies the other base. Its pada text divides *viśva-dānīm*. In continuous speech final m is represented by anusvāra before *sumanasaḥ*. The speaker wishes that **we** should be well-disposed **always**. Sāyaṇa glosses the adverb with *sarvadā*. *Viśva* therefore quantifies occasions. The following nominative plural *sumanasaḥ* and first-person plural *syāma* belong to the predication, not the adverb’s inflection. Whitney §1103c groups the two words under *dānīm* and distinguishes the accented *viśvadā́nīm* from toneless *tvadānīm*. The plain MS electronic text alone cannot decide accent. The independent adjective *viśvadāni* cited by Whitney has a separate lexical type. [Ṛgveda 6.52.5 and Sāyaṇa, revision 200265](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=200265) · [Whitney §1103c](https://en.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=13777093)
