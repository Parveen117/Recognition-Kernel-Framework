# PG260 · The cultivator in two Yajurvedic lists and the Marut predication

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā pairs **írāyai kīnā́śaṃ** at 30.11a: a dative and an accusative singular. Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa 3.4.9.1 transmits the corresponding pair. In another passage, TB 2.4.8.7 calls the Maruts the ploughmen and Indra lord of the plough. The plural noun there stands in a predication with *āsan* (‘were’).

The two occupational lists each contain ten entries. Their fourth and fifth pairs change order, but the sheep keeper stays with *vīrya* and the goat keeper with *tejas*. At the final entry the dative differs in shape: Vājasaneyi *ādhyakṣyāya*, Taittirīya *adhyakṣāya*. The accompanying table records each actual sequence and source spelling, including TITUS’s word division *téjase japālám*.

| Keeper | Vājasaneyi order | Taittirīya order | Accusative singular |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Cattle | 3 | 3 | gopālam |
| Sheep | 4 | 5 | avipālam |
| Goats | 5 | 4 | ajapālam |

The three masculine keeper nouns add **72 positions**. The ten pairs supply **20 source head occurrences** across the two lists; the Marut passage adds one. Three of these 21 head occurrences concern *kīnāśa*. They document its sense, case and a transmitted accent. The independent operation of the five-pāda affix marker remains open.
