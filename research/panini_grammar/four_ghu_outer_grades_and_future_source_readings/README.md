# PG258 · The four remaining ghu roots outside the present system

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Bhāṣya gives **praṇidātā** and **praṇidhātā** when explaining why the designation *ghu* must survive a change in the shape of a root. The pinned Kāśikā field at 1.1.20 has **prāṇidātā** under *dāṇ*. The written table follows the Bhāṣya’s *pra-* and preserves the longer reading in its own source field. No additional person–number position arises from the spelling difference.

| Third singular after *pra + ni* | *dāṇ* and *do*, active | *deṅ*, middle | *dheṭ*, active |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Periphrastic future | praṇidātā | praṇidātā | praṇidhātā |
| Simple future | praṇidāsyati | praṇidāsyate | praṇidhāsyati |
| Conditional | praṇyadāsyat | praṇyadāsyata | praṇyadhāsyat |
| Benedictive | praṇideyāt | praṇidāsīṣṭa | praṇidheyāt |

Maitreya gives **dātā** for *dāṇ* and **dhātā** for *dheṭ*. The four selected lakāras no longer exhibit *yacch*, *dy*, *day* or *dhay* from the present. In the middle of *deṅ*, the benedictive retains *ā*.

With the two class-three roots in PG254, each fixed prefix, lakāra and person now has eight root–pada requests. In all six third-person *luṭ* comparisons (two prefix pairs times three numbers) there are only two written words. The other 66 comparisons have four each. The four additional roots now account for **576 of 720** positions. This packet adds **288**.
