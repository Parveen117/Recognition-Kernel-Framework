The list has **प्रोह्यपदि**. Both vowel lengths therefore occur on one printed page, in different textual positions. The named short member has evidence in the print independently of the Vācaspatyam headword. Only the worked sentence on this page gives the elephant-driving context. The separate striking sense supplied by the dictionary stays with that source. The coexistence of the spellings gives no freely optional shortening operation.
