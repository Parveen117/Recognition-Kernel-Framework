# PG57 — चक्ष् substitution with an addressed affix domain

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

## Adopted source and input policy

The input is identified चक्षिङ्, root record 1020007, in the ordinary
व्यक्तायां वाचि use. Preserve the original root, its old ङित्/marker account,
the requested sense, register, affix prescription, and the substituted root
as distinct records. This packet selects the ख्याञ् branch described by
Kāśikā 2.4.54. The क्शा/ख्शा account is acknowledged but not silently
identified with this branch at intermediate steps.

The previously written [2.4 contracts](../compound_case_derivation/RULES_2_4_B.md)
already contain the substitution, its sense exceptions and the perfect
option. PG57 supplies the missing application to owned formations and corrects
the nine-affix classification in the companion handoff. These are new written
applications of existing rules, with zero new catalogue-ID or paradigm credit.

K = Kāśikā, N = Nyāsa, P = Padamañjarī. The selected literal fields are in
[the source archive](SOURCE_EXCERPTS.json), at edition
5744762f010d677cfb43f347a42d02796cf615d6. Other ordinary marker, ending and
sound operations use the already written rules at the same edition. The
following are unaccented stem or explicitly identified 3sg constructions;
they do not claim nine-slot paradigms or exhaustive optional branches.

## H04 — Eight आर्धधातुक kṛt targets

Each row supplies its own ordinary semantic prescription: past निष्ठा,
prospective action obligation, purposive infinitive, same-actor prior action,
or agent formation, respectively. “kṛt” alone does not supply these meanings.
Forms in the table are stem keys or indeclinables, not arbitrary declined
words. Prefixless क्त्वा is intended; a prefixed ल्यप् request is a separate
construction.

All eight start with G1–G6 and ख्या. In particular the root's old cluster
does not survive into 8.2.29. No इट् is inserted in the selected branch.

## H06 — Perfect optionality keeps two root histories

For a direct ordinary आ + चक्षिङ् + लिट् request, 3.4.115 supplies the
special आर्धधातुक designation and 2.4.55 changes the mandatory replacement
to an option. K 2.4.55 supplies these exact examples:

The outer लिट् of an already formed णिच्/सन्-ending root does not
retroactively remove that inner formation's substitution. A program-wide
`lak != "liṭ"` shortcut is not a substitute for an addressed 2.4.55 domain;
nested perfect outputs still require their own written continuation.

## C01 — A grammatical label cannot classify every member of kṛt

H04's eight prescriptions are आर्धधातुक; H07's शानच् is शित् and
सार्वधातुक. Reject the implication “kṛt implies आर्धधातुक.” The written
classification is **8 + 1**, not a blanket adjudication against all nine
oracle keys.

## C02 — Equal सन् spelling does not imply equal designation

K 3.1.7 explicitly makes its desiderative सन् आर्धधातुक and contrasts
the preceding lexical formation. K/N/P 3.4.114 use जुगुप्सते as a
counterexample. The designation query needs prescription identity, not the
letters सन् or a generic `san` boolean alone.

## C03 — The वर्जन exception retains the old consonant-final base

K 2.4.54 supplies दुर्जनाः संचक्ष्याः, meaning “to be avoided.” N
identifies ण्यत् (3.1.124). Here the admitted semantic exception blocks
ख्याञ्, so the retained consonant-final host licenses the appropriate
suffix. H05's ordinary telling construction cannot overwrite this result.

## C04 — अस्/अन and names need a witnessed exception policy

Keep नृचक्षा रक्षः and विचक्षणः पण्डितः as K 2.4.54 witnesses;
do not infer a universal ban on every suffix with the string अन.
N analyses विचक्षण through ल्युट्, whereas P uses युच् under
अनुदात्तेतश्च हलादेः. Preserve this disagreement and K's संज्ञा/छन्दस्
qualification. This packet does not resolve its accent or create a general
semantic classifier for lexical names.

## C05 — ञ् is operative even though it is not pronounced

Retaining the original root as provenance does not force its obligatory A
designation onto the substitute. K 2.4.54 expressly rejects that blanket
स्थानिवद्भाव inference. For H01 use voice, for H02 use the causative
frame, and for H03 use 1.3.62 with the substituted root's condition.
Unknown actor-benefit information is OPEN for that pada query, not evidence
that both padas are interchangeable in every sentence.

## C06 — An outer designation does not replace the inner affix record

The final present ending can be सार्वधातुक while the root's यक्,
desiderative सन् or णिच् is आर्धधातुक. Conversely, an outer शानच्
or लिट् record does not undo a substitution already performed while forming
an inner णिच्/सन् host. Preserve nested host ownership and derivational
intent; full nested paradigms remain open.

## C07 — The alternative क्शा analysis is not an intermediate synonym

K admits क्शा examples; P discusses a ख्शा analysis with optional later
श् → य् and explicit असिद्ध consequences. Different earlier queries can
therefore see different constituents even when a final ख्या spelling agrees.
PG57 selects the K ख्याञ् path. It does not project all P policy histories
onto a single `khyā` flag or claim their complete realization.

## C08 — Unknown meaning and surface agreement do not close the gate

If the input lacks the sense/register/affix authority needed by G1–G8,
record OPEN. Matching a fixed oracle string cannot supply those premises.
The written gate supports the khyā substitution in the qualified three
verbal formations and H04's eight kṛt targets; it does not certify 152 or
153 executed cells, every branch, or the overall Sanskrit grammar.
