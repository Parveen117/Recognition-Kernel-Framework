# What remains in the Pāṇini grammar after PG176

Monty · 11 November 2026

A **finite position** is a selected verb request with a particular person and number. It may have several licensed readings. A **Uṇādi address** identifies a separately numbered supplementary sūtra. The two measures serve different purposes.

| Written layer | Established at this point | Work to continue |
