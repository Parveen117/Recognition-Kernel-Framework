the teaching i/r disappear. **3.1.57 irito vā** permits cli→aṅ in

## H04. Three kṣṇu 2pl alternatives and two coincident ṇal paths

Apply the pinned PG20 interpretation of **8.3.78–79**. Kṣṇu's
blessing/perfect/aorist 2pl iṭ follows an iṇ sound (the constructed
root-final v), so each has **dh and ḍh** alternatives under 8.3.79.
Its sīyuṭ ī is not a long iṭ. Yuj's corresponding pre-iṭ constituent
ends in j, or its aniṭ contact ends in a velar; neither is iṇ.
Consequently every yuj 2pl retains dental dh. A k/ku ṣatva trigger
is not thereby an iṇ trigger for dh-retroflexion.

For the two P perfect 1sg keys, **7.1.91** retains the ṇit-work
choice. Neither 7.2.115's final-ik nor 7.2.116's a-upadhā vṛddhi
condition applies to yuj. Both paths therefore have the same guṇa
word **yuyoja** before prefixing. Following the pinned PG71 convention,
record two histories but only one written reading per such key.
No other slot receives that option.
