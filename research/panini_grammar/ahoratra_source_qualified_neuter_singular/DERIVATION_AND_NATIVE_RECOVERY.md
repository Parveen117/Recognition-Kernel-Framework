# PG889 · Derivation and native recovery

## Positive source decision

The Mahābhārata witness reads `ahorātram idam dvandvam`. The two agreeing
neuters `idam` and `dvandvam` select `ahorātram` as nominative neuter singular,
not the homophonous masculine accusative or indeclinable adverb. Ṛgveda
10.190.2 gives accented `ahorātrāṇi` as the object of `vidadhat`; this confirms
the independently inherited neuter plural and its Vedic register without
licensing unquoted Vedic endings. These are positive source decisions, not a
meaning guessed from the compound.

PG448 already owns the source-qualified neuter `ahan+rātri→ahorātra` compound,
its day-night-period sense, 2.4.28 register distinction and selected common-
*sup* dual/plural fibre. PG889 adds only the missing singular fibre. The
nominative and accusative share `ahorātram` but retain different original
endings and roles. Vocative `ahorātra` follows `su→am` under 7.1.24 followed by
the address-conditioned m-lopa already reviewed for ordinary neuter *a*-stems.
The ablative has the separately counted pause/continuation readings
`ahorātrāt / ahorātrād`. Root, preverb, verbal pada, voice and verbal affix are
null.
