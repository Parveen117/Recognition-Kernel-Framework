## Decisions requiring fuller commentary

**3.3.49, restricted backward optionality.** Kāśikā accounts for समुच्छ्रय
by taking the next rule's विभाषा backward. Nyāsa explicitly asks why this
does not make यु/पू/द्रु optional too and answers व्यवस्थितविभाषा. The
contract therefore preserves the reported श्रि alternative without extending
free optionality to every root. Kaumudī explains the witness by बाहुलक instead.
Both explanations are recorded; a common surface witness does not merge their
source policies. Kāśikā's उद्द्राव and Kaumudī's उद्राव spellings remain literal
in their respective archived fields.

**3.3.65, three admissions.** Kāśikā, Kaumudī and Nyāsa support unprefixed
क्वण्, नि + क्वण्, and the वीणा domain with other prefixes such as प्र.
The वीणा gate expands the prefixed domain; it is not an additional necessary
condition on the first two clauses. प्रक्वण/प्रक्वाण is a qualified example.

**3.3.70, lexical base policy.** Kāśikā and Nyāsa derive ग्लह from ग्रह्:
अप् already follows from 58, and this rule supplies लत्व. Kaumudī specifies
the stake in dice play. Kāśikā also reports an alternative separate ग्लह्
root analysis and its ग्लाह control. The adopted row uses ग्रह् and retains
the alternative as a distinct source policy.

**3.3.76, च retains घञ्.** The अप् + वध clause does not erase घात. Kāśikā
and Kaumudī expressly preserve घञ् by च. Prefixlessness, भाव and the stated
accent belong to the वध clause, not to every हन् derivative.

**3.3.87, equal height and girth.** The compact निमित explanation could be
misread as mere measurement. Nyāsa explicitly says the referents are alike
in height and girth. The contract uses that equality condition. It does not
infer the predicate from a name or permit every measurable object.

**3.3.88 and control 4.4.20, dependent suffix and marker spelling.** Kāśikā
states the inherited भाव/non-agent authority at 88; Kaumudī describes actual
use as भाव एव स्वभावात्. The instantiated contract adopts that qualified
भाव use, while preserving the broader inherited wording as source evidence.
No bare independent क्त्रि output is licensed. The pinned catalogue heading
for 4.4.20 reads **क्त्रेर्मम् नित्यम्**, but the full Kāśikā, Nyāsa and Vasu
fields consistently identify the following affix as **मप्**. The contract uses
मप् with that explicit commentary support. No source text is silently repaired;
the heading/body discrepancy remains recorded. Vasu's description as an
invariable augment does not override the native affix ownership here.

**3.3.96–98, accent and register.** Kāśikā, Kaumudī and Nyāsa preserve the
उदात्त condition and the special admission of क्तिन् from इष् against इच्छा
(101). 97 explicitly drops the मन्त्र restriction while retaining उदात्त;
98 retains उदात्त क्यप् in feminine भाव. The literal accent marks and root
histories are archived. Unaccented sample spellings are not presented as
independent accent verification. Alternative साति and हेति root analyses
retain distinct histories even where the surface is identical.
