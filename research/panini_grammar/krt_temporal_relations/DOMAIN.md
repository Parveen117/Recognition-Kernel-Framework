## D1. Retained input

iota, pairing, dagger, complementary channels, tensor types and ordered history.
The [native continuation](NATIVE_CONTINUATION.md) instantiates the inherited
adapter. An input carries the following addressed evidence.

|Component|Required distinctions|
|---|---|
|Source|Immutable edition, adopted commentary policy, register, reported alternative and unresolved source damage|
|Lexeme|Root identity and homonym, prefix, marker, semantic use, conventional name, supplied gaṇa membership|
|Upapada|Its role, case relation, exact lexical member, bound versus unbound status, correspondence to the intended action|
|Meaning|Actor, action, instrument or other non-agent relation; disposition, practice, proficiency, purpose, naming or concrete sense|
|Time|Action interval, speech/reference frame, recollection, remoteness, relation between two actions; no physical clock is inferred|
|Affix history|Direct prescription versus replacement of a particular lakāra; marker identity, original token and downstream consumers|
|Dependency|Shared actor, earlier action, comparison action, repeated or aggregated action, accompanying finite expression|
|Observer|Current grammatical query, source view, accent/pada consumer, allowed exception and remaining obligations|

Use T/F/? cuts for every decisive predicate. Unknown purpose, naming status,
actor identity or lexical membership remains Open. A residual or optional
branch requires its own admission and resolved exclusions. Source examples
supply bounded witnesses, not a recognizer for unrestricted natural language.
