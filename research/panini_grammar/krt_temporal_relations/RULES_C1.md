# PG44 — Scoped घञ् selection, 3.3.23–47

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Each row inherits only the qualified भाव/non-agent कारक authority of 3.3.18–19.
Semantic, prefix and lexical gates are supplied evidence; unknown gates stay Open.

|3.3.23|सम् with three roots|सम् addresses यु, द्रु or दु in the inherited nominalization domain|Select घञ्|No extension to every prefix or superficially similar root|K: संयावः, संद्रावः, संदावः; excluded प्रयवः|
|3.3.24|Unprefixed श्रि/नी/भू|The root has no उपसर्ग in the addressed derivation|Select घञ् instead of the applicable अच्/अप्|प्रभाव can instead be a later प्रादि compound of भाव; नय is separately admitted through 113's बहुल policy|K: श्रायः, नायः, भावः; प्रश्रयः, प्रणयः, प्रभवः|
|3.3.25|वि with क्षु/श्रु|वि is the उपपद of one of the two roots|Select घञ् over अप्|The unprefixed root retains its ordinary suffix route|K: विक्षावः, विश्रावः; क्षवः, श्रवः|
|3.3.26|अव/उद् with नी|Either named prefix addresses नयति|Select घञ्|उन्नय in the reported sense is a separate 113 बहुल admission, not automatic free alternation in every context|K: अवनायः, उन्नायः; उन्नयः पदार्थानाम्|
|3.3.27|प्र with द्रु/स्तु/स्रु|प्र addresses the selected root in the inherited domain|Select घञ् over the ordinary alternative|The three roots are a list; unprefixed forms are controls|K: प्रद्रावः, प्रस्तावः, प्रस्रावः; द्रवः, स्तवः, स्रवः|
|3.3.28|Ordered prefix–root pairs|निर् with पूङ्/पूञ्, or अभि with लूञ्|Select घञ् in the corresponding pair|यथासंख्य links निर् to पू and अभि to लू; it does not admit their Cartesian product|K: निष्पावः, अभिलावः; पवः, लवः|
|3.3.29|उद्/नि with गॄ|उद् or नि addresses the admitted sound/swallowing root identity|Select घञ्|Both named root entries are admitted; no general replacement of unprefixed गर|K: उद्गारः समुद्रस्य; निगारो देवदत्तस्य; गरः|
|3.3.30|Grain scattering|उद्/नि + कॄ in the scattering sense, with grain as its object domain|Select घञ्|The hurting homonym is excluded; grain qualification does not extend to flowers or alms|K: उत्कारो धान्यस्य, निकारो धान्यस्य; भैक्ष्योत्करः, पुष्पनिकरः|
|3.3.31|Sacrificial संस्ताव|सम् + स्तु denotes the specified sacrifice-related locus/action|Select घञ्|यज्ञ is required; ordinary acquaintance or praise between students has another route|K: संस्तावश्छन्दोगानाम्; संस्तवश्छात्रयोः|
|3.3.32|Non-sacrificial प्र + स्तृ|स्तृञ् आच्छादने with प्र, outside sacrifice usage|Select घञ्|The अयज्ञ exclusion is decisive; the sacrificial बर्हिष् formation remains distinct|K: शङ्खप्रस्तारः; बर्हिष्प्रस्तरः|
|3.3.33|Extent of a nonverbal object|वि + स्तृञ् denotes breadth/extension and that extension is not of speech|Select घञ्|Both प्रथन and अशब्द are required; an arrangement of grass and prolix speech are separate readings|K: पटस्य विस्तारः; तृणविस्तरः, विस्तरो वचसाम्|
|3.3.34|Name of a metre|वि + स्तृ is a constituent in the conventional name of a metrical form|Select घञ्|छन्दस् here means metre, not the whole Vedic register; the complete compound, not necessarily its घञ् constituent alone, is the name|K: विष्टारपङ्क्तिः, विष्टारबृहती|
|3.3.35|उद् with ग्रह्|उद् addresses ग्रह् in the inherited nominalization domain|Select घञ् over अप्|The Vedic नि extension and ह→भ realization belong to the reported ladle-raising/lowering clause, not every नि + ग्रह्|K: उद्ग्राहः; Vedic उद्ग्राभम्, निग्राभम्|
|3.3.36|Fist grip|सम् + ग्रह् denotes the firm finger arrangement called मुष्टि|Select घञ्|A collection of grain lacks the fist gate|K: मल्लस्य संग्राहः; संग्रहो धान्यस्य|
|3.3.37|Two ordered semantic pairs|परि + नी in dice play, or नि + इण् in orderly performance without deviation|Select घञ् in its addressed pair|Both prefix/root and meaning assignments are ordered; neither meaning spreads to the other root|K: परिणायेन शारान् हन्ति; एषोऽत्र न्यायः; परिणयः, न्ययम्|
|3.3.38|An allotted turn|परि + इण् denotes keeping the sequence of a due turn, अनुपात्यय|Select घञ्|Passing beyond a time is a different meaning and does not acquire this suffix|K/SK: तव पर्यायः; कालस्य पर्ययः|
|3.3.39|A turn to lie or attend|वि/उप + शी denotes an assigned turn|Select घञ्|The पर्याय condition is semantic; ordinary lying/attendance is outside it|K: तव विशायः, तव राजोपशायः; विशयः, उपशयः|
|3.3.40|Reachable hand picking|चि denotes taking an object close enough for hand collection, without theft|Select घञ्|हस्तादान implies reachability; theft and remote tree-top collection fail admission; reported उच्चय prohibition is retained|K: पुष्पप्रचायः, फलप्रचायः; वृक्षशिखरे फलप्रचयम्|
|3.3.41|Four चि meanings|चि denotes a dwelling locus, a built चिति, a body, or heaping as उपसमाधान|Couple घञ् with initial च→क|Mere numerical abundance is not heaping in this sense; the change is owned by this clause|K: निकायः, आकायम्, कायः, गोमयनिकायः; काष्ठनिचयः|
|3.3.42|A community of living beings|चि denotes a संघ united by a common property, without physical superposition|Couple घञ् with initial च→क|औत्तराधर्य groups and nonliving aggregations are excluded; not every collection becomes निकाय|K: भिक्षुकनिकायः, वैयाकरणनिकायः; सूकरनिचयः, प्रमाणसमुच्चयः|
|3.3.43|Feminine reciprocal action|A feminine भाव reading denotes participants doing the action mutually|Select णच् with its marker identity retained|5.4.14 owns the ensuing स्वार्थिक अञ्; reported बहुल inclusions/exclusions are lexical, not permission for every reciprocal verb|K: व्यावक्रोशी, व्यावहासी; व्यतीक्षा/व्यतीहा excluded, व्यात्युक्षी admitted|
|3.3.44|Pervasive action|भाव is related exhaustively to the action/quality in the अभिविधि reading|Select इनुण्|Repeated भाव excludes generic घञ् co-selection; reported ल्युट् coexistence is separately licensed by 113 बहुल|K: सांकूटिनम्, सांराविणम्, सान्द्राविणम्; संकूटनम्|
|3.3.45|Imprecatory अव/नि + ग्रह्|The expression is an आक्रोश in the sense of a curse|Select घञ्|The inherited suffix is घञ्, not the immediately preceding इनुण्; ordinary word separation or restraining a thief is excluded|K: अवग्राहः/निग्राहः in a curse; अवग्रहः पदस्य, निग्रहश्चोरस्य|
|3.3.46|Seeking acquisition|प्र + ग्रह् is used with लिप्सा, a wish to obtain the stated object|Select घञ्|Mere taking without the acquisition-intent condition does not obtain this special formation|K: पात्रप्रग्राहेण भिक्षुः, स्रुवप्रग्राहेण द्विजः; प्रग्रहः|
|3.3.47|Sacrificial परि + ग्रह्|The derivative denotes the sacrifice-related object/action|Select घञ्|यज्ञ qualifies the derivative's referent, not the mere presence of a ritual speaker|K: उत्तरपरिग्राहः; परिग्रहो देवदत्तस्य|
