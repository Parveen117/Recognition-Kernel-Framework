## Meaning and affix scope

**3.3.99:** the narrower भाव restriction of 95 stops; the older भाव/non-actor
kāraka frame remains, and conventional naming chooses the actual meaning.
Kāśikā and Kaumudī show मन्या; the Nyāsa field instead prints मत्या with a
तुक् explanation. Preserve both literals; the authored main witness avoids
choosing between these lexical displays. Nyāsa also explains why समज्या retains
अज् instead of changing it to वी. Naming is evidence, not a string heuristic.

**3.3.100 and 107:** the split reading gives क्रिया/कृत्या/कृतिः, not just two
suffixes. For 107, the exception within the feminine section does not prohibit
ण्यत् from another authority. Distinguish क्र्यादि श्रन्थ and the separately
covered ण्यन्त चुरादि root; non-desiderative इष् and परि retain their own clauses.

**3.3.111–113:** optionality reaches ण्वुच् at 111, and stops at the negative
curse rule 112. The ण्वुच्/ण्वुल् difference carries accent information even
where the visible form looks alike. Nyāsa on 113 explicitly explains why
semantic बहुल extensions do not make ordinary role prescriptions redundant:
the ordinary distribution remains primary, exceptional roles are limited.
The masculine instrument/locus clauses 117 onward are not restricted to
115's neuter भाव frame.

**3.3.116:** the additional contribution is obligatory compounding, since

**3.3.130:** Kāśikā prints an awkward repetition of गत्यर्थेभ्यः after

explicitly says roots *other than* motion roots; Nyāsa confirms the extension
and separately explains the five prose roots. Adopt that bounded interpretation
