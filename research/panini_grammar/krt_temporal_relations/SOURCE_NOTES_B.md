## Habit, duty, skill and competition

3.2.134 is an authority clause, extending through **3.2.177 inclusively**.
Its three actor qualifications are distinct: natural disposition without
regard to fruit, customary duty, and skilled performance. They are not one
universal frequency threshold. The subsequent 178 explicitly extends
habitual क्विप् to attested further formations; it does not make the endpoint
of 134 indefinite.

Kāśikā 146 and 150 derive restrictions on ordinary and mutual ताच्छीलिक
वासरूप coexistence. **153 explicitly calls the restriction प्रायिक** and
retains कम्रा/कमना and कम्प्रा/कम्पना. Kaumudī confirms this qualification.
Therefore no unconditional “special habitual suffix suppresses every other
affix” scheduler is adopted. मधुसूदन is separately explained by a qualified
exception, नन्द्यादि membership or ल्युट् under 3.3.113 in Kāśikā; Kaumudī
selects the नन्द्यादि account. These are alternative derivational histories,
not a general repeal of the सूद् exclusion.

## 3.2.139: marker identity is a source policy

The adopted local policy follows Kāśikā 3.2.139 and Kaumudī: क्स्नु is
**गित्, not कित्**. In that account स्था does not undergo the ई substitution
of 6.4.66. The grade prohibition of 1.1.5 and the relevant iṭ prohibition
are explained through their special inclusion of ग्. Kāśikā 1.1.5 supports
that inclusion; the source says the Vedic दंश् extension retains its nasal.

This is **not unanimous even within the pinned commentary collection**.
Nyāsa 3.2.139 expressly reports Vāmana's disagreement and says a कित्
analysis needs a separate account for preserving the nasal in दङ्क्ष्णवः.
Kāśikā 7.2.11 reports the ग्-inclusion account but then declines its necessity,
using an alternative segmentation at 3.2.139 for स्थास्नु. Accordingly the
row declares its chosen policy. It does not combine कित् with the effects
of the गित् account or claim that the source disagreement is closed.

Nyāsa's damaged strings, including its opening quoted marker rendering, stay
literal in the source archive. The explicit surrounding argument determines
the adopted analysis, supported independently by the Kaumudī field. No
manuscript or edition repair is claimed.

## Root identities and dependent operations

Nyāsa 3.2.157 resolves the compressed list: इण् is a member, and प्रसू is
**षू प्रेरणे**, not either birth-denoting homonym. Its note on the later
इण्/जि क्वरप् rule is retained as a competition dependency. The prefix and
root list is not reconstructed from its examples alone.

3.2.168 requires the **सन् affix**, not the homonymous root. Its आशंस् is
desire-denoting, not praise-denoting शंस्. 170 admits the three actual
क्यच्/क्यङ्/क्यष् origins, and 171's कि/किन् merely receive specified
लिट्-like behavior; they are not finite लिट् endings.

3.2.186 has exactly two ordered clauses: **ऋषि–करण** and **देवता–कर्तृ**.
Crossing these lists would wrongly manufacture two further clauses. Likewise,
169 pairs विद् with नुम् and इष् with छ; 161/162 distinguish कर्मकर्तृ
witnesses from arbitrary external actors. Role and source history survive
surface projection.

3.2.188 does not assign present time to every listed witness: the commentary
expressly assigns **कष्ट to future**, then अमृत and other named witnesses
to present. These are admitted lexical exceptions, not a free temporal
reinterpretation of all क्त forms.

## Future clauses and nominal action

3.3.1 supplies a bounded interface to attested उणादि formations. बहुलम्
and inferred conventional forms are source qualifications; neither amounts
to full construction of the separate Uṇādi corpus. 3.3.2's past extension
also remains tied to observed usage.

3.3.6 requires solicitation in addition to the delimited किम् form family.
7 licenses encouragement by a prospective benefit without requiring किम्.
8/9 select the future action serving as a sign for a command, not the
command itself. The source term ऊर्ध्वमौहूर्तिक remains a supplied temporal
condition; no experimental or physical clock calibration is asserted.

**3.3.14 is व्यवस्थित विभाषा.** Kāśikā, Kaumudī and Nyāsa agree on
obligatory participial forms in the specified non-nominative, vocative and
action-sign/cause contexts, with the relevant nominative option retained.
Kaumudī/Nyāsa also name affix/following-member contexts. Nyāsa explicitly
excludes the **past recollective लृट् of 3.2.112**, since the current authority
is future. A spelling-based “all लृट् becomes participle optionally” rule
would lose both distinctions.

3.3.11 re-admits only the applicable भाववचन affixes under their original
root/sense qualifications. 12 jointly requires object and purpose-action
