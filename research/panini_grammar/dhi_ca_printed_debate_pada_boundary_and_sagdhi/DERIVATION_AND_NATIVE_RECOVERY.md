## The external boundary has two licensed explanations

Nyāsa specifies an internal domain for a broad interpretation of the s recipient. The condition and its target must be in the same pada. At the distinct padas *payas* and *dhāvati*, the projected s retained for contact is the nominal's s. It is never the root s of *cakās* or the vikaraṇa s of siC. It receives ru under 8.2.66. This is the named separate-word control, with a positive derivation and with the same-pada selection false for the broadened lopa. Absent boundary evidence would leave it Open.

Haradatta instead gives a rule-relative argument for keeping this output. In his Padamañjarī on 8.2.25, the ru operation depends on one pada and is antaraṅga. The proposed cross-pada s-lopa depends on both padas and is bahiraṅga. In this addressed account the latter does not feed the view on which u is assigned. The resulting letter query does not reinstate the displaced s via sthānivad, so the path also reaches *payo dhāvati*. Keep these two reasons separate in the history. Neither licenses a general always-skip strategy for external sandhi or a cross-policy rewrite of the existing luṭ families.
