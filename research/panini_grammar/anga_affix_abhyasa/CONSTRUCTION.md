# PG40 — Construction from addressed suffix and copy histories

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Use [Domain](DOMAIN.md) and the [register](RULE_REGISTER.json). This is a
written selection-and-event relation, instantiated in the inherited
[native carrier](NATIVE_CONTINUATION.md), not a runtime grammar.

## C1. Admission, owner and branch

For each row retain the admitted input, source policy, decisive gate
evidence, selected clause and addressed event list. Replacement updates an
active token while preserving its origin; deletion makes a token inactive
without erasing it. An augment has an owner and insertion position.
Transfer imports only named operations with their conditions. Copying
creates a new token linked to the original, not a second unrelated root.

The clause set is dependent on the admitted input. Optional operations in
one clause are not independent booleans. Rejected and Open alternatives
remain separately recorded. Equal visible words can have different legal
histories, and different source schools are not grammatical free choices.

## C2. iṭ is an admission decision before it is a vowel

Start with the selected suffix and its original markers. The 7.2.35
वल्-initial ārdhadhātuka grant is constrained by the root, affix and
semantic restrictions written before and after it. 7.2.10 queries original
one-vowel/anudātta instruction, not a later surface accent. 7.2.15 carries
the *qualification* of an optional-iṭ rule into निष्ठा; it does not make
all homonymous विद् roots anit.

|Boundary|Admitted written continuation|Blocked inference|

|6.4.52|Set निष्ठा first admitted, then qualified णि loss|Deleting णि first to manufacture anit status|
|6.4.62|Selected चिण्वत् branch owns specified aṅga operations and suffix इट्|Copying every चिण् operation, including unrelated वध्/गा substitutions|
|7.2.37|Ordinary ग्रह् इट् lengthens outside लिट्|Lengthening चिण्वत्-origin इट् in ग्राहिता|
|7.2.38–42|वृ/ॠ set branch may lengthen; लिङ् blocks length|Choosing absence, short इ and long ई independently of admitted pada/lakāra|
|7.2.58–60|Current same-word middle relation and its origin retained|Classifying every detached kṛt by the visible ending of its source example|
|7.3.93/96|Ending ईट् with its own scope|Treating it as ordinary ārdhadhātuka इट्|

For तितीर्षति / तितरिषति / तितरीषति, the first branch lacks इट्; the
second and third share its admission but differ in qualified lengthening.
For लिङ् the length branch is absent. The forms here are source witnesses,
not additional completed paradigm positions.

## C3. Query-relative visibility and completion of an operation

An आभीय query records its rule, operation event, both आश्रय addresses,
domain and explicit exception. Same-आश्रय suppression is a view, not a
rollback. वस् सम्प्रसारण at 6.4.131 can be visible to आ loss because its
case trigger differs. वुक्/युट् have named visibility for उवङ्/यण्.
Outside this domain, do not assume the same invisibility automatically.

Some source passages explicitly prohibit a displaced rule from recurring.
Record the prior conflict and completed event: 7.1.50 जस् with असुक् does
not reopen सर्वनाम शी; 7.1.77 Vedic ई does not reopen displaced नुम्;
7.2.101 जरस् does not revive a सुप्-luk already displaced by अम्; 7.3.78
पिब does not reopen unwanted गुण. These are addressed conflict decisions,
not a blanket rule that nothing may ever operate twice.

## C5. Grade and nasal ownership

नुम् at 7.1.58 is inserted into the root at instruction time; at 59 it
depends on श, and at 70–73 on nominal designation/case. नुट् at 7.1.54
belongs to genitive आम्, whereas 7.4.71 inserts नुट् into the stem following
the lengthened perfect copy. These operations cannot be selected just
because an output contains न्.

At 7.1.65, nasal admission precedes suffix selection, changing the eligible
यत्/ण्यत् route and therefore accent. At 7.1.68 only actual extra उपसर्ग
changes the block: कर्मप्रवचनीय अति does not do so. At 7.3.83 the two
independent sources of ङित्त्व must remain separate; overriding one does
not override the other.

Compound grade similarly addresses members. 7.3.25 makes first-member
grade mandatory and final-member grade optional; 26 reverses which member
is optional. At 31 the first-versus-final alternatives are exclusive.
7.3.22/23 read सदस्य identity before coalescence. No merged surface string
can reliably reconstruct those choices.

## C6. Copy processing and restricted transfer

The selected 6.1 copy unit and its triggering suffix are prerequisites.
7.4.1 shortens the relevant णि-stem उपधा before copying; 2 preserves
अग्-loss, शास् and ऋदित् exclusions. Later copy reduction retains an
initial consonant, or the specifically admitted खय् after शर्, then
performs applicable copy-vowel/consonant operations. सम्प्रसारण can precede
the default consonant deletion. Root change and copy change have different
addresses, even when both affect the same phoneme.

The 7.4.54–57 desiderative clauses require an actual स्-initial सन्.
For ज्ञपि, णि deletion precedes the remaining vowel's ई replacement.
7.4.58 deletes the whole copy only on the admitted special route; ordinary
set सन् retains it. The no-copy मोक्षते branch is coupled to गुण, while
मुमुक्षते keeps its copy. A सन्वत् label at 7.4.93 does not transfer this
deletion to अदीदपत्. That control is archived without new PG40 credit.

Fixed Vedic 7.4.74 has three linked properties—parasmaipada, वुक् and
copy अ. It is not three independently selectable exceptions. The earlier
18-form register at 65 likewise contains distinct operations per lexical
witness. No executable Sanskrit generator or all-form expansion is claimed.
