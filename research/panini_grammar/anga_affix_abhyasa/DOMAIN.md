## D1. Required input

Retain root identity with class, sense, original accent and markers;
original/current sounds; compound tree and member addresses; feminine
affix origin and same-sense masculine usage; referential gender/number and
external agreement separately; suffix identity, markers and original case;
vikaraṇa, lakāra, pada and cause of pada selection; iṭ admission and origin;
register, textual tradition, source policy and lexical-list evidence.

Copy operations additionally require a selected 6.1 copy unit, its source
address, अभ्यास versus अभ्यस्त designation, and the event that caused the
copy. Current spelling alone does not supply this information.

For every decisive input distinguish **True, False and Open**. A missing
root class, lexical membership, semantic relation or source-policy choice
stays Open. A negative condition is not satisfied merely by missing data.
Only an admitted row and selected dependent branch permit a legal event.
