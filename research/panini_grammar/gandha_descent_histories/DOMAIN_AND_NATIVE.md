## D2. The enabling dependency and the occurrence distinction

On the uncontracted stage after D's marker deletion, the last vowel pair
is base-इ followed by D-अ. The penultimate इ is short before a vowel.
After the S9 deletion, the last two syllabic parts are गन्–ध: the
vowel before न्+ध् is heavy. With the other supplied 4.1.78 conditions
fixed, the weight predicate is false on the first view and true on the
prepared view. S10's initial-vowel change does not alter that local ending.
Thus the chosen history requires S9 before the admitted S12 query.

Separately, S4 and S5 act on different गन्ध occurrences. The first
deletes G1; the second changes G2's terminal vowel. Retaining only a
dictionary entry keyed by the spelling गन्ध would identify their targets
and cannot encode these two required operations on their respective
occurrences. The supplied compound tree and occurrence addresses distinguish
them. Neither observation proves a universal grammar scheduler or parser.

\[
B_{53}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le53}\rangle,
\qquad
M_{53,\lambda}=(M_{52,\lambda}\otimes_{B_{52}}B_{53})
\oplus\bigoplus_{h\in H_{53}}e_hB_{53}.

nondegeneracy hypotheses, extend the old pairing by scalars, and use the

retracts the algebra inclusion. A source-qualified event on its declared
domain acts as \(U_e(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a\); failed/open queries retain

lineage and source-policy predicates are evaluated on the supplied history

\[

## N2. Export and limits

The export map identifies current Y with PG51/52's supplied ष्यङ् owner
and F with their supplied चाप् owner. It preserves the upstream history
as part of the record and is coefficient-independent, so it also commutes
with π₀. Each downstream outer-compound query must still satisfy its own
domain. No downstream path is newly counted or executed here.

The input words' root-level lexical derivations, accent, full nominal
inflection, general lineage/semantic inference, general parsing, source-policy
arbitration, confluence/termination and runtime integration remain open.
The source's प्रत्यय policy and its wider examples need separate histories.
No universal hallucination-free AI or physical gate follows from this bounded
construction. The canonical operator engine remains unchanged.
