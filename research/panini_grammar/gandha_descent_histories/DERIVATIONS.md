# PG53 — From the उपमान compound to the qualified feminine stem

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

PG51/52 supplied कारीषगन्ध्या or कौमुदगन्ध्या with their qualified
suffix provenance. This packet constructs that preparation from the supplied
nominal stems **करीष / कुमुद** and **गन्ध**, their meanings and the
source's admitted descent relation. It does not derive those lexical stems
from verbal roots or produce their full case paradigms.

The two histories instantiate one shared chain. Keep two occurrences of
गन्ध distinct: G1 belongs to the comparison's inner first member; G2
belongs to the outer बहुव्रीहि. Keep the ancestral referent, descendant,
female request, internal case owners and each affix version separately
addressed. D is the original अण् owner, Y its ष्यङ् replacement, and
F the later चाप्. D is historical after replacement, not a second active
affix alongside Y.

for every later sound rule after replacement. The selected sequence is a
written dependency construction, not a universal scheduler or proof that
all alternate legal schedules agree.

## C01 — The two गन्ध occurrences have different fates

S4 removes G1 in the first member. S5 changes G2's terminal vowel. If
the two identically spelled occurrences are collapsed to one address, the
derivation cannot both delete the prescribed first occurrence and retain
the second target. “Delete गन्ध” without an occurrence/node address is
not an adequate instruction. This construction selects the source's lopa
branch; the unshortened compound branch is not developed here.

## C02 — Not every गन्ध compound takes इ

The input to 5.4.137 must be the specified उपमान बहुव्रीहि. An
ordinary inner genitive smell-expression at S2 has not yet met that domain.
The separate prescriptions for special prefixes at 5.4.135 and small-quantity
meaning at 5.4.136 are not blanket licenses for arbitrary गन्ध strings.
Missing comparison semantics is OPEN; a resolved different construction
blocks this particular query.

## C03 — Initial वृद्धि has a fixed address

The governing operation is 7.2.117, the first vowel of the तद्धित base.
H01 changes initial अ, H02 initial उ. The other vowels and D's own
अ are not alternative targets. Retain the णित् record through marker
deletion; erasing a marker's pronunciation does not erase its designated
grammatical feature. S10 takes place before D is replaced.

## C04 — The weight guard must see the prepared formation

Before S9, the uncontracted इ+D-अ ending has short penultimate इ
before a vowel. After S9, the current ending has गन्–ध, with the
preceding न्+ध् cluster. Re-evaluate the 4.1.78 condition on that
prepared formation. Do not infer admission from the older long ई in
करीष, from the later vowel growth, or from the spelling of the desired
final word. S9 supplies an enabling dependency, not a universal permission
to apply every later rule early.

## C05 — Descent, गोत्र and ऋषि status remain separate premises

The source's 4.1.78 exclusions include ऋषि descendants, an unsuitable
penultimate syllable, a suffix other than अण्/इञ्, and a non-गोत्र
meaning such as the cited birth-location example. The smell comparison
does not prove any lineage fact. Unknown lineage/status remains OPEN;
a resolved failed premise blocks this ष्यङ् query. अञ् or another
similar-looking suffix is not silently treated as अण्.

## C06 — आदेश and प्रत्यय are distinct source policies

These histories adopt Kāśikā's ष्यङादेश reading and the supporting
Nyāsa account at 4.1.78. Padamañjarī also discusses the प्रत्यय
analysis and attributes its development to Vāmana, including arguments about
औदमेघेय and हास्तिशीर्ष्य. PG53 preserves that discussion without
claiming to resolve it globally. The D→Y replacement history cannot be
silently relabelled as “D followed by a separate Y.” An alternate policy
requires its own admitted path and comparison, not a duplicate count here.

## C07 — The two final suffixes and their vowel contributors survive

After marker deletion Y is य, not the isolated consonant य्. F is
the separate चाप् with residue आ. S15 combines Y's अ with F's आ,
leaving a current long आ and both contributors. The later 6.1.13 target
is Y's य् at an appropriate outer compound node, not F or the whole
word. Exporting just the surface ध्या would lose this necessary evidence.

## C08 — Exporting an input does not rerun downstream work

S16 discharges the previously supplied lexical/descent/feminine preparation
for these two admitted input records. PG51's exact outer-node query and
PG52's principal/subordinate host conditions still apply separately. Their
histories and proofs are reused by pinned reference, not recounted as new
PG53 results. Complete nominal paradigms, accent and general semantic parsing
remain outside this packet; no additional finite-position credit is taken.

## Literal-source and reconstruction boundary

The output words are witnessed in the pinned sources. The shared table
assembles explicit rules and the 6.1.13 appended exposition into one admitted
dependency path; it is not a claim that every intermediate row appears
verbatim in one commentary. The गन्ध deletion selects the stated compound
branch. The आदेश policy is explicit, and the ष्यङ् weight test is
checked after the enabling deletion. Literal source fields retain all spelling
and reference defects; their interpretations are not silently normalized.
