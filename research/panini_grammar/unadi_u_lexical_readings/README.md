### Which condition is inherited?

In 1.8 *aṇu* occurs in the ordinary “minute particle” comparison. Rule 1.9 admits the *aṇ* formation in the meaning of grain and gives the suffix an *n*-marker. Under 6.1.197 that marker selects initial udātta. The Kaumudī entry at 1.9 specifies exactly how far the marker carries, **through 1.18 inclusive**. In 1.13 *iṣu* ‘arrow’ retains this inherited status alongside the locally prescribed *k*-marker. In 1.18 it still applies. At 1.19 that inheritance ends. The grain restriction applies to the 1.9 lexical request. Carrying it through the following rows would exclude their own examples of an arrow, a rope and a spindle. The suffix’s printed *u* cannot decide either semantic admission or accent history by itself.
