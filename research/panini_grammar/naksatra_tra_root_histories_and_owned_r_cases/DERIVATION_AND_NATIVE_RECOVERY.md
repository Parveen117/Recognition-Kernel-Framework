## Two owned traces, one stem observation

Kṣar arm: kṣar|tra → kṣa|tra → kṣatra. Root-final r is deleted under the
stipulation; t/r/a of actual tra remain sounded. The r in the resulting
tra is a separate suffix occurrence, not restoration or movement of the
root r. This is not an operation deleting every r anywhere in the word.

Tattva kṣi arm: kṣi|tra → kṣa|tra → kṣatra. The root vowel i receives

retroflex ṭra; cuṭu 1.3.7 does not make dental t an it marker. No sounded
r is deleted in this arm. Both arms then retain the source-designated
negative's original n/a, 6.3.75: na|kṣatra → nakṣatra. No 6.3.74 nuṭ
prerequisite is created, because the negative n was not deleted.
