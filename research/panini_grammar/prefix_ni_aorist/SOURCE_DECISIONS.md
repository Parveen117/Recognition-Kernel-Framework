## H03. Three different vowel-grade decisions

Gad is seṭ. General 7.2.4 blocks hal-final sic-vṛddhi before iṭ, but **7.2.7** gives the specific option for eligible light short a in a consonant-initial root. Gad meets it and none of 7.2.2/3/5's overriding named conditions. Laghusiddhāntakaumudī 7.2.7 directly prints **अगादीत्, अगदीत्**. For kṛ and kṣi reuse the exact P-sic vṛddhi arguments of PG32 L3 and PG69 H09: ṛ→ār and i→ai by **7.2.1**, with no affix iṭ. Kṛ's sya-specific 7.2.70 iṭ does not travel into sic. Ksa/ṅit-aṅ keep viś/piṣ at short root i, without present śa/śnam or a sic-vṛddhi operation.

## H04. Ksa vowel deletion follows the actual ending state

Kāśikā **7.3.72** states ksa-lopa before a vowel-initial ending. Kaumudī and Bālamanoramā specify A and **1.1.52 final-a lopa**, not deletion of the whole sa. Bālamanoramā explicitly explains the two timing conditions:

- Before original ātām/āthām, ksa-a is removed. The a-final condition for **7.2.81 iy** then fails; the endings remain ātām/āthām, not etām/ethām.
- Original **jha** is consonant-initial. Ksa-a is still present when the jh replacement is selected, so 7.1.5's non-a-final condition fails and **7.1.3 ant** applies. Only then can 7.3.72 delete ksa-a before this actual vowel-initial ant. The n remains.

The source quotes aghṛkṣātām and aghṛkṣanta; Laghusiddhāntakaumudī supplies the fuller duh A series including adhukṣi. Applying the same guards to viś yields **vikṣātām, vikṣanta and vikṣi**. This is a source-rule application, not a quotation of every viś word.

For the plural adopt the explicit ant-then-a-lopa path. Bālamanoramā also mentions pararūpa at the resulting vowel junction; preserve that remark in the archive. This packet does not enumerate all observationally equivalent schedules or turn the remark into a second morphological reading. The chosen path convention matches the predecessors' non-exhaustive scheduler accounting.
