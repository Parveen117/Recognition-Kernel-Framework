# PG968 · Requests to Indra and the Aśvins

The giving interpretation of juhotyādi ḍudhāñ (1030011) in Mādhavīya 741 has direct Śākala evidence in all three second-person numbers of the active imperative. [PG966](../dha_in_the_rbhu_request_and_agnis_superlative/README.md) supplies the plural. The singular occurs both with and without a preverb. Sāyaṇa's paraphrases identify the recipients and distinguish giving from depositing.
