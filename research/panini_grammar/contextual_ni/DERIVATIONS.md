For **lṛṭ/lṛṅ**, nī is aniṭ and does not meet the ṛ/han sya iṭ exception. Root guṇa and the owned sya-s ṣatva give neṣya. For **luṭ**, aniṭ nī+tās gives ne+tās. Reuse PG16's exact A tuple **netā, netārau, netāraḥ; netāse, netāsāthe, netādhve; netāhe, netāsvahe, netāsmahe**. Keep the third-person 2.4.85 replacements, 1sg tās-s→h and the selected 2pl tās-s lopa/dental-dh policy. No perfect auxiliary is involved.

The 2pl ḍh is mandatory, with no iṭ-based option.

## H07. Complete basic A perfect

Liṭ root doubling under 6.1.8, with the 1.1.58 copy-stage protection and abhyāsa shortening under 7.4.59, gives ni+nī. All selected A liṭ endings are apit-kit under 1.2.5, so the root does not take guṇa. The five vowel-initial endings already supply an ac environment. At 2sg, 2pl, 1du and 1pl the independently required iṭ supplies it. In every cell, 6.4.82 replaces root ī by y in the doubled, anekāc, root-internally non-cluster-preceded aṅga. It is the specified exception to the competing iyaṅ route, not an arbitrary removal of ī.

## H08. Aniṭ A sic without the kṛ kit/luk route

For luṅ, select cli→sic under 3.1.43/44 and retain the original sic teaching separately from its realized s. Nī is not tanādi kṛ, so PG74's 2.4.79 dispute does not supply a new option. Nor is its vowel-final ī in the 1.2.11 consonant-final class or the 1.2.12 extension. In A there is no P sic-vṛddhi. Ordinary guṇa gives **ne+s**; no root-facing iṭ or present śap is present.

The A ending tuple from PG32 is ta, ātām, ata, thās, āthām, dhvam, i, vahi, mahi. At ta/thās, the actual root/aṅga end e is not short, so 8.2.27 does not delete sic-s. No single-consonant A ending satisfies the 7.3.96 apṛkta-hal īṭ condition, and 8.2.28's iṭ/īṭ route is absent. Retained s becomes ṣ; the ta/thās contacts give ṭ/ṭh. At dhvam alone, 8.2.25 removes sic-s and 8.3.78 makes dh obligatorily ḍh after e.

The complete augmented interface is **aneṣṭa, aneṣātām, aneṣata; aneṣṭhāḥ, aneṣāthām, aneḍhvam; aneṣi, aneṣvahi, aneṣmahi**. The first-singular i is an ending, not iṭ. Bālamanoramā 8.2.27 explicitly anchors **aneṣṭa**. Its difference from PG74 akṛta is explained by the different kit/guṇa/short-vowel guards, not a manually assigned exception spelling.

Now **upa|a|neṣṭa → upāneṣṭa**, **vi|a|neṣṭa → vyaneṣṭa**. At 2pl this gives **upāneḍhvam/vyaneḍhvam**, with one reading in each position. The liṭ iṭ-based optional dental branch is unavailable here.
