The new distinction is not simply “aniṭ everywhere.” Basic future **kṣeṣyati** lacks iṭ, perfect **cikṣayitha / cikṣetha** has a source-qualified thal option, and perfect **cikṣiyiva** requires iṭ. In **akṣaiṣīt**, the ending owns īṭ but sic ṣ survives because no preceding affix iṭ licenses its deletion. Passive **kṣeṣīḍhvam** has mandatory ḍh, whereas ciṇvat **kṣāyiṣīdhvam / kṣāyiṣīḍhvam** has a coupled iṭ/optional-ḍh history.
