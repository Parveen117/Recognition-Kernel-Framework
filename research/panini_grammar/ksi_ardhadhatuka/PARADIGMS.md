# PG69 — Complete kṣi ārdhadhātuka words

Monty · 7 November 2026

Every position selects **SK_KSI_REPORTED_BHASHA_1050033**, svādi kṣi in hiṃsā, with the source, context and role conditions in H01–H04. There is no prefix. P/A denote padas; karmaṇi agreement is with O, causal kartari with C, and basic/san kartari with A.

In passive P2/P5, slash order is ordinary **O**, then ciṇvat **C**; dh/ḍh choices are grouped inside their own branch. P7 prints coincident O/C spellings once but retains both augment histories. P4's 2pl blessing has ordinary-iṭ dh/ḍh alternatives. In P8, 2sg perfect lists iṭ then no-iṭ, and 1sg lists ṇit then non-ṇit. No alternative doubles the position count.
