# PG249 · Two complete mā histories and an explicitly prefixed aorist

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Mādhavīya supplies the full prefixed aorist **praṇyamāsta** under *meṅ*. It expressly discusses the intervening augment. Under *māṅ* it gives the unprefixed singular **amāsta**, dual **amāsātām** and plural **amāsata**. Both root analyses therefore reach the same prefixed shapes, with the full *pra + ni* singular directly cited for *meṅ*.

| Middle form | Perfect | Aorist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Third singular, *pra + ni* | praṇimame | praṇyamāsta |
| Third dual, *pra + ni* | praṇimamāte | praṇyamāsātām |
| Second singular, *pari + ni* | pariṇimamiṣe | pariṇyamāsthāḥ |
| First plural, *pari + ni* | pariṇimamimahe | pariṇyamāsmahi |

The *liṭ* forms *mame* and *mamiṣe* are explicitly printed in the lexical tradition for both roots. The process involves perfect reduplication and loss of root-final *ā* under 6.4.64. Within the same perfect paradigm the first and third singular both give *mame*.

These two lakāras add **72 positions**, completing **360 of 360**. There are 180 comparisons pairing the two root histories at the same prefix stack, lakāra and slot. The 72 present-system pairs have different words. All 108 pairs in the other six lakāras have the same readings. This count concerns the written outcomes for these particular roots and preverbs.
