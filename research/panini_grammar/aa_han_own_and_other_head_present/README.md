# PG290 · *Ā + han*: own head and another's head split the pada

Monty · 13 November 2026

Kāśikā 1.3.28 gives an exact semantic minimal pair. With one's own head as object it has **āhate śiraḥ** (A); with another person's head it has **āhanti śiraḥ parakīyam** (P). It also explains that *svāṅga* here means one's own limb, not the technical saṃjñā.

This packet writes only those two directly licensed laṭ 3sg requests. It does not extrapolate the digital Mādhavīya's full *ā + han* middle line, whose 2pl prefix spelling still needs printed collation, and it does not turn the pair into complete ten-lakāra grids.
