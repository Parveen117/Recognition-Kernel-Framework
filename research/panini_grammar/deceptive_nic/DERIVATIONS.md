separately from i. Gṛdh's light-upadhā ṛ receives guṇa by
7.3.86, with 1.1.50 r accompaniment: **gardh|i**. The root **vañc** has
palatal ñ before c. Its penultimate sound is ñ, not a; therefore

ay below belongs to ṇic, not the lexical root. Gṛdh's internal
r and dh retain their original root owners; no dh-to-ḍh rule
acts merely because the root contains r.

untouched. There are 36 blessing positions and 38 readings/paths.
