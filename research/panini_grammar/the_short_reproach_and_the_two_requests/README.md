### The apparatus adds a witness to the short vowel

The note linked to the verb of Bhaṭṭi XXI.1 in the Mallinātha edition transcription is **1175**. It attributes the abbreviated reading *tvam aśaṅki°* to **T3, T4 and Com.** Thus the short initial of PG995's unprefixed conditional also has an explicit verse-apparatus report. The note's abbreviation fixes the initial. It does not transcribe the whole word for either siglum, and it does not establish the two copies' descent. The long main text still agrees with the Hamburg catalogue excerpt at this point. These are two existing person-number keys, with zero new finite credit for the collation. ### The enemy's request in XXI.3

Select Dhātupāṭha **10.0447 artha upayācñāyām**, requesting or soliciting, curādi, seṭ, Ā, transitive. Root-class ṇic supplies the *arthaya-* stem. Mallinātha expressly calls the verb *arthayate* middle; beside it the commentary mentions a formation from the noun *artha* with ṇic in the *tat karoti* sense. These have different input types. The prose paraphrases a request made of Sītā. She is the object, and the enemy is the actor. The speaking Fire reports the encounter. His own grammatical person does not determine the ending of the reported verb.

The main GRETIL text at XXI.3 transmits *nā ’rthayiṣyata* before the voiced initial of *durmatiḥ*. The negative particle and following vowel share a sandhi junction. Mallinātha expressly invokes āṭ for the vowel-initial verb and the future base plus secondary endings characterizes the conditional. Note **1178** gives the middle-looking *prārthayiṣyata* for E3, B, C with pra present, and a differently transmitted *prārthayiṣyaś ca* for D. Note **1179** reports *nārthayiṣyad adurmatiḥ* for Com.; its final d before a vowel is compatible with an active -t. The prose also supplies a P-looking form. Leave the P/denominal interpretation attached to its own textual and lexical evidence. The D string does not by itself decide its full underlying ending. The pra and negative are independent observations: the note's positive pra-form cannot establish an additional occurrence of the main verse's negative.
