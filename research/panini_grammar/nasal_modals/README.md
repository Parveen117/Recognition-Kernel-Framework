# PG21 — Nasal-class modals and source-qualified stem transport

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

PG21 develops complete loṭ, vidhiliṅ and blessing-loṭ continuations for
seventeen exact roots already admitted in PG6/PG12's present domain.
It transports their owned root/vikaraṇa procedures to new ending queries,
including both śnu/śnā alternatives and both reviewed tanādi guṇa
accounts. It does not transport a finished laṭ word as a modal stem.

The finite register has **27 pada profiles, 729 morphological positions
and 1,159 word-branch readings**. The reading count is across the declared
source-policy family: the tanādi guṇa disagreement is distinguished from
the explicit grammatical option of 3.1.82. No new present-tense cells,
distinct-string total, executed tests or whole-grammar percentage are claimed.

## Construction

1. [Exact domain](DOMAIN.md): lexical identities, supplied intended meanings,
   kartari/pada conditions and the two tanādi source policies.
2. [Owned stems](STEM_CONSTRUCTION.md): śnu/u/śnā, root n-lopa,
   root-versus-affix guṇa, cross-boundary clusters and branch interfaces.
3. [Modal endings](MODAL_ENDINGS.md): complete reusable constructors,
   why modal augments remove the present m/v u-lopa context, and tātaṅ.
4. [Derivations](DERIVATIONS.md): su, āp, ṛṇ, sku/stambh, pū/lū and
   the marker-sensitive contrasts, with representative full paradigms.
5. [Register](PARADIGMS.md): all 27 profiles, both source/affix branches,
   every slot and the count proof.
6. [Native transport](NATIVE_TRANSPORT.md): full carrier, pairing, cuts,
   typed zero-recovery, actual-cluster refinement and a finite obstruction
   to transporting laṭ alternatives as an information-preserving bijection.

[Source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md), [literal archive](SOURCE_EXCERPTS.json)
and [pins](SOURCE_PINS.json) retain exact predecessor and primary evidence.
[Companion review](COMPANION_UPDATE.md) uses the theorem branch through T135 and

from the grammar/navigation/engine tests that remain deferred.

This is a written classical, unprefixed, unaccented, basic-kartari
construction. Broader senses, voice/derived formations, āśīrliṅ, accent,
Vedic pathways and a general conflict/visibility scheduler remain outside
the new domain. The canonical operator engine is unchanged.
