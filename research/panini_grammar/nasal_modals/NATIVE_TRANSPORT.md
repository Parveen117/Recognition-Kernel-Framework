## N3. Typed specialization and exact finite relation

Let ε:B_21→C_Σ select the empty-word coefficient. It is a unital
dagger homomorphism: multiplying nonempty free words cannot produce the
empty word. Define the typed maps

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(\sum_he_ha_h)=\sum_he_h\epsilon(a_h),\qquad
 \Pi_{0,op}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

**Theorem N3.** On the admitted finite operators and states,

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,op}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
 \epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B
 =\langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_Σ}.
\]

Cuts, complements, products, sums and adjoints specialize compatibly.
Forward unit-start histories recover exactly P6's source-labelled relation,
with its Accepted/Excluded/Open channels.

**Proof.** Each event coefficient has ε(1+z_e)=1, while its guard's
history/owner data survive unchanged. The homomorphism gives the product,
adjoint and pairing identities. Induct over the finite event compositions.
Every accepted zero path has the same native guarded event sequence and
every native forward path has the corresponding zero path. Each terminal
history retains its own coordinate with constant coefficient 1. □

The support statement is for those forward paths, not arbitrary signed
linear combinations, which may cancel. It does not authorize reverse
grammar via adjoints or prove a universal scheduler.

## N4. A source-policy choice must refine the later cluster query

Take ṛṇ P ordinary loṭ 2sg after its root policy but before 6.4.106.
Both G0 and G1 have the same lexical identity, u-affix, weak ending
and slot. G0's ṛṇ∣u has c=false; G1's arṇ∣u has c=true. Thus
the hi-luk admission cut is D=diag(1,0) on their two history coordinates.

Let q retain lexical ID, affix u, outer weak flag and slot while forgetting
the policy and realized root sounds. It identifies both inputs: q(e_0)=q(e_1).

**Proposition N4.** The hi-luk cut does not factor through q. Keeping
the actual cluster view, or the policy plus its owned root result, repairs
this displayed query.

**Proof.** e_0−e_1 lies in ker(q), but qD(e_0−e_1)=q(e_0)≠0.
Thus D does not preserve ker(q) and cannot descend to the observation.
S4 supplies the different H outputs ṛṇu/arṇuhi. The same argument
applies to tṛṇ/ghṛṇ, while kṣiṇ's two policies have the same cluster
value and do not supply this particular witness. N3 retains all labels. □

This is a finite information boundary, not a lower bound for arbitrary
parsers. The source-policy branch is resolved before its dependent cluster
cut; it cannot be combined with an independently chosen hi-loss toggle.

## N6. Present alternatives do not transport bijectively to loṭ

This is an explicitly declared **comparison adapter**, not a source rule
turning a completed utterance into another. It copies the lexical, role,
person and G-policy evidence into a newly supplied compatible loṭ meaning
frame, then constructs the new ending and its root-facing continuation.
The source-derived laṭ/loṭ profiles are each independently admitted.

context. Hence the two G0 donor choices do not become two separate
modal choices. On the comparison spaces that forget donor-history tags,
the zero-level matrix in these ordered bases is

\[
 R=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

**Theorem N6.** This word/source-policy comparison has rank two and
kernel span{e_0k−e_0d}. It is not an information-preserving bijection
or pairing isometry between the two displayed branch spaces.

**Proof.** The two row coordinates are independent. The first two
columns agree and the third is independent, giving the stated kernel.
With the native coordinate pairing, R†R has a 2×2 all-ones first
block and a final 1, so R†R≠I. In particular two orthogonal donor
histories have the same observed target. □

There is a history-preserving repair. Target coordinates (new-modal-history,
donor-label) give three distinct images, with a structural relabelling
J satisfying J†J=I. The word observation then forgets the donor label
and produces R. That J is a coefficient-1 label injection; the separate
residue-weighted grammar event operators are not being asserted unitary.
The label injection, forgetting map and events all satisfy N3's typed
specialization laws. Zero-specialization itself forgets neither donor
labels nor grammatical histories.

Thus a register should reconstruct the modal fibre from its actual
queries, not copy the cardinality of the laṭ option set. The statement
is exact on this three-to-two comparison and makes no global claim about
all mood transports or all Sanskrit ambiguities.
