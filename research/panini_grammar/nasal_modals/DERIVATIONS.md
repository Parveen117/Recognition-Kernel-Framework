# PG21 — Worked nasal-class modal derivations

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## R1. Su: root u and affix u have different triggers

Start from exact su 1050001 and an admitted kartari frame. Śnu is
ṅitvat at the root boundary, so root su does not become so. At the
outer boundary loṭ 3sg tu is pit: su∣nu∣tu → su∣no∣tu, **sunotu**.
At 3du, apit tām blocks that guṇa: **sunutām**. At 3pl the resolved
ant vowel triggers 6.4.87: su∣nu∣antu → **sunvantu**, a literal
Kāśikā 6.4.87 example.

The ordinary P 2sg has cluster-free affix-u plus hi. Rule 6.4.106
removes hi, giving **sunu**. First person receives pit āṭ: su∣nu∣ā∣ni
→ su∣no∣ā∣ni → **sunavāni**. No direct m/v u-loss context is
present in the dual/plural. Kaumudī 3.1.73 quotes sunu, sunavāni,
sunavai and sunuyāt.

The complete I tuples are

\[
 (sunotu,sunutām,sunvantu,sunu,sunutam,sunuta,
 sunavāni,sunavāva,sunavāma),
\]
\[
 (sunutām,sunvātām,sunvatām,sunuṣva,sunvāthām,sunudhvam,
 sunavai,sunavāvahai,sunavāmahai).
\]

In P vidhiliṅ, explicit ṅit blocks outer-u guṇa and the y-initial
augment does not cause general u-lopa. In A, resolved īy is vowel-initial
and 6.4.87 supplies sunv. Thus

\[
 (sunuyāt,sunuyātām,sunuyuḥ,sunuyāḥ,sunuyātam,sunuyāta,
 sunuyām,sunuyāva,sunuyāma),
\]
\[
 (sunvīta,sunvīyātām,sunvīran,sunvīthāḥ,sunvīyāthām,
 sunvīdhvam,sunvīya,sunvīvahi,sunvīmahi).
\]

On B, add **sunutāt** to both P singular slots. The ordinary sunu
and optional sunutāt have different actual ending targets. Ci has the
same complete constructor with Q=cin/J=cinv, preserving its own identity.

## R2. Āp/śak: a cross-boundary cluster changes two queries

In āp∣nu, p+n forms a cluster immediately before the affix u.
Rule 6.4.106 therefore retains hi: **āpnuhi**. At the weak vowel
junction, 6.4.87's no-cluster premise is false and 6.4.77 supplies uv:
**āpnuvantu**. At P vidhiliṅ the first sound is y, so neither
vowel-junction rule applies: **āpnuyāt**, quoted by Kāśikā 6.4.77.

The complete P tuples are

\[
 (āpnotu,āpnutām,āpnuvantu,āpnuhi,āpnutam,āpnuta,
 āpnavāni,āpnavāva,āpnavāma),
\]
\[
 (āpnuyāt,āpnuyātām,āpnuyuḥ,āpnuyāḥ,āpnuyātam,āpnuyāta,
 āpnuyām,āpnuyāva,āpnuyāma).
\]

Śak has Q=śakn/J=śaknuv/H=śaknuhi and the same source conditions.
Its **śaknuyāt** is also literal in 6.4.77. Both are P-only in D2;
an A constructor is not activated merely because it is algebraically defined.
The B additions are **āpnutāt** and **śaknutāt** at their eligible slots.

## R3. Ṛṇ: source policy changes hi-luk, not just a root vowel

On G0, ṛṇ∣u has a vowel ṛ followed by one consonant ṇ before u.
On G1, the root guṇa produces arṇ∣u, with two consonants rṇ.
Resolve the outer loṭ 2sg hi after this selected root query:

\[
 ṛṇ|u|hi\xrightarrow{6.4.106}ṛṇu,\qquad
 arṇ|u|hi\xrightarrow{\text{cluster excludes }6.4.106}arṇuhi.
\]

At 3pl, both have the **u** affix, so 6.1.77 supplies v even in
G1's cluster case: **ṛṇvantu/arṇvantu**. This is different from
śnu's **āpnuvantu**. Bālamanoramā's literal arṇvanti explanation
provides the exact u/yaṇ interface, transported here to antu.

The two complete I P tuples are

\[
 (ṛṇotu,ṛṇutām,ṛṇvantu,ṛṇu,ṛṇutam,ṛṇuta,
 ṛṇavāni,ṛṇavāva,ṛṇavāma),
\]
\[
 (arṇotu,arṇutām,arṇvantu,arṇuhi,arṇutam,arṇuta,
 arṇavāni,arṇavāva,arṇavāma).
\]

The A profiles are E3 with (Q,J)=(ṛṇ,ṛṇv) or (arṇ,arṇv).
P O gives O_P(ṛṇu)/O_P(arṇu), A O gives O_A(ṛṇv)/O_A(arṇv).
The B additions are **ṛṇutāt/arṇutāt**; actual tāt is retained on
both, regardless of H's earlier hi-luk distinction.

Tṛṇ and ghṛṇ have the same new-cluster consequence. Kṣiṇ's i→e
creates no rṇ cluster, so **kṣiṇu/kṣeṇu** both delete hi. These are
written rule applications, not claims that the source quotes every modal word.
PG12's ghṛṇ exact-present-word witness gap remains separately recorded.

## R4. Sku and the four stambh roots: two affixes, separately completed

For sku, 3.1.82 admits both sku∣nu and sku∣nā. On 3sg loṭ their
outer pit ending gives **skunotu/skunātu**. Ordinary 2sg gives
**skunu/skunīhi**; P vidhiliṅ gives **skunuyāt/skunīyāt**;
A vidhiliṅ gives **skunvīta/skunīta**. The final ī of skunīta
belongs to the resolved augment after śnā-lopa, not to a surviving śnā ā.

Its two full A O tuples are

\[
 (skunvīta,skunvīyātām,skunvīran,skunvīthāḥ,skunvīyāthām,
 skunvīdhvam,skunvīya,skunvīvahi,skunvīmahi),
\]
\[
 (skunīta,skunīyātām,skunīran,skunīthāḥ,skunīyāthām,
 skunīdhvam,skunīya,skunīvahi,skunīmahi).
\]

For stambh, retain the teaching form's root n, delete it under 6.4.24,
then retain the different n of the chosen affix. The two 3sg I words
are **stabhnotu/stabhnātu**. The śnu branch now has a bh+n cluster:
**stabhnuhi/stabhnuvantu**, whereas the śnā branch has
**stabhnīhi/stabhnantu**. P O gives **stabhnuyāt/stabhnīyāt**.
Its B additions are **stabhnutāt/stabhnītāt**, each at the two
eligible P singular slots.

The other three named roots have stubhn/skabhn/skubhn in the same
owned constructions. No A profile is added for those four P-only roots.
The two affix branches are grammatical options, unlike R3's explicitly
labelled union of commentary policies.

## R5. Pū/lū and śru retain their earlier root transformations

Pū's śit-triggered shortening yields pu∣nā. Outer ending changes do
not restore root ū. Its complete O paradigms are **O_P(punī)** and
**O_A(pun)**, giving punīyāt versus punīta. Its I P tuple is

\[
 (punātu,punītām,punantu,punīhi,punītam,punīta,
 punāni,punāva,punāma).
\]

I A is I_A^ā(pun), and B adds **punītāt** in the two P singular
slots. Lū independently gives the lun versions, including lunīhi,
lunīyāt/lunīta and lunītāt. The selected pvādi membership is inherited,
not inferred from these outputs.

Śru's 3.1.74 couples the śṛ substitute with śnu. With Q=śṛṇ,
I P gives śṛṇotu/śṛṇutām/śṛṇvantu and śṛṇu, while first person
uses śṛṇavāni/śṛṇavāva/śṛṇavāma. O P is O_P(śṛṇu), and B
adds śṛṇutāt. No extra A context is supplied for the P-only root.

## R6. Written continuation theorem

Every example above is a component of E's complete tuples at S's
owned interface. Together R1–R5 cover every rule-sensitive kind in D2:
cluster-free śnu, clustered śnu, u, weakened śnā, dual-affix roots and
source-policy-created clusters. P's register supplies every individual
root/pada branch. Literal examples are identified separately in SOURCE_REVIEW;
the remaining words are written source-relative consequences.
