Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## P1. Register conventions

Each position fixes exact root/sense, basic kartari, qualified pada,
I/O/B profile and person/number. Repeated contextual witnesses are factored
out. For the four disputed tanādi roots this aggregate position count
also factors out the G0/G1 source-policy label; **readings retain it**.
Within a derivation the source policy is fixed as D3 requires.

All constructors below are complete nine-slot tuples, not 3sg templates.
Abbreviate E3's I_P^u/I_A^u by **U_P(Q)/U_A(Q)** with J and H
uniquely supplied by the named Q row of S4. Abbreviate I_P^ā/I_A^ā
by **A_P(S)/A_A(S)**. O_P and O_A are exactly E5's constructors.
Braces mean the union of complete licensed branch tuples, retaining
their affix or G-policy labels. They never mean independent per-letter choices.

For B use the same I tuple and add the last-column word at **P 3sg and
P 2sg only**, on its matching branch. For A, B is just I on the new
āśīs frame. On a two-branch row the first added word belongs to the
first branch and the second to the second; there is no cross-product.

## P3. Position count

There are 27 pada profiles and three morphological/meaning profiles,
each with nine positions:

\[
 27\cdot3\cdot9=729.
\]

This comprises **243 I**, **243 O** and **243 B** positions. The
B ordinary words coincide with their I counterparts; the intended
meaning records are different. Neither these 729 positions nor the
reading count below is a count of distinct words.

## P4. Ordinary branch counts

The thirteen PG6 pada profiles each have one stem branch. The six
3.1.82 pada profiles each have two. The eight G-policy profiles each
have one branch per policy, hence two in the aggregate source family:

\[
 13+6\cdot2+8\cdot2=41
\]

nine-slot branch paradigms for each of I, O and ordinary B. Each gives
**369** readings. No 6.4.107 branch, A 2pl retroflex alternative or
Vedic hi-retention choice increases this number on D4's selected profile.

## P5. Blessing branches and total

Among the 41 branches, P contributes 8 fixed PG6 branches, 10 from
the five two-affix roots and eight from the four two-policy roots:
**26** P branches. Each gains one tāt reading at each of two slots.
Thus B has **369+52=421** readings.

**33·27+44=935 readings**, still over the same 729 aggregate positions.
The difference is source-policy indexing, not a probabilistic mixture.
