# PG283 · Han causatives: four present-system lakāras and the result condition

Monty · 13 November 2026

The new series is **hetumati ṇic**, an actual causing or commissioning activity, with its prayojaka given both hetu and kartṛ designations under 1.4.55. The Kāśikā directly cites *ghātayati* under both 7.3.32 and 7.3.54. Its root-final t, initial gh and long vowel have their own rule and marker histories; they are not obtained by adding an unqualified “aya” to the surface *hanti*.

The pada condition matters. Under **1.3.74**, a causative takes A when its action-result is intended for its kartṛ. The present packet selects the residual P domain of 1.3.78, following the source's *kārayati parasya* contrast. It therefore does not claim that han causatives are unconditionally P.

The root t stays dental; the ṇ belongs to inner ni. Four individual *-tāt* guards retain the blessing condition already reviewed in PG280.

There is also one separately recorded source observation. The vārttika under 3.1.26 says **कंसवधमाचष्टे, कंसं घातयति**: narrating Kamsa's killing. That is a different construction from causing someone to kill. A shared *ghātayati* string does not let a compiler discard the supplied semantic frame or merge the derivation histories. The narrating construction receives **no new position credit** here.
