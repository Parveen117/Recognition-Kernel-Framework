## H05. Blessing: ud with yāsuṭ, vad with iṭ-sīyuṭ

\[

\]

initial aṭ is present. The s that survives in the t-bearing dual/second-plural
paths is suṭ-owned; 7.2.79's sārvadhātuka s-lopa is unavailable. Attach
vi+pra outside this root/ending construction: pra+a is not the junction;

after affix i, producing PG20's factor Z=vadiṣ. The separate singular
t/th-addressed suṭ follows ī and also takes ṣ; dual suṭ follows ā and
stays dental. The complete A tuple is

\[
(vadiṣīṣṭa,vadiṣīyāstām,vadiṣīran,vadiṣīṣṭhāḥ,
vadiṣīyāsthām,vadiṣīdhvam,vadiṣīya,vadiṣīvahi,vadiṣīmahi).
\]

At 2pl, iṭ is preceded by root d, which is outside iṇ. Neither 8.3.78's
direct iṇ-final-aṅga condition nor 8.3.79's pre-iṭ iṇ condition permits
ḍh. Sīyuṭ's later ī is not an interchangeable aṅga-final vowel. Thus all
counted A 2pl blessings retain dental dh and one reading.
