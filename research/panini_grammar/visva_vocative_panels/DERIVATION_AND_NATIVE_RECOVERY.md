## Nine selected words and owned endings

Masculine singular has short-*a* sambuddhi and 6.1.69 ending-consonant loss:
`viśva`. Dual is `viśvau`; `jas → śī` gives plural `viśve`. Feminine
`viśvā` plus the corresponding singular and dual operations gives `viśve`
twice, while plural is `viśvāḥ`.

Neuter singular is not in 7.1.25's named `adḍ` domain. Ordinary 7.1.24
`am` plus 6.1.69 m-lopa gives `viśva`. Neuter dual is `viśve`; plural
uses short `śi`, `num`, length and retroflexion to give `viśvāṇi`.
The four written `viśve` outputs—M plural, F singular, F dual and N dual—
retain separate original endings and gender-number addresses. The ledger
therefore has nine positions and nine readings, not a surface inventory.
