# PG893 · *Viśva* vocative panels

Monty · 25 November 2026

Śabdamañjarī first names `viśva` among the demonstrative pronouns with the
meaning “all”, then explicitly extends the masculine `sarva` paradigm to
`viśva`, the feminine paradigm to `viśvā`, and the neuter paradigm to
`viśva`. Those positive source statements license the nine ordinary M/F/N
vocative keys across singular, dual and plural; they do not merely infer a
class from spelling.

The selected words are `viśva / viśvau / viśve`,
`viśve / viśve / viśvāḥ`, and `viśva / viśve / viśvāṇi`.
The neuter singular follows ordinary `am` plus m-lopa, not `adḍ`; equal
`viśve` surfaces retain their separate gender-number and ending histories.

construction/nine interfaces, one address construction/nine interfaces,
