# PG696 · Selected Classical cases and exact Śākala case

Take the body before the applicable ending in each of
the three pinned case schedules. Ṛ/r sees the ending n
across the vowels and labial m under 8.4.2 in all six
ktri bodies. It reaches the a-stem instrumental n,
genitive nuṭ and the neuter plural num at their own
addresses. The ā-stem's genitive plural has its own
nuṭ, with the same rule for *kṛtrimāṇām*. Masculine
accusative plural final n stays dental under 8.4.37.
The contrasting pākima stem uses the dental schedule.
