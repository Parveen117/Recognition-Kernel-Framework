## C01 — Three distinct शीर्ष records

|Admitted context|Licensed record|Scoped outcome|
|---|---|---|
|Vedic lexical alternative, 6.1.60|शीर्षन् as a separate synonymous lexical item|Accept that lexical alternative without inventing a prior replacement event|
|शिरस् before admitted य-initial तद्धित, 6.1.61|शीर्षन् as an आदेश|Apply the specified replacement with its own cause|
|शिरस् before admitted vowel-initial तद्धित, 6.1.62|अ-final शीर्ष as an आदेश|Apply this different replacement; do not import न् from 60/61|

Kāśikā and Padamañjarī distinguish the first two analyses. Kāśikā 6.1.62
uses स्थौलशीर्षम् to explain why its substitute is अ-final: an
अण्-conditioned शीर्षन् proposal would encounter 6.4.167 preservation.
Equal meaning or a shared written sequence does not identify these records.

**Limit:** Vedic register, lexical identity and the required suffix are
supplied. None is inferred from bare spelling.

## C02 — य adjacency alone does not license 6.1.61

**Target:** the तद्धित predicate of the suffix causing the query.

Kāśikā gives शीर्षण्यः as a positive taddhita witness and शिरस्यति
as a negative denominative witness. In the separately admitted तद्धित
context, the शिरस् → शीर्षन् request can apply. For the verbal witness
it is BLOCKED because the required domain is absent. A displayed य alone
does not supply that domain. The locative taddhita witness refers to 4.3.55;
its full inflection is not reconstructed here.

The hair-specific option reported under 6.1.61 remains within its stated
semantic use. It is not a license to make every शिरस् + य replacement
optional. Missing decisive suffix/meaning evidence gives OPEN.

replace it by ण्.** The diagnostic branch is not an accepted feminine output.

## C04 — औडुलोम्या requires its enabling deletion first

**Target:** the गुरूपोत्तम predicate admitting 4.1.78.

Nyāsa and Padamañjarī 4.1.78 explicitly order this comparison. In the
admitted इञ् formation, 6.4.144 टि-lopa occurs first; only then does the
host acquire the required heavy penultimate condition. A proposal to install
ष्यङ् earlier and invoke 6.4.168 preservation is BLOCKED at the earlier
4.1.78 admission itself.

This is the dependency of that source witness, not an instruction that
6.4.144 always precedes 4.1.78. हस्तिशिरस् has a different enabling
event, its अ-final शीर्ष replacement. The present control isolates
admission order; it supplies no extra औडुलोम्या paradigm.

## C05 — Deleted intervening इ and replaced इ are different histories

**Target:** the view used by the later 6.1.61 adjacency query.

In the added-affix account, ष्यङ् follows the इञ्-ended formation.
6.4.148 then deletes its intervening इ. Kāśikā 6.1.62 preserves that
intervention through the lopa's स्थानिवद्भाव for this preceding operation.
The adjacency request is BLOCKED despite the absence of इ in the current
pronounced sequence.

In the replacement account, ष्यङ् replaces इञ् itself. This is not a
separate intervening इ deleted by 6.4.148. Importing the added-affix
history's deletion witness is BLOCKED as a wrong-event justification.
Replacement needs its own selected safeguard.

Retained इ is query evidence, not an active restored sound. Unknown event
identity or unresolved authority for the view gives OPEN. The source-qualified
1.1.57 use does not authorize arbitrary historical access or identify all
स्थानिवद्भाव with असिद्धवत्.

## C06 — A local safeguard is not a universal prohibition

**Target:** the proposal that every later operation must preserve every
condition of all its prerequisites.

Under the selected replacement account, the शीर्ष-dependent ष्यङ् must
not enable the defeating later शीर्षन् substitution. That particular
proposal is BLOCKED. The same Padamañjarī field raises the converse:
शीर्ष was conditioned by a vowel-initial suffix, yet ष्यङ् changes that
environment. A universal safeguard would wrongly block the required ष्यङ्.

The source answers **अनित्या सन्निपातपरिभाषा**. Keep the local permission
and restriction as separately justified decisions; reject the stronger
unrestricted invariant. A new example without an applicable witness remains
OPEN. This supplies neither a general scheduler nor a confluence theorem.
