# PG804 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 5.2.123 gives `yus` to `ūrṇā`; its final s establishes pada status and protects the displayed result. The competing view that inherited `chandasi` limits the rule remains Open. Rule 124 uses the full `gmini`, not `min`, and supplies singular, dual and plural. Rule 125 replaces it with `ālac/āṭac` only for censured excessive speech; purposeful eloquence remains `vāgmin`. Rule 126 similarly reserves `āmin` for lordship, while ordinary `svavān` remains the already-owned control.

Rule 127 admits the displayed arśādi, bodily-defect and colour cases while leaving undisplayed roster members unconstructed. Rule 128 selects `ini` for living-being dvandvas, disease and censured traits, excluding `ṭhan` only there. Tree possession, ordinary body-part possession and non-a-final compounds retain matup controls. Rule 129 adds `kuk` with `ini` in disease sense; the cave control remains ordinary matup. Rule 130 restricts age ordinals to `ini`; the non-age fifth-rāga request takes matup.
