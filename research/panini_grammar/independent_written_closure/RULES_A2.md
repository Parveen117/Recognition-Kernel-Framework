# PG46 — Sound classes, कित् behavior and grammatical categories

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These 17 already-combined IDs now receive independent written contracts.
Designation, interpretation and marker-related operations have separate
types; none asserts executable grammar coverage.

|1.1.67|Ablative environmental direction|A rule specifies its environment by the relevant fifth-case wording|Apply its licensed operation to the immediately following eligible target|निर्दिष्ट and adjacency continue from66; no operation is licensed merely by encountering an ordinary ablative in a sentence|K/SK: तिङ्ङतिङः accent relation; reversed order is excluded|
|1.1.69|सवर्ण-denoting sound notation|Eligible अण् citation using the later ण् marker, or an उदित् sound citation|Let the citation designate its own sound and the admitted सवर्ण class|Exclude prescribed outputs, including suffixes and the stated substitution/augment cases; this is not permission to choose a longer output sound|K/N/SK: आद् गुणः; चु/टु/कु classes; prescribed उ suffix control|

|1.2.19|Named roots before सेट् निष्ठा|शीङ्, स्विद्, मिद्, क्ष्विद् or धृष् precedes इट्-bearing निष्ठा|Remove कित् behavior in that branch|Require निष्ठा and actual इट्; the alternative अनिट् branch such as स्विन्नः is distinct. |1.2.26|Optional कित् with short i/u उपधा|Consonant-initial, रल्-final root has इ/उ उपधा and takes सेट् सन् or क्त्वा|Optionally assign कित् behavior|All conditions are conjunctive;8's named-root compulsory cases retain priority and अनिट् forms do not receive this option|K/SK: द्युतित्वा/द्योतित्वा; दिद्युतिषते/दिद्योतिषते; देवित्वा control|
|1.2.27|Vowel-duration designations|A single vowel has the phonetic duration corresponding to उ, ऊ or उ३ in the source system|Assign ह्रस्व, दीर्घ or प्लुत respectively|These are phonetic measure classes, not durations of consonant clusters or multi-vowel strings and not a physical clock for the native carrier|K/SK: दधि; कुमारी; देवदत्त३; तितउच्छत्रम् control|
