## Phonetic and lexical designations

**1.1.7–10:** a consonant group, a single articulated sound and a mutual
सवर्ण relation are different objects. संयोग designates the group, not each
member separately. Joint pronunciation and vowel absence are phonological
conditions rather than a test on Unicode character adjacency. अनुनासिक uses
the source's joint mouth/nose articulation. सवर्ण requires both place and
internal effort, with the subsequent vowel/consonant exclusion retained.

Kaumudī's 1.1.9 distinguishes the open process-stage अ from its closed
pronunciation-stage description. The source also separately admits ऋ/ऌ
सवर्ण behavior. These assumptions cannot be replaced by one unspecified
modern acoustic-distance score or by equality of spelling. Source phonetic
classification is evidence supplied to the contract.

**1.1.11:** dual origin matters. A feminine singular ending in ई does not
gain प्रगृह्य merely from that vowel. Kāśikā reports मणीव/दम्पतीव-type
exceptions; Nyāsa explains them with a restricted Śākalya option. Kaumudī
instead analyzes the relevant मणीव example through व/वा in the sense of
इव. Preserve these source policies rather than silently rewriting the source
word. Nyāsa also discusses why an eligible underlying dual does not simply
lose the designation when a later प्लुत reading is involved.

**1.1.24:** षट् is a technical numeral designation. Kāśikā uses the prescribed
ending identity to exclude later नुम्-bearing शतानि/सहस्राणि. Kaumudī
explains the same exclusion by the संनिपात restriction. Preserve the
derivational identity; do not infer the class from the last few visible letters.

**1.1.27:** the archive supplies the complete local सर्वादि list and its
sense restrictions. The subsequent controls 28–36 show that compound class,
relative direction, established naming, kin/wealth senses and the particular
जस् consumer can alter the result. For example, सम in the sense of सर्व
differs from ordinary equal/level सम. A supplied list-membership decision
must also retain these local conditions. The contracts do not replace
lexicography or infer a person's identity from a name.

**1.1.38:** असर्वविभक्ति is a grammatical limitation, not missing data in an
observed corpus. Nyāsa explains the restricted case/number behavior of the
examples. Kaumudī adds the operative enumeration: **तसिलादि before पाशप्;
शस् onward before समासान्त; अम्; आम्; कृत्वोर्थ; तसि/वति; नानाञ्**.
The adopted bounded clause retains this qualification so that
पचतिकल्पम्/पचतिरूपम् are not falsely designated अव्यय. Full family
recognition remains a declared lexical/morphological input.

**1.1.41–42:** an अव्ययीभाव compound and the शि replacement are the actual
designation bearers. अव्यय does not erase all downstream exceptions: the
source's तोसुन्/कसुन् control keeps its genitive relation. सर्वनामस्थान
for शि does not make its nominal base a pronoun.

## Interpretation and sound-reference scope

**1.1.49–50:** a genitive with an undetermined relation receives substitution
interpretation. The nearest-substitute rule then selects among substitutes
already licensed by another prescription. Nyāsa expressly denies that 50
independently creates endless substitutions of every word. Place, sense,
quality and duration are the source's relevant affinities; place has the stated
priority where affinities compete. No universal numeric metric is constructed.

**1.1.55:** अनेकाल्/शित् determines replacement extent relative to the
specified original. Read it with the final-sound and initial-sound controls
52–54; a marker-specific instruction is not discarded merely because another
replacement has more than one sound.

**1.1.63:** retain which deleted affix produced the relevant अङ्ग relation
and triggered the operation. Nyāsa explicitly includes affected base accent
operations outside the textual अङ्ग authority. Conversely, पद designation
and its proper consequences can survive because they address a different
object. Deletion by लुक्/श्लु/लुप् is not equivalent to ordinary लोप: the
source's कार्यते example retains the latter history and its appropriate effect.

**1.1.64–67:** टि and उपधा use the addressed expression's boundaries. The
locative and ablative interpretation rules require the adjacent preceding or
following eligible target respectively. They do not turn every sentence case
ending into a grammar operation and do not license skipping intervening sounds.

**1.1.69:** Nyāsa explicitly calls this a **संज्ञासूत्र**, not a परिभाषा.
Use the later ण् endpoint for अण्. अप्रत्यय excludes prescribed outputs:
Nyāsa describes both an exclusion through च and another derivation from
the broader “prescribed” sense of प्रत्यय; Kaumudī explicitly adopts the
latter description. These routes agree that suffixes, substitutes and the
stated augment cases must not acquire arbitrary सवर्ण output lengths.

**1.1.70–71:** the former designates same-duration classes for the appropriate
त्-qualified sound, while the latter defines abbreviation membership from an
admitted inventory and actual इत् endpoint. Nyāsa explicitly treats 71 as a
designation rule and explains why the denoted sounds are individual members,
not one giant phonological cluster. The terminator is part of the name, not
one of its denoted sounds. For record typing, 69–71 are designation contracts;
72 is an interpretation contract governing reference extension.

**1.1.72:** retain the reported compound/affix-prescription restriction and
the उगित्/वर्ण exceptions. The additional अल्-based environmental discussion
concerns beginning-with scope. None of these clauses licenses an unrestricted
substring search over names or arbitrary compounds.

## कित् behavior and nominal categories

**1.2.5–26 selected gaps:** distinguish literal इत् markers from prescribed
कित् behavior and keep the exact target affix. 5 retains अपित्; 6 supplies
separate exceptions. 8 supplies specific compulsory सन्/क्त्वा behavior.
9 drops the inherited क्त्वा; 10 requires the इक् relation before the final
consonantal material, including source-admitted consonant-group cases.
17 couples इ substitution and सिच् behavior in the A-ending environment;
the घु list explicitly excludes the named दाप्/दैप् roots.

18's सेट्-क्त्वा default does not override specific compulsory or optional
rules. 19 additionally requires निष्ठा and its specified root entry. 26
requires the whole conjunction: consonant initial, रल् final, इ/उ उपधा,
actual इट् and the specified सन्/क्त्वा target. Unknown marker or augment
history cannot be resolved from a similar surface word.

**1.2.27** classifies one vowel by phonetic measure. It does not classify
an entire multi-sound expression and does not provide a physical clock for
the native operator/history construction.

**1.2.42–43:** कर्मधारय requires an already established तत्पुरुष and shared
reference. प्रथमानिर्दिष्ट in 43 refers to the applicable **compound rule's
wording**, not a sentence's surface nominative. Nyāsa stresses that
उपसर्जन is relative to its particular governing relation; राजकुमारी
nesting must not mark every component subordinate merely because some
unrelated compound rule can mention its case. Its discussion also preserves
the stated special conventional cases rather than reducing the designation
to a universal semantic-head heuristic.

**1.2.45:** अर्थवत् means having signification. Nyāsa expressly includes
absence expressions such as **शशविषाण**; the designation does not certify
the external existence or truth of what is said. The प्रत्यय exclusion
also reaches the relevant affix-ended expressions, as Kaumudī and Nyāsa
explain. Control46 separately establishes the kṛt, taddhita and compound
cases, and limits an arbitrary meaningful sentence from becoming one
प्रातिपदिक. The reported निपात exception is retained. Meaning detection
and external truth verification remain open inputs, not achievements of this
designation rule.
