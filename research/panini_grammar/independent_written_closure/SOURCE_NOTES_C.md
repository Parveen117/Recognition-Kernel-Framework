## Root identity and substitution history

2.4.35 uses an intended आर्धधातुक domain. A final displayed vowel may be
either pronunciation material or an operative part of the substitute, and
the two cannot be conflated. At 36, जग्धि's इ is pronunciation-only; at
46–47 and 53, the same is true of गमि/वचि. At 42, by contrast, वध is
**अ-final**. Nyāsa explains that the author's use of pronunciation इ for
consonant-final substitutes distinguishes this case. The deleted अ's history
prevents treating the result as originally consonant-final for the cited
वृद्धि consumer. Kāśikā and Nyāsa give different embedded rule references
for that वृद्धि discussion; their literal references remain unchanged.

The आर्धधातुक लिङ् at 42 is the blessing-domain लिङ् under 3.4.116, not
ordinary विधिलिङ्. 43–44 separately provide the aorist substitution and its
qualified A optionality. 45's repeated लुङ् prevents that A-only option from
continuing into इण् → गा. At 37, the **ऌ marker** on घसॢ remains operative
for 3.1.55. At 53, ब्रू provenance can still support the stated A behavior
after replacement by वच्.

## Desire and intensive formation

3.1.7 requires the desired action to be the object of wishing and its actor
to be the wisher. Prefixes are outside the selected root address. The
optional suffix coexists with the analytic clause; it is not an optional
change of participant identity. The ban on a second **इच्छासन्** leaves
the explicitly reported lexical सन् cases different. The आशङ्का extension
is a separate source meaning, not a claim that a river bank literally wishes
to fall. 3.4.114 also preserves the distinction between this root-prescribed
सन् and earlier lexical सन् when assigning आर्धधातुक status.

3.1.22's repetition/intensity sense has its own one-vowel and consonant-initial
gates, plus named exceptions and exclusions. It must not be equated with
every use of the word समभिहार elsewhere in the grammar.

## आम् policy, noncircular admission and auxiliary ownership

**3.1.35.** The literal prescription refers to कास् and प्रत्ययान्त roots.
Kāśikā reports the अनेकाच् supplement; Kaumudī explains replacing the
प्रत्यय wording by अनेकाच्. Nyāsa discusses why literal प्रत्ययान्त alone
can overgenerate आम् for one-vowel denominatives while missing चकास्,
दरिद्रा and the reported चुलुम्प्. It also presents an alternative use of
36's च plus व्यवस्थितविभाषा. The contract adopts the explicitly declared
अनेकाच् admission policy for the bounded cases. The alternatives remain
source policies, not silent textual corrections or claims of consensus.

अमन्त्र is a register domain: Nyāsa admits ordinary language and Brāhmaṇa
while excluding मन्त्र. It does not mean merely that no mantra word follows.
The आम् affix's म is not treated as a मित् instruction to insert the affix
inside the root. Nyāsa records alternative explanations of that identity;
the literal source is preserved without choosing an undocumented spelling.

**3.1.36.** इच् is a pratyāhāra, not just the letter इ. Nyāsa rejects a
circular use of guṇa produced by लिट् to create गुरुमत् status and thereby
license आम् that removes that same primary लिट्. Augment-based heaviness
has a separately admitted explanation, illustrated by the ऋच्छ् exclusion
and उच्छ् discussion. Thus the input must retain the origin of its syllable
weight; a flat surface-heavy test is insufficient. ऊर्णु's exclusion is also
explained through explicit alternative commentary routes.

**3.1.39.** Nyāsa identifies **कार्य-अतिदेश**, specifically द्वित्व and
इत्त्व, in श्लुवत्. The transfer does not turn आम् into actual श्लु deletion.
The ordinary perfect and the आम् branch retain different histories.

**3.1.40.** The admitted auxiliary set is कृ/भू/अस्. The primary लिट्
following आम् is removed by 2.4.81; the auxiliary has its own लिट्. Nyāsa
requires the auxiliary after and without an intervening unrelated word.
Auxiliary अस् is expressly protected from 2.4.52's भू substitution.
1.3.63 transfers the relevant pada behavior specifically to कृ, not to the
entire three-member set. Kaumudī excludes सम्पद् from the auxiliary set
through its meaning relation; Nyāsa explains that its prefixed aggregate is
not the required bare धातु. The same admitted set does not erase these
different explanatory policies.
