source policy or semantic relation remains Open. A supplied assertion's
signification does not establish its external truth; the अर्थवत् predicate
is not a fact-checking oracle. Source examples and historical labels are
grammatical evidence, not inferred descriptions of present-day people.

## D2. Scoped interpretation and selection

55 and preceding/following adjacency at 66/67 address different parts of a
rule. They do not erase other locally supplied relations. टि and उपधा have
distinct segment definitions and consumers. A marker or visually equal sound
does not identify a selector with the output it prescribes.

1.1.50 uses source-defined resemblance criteria and precedence, not a universal
numerical distance. 1.1.63 blocks specified stem operations after लुमत्
deletion, not every query about a deleted affix. 1.1.72 has explicit limits
on extension to words ending in a named element. The general rule is not a
license for arbitrary substring matching.
