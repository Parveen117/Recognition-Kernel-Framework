already-written clauses, with the following concrete records and decisions:

histories, with explicit Open exits and a named downstream obligation for every
unrealized step. It adds **zero new catalogue IDs**. A general parser, lexicon,
scheduler or runtime implementation requires its own later construction.

## F4. Lexical and executable frontiers

Maintain separate supplied/recognized registers for gaṇa membership, root
homonyms, lexical meanings, compounds and the Uṇādi corpus. A list in a
commentary is evidence, not an automatically complete recognizer. Preserve the
distinction between signification and external truth throughout the interface.

Executable integration has one home: `operator_foundation/` on main. The
existing full carrier, typed cuts, pairing, dagger, tensor/history and
zero-reading compatibility remain its construction boundary. No second engine
is created here. Grammar tests, generators, replay and navigation/operator
suites remain deferred under the written-first instruction.
