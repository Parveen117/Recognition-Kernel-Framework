## C2. Similarity and conflict are different questions

At 1.1.50 first retain the admitted substitute family, then the applicable
place/meaning/quality/duration comparison. The source gives place priority
where multiple kinds of resemblance compete, and a most-similar requirement
for the illustrated combined features. This is a source-qualified selection
relation, not an invented Jaccard score or proof that all grammatical choices
share one metric. An unresolved comparison stays Open.

1.4.2 is applied only after independently admitting both conflicting operations
and determining equal strength in the source's domain. General/special,
obligatory/non-obligatory and internal/external precedence are not erased by
a global maximum-ID operation. The वृक्षेभ्यः witness concerns the specified
simultaneous collision of lengthening and ए behavior. The source policies for
later reapplication remain separate; selecting a winner does not by itself
prove a global termination or confluence theorem.

## C3. Designations and deletion are consumer dependent

The distinct 1.2.44 designation expressly excludes that ordering effect.
Store the designation's origin and allowed consumer; one untyped global flag

At 1.1.63 retain both the deleted affix and its लुमत् deletion cause. The
specified अङ्ग operations are blocked, while unrelated observations do not
automatically disappear. Deletion is inactivity of the addressed token, not
erasure of its provenance. A partial deletion or another kind of lopa does
not satisfy this gate merely because something is absent from the string.

The ordinary आर्धधातुक clauses at 3.4.115/116 differ from the qualified
consumer-specific Vedic behavior at 117. The blessing/non-blessing liṅ distinction
must remain present; no operation is authorized by the string लिङ् alone.

At 1.2.45, अर्थवत् concerns signification. Nyāsa's absence and शशविषाण
examples make clear that a nominal expression need not name an existing object.
Grammatical admission therefore does not certify the truth of the sentence
in which the expression appears.
