# Eight joined two-hetu active panels and one lexical witness

## A joined lexical control with addressed source layers

The mantra at Vājasaneyī 17.19 has sam before an
intervening instrumental and then the root *dham*.
Uvaṭa and Mahīdhara give explicit joined *saṃgamayati*
in their commentary. Uvaṭa connects it with the arms in
the instrumental and also offers noncausal motion
glosses. Mahīdhara supplies both a conjunction-reaching
noncausal gloss and an explicitly ṇic analysis in which
Viśvakarman brings beings together with the means
indicated by the instrumental expressions. The latter
has accusative *jīvān*. Keep these alternatives and
arguments in their respective commentarial readings.

This identifies a joined P word in the commentary
and an independently addressed meaning for sam–gam.
It does not place gam in the mantra or supply A for a
causative by using a noncausal gloss. The single visible

H1. Its agreement with a joined P3sg laṭ word below is
a surface and lexical control. The two-hetu finite
profiles here remain written compositions.
