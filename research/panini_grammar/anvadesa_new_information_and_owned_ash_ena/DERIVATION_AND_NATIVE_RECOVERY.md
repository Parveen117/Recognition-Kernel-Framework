## Same referent is necessary; later mention alone is insufficient

Devadatta followed by a different Yajñadatta does not qualify, however
close the mentions are. K2.4.34 further requires a genuinely informative
first use and a different informative continuation. Its ayam daṇḍo
harānena control makes the distinction: merely identifying the object and
then giving one instruction is not the required two informative uses.
Do not infer admission from surface chronology, co-occurrence, a pronoun
string or an imperative ending alone.

**apūrvaṃ bodhayitum**, conveying new information. Hence this is not a
requirement that both predicates be commands. The K night/day study and
conduct/property controls are declarative predications and remain positive.

than the needed new assertion; mood alone does not settle that evidence.
