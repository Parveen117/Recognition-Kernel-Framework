# PG895 · Derivation and native recovery

## Seven source-listed directional inputs

Śabdamañjarī lists `para, avara, dakṣiṇa, uttara, apara, adhara,
antara` with `pūrva` in its directional class. Entry 94 extends the M
`pūrva` pattern, including both plural alternatives, to `para` and the
remaining sixth-group members; entries 95 and 96 extend the F and N patterns.
The four source facts license exactly seven directional sense requests.
Non-directional, temporal, relational, proper-name and compound readings do
not inherit the panel.

## Literal positions, forks and ownership

Each stem contributes nine gender-number vocative positions. M singular uses
short-*a* sambuddhi, dual `au`, and plural has two histories: pronominal
`jas → śī` gives `-e`, while the source-retained adjectival channel gives
`-āḥ`. F `-ā` stems give `-e/-e/-āḥ`. N singular uses ordinary `am`
plus m-lopa and dual gives `-e`. In N plural short `śi`, `num` and length
give `-āni`; the six r-bearing stems then license retroflex `-āṇi`, whereas
`dakṣiṇa` has no preceding r or ṣ trigger for the newly inserted nasal and
therefore gives `dakṣiṇāni`. Thus 63 positions carry 70 readings. Equal
surfaces never erase lemma, sense, gender-number or original ending.
