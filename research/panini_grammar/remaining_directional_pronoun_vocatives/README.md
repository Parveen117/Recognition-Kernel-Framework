# PG895 · Seven remaining directional-pronoun vocative panels

Monty · 25 November 2026

Śabdamañjarī's directional list names `para, avara, dakṣiṇa, uttara,
apara, adhara, antara` after `pūrva`. Entries 94–96 explicitly extend the
same M/F/N patterns to `para` and the remaining sixth-group members. The
seven stems therefore add **63 nominal positions/70 readings**. Each M
plural retains two source-labelled readings (`-e / -āḥ`), so seven extra
readings are preserved rather than normalized away.

This stage has seven morphology constructions/seventy interfaces, one shared
address construction/seventy interfaces, four source observations and three
policy decisions. Neuter singular uses ordinary `am` plus m-lopa; N plural
retains `dakṣiṇāni` against the six r-triggered `-āṇi` outputs. Identical
`-e` surfaces keep distinct gender-number and ending histories.
