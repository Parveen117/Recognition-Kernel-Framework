## S1. Edition and evidence

The primary edition is `ashtadhyayi-com/data` at
[`5744762f010d677cfb43f347a42d02796cf615d6`](https://github.com/ashtadhyayi-com/data/tree/5744762f010d677cfb43f347a42d02796cf615d6).
Its `sutraani/data.txt` contains **3,983 numbered records**. PG36 preserves
that denominator and its numbering; it does not infer completeness from
the number or conflate alternative traditional counts with this inventory.

The literal [archive](SOURCE_EXCERPTS.json) copies sūtra text and complete
Kāśikā/Vasu fields for the 303 operative IDs plus inherited 4.1.76/82 and
the cross-rule 7.1.2 control. Three Nyāsa fields (4.4.117, 4.4.132,
5.1.89) and one Kaumudī field (5.1.66) are targeted additional controls.
All bytes come from `git show` at the edition pin. Spelling, accents, HTML,
damaged typography and contradictory wording remain literal.

Working interpretations in the authored tables are separate from the
archive. Kāśikā supplies the ordinary policy; the explicitly labelled
Nyāsa/Kaumudī refinements below do not silently rewrite it. The live
[4.4.117 page](https://ashtadhyayi.com/sutraani/4/4/117) and
[Nyāsa 5.1](https://ashtadhyayi.com/nyaas/18?scroll=nyaas-51089)
were also cross-checked, but the archived pinned text is the reproducible
evidence. Live prose is not substituted for the fixed primary fields.

## S3. Genuine variants and targeted refinements

**4.4.117: घ / घच्.** Nyāsa at 117 explicitly contrasts घे and घनि,
and the live exposition segments घ + छ. The adopted policy follows that
reading, retaining four histories with protected यत् and carried घन्.
However, K at **7.1.2** expressly refers to `घचश्चित्करणम्`, agreeing
with Vasu's घच्. It is therefore wrong to dismiss घच् as an invented
affix or a simple English typo. The cross-rule marker/accent reconciliation
remains Open. The alternative must not be silently added as a fifth branch
of a supposedly settled single policy.

**4.4.132: carried यल् / literal यत्.** K says चकाराद् यत्, while
131 explicitly selects यल् and Nyāsa discusses the unpaired two-base,
two-suffix construction with यल्/ख. PG36 safely constructs ख for both
bases and retains the separately established 131 यल् route. Whether the
literal यत् wording adds or substitutes another marker-sensitive route
is left unresolved. No Cartesian one-base/one-affix pairing is imposed.

**5.1.66: य / यत्.** K literally has यः प्रत्ययः; the headline in this
edition names no suffix. Kaumudī explicitly reads `एभ्यो यत् स्यात्`,
and Vasu reports both explicit and inherited यत् versions. PG36 adopts
यत् under the named Kaumudī control policy, while preserving K's exact
wording. It does not identify the two original affixes.

**5.1.89: obligatory deletion is not all sentient duration.** K's prose
is broad. Nyāsa explicitly raises the counterexample of a person hired
for two years and limits the obligatory लुक् to **भूत**. PG36 adopts
that restriction. Non-भूत engagement/hiring inputs still use 88's
optional paths. The preserved simple दारक example alone would not expose
this distinction.

**5.1.121: damaged negative-compound segmentation.** The catalogue's
continuous headline and K's exception outputs are not silently normalized
into a speculative universal member list. The eight attested output
readings are printed in the rule table. Unreviewed base segmentation,
especially the युध/बुध-looking area, stays Open. Negative तत्पुरुष and
negative बहुव्रीहि still have an explicit constructed distinction.

Other labelled alternatives include split मांसौदन at 4.4.67, the two
load readings at 5.1.50, completion continuation at 85/86, person/vow
analyses at 94, and ACC gestation versus LOC childbirth at 5.2.12.
The broader imminent-event reading at 5.2.13 is not automatically united
with the core childbirth reading. A source variant is not the same as
grammatical optionality within one settled policy.

## S4. Boundaries kept open

Full accent, phonology, feminine formation and declension are not derived
by selecting an affix. Qualified compound extensions cited in Vasu but not
expanded in K remain attributed extensions, not a general तदन्त algorithm.
Unreviewed lexical intersections, complete gaṇa normalization and the
remaining 4.1/5.2–5.4 families are open. The sparse named witnesses do not
constitute an empirical accuracy study or a universal no-hallucination
result. [Pins](SOURCE_PINS.json) identify the bytes; [coverage](COVERAGE.json)
identifies the bounded construction credit.
