# PG36 — Instrument, occupation, allocation and residence

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These are bounded written selection contracts, not executed word derivations.
Read [DOMAIN](DOMAIN.md) and [CONSTRUCTION](CONSTRUCTION.md) first. INSTR,
ACC, GEN, DAT, LOC and NOM name the supporting semantic case, not a command
to keep an inflected ending in the derived stem. A gaṇa gate requires its
literal Kāśikā member and attached condition. Witnesses are source readouts,
not new paradigm positions. `Select` preserves the original upadeśa.
