\[
B_{36}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le36}\rangle,
\qquad
M_{36,\lambda}=(M_{35,\lambda}\otimes_{B_{35}} B_{36})
 \oplus\bigoplus_{h\in H_{36}} e_hB_{36}.

coordinates are disjoint from the inherited corner. The algebra retraction

distinct inherited vectors. Extend the old pairing by scalar extension
and use \(\langle x,y\rangle=\sum_hx_h^*y_h\) on the new coordinates,
with old/new summands orthogonal. Coordinate units prove nondegeneracy on
the new summand. No positivity or Hilbert metric is needed for that claim.

scope records, every source policy and evidence decision, exact affix
tokens, causal compound edges, all selected branches and operation targets.

## N2. Evidence cuts and semantic reset

For a decided predicate g use the self-adjoint coordinate cuts
\(P_g^T,P_g^F,P_g^?\), mutually orthogonal and summing locally to I.
A missing price-role, sentience, gaṇa, name-status or source-policy witness
is in the ? cut. Residual affix selection is defined only after the
relevant admission is T and every decisive suppressing exception is
settled. Erasing evidence therefore cannot create a newly certified
default path. This is a property of the declared evidence relation,
not a truth theorem about externally supplied annotations.

Scope cuts refer to the **current** view. In particular the A.Deserving
to Q.Perform transition at 5.1.72 has target in \(P_Q\), so an A-only
selector composed after it is zero because \(P_AP_Q=0\). Past A history
remains readable. The same construction separates the P material/sufficiency
family from the earlier Y view and separates abstract state/activity
from a literal comparison input.

## N3. Causal-compound proposition

Let c be a compound token and a the currently selected affix token.
The 5.1.28 domain contains an evidence edge
\(\mathsf{OccasionedBy}(c,a)\), or the separately admitted अध्यर्ध
clause, together with non-name status and the A scope. A bare predicate
\(\mathsf{Dvigu}(c)\) is insufficient.

**Proposition.** The ordinary 28-deletion map cannot delete a new affix
merely because its input contains an old द्विगु constituent.

**Proof.** In the tertiary द्विशूर्प purchase, the old compound c was
occasioned by a different affix token \(a_0\). The new affix a has no
\(\mathsf{OccasionedBy}(c,a)\) witness. Its input is outside the deletion
map's domain; 20 also excludes the automatic शूर्प-अञ् reuse. The
ordinary new ठञ् path remains. In the direct two-शूर्प purchase the
edge is present and 28 applies to the selected token. Retaining the edge
distinguishes the two inputs even if a string observer forgets it. □

This is an ownership/type result on the written relation, not an executed
proof of all compound formation in Sanskrit.

## N4. Dependent branch proposition

For admitted q define \(J(q)\) as the source-labelled surviving clauses.
The constructor's output histories are the dependent disjoint union
\(\coprod_{j\in J(q)}(q,j,\mathsf{ops}(q,j))\). An operation belongs
to its branch; it is not an independent optional bit.

**Proposition.** This construction excludes the unlicensed Cartesian
products in 5.1.54/55 and preserves distinct deleted histories in 88/89.

**Proof.** For 54, J consists of retained ष्ठन्, retained ख, and
ठञ् followed by its prescribed लुक्. Neither deletion of ष्ठन् nor
deletion of ख has a clause, so neither is a member of the output union.
At 55, retained ठञ् is one further clause. For 88, in contrast, optional
deletion is authorized on each of ख and ठञ्; the two deleted records
remain distinct despite the coincident surface word. Under the adopted
Nyāsa refinement, 89 removes retained branches only on the sentient
भूत fibre, not on a hired human's duration fibre. □

The same proof handles ordered versus unpaired lists: 4.4.91 has eight
base/sense pairs, 5.1.41 has two base/affix pairs, and 5.1.69 gives both
affixes for each base. They are different finite dependent domains.

## N6. Typed zero-recovery theorem

For each legal event \(e:h\to h'\), set
\(U_e(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a\). Branch sums use distinct target histories.
Let \(\epsilon:B_{36}\to C_\Sigma\) take the empty-word coefficient,
and let \(\pi_0\) apply it to coefficients while retaining **every**
history coordinate. In a free algebra with nonnegative word degree, a
product has an empty word only from two empty words. Therefore ε is a
unital dagger homomorphism.

On a fixed finite family of admitted inputs and source-labelled clauses,
write \(\mathsf{Select}_{36,\lambda}\) for C1's written selection map.
Then

\[
\pi_{0,\mathrm{out}}\mathsf{Select}_{36,\lambda}
 =\mathsf{Select}_{36,0}\pi_{0,\mathrm{in}},
\qquad
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_{B_{36}}
 =\langle\pi_0x,\pi_0y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

**Proof.** Case, scope, policy, evidence, quantity and temporal cuts
depend on the retained history, hence commute with zero-reading. Each
legal event factor satisfies \(\epsilon(1+z_e)=1\). Multiplicativity
and additivity preserve every finite composition and branch sum, and
dagger compatibility gives the pairing identity. On unit-start legal
paths the specified selection coefficient is one, including the original
affix and every deletion address. Open remains Open. Arbitrary signed
inputs can cancel, so support preservation for all vectors is not claimed. □

The recovered object is the declared selection relation for these bounded
inputs. It is neither complete surface Sanskrit nor an implementation
verification. No physical quantum-gate claim, empirical performance result
or universal hallucination-free guarantee follows from this theorem.
