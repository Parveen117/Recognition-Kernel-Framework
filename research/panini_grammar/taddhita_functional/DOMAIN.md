# PG36 — Domain of functional taddhita selection

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

## D1. Constructed layer and count

PG36 constructs **303 source-relative written contracts**: 4.4.1–144,
5.1.1–136 and 5.2.1–23. Each numbered row owns a semantic admission,
selector, restriction, scope boundary, affix operation or finite stipulation.
The count requires the authored gate/action/priority/witness row; an entry
in the literal archive alone earns no construction credit.

Against PG35's 1,046-ID combined union, 5.1.122 is already present. Thus
PG36 adds **302 distinct IDs**, taking that union to **1,348/3,983**.
All 303 are new in the independent written register: **694 → 997**.
The combined union includes earlier companion-labelled coverage and is not
the same quantity as independently written contracts. Neither is a count
of completely implemented sūtras. No overall completion percentage follows.

This continues PG35's selection layer. It does **not** continue PG31–34's
rate of complete finite word positions: PG36 adds **0 positions and 0
counted branch readings**, leaving **11,552** written positions. Source
words in the tables are witnesses/controls, not newly completed paradigms.
Complete phonology, accent, feminine formation, nominal inflection and
runtime verification remain separate obligations. Tests/replay stay deferred.

## D2. Evidence-bearing input

Continue the [PG35 input](../taddhita_relations/DOMAIN.md), adding explicit
fields where the present families distinguish otherwise similar strings:

\[
q=(x,\rho,\kappa,d,r,p,e,\mu,\tau).
\]

Here x is the nominal identity and retained derivation tree; ρ is the
intended relation; κ its supporting semantic case; d the target denotation;
r the register; p the adopted source policy; and e the evidence tokens.
The additional fields are:

- **μ, measure structure:** number versus technical volume/extent
  (परिमाण), weight (उन्मान), length (प्रमाण), price, unit and quantity;
  whether the target itself essentially is the stated number; whether a
  compound is a causal द्विगु formed for this very affix or an inherited
  already-derived base; and whether a prior affix underwent लुक्.
- **τ, temporal/referent structure:** completion interval versus duration
  of engagement/service/existence, past or future, age versus non-age,
  sentient referent, former-state predicate, and named-grain identity.

These are supplied with evidence. The grammar does not infer a price from
an arbitrary numeral, turn a historical unit into modern currency or
distance, decide an ethical judgment, or certify a traditional omen.
Meaning annotations such as गर्ह्य, धर्म्य, अकार्य and the doctrine in
आस्तिक are statements about the source's usage, not factual endorsements.

Every decisive predicate has T, F or ?. **? is Open, not F**. Missing
name-status, register, compound class, membership, quantity unit or sense
cannot license the residual suffix by absence of evidence. Rejection of
one candidate is `NOT_THIS_RULE`, not a universal rejection of Sanskrit.

## D5. Original affix and member identity

Retain the original upadeśa and owned markers, even when surface readouts
coincide: य/यत्/यन्/यप्/यल्/यक्/ञ्य/ण्य/ण्यत्; अण्/अञ्/ण;
ठक्/ठञ्/ठन्/ठच्/ष्ठन्/ष्ठच्/ष्ठल्/टिठन्; घ/घन्/घस्;
ख/खञ्; ड्यण्/ड्यत्/ड्य; वति/मतुप्; इमनिच्/ष्यञ्/वुञ्.
This list emphasizes distinctions; it is not a complete suffix catalogue.

At 4.4.7 the ṣ is **sandhi**, so the affix is ठन्. At 4.4.10,
5.1.46 and 5.1.54, ष् is a real marker. At 4.4.117 the adopted
Nyāsa-at-117 policy has **घ + छ**, while K at 7.1.2 explicitly says
घच्; this cross-commentary marker issue remains separately unresolved.
At 5.1.25/4.4.67, इ in the
notation टिठन् is a pronunciation aid; marker ownership is still explicit.

Gaṇa membership uses `(edition, source file, source key, ordinal, literal
member, member condition)`. Split-member readings, prefix conditions and
substitutions such as नाभि→नभ or विराग→विरङ्ग belong to that entry,
not to every similar-looking base. ब्राह्मणादि as ākṛtigaṇa requires a
witnessed usage. PG36 supplies a bounded interface plus literal source;
it does not claim a complete normalized Gaṇapāṭha or semantic classifier.

## D6. Source policies and output status

The ordinary Kāśikā exposition is the working policy. Vasu is a historical
translation/control; Nyāsa supplies targeted checks at 4.4.117/132 and
5.1.89, and Kaumudī resolves the adopted affix at 5.1.66.
All archived fields remain literal, including typographical damage.
[SOURCE_REVIEW](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) records corrections and unresolved markers.
Optionality is a positive branch relation after admission, whereas an
unresolved textual choice is Open or an explicitly selected source policy.
These must not share a Boolean `optional` flag.

The result is a finite collection of owned **selection histories**:
input evidence, relation/case, original affix, source clause, substitutions,
augment/elision targets, scope decisions and pending realization work.
Status is `BOUNDED_WRITTEN_CONTRACT_NOT_EXECUTED`. The JSON register is a
transcription of the authored specifications, not an executable compiler.
No output is called `COMPLETE_WORD` or `EXECUTED_PASS` here.
