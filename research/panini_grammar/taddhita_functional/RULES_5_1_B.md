## Two source-sensitive boundaries

At **5.1.66**, the archived Kāśikā literally says `यः प्रत्ययः`, while
the separately pinned Kaumudī explicitly says `एभ्यो यत् स्यात्`.
PG36 adopts the latter original affix, agreeing with Vasu's reported
explicit/inherited यत् readings. It preserves K's divergent wording;
one surface spelling is not proof that य and यत् are interchangeable.

At **5.1.121**, source catalogue segmentation is damaged around the excluded
negative compounds. The adopted Kāśikā expressly illustrates eight positive
exception outputs: आचतुर्य, आसंगत्य, आलवण्य, आवट्य, आबुध्य, आकत्य,
आरस्य and आलस्य. These support the finite exception readings; unreviewed
spellings/segmentations cannot enter by approximate matching.
