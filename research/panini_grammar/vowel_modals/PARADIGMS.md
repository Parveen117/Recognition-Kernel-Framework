## P5. Blessing-loṭ refinement

B first retains all ordinary P1–P3 readings on its own āśīs inputs.
At **P 3sg and 2sg**, additionally choose the following tāt set:

- Each P short-u root Ru: **Rutāt**; for ru/tu/stu also **Ruvītāt**.
- Each ā root: its exact tāt form in P3.
- Iṇ: **itāt**; vī: **vītāt**.

No A tāt is admitted. Ūrṇu's weak tāt is only ūrṇutāt; its
ordinary-tu o/au option is not transported. The three īṭ roots each
have four B 3sg readings and four B 2sg readings. For ru:

\[
 B_{3sg}=\{rautu,ravītu,rutāt,ruvītāt\},\qquad
 B_{2sg}=\{ruhi,ruvīhi,rutāt,ruvītāt\}.
\]

Ūrṇu B 3sg has three: ūrṇautu, ūrṇotu, ūrṇutāt; its B
2sg has two. The other 24 P roots have one additional tāt per target.
Equivalently the 28 P profiles supply 56 base tāt readings, and the
three īṭ profiles supply six more: **62 added B readings**.
B therefore has **288 positions /369 readings**.
