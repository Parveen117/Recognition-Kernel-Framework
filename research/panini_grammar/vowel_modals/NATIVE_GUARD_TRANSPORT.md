## N2. Ordered ending refinement and conditional branching

For a completed position x, let E_x be its ordinary ending and, where
B permits it, its tāt alternative. Complete modal/āṭ/jh refinement
inside that chosen ending. Let A_(x,e) be {0,īṭ} when M1's guard is
true and {0} otherwise. Then query strengthening on the *resulting*
actual right context. Let V_(x,e,a) be the singleton prescribed stem
operation, except for ordinary strong ūrṇu P tu, where it has two.

The reading relation is the dependent sum

\[
 \mathcal R_x=
 \coprod_{e\in E_x}\coprod_{a\in A_{(x,e)}}
 \{(e,a)\}\times V_{(x,e,a)}.
\]

Its events have typed grades: ending refinement → augment choice →
actual-context query → root operation → sound readout → terminal.
A skipped optional augment has its own decision record; it is not an
unobserved failure. Missing source premises instead reach Open.

**Theorem N2 (query transport).** On D, this dependent construction
preserves the source/owner interface and yields P's complete relation.
Selecting a single root-strength result before augment refinement cannot
supply that relation while keeping the same local strength contract.

**Proof.** D4 fixes the ending and effective markers. M1 enumerates
all actual hal/vowel contexts and produces its finite augment fibre.
The first sound is then either the chosen ī or the previously resolved
ending/augment sound. M2–M5 exhaust the consequent root queries;
S supplies one readout per branch. In particular ru P I 3sg requires
rau on the absent branch and ro on the īṭ branch. A single unchanged
preselected result cannot equal both. The source construction instead
retains the augment decision before asking that query. □

This statement does not assert a commutator of two untyped rule names.
Reversing the declared grades is not automatically a lawful grammatical
composition. Nor are the two ūrṇu choices combined with an īṭ branch
that its exact lexical guard excludes.

## N3. A sharp repair for the stated two-history observer

On the post-augment, pre-strength ru P I 3sg block let h_0 have no
īṭ and h_1 have ending-owned īṭ. Both retain ru, original/current
tu and effective pit. Define the coarse observation

\[
 q(e_0a+e_1b)=e_w(a+b),\qquad H=\operatorname{diag}(1,0).
\]

H is the native cut for actual hal first. The observer q forgets the
augment choice and actual first sound. For v=e_0−e_1, qv=0 but
qHv=e_w≠0. Consequently there is no linear cut H̄ with qH=H̄q.
The guṇa/vṛddhi eligibility query cannot descend through this observer.
This obstruction holds over B_26 before zero-specialization.

An explicit repair is

\[
 q^+(a,b)=(a+b,b),\qquad (q^+)^{-1}(s,b)=(s-b,b),
 \qquad \widehat H(s,b)=(s-b,0).
\]

Then q^+H=Ĥq^+. The complement transports to (b,b), and these two
transported cuts sum to the identity. Pull back the native pairing:

\[
 \langle(s,b),(t,c)\rangle_+=(s-b)^*(t-c)+b^*c.
\]

They are self-adjoint for this pairing. They need not be self-adjoint
for an unrelated Euclidean pairing chosen after observation.

**Proposition N3.** At zero, among linear refinements of this q that
permit descent of H, total observation dimension two is necessary and
sufficient: exactly one additional scalar coordinate repairs this block.

**Proof.** The only nonzero vectors of ker(q) are scalar multiples of
(1,−1), and no such vector is stable under H. The kernel of a valid
refinement must lie in ker(q) and be H-invariant, so it is zero.
Dimension is therefore at least two. The displayed invertible q^+ gives
two coordinates already over B_26 and specializes to that bound. □

This is a bound for the stated linear observer and query, not a universal
storage lower bound or a claim about nonlinear encodings of grammar.

## N4. Typed zero-specialization

Let ε:B_26→C_Σ take the empty-word coefficient. Because words have
nonnegative length and dagger preserves that length, ε is a unital
dagger homomorphism. Retain the history basis and set

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(\sum_h e_h a_h)=\sum_h e_h\epsilon(a_h),\qquad
 \Pi_{0,\mathrm{op}}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

For all admitted composable blocks,

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,\mathrm{op}}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
 \epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B=
 \langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

These equalities follow entrywise from ε's algebra and dagger laws.
The 0/1 cuts, inclusions and q^+ repair specialize compatibly. Each
forward event factor has ε(1+z_e)=1, while its history and query
truth values are retained. Induction along N2's ordered grades therefore
recovers exactly **864 positions /991 source-choice-labelled readings**
for forward unit starts, with Excluded and Open channels intact.
Arbitrary signed supports may cancel and are not subject to a support-
preservation claim. Printing a word is a later observation, not π_0.

## N5. Branch counting and scope

From N2, ru B 3sg and B 2sg each have 2+2=4 histories; ūrṇu
B 3sg has 2+1=3; ordinary first persons have one. This recovers P's
991 by summing fibres per position. A branch sum is not asserted to
be unitary, norm-preserving or a probability assignment. The native
pairing and algebraic branch relation do not supply a Born rule.

The result is finite and conditional on D's source/visibility contracts.
It does not prove universal confluence, arbitrary asiddha scheduling,
whole-grammar closure, runtime equivalence or a source-policy adapter
for querying historical sīyuṭ before its resolved ī view. Independent
and formal review remain separate obligations.
