## D1. Input before pronunciation

Begin on N1's full native history carrier. Retain lexical ID, selected
sense, interpreted event/actor, intended modal meaning, qualified pada,
controller, constituent owners, original/effective markers, actual right
context, source view, ordered history, choices, Smriti and Open premises.

O admitted vidhiliṅ and B supplied āśīs with loṭ. These are basic
kartari, unprefixed, classical, unaccented, non-doubling constructions
with the inherited devoiced-pause policy. Unresolved admission stays Open.

## D3. Lexical and register qualifications

Tu retains its सौत्रः annotation and the specific classical luk
license reviewed in PG10. The present construction does not admit the
Vedic śam/am members discussed at 7.3.95 or adopt Āpiśali's alternative
Vedic-only formulation as a second unlabelled policy.

Su is short-u class 2, not class-5 su; sū is long-ū class 2, not
sū with a surviving śa/śyan. Pā is protection, not drinking. Dāp
is cutting, not class-3 dā in giving. Mā is the unprefixed class-2
intransitive measure/containment sense, not māṅ or prohibitive mā.
Khyā and vī retain their reviewed sārvadhātuka-use qualification;
I/O/B meet it. The extra ī root discussed after vī in the commentary
is not inserted into this ID's inventory. Prefix-conditioned iṅ/ik
are outside this unprefixed domain.

## D4. Shared ending interface

2.4.72 supplies retained śap-luk. The original/current tiṅ and all
augments remain separate owners. PG19–25 supply the completed endings:
P ordinary tu/tām/antu/hi/tam/ta plus first-person āṭ, and the
corresponding A endings. All roots here are non-abhyasta. P jh→ant
by 7.1.3, while non-short-a A jh→at by 7.1.5. Thus ordinary P
3pl has antu, A 3pl atām. The fourteen long-ā roots do not meet
the short-a exception to 7.1.5, though only their P is admitted here.

P ordinary 3sg and every ordinary first person are strong. Other I/B
ordinary slots are weak. All O and whole tātaṅ branches are weak
under the inherited effective-marker account. O P faces actual yā/yus;
O A faces resolved modal ī after 7.2.79. Complete ending refinement
and s-lopa before the optional īṭ query; then query root strength
against the resulting actual sound. M gives this source-qualified order.

## D5. Exhaustion

Every completed D2 profile has nine positions in each I/O/B: **864**
new positions. M/S/P give finite, nonempty continuations for all of them.
The earlier modal domains do not include these exact basic I/O/B
profiles. Existing present, future or āśīrliṅ cells have different
position keys. No new causal/passive formation, prefix, accent, full
laṅ, āśīrliṅ, liṭ or derived-word construction is counted.
