## H05. Two blessing constructors, no present stem

Āśīrliṅ is ārdhadhātuka by 3.4.116. P gets yāsuṭ and the special kit
prescription under 3.4.103–104; no śap/śyan is selected. Y is outside val,
so ordinary 7.2.35 iṭ is absent. There is no śit-conditioned root lengthening
and no jhal contact at m+y. PG20 E1–E4 therefore gives

\[
(kramyāt,kramyāstām,kramyāsuḥ,kramyāḥ,kramyāstam,kramyāsta,
kramyāsam,kramyāsva,kramyāsma).
\]

Retain PG20's distinct yāsuṭ-s, t/th-addressed suṭ and ending-s. In the
selected 3sg schedule suṭ-s is deleted as part of the final conjunct before
yāsuṭ-s; in 3du/2du/2pl the surviving s belongs to suṭ. Sārvadhātuka
7.2.79 cannot replace this ārdhadhātuka 8.2.29 account.

A gets **sīyuṭ**, not P's yāsuṭ or kit. H02's root-dependent A restriction
blocks iṭ at its val-initial boundary. H03 excludes the special A kit/nasal
lopa routes. The pre-phonetic interface is **kram | sīy | ending**.
Root m→ṃ before s by 8.3.24; s is not yay, so 8.4.58 leaves that ṃ.
There is no iṇ/ku trigger for the first s: the factor is **kraṃs**, not
kraṃṣ or kramiṣ. PG20 E5–E6 yields

\[
(kraṃsīṣṭa,kraṃsīyāstām,kraṃsīran,kraṃsīṣṭhāḥ,kraṃsīyāsthām,
kraṃsīdhvam,kraṃsīya,kraṃsīvahi,kraṃsīmahi).
\]

The ṣ in 3sg/2sg belongs to the separate suṭ after ī; the dual suṭ
follows ā and stays s. At 2pl root-final m is outside iṇ and there is no
intervening iṭ, so neither 8.3.78's mandatory nor 8.3.79's optional ḍh
condition holds. **Kraṃsīdhvam has dental dh only**. The later ī of sīyuṭ
is not an aṅga-final vowel that licenses those rules by surface inspection.

## H06. Owned ca copy and complete liṭ endings

Liṭ tiṅ is ārdhadhātuka under 3.4.115. Rule 6.1.8 doubles the root, not
the prefix. The copied kram has its own sounds: **7.4.60** retains its
initial k and removes the other consonants; **7.4.62** gives c. The copy
vowel is already short. Thus **ca | kram** is the owned interface, with
the root's kr/m unaffected by copy simplification.

The root is not saṃyogānta, so apit liṭ is kit under 1.2.5. Strong P
3sg ṇal is ṇit: **7.2.116** lengthens the root upadhā a. P 1sg has the
two ṇit-work branches of **7.1.91**. Turning that work off does not make
ṇal apit. The short branch is cakrama. P thal is pit, neither kit nor
ṇit, and mandatory iṭ under H02 leaves root a short.

The contraction of **6.4.120–121** is unavailable: root a is preceded by
the cluster kr, not a single unsupported consonant; additionally the copy's
k→c is a liṭ-induced shape-changing substitution under the inherited
PG72 query. There is no permission to replace ca+kram by a contracted e
stem merely because the ending is kit or thal has iṭ.

P substitutions reuse 3.4.82. A uses 3.4.81 with the inherited ṭit

separate 1sg e. At A 3pl i belongs to irec; it is not iṭ. At A 2sg
the iṭ triggers s→ṣ. At A 2pl iṭ follows m, outside iṇ, so the optional
8.3.79 branch does not arise: **cakramidhve**, only dental dh.

All A liṭ endings are apit-kit, but that does not remove the liṭ iṭ
established by 7.2.13/35. This is the relevant distinction from aniṭ
A future/blessing/sic. There are ten P readings on nine positions and
nine A readings on nine positions. The P 1sg option changes root grade,
not pada or iṭ; A 3sg/1sg homography preserves different ending histories.

## H07. P seṭ sic and A aniṭ sic

For this basic root 3.1.43/44 selects cli→sic. No ṇic/caṅ, śal-final
ksa, puṣādi/dyutādi/ḷit aṅ or named sic-luk premise is present. Reuse
PG32's declared sic-marker policy and separate ārdhadhātuka sic versus
sārvadhātuka tiṅ ownership. Ordinary luṅ licenses initial aṭ by 6.4.71;
the root is consonant-initial, so the initial augment is not āṭ.

**P.** Root-facing iṭ precedes sic. H03's 7.2.5 m-final prohibition
blocks sic-vṛddhi, including the otherwise available light-a option.
The root stays **kram**, and the inner factor is **kram | iṭ | s_sic**.
At 3sg/2sg the apṛkta hal ending receives separate **īṭ** under 7.3.96;
**8.2.28** then deletes sic-s between affix iṭ and ending īṭ, and i+ī
coalesces. Elsewhere sic-s survives and receives ṣ after i. The complete
augmented tuple is

\[
(akramīt,akramiṣṭām,akramiṣuḥ,akramīḥ,akramiṣṭam,akramiṣṭa,
akramiṣam,akramiṣva,akramiṣma).
\]

Third plural has jus under 3.4.109; first singular has am under 3.4.101;
the first dual/plural retain va/ma after 3.4.99. Long ī in akramīt is
the augment junction, not root-vowel length. Root m remains m before iṭ.

**A.** H02 blocks root-facing iṭ. The root ends in m and lacks a kit
trigger, so the owned interface is **kram | s_sic | tiṅ** with short a.
Neither a preceding jhal nor a short-vowel-final aṅga is present before
sic; **8.2.26/27 do not delete s before ta/thās**. No A ending qualifies
as apṛkta hal for īṭ, and 8.2.28 is unavailable. Retained sic-s after
root m stays dental. Late 8.3.24 gives kraṃs, and s blocks parasavarṇa.

At **2pl only**, 8.2.25 deletes sic-s before dhvam while m is still m:

\[
a|kram|s|dhvam\ \longrightarrow\ a|kram|dhvam
\ \longrightarrow\ a|kraṃ|dhvam\ \longrightarrow\ akrandhvam.
\]

The last step is **8.4.58** before dental dh, a yay sound. Its dental
n is too late to feed back into 8.4.1/2 or an earlier nasal-lopa rule.
The dh stays dental: neither an original iṇ-final aṅga nor an iṭ after
iṇ is present. Do not replace it with *akraṇḍhvam or carry kraṃs through
the deletion. PG32 L2 gives the complete tuple

\[
(akraṃsta,akraṃsātām,akraṃsata,akraṃsthāḥ,akraṃsāthām,
akrandhvam,akraṃsi,akraṃsvahi,akraṃsmahi).
\]

At 3pl jha→ata under 7.1.5. At 1sg the final i belongs to the original
ending iṭ; it is not the root-facing augment blocked by 7.2.36. H05's
sīyuṭ and suṭ are absent throughout luṅ. All nine slots have one reading.

## H08. Prefix position, augmentation and complete finite closure

Attach prefixes outside the copied root or initial aṭ. Liṭ has
**upacakrame, parācakrame, vicakrame, pracakrame, ācakrame**. Neither
the prefix nor the perfect copy receives a past aṭ. Blessings have
**upakraṃsīṣṭa, parākraṃsīṣṭa, vikraṃsīṣṭa, prakraṃsīṣṭa,
ākraṃsīṣṭa** with no initial augment.

Luṅ uses the same PG77 aṭ junctions: **upā-, parā-, vya-, prā-, ā-**,
with 6.1.101 a/ā coalescence or 6.1.77 i→y. Reuse the explicit
aṭ-intervention permission for the admitted prefix-dependent A licences;
the coalesced word does not erase the initial augment owner. The source
licence remains attached through perfect reduplication as in the earlier
prefixed perfect construction; copying the root is not copying its prefix.
