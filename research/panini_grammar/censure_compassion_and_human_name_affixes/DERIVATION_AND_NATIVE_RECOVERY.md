# PG814 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 75 replaces general `ka` with `kan` when the contemptuous derivative is understood as a name. Kāśikā's `pūrṇaka` and Vasu's `cūrṇaka` remain separately sourced. Rule 76 changes the motive to compassion/endearment and admits nominal and displayed finite inputs; the `bubhukṣita/bubhukṣi` and `śvasita/viśvasita` source differences stay Open rather than being normalized. Rule 77 attaches the same family to the gift or action used as a compassionate expedient, not to the pitied person itself.

Rule 78 restricts optional `ṭhac` to polysyllabic human names; short names and descriptive epithets are controls. Rule 79 adds `ghan/ilac` while retaining the earlier alternatives. Rule 80 adds the Eastern `aḍac/vuc` options only for names beginning with `upa`, with the earlier suffixes still available. Rule 81 uses a jāti word as a human personal name; it does not license the literal word `jāti`. Rule 82 derives the same `vyāghraka/siṃhaka` surfaces from personal names ending in `-ajina` by separately owned second-member lopa.
