`sambuddhi`; 6.1.69 deletes its consonant after the short-`a` stem, yielding
`nema` and `sama`. The two `sama` rows have equal words but different sense and
status histories.

The feminine inputs retain PG844/843's `ṭāp`. Rule 7.3.106 replaces final `ā`
by `e` before sambuddhi and 6.1.69 removes the ending consonant, yielding `neme`
or `same`. These are neither the masculine plural nor the dual `śī` histories.

direct-case channel rather than 7.1.25 `adḍ`. Kāśikā 6.1.69's neuter-sambuddhi
explanation deletes the consonant of `am`, yielding `nema` or `sama`. There is
no deleted `ḍ_IT`, no licensed stem-final ṭi-lopa and no second pause reading.
