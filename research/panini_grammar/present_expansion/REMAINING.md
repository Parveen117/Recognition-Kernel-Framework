# PG10 — Remaining construction and evidence work

Author: Monty. 1 November 2026.

PG10 brings the bounded present profiles to 91 identified root entries,
121 pada paradigms, 1,089 cells and 1,223 branches. This leaves 2,138 of
the 2,229 source root entries without an admitted complete present profile
in this chain. That count is not a percentage of Sanskrit grammar.

## Next linguistic packages

1. Review the remaining class-3 identities and registers separately.
   Fourteen unmarked entries now have the stated present profiles; all
   twelve tagged entries remain unadmitted. In particular, the tagged
   vowel-initial ṛ entry has a traditional classical-use discussion and
   needs its own copying boundary and source review. Its absence cannot
   be explained away as purely Vedic.
2. Extend class 2 beyond ad/as and the 27 new vowel-final roots. Han,
   consonant-final roots, i and śī require their own substitutions,
   strengthening, junctions, lexical exceptions and source witnesses.
   The source has 76 lexical entries plus a gaṇasūtra placeholder; a
   numerical row is not automatically a root.
3. Collect independent full-word primary witnesses for su, ku, śrā, rā,
   dāp in cutting and prā. Preserve their current distinction between
   lexical/rule evidence and comparison evidence until that work is done.
4. Extend tense/mood, voice, prefix, accent and register coverage through
   separately reviewed domains. Current word readouts assume the declared
   unprefixed classical kartari present profile and pause treatment.
5. Develop nominal, kṛt, taddhita, compound and sentence-level packages
   with their own counting units. Companion nominal outputs must not be
   counted as present-tense verb cells.

General vowel-initial or multiple-vowel reduplication, universal optional
consonant doubling, a general asiddha solver and a general rule-conflict
scheduler are not supplied by this stage. Current reviewed priorities
and phase order must not be expanded by sūtra-number sorting alone.

## Native construction remains a separate obligation

The implemented carrier is an explicit finite linguistic adapter with
typed owners, sources, deletion memory and ordered history. To lift it
to the native full lambda/compass operator carrier, declare and justify
the admitted grammatical operators, domains, composition, pairing and
specialization maps. On each admitted operation establish the typed
compatibility relation

`pi_0(Construct_lambda(inputs)) = Construct_0(pi_0(inputs))`.

Keeping histories in a classical plan does not by itself prove that
relation. Neither the bounded verifier nor agreement with another
conjugator closes it. Quantum-calculator integration remains downstream
of that construction and of the exact linguistic contracts it consumes.

The 3,983-record inventory, 143 bounded definitions / 112 distinct sūtra
IDs, root preprocessing diagnostics, complete present profiles, literal
word evidence and implementation comparisons remain separate measures.
PG1–PG9 evidence stays frozen. Hosted CI and main merging remain deferred.
