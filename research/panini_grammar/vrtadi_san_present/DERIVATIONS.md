# PG118 — Inner san and outer present-system morphology

Monty · 11 November 2026

## H06. Form the copy and identify the san recipient

San attaches to the lexical root under 3.1.7. Its n is an it marker
under 1.3.3/9 and sa is the surviving affix. Keep its identity and the
root-to-san iṭ decision. Reduplication 6.1.9 and the abhyāsa operations
7.4.59–60, 7.4.66 and 7.4.79 yield vi, vi, śi, si and ci in H02.
For syand the copy retains the first s, and y remains in the root.
For kṛp, 7.4.62 palatalizes the copy's k. Its root-initial k persists.
The copy vowel i has a different owner from any inserted iṭ.

In the aniṭ branches of vṛt, vṛdh, śṛdh and kṛp, san has actual
jhal-initial contact after the consonant adjacent to ik. Thus san is
kit by 1.2.10 and 1.1.5 blocks guṇa. On their seṭ branches that
contact fails and 7.3.86 gives ar in the root. Syand has neither
the required short ik-upadhā nor this kit entitlement. Its stem
alternation comes from iṭ and consonant contact. The kṛp aniṭ **sic**
condition in PG117 addresses a different affix and has no work here.

## H07. Twelve licensed inner branches

The following are realized san-dhātus before the outer construction.

| Root | P aniṭ | A seṭ | A aniṭ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vṛt | vivṛtsa | vivartiṣa | — |
| vṛdh | vivṛtsa | vivardhiṣa | — |
| śṛdh | śiśṛtsa | śiśardhiṣa | — |
| syand | sisyantsa | sisyandiṣa | sisyantsa |
| kṛp | cikḷpsa | cikalpiṣa | cikḷpsa |

For vṛdh/śṛdh, dh surfaces as t before san-s by 8.4.55. For syand,
d likewise gives t there, and the lexical n retains its dental readout
under 8.3.24/8.4.58. Its root-initial s survives in this unprefixed
domain. For kṛp, 8.2.18 gives l after guṇa (ar → al) and vocalic
ḷ where guṇa is blocked. Under 8.3.59 the san-owned s after iṭ
appears as ṣ. On the aniṭ paths the intervening dental stop or p
leaves the affix s dental. In the state, san_kit and guna have separate
fields so syand aniṭ is representable without falsely assigning kit.

## H08. Apply the ending to the derived dhātu

3.1.32 gives the completed san form dhātu status. Outer śap under
3.1.68 and the real final san-a have the 6.1.97 boundary. Write C for
the displayed san stem minus its final a solely to read the endings.
Reuse the P and A maps in PG87 DERIVATIONS H07, with their source,
marker and ending ownership. The surface word is the optional initial
past aṭ, then C, then the corresponding map entry. Here the initial
augment is required exactly for the selected laṅ. It precedes the copy.

For example, the P vṛdh request laṅ 3sg reads a + vivṛts + at →
**avivṛtsat**. The A syand request vidhiliṅ 2du reads C + eyāthām,
giving **sisyandiṣeyāthām** or **sisyantseyāthām**. The choice
belongs to the inner branch and leaves the outer ending unchanged.

## H09. Carry the intended ending through the final sound operations

In ordinary P loṭ 2sg, the finite ending is addressed directly.
Hi-lopa under 6.4.105 yields the a-final command. Retain the P
recipient and its history even when hi has no surface material. In P
loṭ 1sg the ending's āṭ is separate from the initial laṅ aṭ.
All P stems end in san's dental s, which intervenes before the n in
āni. The three root-ṛ profiles therefore also take **āni**. The A
seṭ stems end in ṣa, but have no P 1sg loṭ ending. Use PG87's
pausal ending and nasal policy, including the dental dh of these A maps.
The counted imperative is the ordinary PG87 reading; specialised
future-reference tātāṅ and other registers lie outside this selection.

## H10. Preserve the two interpretations of a shared word

For each of the 36 P lakāra/slot choices, vṛt and vṛdh yield the
same string. For each corresponding A choice their words differ at the
retained t/dh. This collision follows from root-to-san kit on P plus
8.4.55 on vṛdh. The lexical IDs and intended inner events therefore
stay distinct on both sides of the relation. Each rendering links to
one of twelve licensed stem branches, the outer context, and the selected
ending. One position is (lexical profile including pada, lakāra, slot).

There are 5 × 2 × 4 × 9 = 360 positions. The 72 positions belonging
to the syand/kṛp A profiles admit two readings apiece; the other 288
admit one. Thus there are 432 retained paths and 432 readings counted
within their own positions. Equal words across different positions do
not turn these figures into a count of distinct strings.
