## N1. Domain, branching relation and carrier

The input consists of lexical entry and sense, the two events with a shared
actor, a san witness, kartari, empty prefix sequence, pada choice, outer
context, person and number. The chosen pada is a request coordinate. To
enumerate both padas, take the union of the two admitted fibres. H01–H05
define the scope. Missing evidence has a typed HOLD output and a known
failed domain guard has an EXCLUDED output, each with its reason.

Extend the PG117 unital dagger algebra B117 by free event generators
z_e and z_e* to B118. Use the direct sum of history-labelled right modules
e_h B118, retaining the inherited source/role tensor factors and pairing.
Discrete records distinguish the actor equation, admission, original root
consonants, copy, san, iṭ, kit/grade, actual contacts, derived dhātu,
outer ending and its surviving material. The lexical effects and
pronounced segments have distinct recipient addresses.

A choice of pada restricts the allowed inner branches before the finite
readout. Each admitted event e sends e_h b to e_h′ (1+z_e)b, with h′
retaining its source, decision and recipient. Complementary guard

Compose arrows in their grammatical order. Use one summand per history
when choices share an output word. The dagger is algebraic. No physical
clock or calibrated scalar is needed for this finite composition.

## N2. A surface-only change of pada is underdetermined

Fix the same laṭ 3sg request coordinates apart from lexical entry and
its source meaning. Let P(r) and A(r) be the sets of surface readings.
Then P(vṛt) = P(vṛdh) = {vivṛtsati}, whereas
A(vṛt) = {vivartiṣate} and A(vṛdh) = {vivardhiṣate}.
A function from the P surface to the **exact A readout of the same
lexical request** would have to give two unequal singleton sets at the
same argument. It therefore cannot exist on this two-entry domain.
A word analyser may lawfully return both lexical candidates. Retaining
the lexical identity lets a subsequent change of pada address the
requested entry. This finite result makes no uniqueness claim for
analysis across Sanskrit.

The second distinction concerns iṭ. Fix P, syand and san. The inner
branch set is {aniṭ}. In A it is {seṭ, aniṭ}. An independent Boolean
switch attached to every profile would add an unlicensed P path. On
aniṭ, syand and kṛp also differ in san-kit. The machine record keeps
the result of that actual interface query distinct from the iṭ flag.
