## N2. Why present morphology cannot decide the historical guard

prefix, ṇic, controller, label and slot. A complete qualifying history
and a history with an absent or different same-action witness can have
the same erased current data. The first is admitted under 1.3.67;
the second is HOLD or EXCLUDED. Hence no function of that erased data
alone reproduces the qualified admission map. This is a bounded witness
obstruction, not a claim that all semantic interpretation is solved.
