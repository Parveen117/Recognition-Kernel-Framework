# PG974 · Who confines the cow?

Mādhavīya 1039 gives two finite forms under its express *karmakartari* heading. Both concern the cow which undergoes confinement and is itself represented as bringing it about. The entry is √rudh 1070001, *āvaraṇa*, simple and unprefixed. The singular cow controls both middle endings. [Mādhavīya 1039](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/scl/dhaatupaatha/files/mA1039.html)

## How Bhoja reads the same ending

In the third prakāśa the 1955 Josyer print first lists अनुरुद्ध with the self-acting cow. Its spelling stays on its own source record. The next paragraph expressly attributes a further group, including अरोधि with the cow and *svayam eva*, to *anye*. The explanation then separates three ways to understand the unprefixed verb.

| Clause evidence | What the hearer can recover |
|---|---|
| Cowherd in the instrumental | The cow undergoes confinement. With a patient intended, the text expressly licenses ciṇ even for √rudh. |
| Cow with *ātmanā eva* or *svayam eva* | The cow itself supplies the agency on the author’s analysis. The comparison with the explicit instrumental explains how the companion word contributes this sense while the verbal formation can still express the patient. |
| Bare *arodhi* | Without another word expressing the agent the author says self-agency cannot be inferred. The verbal ending alone supplies insufficient evidence for this interpretation. |

The report of other grammarians is an attributed reading of the clause. It supplies no free ciṇ alternative for the Mādhavīya self-agent slot. The print reads *tattu samyak* at this transition. Its positive explanation twice identifies ciṇ, then the final recap visibly prints **णिच्**. A causative would require a further lexical operation and a different derivation. ## The two forms with anu in the Dvivedī edition

The IGNCA print gives **अनुरुन्धे** and **अन्वरुद्ध** together before its paragraph on the other grammarians. This supplies a present and an augmented aorist, both middle third singular, in its own self-agent list. In the present, prefix *anu* precedes the nasal class-7 stem, as in PG922’s surface control *rundhe*. The print gives no yaK in this word. In the aorist *anu + a-* yields *anv a-* before the weak *ruddha*. The single preverb distinguishes these two positions from the PG43 pair with *anu + ava*. The edition supports its own full pair. How Josyer came to print a single अनुरुद्ध remains open. The same IGNCA page prints both *ciṇ* in the explanation and *ṇic* in the recap, establishing the discrepancy in two editions. Its repeated cowherd clause is retained at this source grade. Neither print establishes manuscript priority. [Dvivedī edition, vol. I, p. 144](https://archive.org/details/sringaraprakasabhojarewaprasadadwivedisantanasadashivakumaradwivedivol1ignca/page/n246/mode/1up)
