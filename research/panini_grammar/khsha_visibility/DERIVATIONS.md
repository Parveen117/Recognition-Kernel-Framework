# PG58 — ख्शा, late यत्व and the rule that reads the history

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

## Source model and domain

Continue [PG57's qualified चक्षिङ् domain](../caks_substitution_scope/DERIVATIONS.md),
including its ordinary telling sense, exceptions, affix ownership, recognition
of ञ् and separately supplied pada premises. PG57 selected a directly
presented ख्याञ् path. This packet constructs the alternative single
**ख्शाञ् / khśāñ** analysis: optional later श् → य् yields ख्या;
retaining श् allows later चर्त्व to yield क्शा.

Mahābhāṣya 2.4.54 gives the alternative analysis and its purposes.
Padamañjarī 2.4.54 and Tattvabodhinī 2.4.55 place the optional यत्व
after the णत्व section; Padamañjarī says it is already सिद्ध for the
later चर्त्व query. These supply a partial order, not an invented numbered
sūtra. `Y_OPT` below is an editorial event name for this source-qualified
vārttika. It is not an Aṣṭādhyāyī ID or a new catalogue rule.

K = Kāśikā, P = Padamañjarī, T = Tattvabodhinī. Newly selected fields
are archived locally; the unchanged P 2.4.54 field is inherited from PG57.

The selected pronunciation paths omit optional added consonant doubling.
H02 explicitly chooses the permitted 8.4.64 collapse of two independently
present य् occurrences. Full doubling alternatives, accent, all case forms,
all perfect endings and all word uses are outside these examples.

## C01 — Do not choose चर्त्व before the Y branch

P makes यत्व सिद्ध for चर्त्व. Replacing ख् by क् before choosing
यत्व would produce a क्य sequence on the Y branch. That is not the
written ख्या branch. Conversely, a declined Y choice leaves श्, and
8.4.55 then has its actual खर् trigger.

## C02 — A final spelling is not the तद्धित query's complete input

Both H02 and H03 display सुप्रख्य/सौप्रख्य forms, yet their source
queries do not obtain a योपधा premise from the late य्. Preserve the
भाव and named-country semantic admissions separately; neither the string
nor a shared “taddhita” flag supplies both frames.

## C03 — Alternative explanations do not repeal standard rules

PG57's direct ख्या history and H04's ख्शा history can both retain
निष्ठा त with different reasons. Do not remove 8.2.57 globally or
declare that its whole operation was unnecessary. Keep a selected source
policy on every proof record, including the independent ख्या root whose
underlying analysis P also discusses.

## C04 — Marker silence and perfect इट् remain separate decisions

The recognized ञ् belongs to both khśā-derived branches. Neither spelling
alone determines pada. Likewise the ordinary अनिट् examples do not
erase Bālamanoramā's explicitly recorded perfect इट् alternatives. No
complete perfect branch set is inferred from H01's two stem readouts.

## C05 — Damaged readings remain literal, with explicit interpretation

Bālamanoramā 2.4.55 repeatedly prints शस्य वत्व where the other
sources and the actual चख्यौ/चख्ये examples require यत्व. P 2.4.54
prints an inconsistent ख्यादिरेव in the alternative's introduction and
अख्यात in the participle example. The raw archive and inherited archive
are unchanged. The adopted khśā/optional-Y model is grounded in the
explicit ख्शाञः clause, Mahābhāṣya's analysis and T's ordering, not
in an unmarked editorial rewrite of any one field.
