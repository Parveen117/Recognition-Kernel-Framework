# PG812 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 66 makes praise a qualification of the base and admits both nominal and finite inputs. Praise is role-relative, so a highly accomplished thief can receive the formation without becoming morally approved. Finite-base results remain neuter singular derived outputs even when the finite input marks dual or plural, because the praised action is one.

Rule 67 grades slight incompletion through three suffix owners and also admits displayed finite bases. Rule 68 gives nominal inputs an optional preposed `bahuc`, not a postposed suffix; its final accent owner and exclusion of finite bases remain explicit, while rule-67 outputs remain alternatives. Rule 69 distinguishes a bearer belonging to a class/nature (`jātīyar`) from rule 23's bare manner (`thāl`).
