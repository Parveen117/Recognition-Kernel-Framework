# PG224 · Star names, birth names and the reintroduced feminine affix

Monty · 12 November 2026

The nakṣatra and a woman born under it can both be **Revatī**. In the birth-name analysis, 4.3.34 removes the natal taddhita, 1.2.49 removes the subordinate feminine affix of the star, and a new feminine affix belongs to the person. The Nyāsa explains the same sequence for **Citrā** with a fresh *ṭāp*. For *Revatī* and *Rohiṇī* the last affix is *ṅīṣ*. Its authority is expressed as *gaurādi* in the Kāśikā and as open *pippalyādi* in the Kaumudī.

| Birth context | Result in the traditional examples | Derivation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The ten star names directly listed at 4.3.34, masculine | śraviṣṭhaḥ, phalgunaḥ, anurādhaḥ, svātiḥ, tiṣyaḥ, punarvasuḥ, hastaḥ, viśākhaḥ, aṣāḍhaḥ, bahulaḥ | Natal affix undergoes *luk*. Where the source star already has a feminine affix, that subordinate affix also undergoes *luk*. |
| Woman born under Citrā, Revatī, Rohiṇī | citrā, revatī, rohiṇī | Named extension; new feminine for the person. |
| Woman born under Phalgunī or Aṣāḍhā | phalgunī, aṣāḍhā | Special *ṭa* followed by *ṅīp*, or *an* followed by *ṭāp*, respectively. |
| Man born under Śraviṣṭhā or Aṣāḍhā | śrāviṣṭhīyaḥ, āṣāḍhīyaḥ | An additional *chaṇ* account gives initial vṛddhi and *īya*. |

The tables have **360 distinct birth-context, gender, case and number positions** with **418 readings**. The two *chaṇ* alternatives occupy existing positions. The stellar locative can be plural or dual, as in *śraviṣṭhāsu* and *phalgunyoḥ*. The resulting person has independent outer case and number.

The Bhāṣya records the proposals and also the objection that the broader provision for *luk* suffices. The Tattvabodhinī records a short-*i*/long-*ī* disagreement for *Svāti* that matters to a separate *sauvāta* derivation. Apte’s *rauhiṇa* is recorded as another lexical head. Neither qualification is needed to recognize the directly printed feminine *Rohiṇī*.
