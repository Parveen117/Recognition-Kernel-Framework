# PG768 · Accent, case and ownership in RV 1.20.7

The Ṛbhus are asked to give. The verse puts the treasures in relation to the Soma-presser and a number of sevens. Sāyaṇa understands a separate request concerning three sets of ritual acts, seven havis sacrifices, seven cooked offerings and seven Soma rites. He applies the threefold description also to treasures ranked by quality. Jamison and Brereton attach the number to the treasures. Griffith assigns the three-times-seven libations to the presser. Aitareya Brāhmaṇa 5.21.12 cites two portions of the verse and uses the occurrence of three to characterize the ninth day. This attests the ritual reuse. It does not choose between the competing readings of the object.

The ṭi of saptan falls before the Vedic añ of 5.1.61 under 6.4.144. The ñ marker licenses initial strengthening under 7.2.117 and initial accent under 6.1.197. Sāyaṇa actually gives a singular definition of one group and explains the plural by its members. His citation nominative singular is one new position. PG755 already owns the group accusative plural. Its TS passage gratifies seven groups of Agni. Here dhattana is a second-person plural imperative with tana in place of ta under 7.1.45. Neither that verb nor the Brāhmaṇa citation supplies the creation quotation. This packet uses the imperative as a source guard and assigns it zero finite positions and readings.

For ratna, PG's Pañcapādī 3.14 gives na and the change of m to t in ram. Sāyaṇa calls it 3.294 and attributes the accent on the first syllable to inherited n-it. That t is an internal substitution. In sunvaté the final acute belongs to the dative ending of the present participle. The same unaccented letter sequence occurs as a finite middle form elsewhere. The recipient of this verse supplies a different grammatical input. For distributive ékam-ekam the second item is designated āmreḍita and loses its accent under 8.1.2–4. In suśastíbhiḥ Sāyaṇa derives śasti with instrumental ktin from śaṃs, attributing the nasal loss to its k marker. In the compound the specific 6.2.151 rule places the acute on the final vowel of the second stem. This is an independent control for how a commentator states a marker effect. S6 requires that evidence on its own 5.56 affix.

Vedaweb and Hellwig label the identical neuter plural endings differently. In Sāyaṇa's imperative construction the objects of giving or accomplishing are accusative. He glosses the presser as the sacrificer in the dative. These three contexts distinguish word form, clause role and paid ownership. Two compounds' accent readings do not split their words into extra case requests.
