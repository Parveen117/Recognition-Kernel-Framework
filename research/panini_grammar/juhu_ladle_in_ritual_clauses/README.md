# PG945 · The juhū ladle as object, implement, receptacle and addressee

Kāśikā 3.2.178 gives the attested kvip formation *juhūḥ*. Its vārttika supplies both reduplication and lengthening for √hu. The derivation keeps the lexical root, copy ju-, long ū and zero-sounding kṛt affix. PG's earlier rule contract already owns the nomination. Mādhavīya 732 explicitly gives the dual *juhvau* under 6.4.83. Kṣīrataraṅgiṇī 754 identifies the item as a *sruk*, a ritual ladle. These histories distinguish the noun from the present stem of *juhoti*, the food sense, and a passive yaK formation. In the same prayoga, *juhūr asi* identifies the ladle in the nominative and *juhūm ādatte* puts the very same object in the accusative. The repeated accusative receives one position. The nominative control and the commentary's dual receive zero credit. The locative, instrumental and vocative come from a ritual practice text. Their presence supports those particular contexts without deciding a single accent or reading across recensions.
