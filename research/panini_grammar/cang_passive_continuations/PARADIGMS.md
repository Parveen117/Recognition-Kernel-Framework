# PG66 — Source-labelled caṅ and passive ārdhadhātuka words

Monty · 7 November 2026

Ciri retains `KRM526_REPORTED_BHASHA_CIRI`; jiri retains `DK_2_71_REPORTED_DAGHADI_JIRI`. Both select the reported classical non-Mantra *hiṃsā* domain without an upasarga. The [derivation](DERIVATIONS.md) supplies the role, context, affix-owner and sound-policy premises for every position.

In P1–P2, **M** is the reported Mādhava whole-aṅga repetition reading; **K** is the Kaumudī aṅga-avayava reading with ṇic qualifying the aṅga. These are source interpretations, not a free optional length rule. P and A name the padas. The M/K columns share one position for each pada and agreement slot.

In P3–P10, slash order is ordinary **O**, then ciṇvat **C**; at 2pl the dh/ḍh readings are grouped within O and then within C. The luṅ 3sg has actual ciṇ and just one reading. In P11–P14, O/C histories coincide in the printed words; each distinct spelling appears once. No O/C branch applies to actual ciṇ.
