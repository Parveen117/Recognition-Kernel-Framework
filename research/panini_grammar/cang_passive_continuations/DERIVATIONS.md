## E01. Position, spelling and path exhaustion

The four caṅ paradigms have 36 positions, 72 M/K readings and 72 labelled paths. Basic and causal karmaṇi each have ten paradigms: per root, 27 future/conditional positions with 54 readings, nine blessing positions with 20 readings, and nine luṅ positions with 19 readings. Each formation therefore contributes 90 positions and 186 readings over both roots.

San karmaṇi contributes 90 positions and 90 spellings. Of its 45 positions per root, 44 admit the two coincident O/C histories; actual-ciṇ 3sg has one. That gives **89 paths per root**, or 178. Overall:

\[
36+90+90+90=306\ \text{positions},\quad
72+186+186+90=534\ \text{readings},\quad
72+186+186+178=622\ \text{labelled paths}.
\]

A position is one qualified lexical/formation/voice/pada/lakāra/person-number request before the declared commentary or optional branch choice. A reading is one distinct spelling within that position. Paths retain the stated source-interpretation, O/C and dh/ḍh choices; no claim is made to enumerate every possible rule schedule. The two interpretations do not inflate positions and coincident san paths do not inflate readings. PG65's 12,452 positions become **12,758**. The source-relative caṅ hold is discharged by H01–H02 for these named interpretations; broader consensus, Vedic/accent extensions and executable verification remain separate obligations.
