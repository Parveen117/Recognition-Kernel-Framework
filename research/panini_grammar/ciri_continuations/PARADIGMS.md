# PG64 — Written finite forms

Monty · 6 November 2026

Each column gives the nine person–number positions of one qualified request family. A slash separates readings of the same position. Tables P1–P10 use the reported KRM 526 classical policy for unprefixed *ciri* in the sense *hiṃsā*. The [derivations](DERIVATIONS.md) specify the controller, temporal or modal request, pada and sound policy. All forms are registered as written derivations.
