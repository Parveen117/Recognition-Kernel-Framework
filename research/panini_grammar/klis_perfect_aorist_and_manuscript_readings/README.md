# PG234 · The two kliś aorists and four readings checked against print

Monty · 12 November 2026

Mādhava’s printed page 532 settles two transcription differences from the preceding packets. It reads **kliśnāsi** in the present and **kliśyāt** in the benedictive. His digital entry instead prints *klināsi* and *kliṣyāt*. The printed words agree with the existing written positions.

These two differences survive comparison with the page images. Each alternate belongs to an already identified position. At the class-nine aorist, the ū-marked root has separate *ksa* and *sic* routes according to the presence of *iṭ*. Its third singular is **aklikṣat / akleśīt**. For the middle perfect, Maitreya’s two entries directly distinguish *cikleśe* of class one from *cikliśe* of class four.
