# PG25 — Actual vowel seams and consonant contacts

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## S1. Resolve the owned boundary before its sound

M fixes the copy/root and ending choice. The readout retains that
immutable morphological source and applies only the addressed contacts.
Do not reuse a completed strong present stem in weak hi/y/tāt cells,
or use a bare ending behind the surviving modal augment.

Weak root ṛ before vowels takes r by 6.1.77; consonant-facing ṛ
remains vocalic. The pṝ ur output is instead a root-owned u+r
sequence; 8.2.77 reads its u before hal, including O's actual y.
For first-person strong e, 6.1.78 gives ay before ā/ai. The ā
roots use their admitted vowel coalescences. All are constituent seams,
not erasure of copy/root/augment ownership.

## S2. Hi is selected before dhi or the special hāk query

For nij/vij/viṣ and cakās, the actual root is jhal-final, so
6.4.101 supplies dhi from hi. The other new hi roots are vowel-final
or r-final after the pṝ operation, and do not meet that jhal condition.
Hāk instead receives the three vowel outcomes of M3 before its hi.
None of these routes imports PG23 han→ja or śās→śā.

The tāt branch has no actual hi and therefore does not acquire dhi,
nor hāk's special ā-before-hi alternative. An original ending in
Smriti is not automatically a present target for every operation.

## S3. Nij/vij and viṣ

For nij/vij, 8.2.30 changes root j to a velar before jhal.
Subsequent voicing/devoicing supplies the actual contact:

| Boundary | Nij | Vij |
|---|---|---|
| strong tu | nenektu | vevektu |
| weak tām/tam/ta | nenikt- | vevikt- |
| ordinary hi→dhi | nenigdhi | vevigdhi |
| A sva | nenikṣva | vevikṣva |
| A dhvam | nenigdhvam | vevigdhvam |
| weak tāt | neniktāt | veviktāt |
| actual vowel/y | root j retained | root j retained |

The k in the sva path licenses affix-s ṣatva under 8.3.59.
Neither raw root j nor its g substitute is jhaṣ; there is no new
generic t→dh rule on the tām/tu route. Dhi already contains dh.

For viṣ, original root ṣ makes following t retroflex by 8.4.41;
at dhi/dhvam the root ṣ voices to ḍ and following dh becomes ḍh.
Thus veveṣṭu, veviṣṭām, **veviḍḍhi/veviḍḍhvam**, and
veviṣṭāt have their source-local contacts. Before sva, 8.2.41
and affix ṣatva give **vevikṣva**. Actual y/ī leaves the root ṣ,
yielding veviṣyāt/veviṣīta rather than a blanket velar substitute.

## S4. Cakās's selected s-lopa reading

Retain PG11/24's declared broad dh-initial-affix reading of 8.2.25.
After hi→dhi, cakās∣dhi loses root s, giving **cakādhi**.
Kaumudī/Bālamanoramā at 6.4.112 explicitly contrast cakāddhi
under the sic-restricted account with the Bhāṣya-supported cakādhi
reading. Kāśikā 8.2.25 itself records the debate and the latter
Bhāṣya statement. PG25 continues the selected source policy.

This is not an optional 8.4.65 deletion of a doubled d. Under the
alternative account s would become d before dh; under the selected
account s has already been removed. The two source histories remain
different. Only the selected word is counted in P.

Before t, cakās retains s: cakāstu/cakāstām/cakāstāt. Before
actual y it gives cakāsyāt. No source clause turns every s into ṣ
or deletes it throughout the root's paradigm.

## S5. Ṇatva and absence of another sound-choice fibre

Apply the inherited 8.4.1/2/37 context. The first-person n becomes
ṇ after the eligible r/ṣ with an admitted intervening span:
jihrayāṇi, piparāṇi, bibharāṇi, iyarāṇi, jāgarāṇi,
daridrāṇi and veviṣāṇi. Bibhayāni, jahāni, nenijāni,
vevijāni and cakāsāni retain dental n. Final n in O A's ran
does not acquire an invented ending-final ṇ.

No new PG25 output meets the full optional-collapse field of 8.4.65.
Viṣ's ḍ∣ḍh follows a vowel; nij/vij's g∣dh is not savarṇa;
ṣ∣ṭ has different articulation manner. Cakādhi's selected source
path has already deleted s by a different rule. Optional doubling
remains outside D. No other pair introduces a collapse branch.
