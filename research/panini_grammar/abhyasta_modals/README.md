# PG25 — Abhyasta modal families and reusable constructor interfaces

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

PG25 closes the declared ordinary-loṭ, vidhiliṅ and blessing-loṭ
block for **15 exact roots /19 qualified pada profiles**. Twelve
roots have an owned ślu copy; jāgṛ, daridrā and cakās instead
have lexical abhyasta designation. The register has **513 new
positions /570 written readings**.

The new distinctions are hāk's three hi readings and separate y-lopa,
bhī's two weak-consonant readings, daridrā's short i, pṝ versus pṛ,
and the light-upadhā restriction on 7.3.87. First-person nenijāni
does not imply that final-ik bhṛ must lose the guṇa of bibharāṇi.
Copy guṇa and root guṇa retain different owners.

Read [domain/interfaces](DOMAIN_AND_INTERFACES.md),
[stem and choice construction](STEMS_AND_CHOICES.md),
[owned sound readout](OWNED_READOUT.md) and the
[complete paradigms](PARADIGMS.md). The [native construction](NATIVE_FAMILIES.md)
proves source-qualified block assembly, typed zero-recovery and
dependent choice counting; its reuse theorem does not license an
unchecked Cartesian product of roots and endings.

and **574 distinct bounded-rule IDs**, including new 6.4.117 and
