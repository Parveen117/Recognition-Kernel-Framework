## P5. Exact count and recovery

**Theorem P5.** The PG25 domain has **513 positions /570 readings**.

| Mood | Positions | Base readings | Extra ordinary stem readings | Extra tāt readings | Total readings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I | 171 | 171 | 9 | 0 | 180 |
| O | 171 | 171 | 9 | 0 | 180 |
| B | 171 | 171 | 9 | 30 | 210 |
| **Total** | **513** | **513** | **27** | **30** | **570** |

**Proof.** Nineteen profiles each have nine positions per mood.
M5 gives four extra bhī readings and five hāk readings in I/B;
O has only bhī's nine. P4 counts the ending-dependent tāt fibres.
S5 excludes further sound choices. Hence 3·171=513 and
513+27+30=570. N3/N4 recover this source/choice-labelled relation
under the typed zero map. □

These counts distinguish formation and meaning, not unique spellings.
Identical strong pṛ/pṝ outputs and ordinary I/B overlaps are retained
under their different declared inputs. No runtime execution is claimed.
