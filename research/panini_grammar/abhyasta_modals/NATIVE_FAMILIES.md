# PG25 — Source-qualified family assembly and dependent choice fibres

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## N1. Primitive carrier, pairing and cuts

Start with the canonical cut scalar field C_Σ, derived iota and dagger.
Extend the retained event alphabet by PG25's owned copy, lexical
designation, vowel-target, hi/y/tāt and contact events. Construct

\[
 B_{25}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le25}\rangle,\qquad
 M_{25,\lambda}=M_{\mathrm{old},\lambda}\oplus
               \bigoplus_{f\in F_{25}}M_{f,\lambda},
 \quad M_{f,\lambda}=\bigoplus_{h\in H_f}e_hB_{25}.
\]

F_25 is the set of nineteen qualified profiles. H_f retains D1/D4's
exact source/semantic data, constituent owners, copy links or noncopy
designation, original/effective markers, actual right contexts, chosen
endings, ordered events, lopa Smriti and unresolved premises. Restrict
to finite words and finite coefficient supports. M_old,λ here means
the predecessor module after scalar extension to B_25. Old coefficients
embed without new relations, and old operators extend by zero on the
new summand.

The grades are semantic/pada admission, class/copy, ending refinement,
source-target and next-context query, selected vowel branch, sound
readout and Accepted/Excluded/Open terminal. Refine a grade into the
finite ordered events of M/S. Composition requires matching types;
ordered derivation is not physical elapsed time.

Retain the native pairing
\(\langle x,y\rangle_B=\sum_h x_h^*y_h\).
It is algebraically nondegenerate by pairing against coordinate units.
Each addressed query has orthogonal self-adjoint coordinate cuts
P^T,P^F,P^?, summing to its local domain identity. False and unknown
remain different source channels. No primitive Hilbert space or metric
is assumed. This extends the one canonical engine's application domain.

For an admitted e:h→h′ set T_e(e_h a)=e_h′(1+z_e)a, zero
on its other domain coordinates. The dagger reverses the coordinate
entry and stars the coefficient; it does not license grammatical
inversion. Admit the finite blocks and their finite sums, products and
adjoints, with bounded prefix-retaining paths ensuring finite supports.

## N2. Block assembly without cross-root leakage

Let J_f:M_f,λ→M_25,λ be the coordinate inclusion. Derive its dagger
from the pairing: J_f† is the matching coordinate projection.
Then J_f†J_g=δ_fg I_f and P_f=J_fJ_f† are orthogonal cuts.

Suppose A_f is an admitted operator between corresponding grades of
profile f. Its assembled operation is

\[
 \mathcal A=\sum_{f\in F_{25}}J_f A_f J_f^\dagger.
\]

**Theorem N2 (source-qualified block assembly).** Assembly preserves
finite sums, composable products and dagger; the direct-sum pairing and
three query channels decompose profilewise. It introduces no transition between distinct lexical/pada
profiles. A shared constructor may be reused when its parameter binding
preserves its exact guard values, addressed source view, owners and
typed event targets. These conditions are discharged for D2 by M/S.

**Proof.** Orthogonality kills all terms with f≠g in an assembled
product; the remaining terms are J_f A_f B_f J_f†. Dagger reverses
the local operation and the inclusions. The pairing is the sum of
the local pairings, and the query channels sum to the local profile/
grade identities. Parameter binding is admitted only after each guard
and target is identified with its local source contract. Induction over
the common typed constructor then identifies each event and subsequent
query with its instance. M1–M5 and S1–S5 supply that finite partition
for all nineteen profiles. □

Event-symbol renaming into the common free alphabet preserves dagger
and the empty word. It cannot substitute for the grammatical guard
proof. A new root with an unreviewed exception therefore does not become
admitted just because its letters fit a stored stem template.

This is the reusable part: ending construction and the algebraic
compatibility proof need not be reproved nineteen times, while each
root's new source conditions still have to be supplied.

## N3. Typed zero-specialization and recovery

Let ε:B_25→C_Σ take the empty-word coefficient. It is a unital
dagger homomorphism. Retain the same history labels in M_25,0 and set

\[
 \pi_{0,M}\Bigl(\sum_h e_h a_h\Bigr)=\sum_h e_h\epsilon(a_h),
 \qquad \Pi_{0,\mathrm{op}}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

**Theorem N3.** On every admitted composable domain,

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,\mathrm{op}}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
 \epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B=
 \langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

The inclusions, block assembly, all complementary cuts, finite sums,
products and adjoints specialize compatibly. The forward unit-start
construction recovers exactly P's 513 positions /570 source-choice-
labelled readings and retains the other two terminal channels.

**Proof.** Apply ε entrywise; every event factor has ε(1+z_e)=1.
Inclusions and cuts have 0/1 entries, and all source-query data remain
in the history labels. Thus assembly commutes with specialization and
the same truth channel is selected at every grade. Induction over the
finite source-qualified paths, using M/S and P's exhaustive fibres,
gives both directions of accepted-path recovery. □

Support recovery is restricted to these forward unit-start paths;
arbitrary signed combinations can cancel. Zero-specialization is not
the later observation that forgets history and prints a word.

## N4. Ending refinement gives a dependent sum of choices

For one completed B position x, let E_x contain the admitted ordinary
ending and, only where eligible, the tāt ending. Resolve the ending
before querying its vowel choice. Let C_(x,e) be that source-qualified
choice set. The terminal reading set is

\[
 \mathcal R_x=\coprod_{e\in E_x}\{e\}\times C_{(x,e)},\qquad
 |\mathcal R_x|=\sum_{e\in E_x}|C_{(x,e)}|.
\]

Distinct e/choice histories give orthogonal terminal coordinate cuts
already over B_25. These are not mutually independent switches to
multiply at every ending.

**Proposition N4.** Hāk B 2sg has five terminal histories, hāk B
3sg three, bhī B 2sg four and bhī B 3sg three. At O hāk has
one per slot, while bhī has two. These counts survive N3.

**Proof.** At hāk hi the choice fibre is {ā,i,ī}, whereas at tāt
it is {i,ī}; the disjoint union has 3+2=5. Hāk tu has only ā,
so its 3sg count is 1+2=3. Bhī has two weak hi and two weak tāt
choices, but one strong tu. Before O's y, 6.4.118 makes hāk's
fibre a singleton, while 6.4.115 retains bhī's two alternatives.
All these cuts and labels remain at zero. □

The terminal history module for hāk B 2sg has five basis coordinates;
this is not a claim that a map from one input vector has rank five.
P counts readings per morphological position, not Cartesian products
of independent choices across a whole nine-slot paradigm.

## N5. Target type is necessary for the guṇa query

Choose the pre-guṇa histories for nij I P 1sg and bhṛ I P 1sg.
Both are abhyasta, effectively pit and actually vowel-facing after
āṭ. Nij queries a light upadhā; bhṛ queries final ik. On this
two-history block the admitted guṇa cut is G=diag(0,1).

Let q retain only those three common Boolean fields and identify their
coordinate units. Then v=e_nij−e_bhṛ lies in ker(q), but
qGv=−e_w≠0. Hence G cannot descend through this coarse observation.
Retaining target type repairs this pair. It does not identify complete
stems or assert that target type suffices for every lexical exception.

This obstruction already holds over B_25 and remains at zero. M2's
source restriction is therefore a necessary part of the shared guard,
not a cosmetic distinction between the printed nenijāni/bibharāṇi.

## N6. Boundary

The construction is finite and source-relative. N2 supplies lawful
reuse under stated interface conditions, not automatic root coverage,
universal confluence or a general asiddha solver. Source variants,
runtime equivalence, machine replay and independent/formal review remain
separate obligations. Frozen packets and canonical engine code are unchanged.
