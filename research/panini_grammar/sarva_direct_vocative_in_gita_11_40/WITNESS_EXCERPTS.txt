location: Bhagavadgītā 11.40 and the two following printed explanations
verse:
नमः पुरस्तादथ पृष्ठतस्ते
 नमोऽस्तु ते सर्वत एव सर्व ॥
अनन्तवीर्यामितविक्रमस्त्वं
 सर्व समाप्नोषि ततोऽसि सर्वः ॥ ४०॥

commentary observations:
नमोऽस्तु ते तुभ्यं सर्वत एव सर्वासु दिक्षु स्थिताय हे सर्व ।
हे सर्व सर्वात्मन्सर्वास्वपि दिक्षु तुभ्यं नमोऽस्तु ।

Policy: the verse and the two explicit he sarva explanations establish one
direct M vocative-singular address and distinguish it from the same verse's
N accusative sarvam and M nominative sarvah. They do not attest PG887's other
gender/number vocatives, exact accent, a Vedic recitation or unrestricted
compound/class-sensitive use.
