# PG890 · Direct *sarva* address in Bhagavadgītā 11.40

Monty · 25 November 2026

Bhagavadgītā 11.40 places unmarked **sarva** between the second-person
`te` and the separately inflected `sarvam ... sarvaḥ`. Two commentarial
expansions on the pinned printed page explicitly read the first token as
**he sarva**. This positively closes direct textual attestation for PG887's
masculine vocative singular, without recrediting its nominal position.

The verse itself preserves a useful three-way same-lemma contrast:
`sarva` is the addressed masculine singular, `sarvam` is the neuter
accusative singular object of `samāpnoṣi`, and `sarvaḥ` is the masculine
nominative singular predicate. One source-syntax construction has three
interfaces; one semantic discrimination has three records; three source
observations and two source-policy decisions are new. Finite and nominal
position/readout counts remain **52,531 / 26,040 / 29,690**.
