## Adopted source and spelling policy

Use the **replacement analysis** of 4.1.78: the already licensed inner
इञ् is replaced by ष्यङ् and carries its qualified अपत्य origin into the
new value. Nyāsa explains this analysis; the pinned Padamañjarī 4.1.78 gives
the coherent spelling family **उदमेघ → औदमेघि → औदमेघ्या → औदमेघेय**.
That family is the adopted readout here.

The pinned Nyāsa field mixes **उदमेघ** with **औदमेध्या/औदमेधेय**. Those
literal bytes remain in the source archive. This history introduces no घ→ध
operation and does not claim an independent manuscript correction. Padamañjarī
also reports Vāmana's distinct **प्रत्ययपक्ष**, with an additional explanation
for अपत्य status. That is a separate source policy, not an optional stem
form or a universally disproved analysis. See [A source notes](SOURCE_NOTES_A.md).

record. The आ is one active sound with two source contributors, not two
successively displayed vowels. Recognizing `F` as feminine-affix-ended does
not turn every later phonetic query into a query about either old vowel;
6.1.85 itself restricts sound-based uses of its transfer.

The initial वृद्धि is not lost when `f` is replaced. Padamañjarī explicitly
discusses why the इञ् marker must have received its इत् identity rather than
having the replacement chosen prematurely so that the needed वृद्धि vanishes.
This step order therefore retains the cause of the already obtained औ.

चुटू marker-deletion pass cannot remove that ढ् before its prescribed
replacement. The outer कित् condition at step 11 is distinct from the inner
ञित् condition at step 2.

### Cause and view checks for the deletion pair

Both outer deletion instructions inspect the **same outer `t`** as their
निमित्त. Under 6.4.22's समानाश्रय account, the first deletion is not used
to manufacture a prerequisite for the second: the final आ, भ view and
taddhita environment required at step 13 were present both before and after
step 12. The inner अ-lopa of step 3 instead had **`f`** as its cause; its
event is not interchangeable with the later outer आ-lopa.

For a separately requested **148-first** query, an implementation would need
an expressly admitted 6.4.22 view preserving the pre-148 उपधा relation for
151, indexed by this same `t`. Nyāsa and Padamañjarī 6.4.149 explain the
model distinction: a deletion before an earlier अण् and a later ई-consumer
have different causes, while two operations triggered by that same ई use the
same-cause असिद्धवत् view. This supplies the local view principle; it does
not justify using any convenient old string. The 148-first order is not a
second counted derivation or a general commutation theorem here.

The special **1.1.58 यलोप** restriction on lopa-sthānivad is a different
instruction from 6.4.22. Padamañjarī 6.4.149 explicitly excludes ordinary
sthānivad for both of its vowel-deletion events in a य-lopa query while
still distinguishing their असिद्धवत् behavior. Nor does that restriction
erase 1.1.56's source-authorized **अपत्य status** transfer from the inner
suffix: an origin property is not the missing vowel's active phonetic value.

### Why the output needs the retained origin

The 6.4.151 query at step 12 requires the inner य's अपत्य provenance.
Merely appending a य-shaped feminine suffix without a supplied transfer
policy cannot manufacture this fact. Nyāsa's replacement account establishes
it through the original इञ्. Its attempted fallback to **6.4.150** also
fails in this environment: that rule requires **ई**, whereas the actual
outer instruction has **एय**. The two vowel onsets are not interchangeable.

This claim is relative to the adopted replacement policy. Padamañjarī's
reported Vāmana account supplies another explicit argument for अपत्य status
in a प्रत्यय analysis. It must remain a different supplied policy; the
countermodel without any such evidence is not a refutation of that account.

There are two visible य identities at step 11. The deletion selects `y₁`
inside the preceding भ host. `y₂` belongs to the outer suffix, follows ए
rather than a consonant, and is outside that target address. The final
औदमेघेय therefore retains `y₂` while the earlier inner `y₁` survives only
in provenance. Empty surface contribution does not erase `f` or `g` from
the nested history.
