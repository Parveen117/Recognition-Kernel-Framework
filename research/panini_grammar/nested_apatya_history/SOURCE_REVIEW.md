## S2. Coherent spelling without an invented sound change

Nyāsa 4.1.78 begins its relevant example with **उदमेघ** but gives later
**औदमेध्या/औदमेधेय** displays. The stated derivation contains no घ→ध
instruction. Full Padamañjarī 4.1.78 instead provides the coherent family
**उदमेघ / औदमेघि / औदमेघ्या / औदमेघेय**. H01 explicitly adopts
that spelling policy and preserves the mixed Nyāsa field literally.
Kaumudī and Vasu 4.1.78 do not independently supply this particular example;
their inclusion cannot be cited as an additional spelling witness.

The resulting local stem construction is evidence for its declared reading,
not a critical-edition correction to every occurrence of the name. PG47's
prospective ध-family references are left frozen and qualified by the present
source decision rather than silently rewritten.

## S3. Replacement and addition are distinct source accounts

Nyāsa argues that the specified अण्/इञ् replacement inherits the original
अपत्य status needed by 6.4.151. A proposed fresh feminine suffix without
that cause cannot obtain the deletion merely because an earlier descent
suffix exists elsewhere in the tree. Its suggested 6.4.150 fallback fails
because the following **एय** does not satisfy that consumer's **ई** gate.

The full Padamañjarī field is material: after presenting the replacement
argument it also reports **Vāmana's प्रत्ययपक्ष**, granting ष्यङ् the
relevant अपत्य status when prescribed after an अपत्य formation. Therefore
the correct negative control is a fixed-policy model **without a licensed
transfer**, not a universal claim that every new-affix analysis fails.
The alternate policy needs its own full construction and is not an optional
output branch inferred from this disagreement.

H01 adopts the addressed replacement policy and its source-qualified
transport. The [domain proposition](DOMAIN_AND_PROVENANCE.md) compares
candidate/query records, including a rejected analysis; it does not claim
two legal Sanskrit histories or a general impossibility for parsers that
can recover additional evidence.

## S5. Local correction and next-policy sources

Nyāsa **6.4.151** cites **4.2.79** for the चातुरर्थिक ण्य source of
सांकाश्य/काम्पिल्य. Kāśikā **4.2.80** actually provides that list and
those examples; 79 concerns its separate ककारोपधा → अण् prescription.
The adopted control uses 80 while the erroneous literal number remains.

The next heading **ये च तद्धिते** is exactly **6.1.61**; the concrete
हस्तिशिरस् discussion is in **6.1.62 अचि शीर्षः**. Kāśikā 62 explicitly
contrasts intended **हास्तिशीर्ष्या** with unwanted **हास्तिशीर्षण्या**.
Padamañjarī preserves both the added-suffix explanation and a replacement
account with a specific सन्निपात safeguard; its 4.1.78 discussion also
prevents treating that safeguard as an unrestricted invariant. These are
verified next-work inputs, not another completed derivation in PG48.
The empty Nyāsa 61/62 keys supply no independent account.
