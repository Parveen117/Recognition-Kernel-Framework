## Coherent घ spelling and a declared replacement policy

**औदमेध्या** and **औदमेधेय**. No operation in the stated chain accounts

coherent **उदमेघ / औदमेघि / औदमेघ्या / औदमेघेय** family. H01 adopts
that readout rather than manufacturing a consonant change. This is a local
source-qualified spelling choice; no manuscript-wide collation or corrected
critical edition is claimed.

Nyāsa argues for **आदेशपक्ष** because the original अण्/इञ् supplies the
अपत्य status that its substitute needs for later 6.4.151. Padamañjarī first
develops that account but then reports Vāmana's **प्रत्ययपक्ष**, with an
explicit explanation that ष्यङ् prescribed from an अपत्य derivative can
itself receive the relevant अपत्य status. It also discusses the separate
हास्तिशीर्ष्य problem. Thus H01 is an adopted bounded replacement analysis,
not a claim that every source agrees or that adding a suffix is intrinsically
incapable of that status. A new-affix countermodel **without** the required
