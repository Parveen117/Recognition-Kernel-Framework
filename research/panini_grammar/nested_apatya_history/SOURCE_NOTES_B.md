## Consumer scope is not one deletion flag

Kāśikā and Nyāsa **6.4.150** expressly withdraw the preceding “in a
taddhita” condition and retain **ई**. The target is the consonant-preceded,
उपधा य् of a taddhita in its admitted भ view. The source's कारिकेयी
blocks deletion because the preceding ए is not हल्; वैद्यी separately
guards the taddhita-origin condition. C06 uses the explicit **सौमी इष्टिः**
case, whose inner suffix has the देवता meaning rather than अपत्य.

At **6.4.151**, the target's apatya identity, preceding consonant and
following non-आ-initial taddhita are jointly required. The outer suffix need
not itself mean offspring: **गार्गकम्** has the collective वुञ् after
an inner apatyārtha यञ्. Conversely, **सांकाश्यकः** retains its य्
because the inner ण्य is geographical/chāturarthika, even with a suitable
outer वुञ्. These are different semantic owners in the same history.

The exclusion **अनाति** concerns long आ, not all अ-class vowels. The
अक output of 7.1.1 in C02 is therefore distinct from the आयन output of
7.1.2 in C04. The latter is present at प्रत्ययोपदेशकाल in Kāśikā and
Nyāsa; retaining original फ् does not license a later consumer to ignore
its prescribed initial आ. The same distinction lets the main chain retain
ढक् origin while testing its current एय onset.

## Provenance transport and the policy boundary

Nyāsa **4.1.78** argues that the replacement ष्यङ् inherits the relevant
apatya identity of the original अण्/इञ्. In its hypothesized new-suffix
analysis, that license is absent and the later 6.4.151 operation would fail.
It explicitly denies 6.4.150 as a fallback because the following एय does
not satisfy ई. C01 isolates precisely that consumer and cause distinction.

The full **Padamañjarī 4.1.78** field supplies a necessary qualification:
it also reports Vāmana's प्रत्ययपक्ष, with a separate argument granting
apatya identity to ष्यङ् prescribed after an apatya formation. C01's
countermodel is therefore expressly stipulated to lack a licensed transfer
under the selected Nyāsa policy. It does **not** prove that every possible
new-affix account must fail. Source-qualified alternatives belong to distinct
policy inputs; they are not automatically optional grammatical branches.

Padamañjarī's fuller discussion also invokes the **भस्याढे तद्धिते**
supplement, explaining why पुंवद्भाव does not remove the feminine layer
before this ढक्. Kāśikā and Kaumudī **6.3.35** preserve that supplement;
Kaumudī further identifies the relevant ढक् as the one from स्त्रीभ्यो
ढक्, distinguishing the अग्नेर्ढक् case. A suffix-spelling-only exclusion
would miss that origin qualification. The main derivation owns its explicit
use; these controls do not silently add a universal पुंवद्भाव prohibition.

## Local reference correction and source damage

Nyāsa **6.4.151** attaches **4.2.79** to its चातुरर्थिक ण्य explanation.
The actual **4.2.80** Kāśikā list supplies संकाशादि → ण्य and the
relevant सांकाश्यम्/काम्पिल्यम् forms. Rule 4.2.79 instead concerns
ककारोपधा → अण्. C03 uses the corrected dependency and leaves the literal
wrong reference unchanged in the archive.

The same Nyāsa field has damaged spellings such as साङ्काख्यकः and
गाग्र्यायणः; legible control labels follow the Kāśikā witnesses. The
Padamañjarī field also contains substantial OCR-like damage. Its reported
alternative is attributed to that field and is not an independent manuscript
collation. The औदमेघ/औदमेध spelling policy belongs to the packet's
[source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md), not to an undocumented sound change.

## Visibility and outcome limits

Kāśikā/Nyāsa **6.4.22** and Kāśikā **6.4.149** distinguish same-condition
and different-condition effects. In particular, the 149 discussion treats
तद्धित and ई environments separately. A target's being retained in history
is not by itself permission to read it in every consumer view. The controls
declare their local views and leave actual ordered realization to the main
history instead of using a blanket “all deletions commute” assumption.

Source witnesses are distinguished from completed word derivations. Unknown
origin remains OPEN; a decisive wrong-origin, wrong-predecessor or wrong-onset
condition is BLOCKED. None of the six records adds a sūtra, an agreement
position or an executed test. Accent, complete inflection, general lexical
admission and runtime/replay remain outside the work performed here.
