# PG48 — Consumer controls for inherited apatya identity

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These six written controls accompany the [nested derivation](DERIVATION.md).
They use the full operator/history carrier and PG47's revision-bound
instruction interface. A sound address names an occurrence and its owner;
it does not name every visible य्. Each APPLIED/BLOCKED/OPEN result is local
to its stated consumer. None is a generated paradigm or a new catalogue ID.

The consumer domains are different:

|Consumer|Owned य् and environment|
|---|---|
|6.4.150|The consonant-preceded, उपधा य् of a taddhita in its admitted भ view, before ई; the inherited following-taddhita condition is withdrawn|
|6.4.151|The consonant-preceded apatya य्, before a taddhita whose admitted initial is not long आ|

The second rule restricts the origin of the **target य्**, not the meaning
of every following suffix. The first rule is not a general fallback when
the second fails: its ई condition remains necessary. Active sound, affix
origin and source-qualified consumer view are separate coordinates.

## C01 — Replacement cause, a rejected addition model and missing origin

**Sources:** Kāśikā/Nyāsa 4.1.78, 1.1.56, 4.1.120, 6.4.150–151
and 7.1.2; Padamañjarī 4.1.78 for the explicitly separate policy.

**Given query.** The addressed inner य् is preceded by a consonant. The
qualified feminine formation and following ढक् history are supplied; the
outer suffix has its prescribed एय onset. Fix the adopted Nyāsa replacement
policy and the remaining phonological/भ gates. Compare three proposed
provenance records with the same relevant active display.

|Proposed record|Origin evidence at the inner य्|6.4.151 decision|
|---|---|---|
|Replacement history|The same inner suffix was admitted as apatyārtha इञ् and replaced by ष्यङ्; Nyāsa explicitly licenses its inherited अपत्य identity|APPLIED: delete that inner य् under the supplied consumer conditions|
|Rejected new-suffix model|A freshly added feminine ष्यङ् is stipulated to have no licensed अपत्य transfer; the earlier इञ् is a different token|BLOCKED under this fixed policy: the target's required origin is false|
|Incomplete history|The word is visible, but whether its addressed य् has a licensed apatya origin/transfer is unresolved|OPEN: request the source clause, policy and token ancestry before mutation|

1. Read the cause record on the addressed inner token. Merely finding an
   earlier apatyārtha suffix elsewhere in the tree does not transfer its
   permissions to a new token.
2. In the replacement history, use the specific transfer argument of Nyāsa
   4.1.78. The retained origin supports 6.4.151 even though the current
   payload differs from the original इञ्. This is not unrestricted copying
   of all original phonological properties.
3. In the stipulated addition model, do not invent that transfer. Nor can
   6.4.150 rescue the deletion: the outer suffix begins ए, not ई. Nyāsa
   expressly rejects that fallback in its argument.
4. Record the local decision and its cause without deleting the outer एय's
   own य् or rewriting the original base. The main derivation owns the
   remaining vowel deletion, assembly and output obligations.

**Policy boundary.** The rejected model is an analytical countermodel, not
a second admitted Sanskrit branch. Padamañjarī also reports Vāmana's distinct
प्रत्ययपक्ष, which grants a new ष्यङ् an apatya status because it is
prescribed after an apatya formation. That source-qualified policy does not
satisfy the countermodel's “no licensed transfer” premise. It must be a
separately declared input, not silently rejected or mixed with the selected
replacement proof. [Source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) preserves this distinction.

**Closed scope:** the addressed origin/consumer decision under a fixed policy.
**Open obligations:** a general ancestry recognizer, reconciliation or full
construction of alternative source policies, accent and declined paradigms.

## C02 — गार्गकम्: short अ does not trigger the long-आ exclusion

**Sources:** 4.1.105, 4.2.39, 7.1.1 and 6.4.151.

**Given history.** A supplied गार्ग्य base retains its actual apatyārtha
यञ् origin. A distinct outer वुञ् is admitted in the collective meaning
under 4.2.39. The relevant marker, भ and token-ownership evidence is supplied.
The य् address belongs to the inner suffix, preceded by ग्.

1. The outer वुञ् has the qualified वु material addressed by 7.1.1;
   record its अक substitution at that outer suffix. This is a distinct
   event from deleting any inner sound.
2. Query 6.4.151 on the inner य्. Its apatya origin and consonant-preceded
   gate are T, and the following suffix is a taddhita.
3. Test **अनाति** against its admitted onset: अक begins in short अ.
   The exclusion is long **आ**, so it is F here. Do not block all
   अ-initial derivatives by collapsing vowel duration.
4. Apply the inner य्-lopa decision, retaining the original यञ् and
   its cause in history. No deletion of the outer अक is licensed.

**Result:** APPLIED for the specified य्-lopa. Kāśikā's **गार्गकम्** is
the word witness; this control stops at the addressed deletion and does not
newly derive every initial grade, vowel junction, case ending or accent step.
The outer meaning is **समूह**, while the inner target's provenance is
**अपत्य**. Thus 6.4.151 does not require the outer suffix itself to mean
offspring in order to consume an inner apatya य्.

## C03 — सांकाश्यकः: a taddhita य् need not be apatya

**Sources:** 4.2.80, 4.2.121, 7.1.1 and 6.4.150–151.

**Given history.** The सांकाश्य base has its supplied चातुरर्थिक ण्य
origin, with its संकाशादि admission; a distinct outer वुञ् is licensed
by 4.2.121 in the stated residual geographical domain. Its qualified वु
has the अक reading. The inner य् is consonant-preceded, but its origin
is affirmatively **non-apatya**.

1. Retain both suffix tokens and their different meaning clauses. An ण्य
   marker or a general तद्धित label does not itself imply अपत्य.
2. Query 6.4.151: the required apatya-origin gate is F. Record BLOCKED
   without deleting that य्, despite the admitted non-आ outer onset.
3. Query 6.4.150 as a proposed fallback: its following ई gate is F;
   the outer अक is not ई. Record that separate denial.

**Result:** BLOCKED for both attempted य् deletions in this environment.
The retained य् agrees with the source's **सांकाश्यकः**, with
**काम्पिल्यकः** as the companion control. Full formation of the supplied
geographical base, outer realization, inflection and accent remain outside
this local decision.

Nyāsa 6.4.151 gives an embedded **4.2.79** reference for the ण्य origin.
The operative list is **4.2.80**, whose Kāśikā field explicitly supplies
संकाशादि → ण्य and the examples सांकाश्यम्/काम्पिल्यम्. Rule 4.2.79
instead prescribes अण् for its ककारोपधा domain. The reference correction
is local; the literal Nyāsa field is retained unchanged.

## C04 — गार्ग्यायणः: prescribed आयन is the relevant onset

**Sources:** 4.1.101, 7.1.2 and 6.4.151.

**Given history.** The admitted यञ्-ended गार्ग्य base is followed by
the source-qualified फक् of 4.1.101, in its गोत्र/युवन् domain. The
inner य् has its apatya origin and consonant-preceded relation. The outer
suffix's original फ् and its prescribed replacement remain distinct views.

1. At the admitted suffix instruction, 7.1.2 replaces the addressed initial
   फ् by आयन्, preserving the remainder of the suffix and its markers.
   The operative suffix therefore has an **आयन** beginning.
2. Kāśikā and Nyāsa 7.1.2 place these substitutions at प्रत्ययोपदेशकाल.
   A subsequent onset query cannot test only the old consonant फ् and
   pretend that the prescribed आ has not been introduced.
3. Query 6.4.151. The **आ-initial** exclusion is T. Return BLOCKED
   for the inner य् deletion and retain its active value.

**Result:** BLOCKED, agreeing with the source witness **गार्ग्यायणः**
and its companion वात्स्यायनः. This is not a universal phonetic rejection
of every long vowel: the source names आ. No optional deletion branch is
licensed by retaining the old फ् in history. Other realization, inflection
and accent steps remain downstream from this onset/deletion control.

## C05 — कारिकेयिः: apatya identity does not erase the hal condition

**Sources:** 4.1.120, 4.1.95, 7.1.2 and 6.4.150–151.

**Given history.** The first derivative कारिकेय retains its supplied
कारिका + ढक् origin and the एय replacement. A second apatyārtha इञ्
is independently admitted from that अ-final derivative. Address the **य्
inside the first एय**, immediately preceded by ए.

1. The target's apatya provenance is affirmative, and the new suffix is
   a taddhita with a non-आ beginning. These facts do not settle the query.
2. Resolve **हल उत्तरस्य** at the target address. Its preceding sound
   is ए, a vowel. That required consonant relation is F.
3. Return BLOCKED for 6.4.151 and preserve that य्. The source witness
   **कारिकेयिः** retains it.

The companion 6.4.150 example **कारिकेयी** makes the same point before
an admitted feminine ई: even the correct following vowel cannot replace
the missing hal relation. The target is not every य् in an apatya expression.
In the main H01 chain, an attempt to delete the **outer एय's own य्**
likewise cannot borrow the consonant relation of the different inner य्.

**Closed scope:** consonant-predecessor admission and the resulting denial.
**Open obligations:** the full supplied base histories, later nominal endings
and accent. No output is generated from this failed mutation request alone.

## C06 — सौमी: the ई consumer can admit a non-apatya य्

**Sources:** 4.2.30, 4.1.15, 1.4.18, 6.4.129 and 6.4.150–151.

**Given history.** The supplied सौम्य base comes from सोम + ट्यण् in
the देवता meaning. Its य् is taddhita but not apatya, and is preceded by
म्. The separately admitted feminine ङीप् supplies ई. Evaluate the named
consumer on the appropriate भ/उपधा view, before treating final-vowel
deletion as a loss of all earlier target information.

1. Query 6.4.150: the source specifically admits this consonant-preceded
   taddhita य् before ई. No apatya requirement belongs to this consumer.
2. Apply the local य्-lopa decision. Its event belongs to the inner
   ट्यण्-origin sound, not to the following ङीप्.
3. Retain the separate final-vowel and accent obligations. The source's
   **सौमी इष्टिः** is a witness, not a claim that the present control has
   independently constructed the entire expression.

**Result:** APPLIED for the 6.4.150 query. Nyāsa 6.4.151 explains why the
later mention of तद्धित preserves this earlier ई domain without importing
an apatya restriction into it. A global “all य-lopa requires apatya” flag
would therefore reject a source-admitted case.

These controls use explicit query views, not a universal rule that deletion
can always inspect any old state. The [main derivation](DERIVATION.md)
records its actual order and visible dependencies. The source discussion of
6.4.22/149 distinguishes same- and different-condition views; neither
retaining history nor the label असिद्ध licenses arbitrary event reordering.
