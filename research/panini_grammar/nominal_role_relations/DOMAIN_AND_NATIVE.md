# Domain and specialization

## N1. Formal input

Use typed variables actor, incorporated nominal and optional free participant. A role certificate identifies SELF_WISH, OBJECT_MODEL, PLACE_MODEL or ACTOR_MODEL and the matching formation. Formulas use equality for actor/beneficiary and separate argument positions for standard, patient and locus. A selected example instantiates a frame; other freely invented nominal-role combinations are outside this packet.

## N2. Relational evaluation

Compose role certificate → nominal-affix history → finite stem → ending. A fixed role may have two supported derivational outputs; a fixed word may recover two roles. Store the ordinary word map and its retained labelled preimages separately. The full carrier extends the previous free unital dagger system using these source-labelled arrows.

## N3. Zero-reading and converse

On each basis input, erasing role-path metadata after construction yields exactly the listed finite request-word pairs. Conversely each listed pair has an explicit path with all input guards satisfied. Extend by union on supports and incidence linearity on finite bases. The converse of the relation retains both putrīyati analyses; counting the two paths neither proves equal probability nor gives an isometry.
