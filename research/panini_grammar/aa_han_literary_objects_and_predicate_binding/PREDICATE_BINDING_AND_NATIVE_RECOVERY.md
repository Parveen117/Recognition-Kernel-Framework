# Predicate-indexed object roles before pada selection

Monty · 17 November 2026

## 1. What the newly read fields decide

Kāśikā 1.3.28 separately admits akarmaka ā + han and the own-limb vārttika;
its external-head counterexample has neither condition. Mādhavīya reports
the literary ājaghne problem, an āja/ghne split which it rejects, and an
own-chest explanation. It questions the Bhaṭṭi example too. Preserve that
reported disagreement rather than replacing it with a universal verdict.

Bālamanoramā explicitly supplies prāpya for the first analysis. It assigns
the relevant karman to attainment and says the object's actual-world
relation to striking is not selected in the presented han analysis. Thus
its akarmaka licence is a source-selected vivakṣā decision, not a claim
that a real strike never has a target. Its explanation distinguishes
supplying an actual lyab word from only its meaning in the separate
lyab-lopa/pancamī objection. This packet records that explanation; it does
not derive a general case rule from the mere presence of an accusative.

The same Bālamanoramā field and Tattvabodhinī supply another analysis:
approach the other participant, then strike the actor's own chest as a
wrestler might. Here chest ownership changes, so the own-limb vārttika
applies. The other participant's chest and the actor's chest cannot be
identified just to keep a preferred surface. These are separate hypotheses
with distinct source/ownership labels. Tattvabodhinī also mentions bhettum
ityādi; the brief cross-reference alone is not a complete purpose parse.

## 2. A finite typed binding construction

Before any zero-reading, start with the full PG14 grammar-history carrier
and extend each grammatical state with a finite typed incidence record:

- predicate IDs, their original lexical identity, finite/nonfinite type,
  printed versus supplied status and ordered composition;
- nominal mentions with case, participant identity and ownership variables;
- labelled role edges (nominal, predicate, role), plus selected and
  unselected semantic relations and their source witnesses;
- the policy choosing an analysis, or an explicit Unknown policy.

For this finite domain define K_han(h) as the set of selected karman edges
targeting the han predicate. Define A(h) as a positively source-supplied
akarmaka decision, consistent with the absence of a selected han karman.
Define S(h) as a selected han karman whose limb owner is coreferent with
the actor, under the own-limb analysis. An absent edge is insufficient to
establish A: in an unfinished analysis the edge may simply be unknown.

The typed admission query, after checking original root/prefix/voice, is:
A(h) true gives the akarmaka route; S(h) true gives the svāṅga route.
If both are established false, these two routes are excluded on that
analysis. If the requisite role, ownership or policy is unknown, retain
Open. Each chosen reading must satisfy its consistency constraints; this
is not a rule that any desired ending can be obtained by deleting an edge.
The source supplies the specific avivakṣā analysis before admission.

In the prāpya analysis the expressed object targets PRAPYA, not HAN.
In the own-chest analysis it targets HAN and owner=ACTOR. The literal
external-object analysis targets HAN with owner≠ACTOR, excluding both
routes. These three finite records formally show why a sentence-global
Boolean `has_accusative_object` cannot determine the pada route: the
accusative is present in each, while the relevant target/owner differs.
Similarly, erasing a role edge without retaining an Unknown/source flag
cannot lawfully manufacture akarmaka admission.

The output is a pair: (pada-route status, full-derivation status). A source
can explain a pada without this packet supplying an entire morphology.
The Bhaṭṭi problem demonstrates that distinction: its prāpya analysis
admits the pada, but mā requires a prohibitive frame expressly excluded
from PG328/329. Do not infer the ordinary-past aṭ history from a matching
āhadhvam string. The original quotation and the separate supplied words
remain distinguishable. Neither the verse location nor authorial intent
is independently established by a commentator's offered reanalysis.

## 3. Actual state-changing native events

Retain PG14's native cut field C, its derived iota and dagger, free cut-path
algebra B=C⟨z_e,z_e*⟩ and typed right module M_lambda=⊕_h e_h B of finite
support, paired by ⟨x,y⟩_B=Σ_h x_h* y_h. Lambda names the whole operator/
history carrier. It is not a scalar truth value, model score or clock.

New events create the supplied PRAPYA predicate and its witnessed role
edge, record the named avivakṣā decision, or bind the own-chest owner under
the alternative source policy. They change actual typed semantic state;
they do not just append a name to an unchanged global transitivity bit.
A policy choice forks histories. Subsequent pada queries append the exact
role/ownership/source evidence. A reported rejected split stays in its
reported-analysis grade and never enters the finite ā + han grade.

For each admitted edge h→h', use T_e,lambda(e_h a)=e_h'(1+z_e)a.
The finite displayed records have no unbounded inference/search event.
Distinct source policies, rejected proposals and unresolved roles retain
their own histories. There are separate source/target grades for the
pada-query output and the full-derivation output; admitting a pada is not
an edge into the completed-word grade. On each status component keep
admitted, excluded and Open cuts plus their complements. Those cuts act
on retained histories, not on residue coefficients. The fixed pairing
and typed compositions are exactly those of PG14, without an analytic
completion or a physical representation.

## 4. Typed zero-specialization and branch recovery

Let epsilon:B→C take the empty-word coefficient, pi_0,M apply it to state
coefficients and Pi_0,op apply it to the differently typed operator
entries. For each new event,

    pi_0,M(T_e,lambda(e_h a))
      = e_h' epsilon((1+z_e)a)
      = e_h' epsilon(a)
      = T_e,0(pi_0,M(e_h a)).

The source-policy, role and owner guards inspect the retained history,
so their answers do not change. The algebra-homomorphism/dagger laws
extend this identity to finite typed compositions, status cuts and their
complements, adjoints and the displayed pairing. Each unit-start zero
analysis has the same guarded lifted edge sequence and product of
(1+z_e) with constant coefficient 1; conversely no analysis edge is created
by specialization. This recovers precisely the displayed labelled finite
analysis relation, including its unresolved full-derivation components.

In particular zero-reading does not erase which predicate owns an object,
turn an unselected actual-world relation into a grammatical karman, merge
the two chest owners or complete a prohibitive derivation. A surface-word
readout can forget those distinctions, but it is a separate observation,
not the typed zero-specialization map. No grammar runtime, universal
ellipsis resolver or complete theory of authorial interpretation follows.
