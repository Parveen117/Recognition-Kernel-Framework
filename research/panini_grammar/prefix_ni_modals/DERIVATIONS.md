## H06. Loṭ and vidhiliṅ through the present interface

Reuse PG19's full thematic P/A ending constructors and kṛ analysis, PG22's śnam construction, and PG68's qualified kṣi modals. The prefix relation is retained outside those verbal interfaces, with PG71's mandatory gad, optional pac/viś and blocked original k/kh/ṣ decisions. No preceding ni alternative changes the present vikaraṇa.

| Root | Loṭ interface | Vidhiliṅ interface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gad | gad + śap; ordinary P thematic endings | gad-a + yāsuṭ → gade- through 7.2.79/80 and the ending seam |
| pac | pac + śap; ordinary P thematic endings | pac-a + yāsuṭ → pace- |
| viś | viś + śa; A thematic endings, no root guṇa | viś-a + sīyuṭ → viśe-/viśey- at the addressed vowel/consonant seam |
| kṛ | kar-u strong, kur-u weak; first-person āṭ gives karavā- | kur-u + yāsuṭ; 6.4.109 deletes u before y, giving kuryā- |
| kṣi | kṣi-nu; strong o/av and weak u/v, with internal ṇ | kṣi-nu + yāsuṭ; retained u, giving kṣiṇuyā- |
| piṣ | strong pi-na-ṣ, weak pi-n-ṣ; special dhi contact | weak pi-n-ṣ + yāsuṭ, read piṃṣyā- |
| khād | khād + śap, lexical ā unchanged | khād-a + yāsuṭ → khāde- |

Loṭ P third singular and all first-person endings are strong on the inherited athematic interfaces; second-singular hi is explicitly apit. Kṛ/kṣi hi-luk therefore gives **pranikuru/pranikṣiṇu** without promoting the remaining stem to a strong form. The first-person ending āṭ separates u from v/m, so the laṭ/laṅ u-lopa option cannot be copied into loṭ **pranikṣiṇavāva/pranikṣiṇavāma**. Vidhiliṅ's intervening yāsuṭ likewise blocks that direct m/v option. Kṛ has the separate y-conditioned 6.4.109 deletion; kṣi does not.

Piṣ loṭ first singular is the owned sequence pra | ni | pi-na-ṣ | āṭ | ni. Only the ending-ni n receives early ṇatva, yielding **pranipinaṣāṇi**. At second singular, weak śnam a-lopa and hi→dhi give pi-n-ṣ-dhi. Follow PG22's source-qualified retroflex contact and nasal restoration, then optionally collapse the stop pair: **pranipiṇḍḍhi/pranipiṇḍhi**. These two kinds of internal ṇ have different histories.

For viś, the thematic A duals distinguish **viśetām** in loṭ from **viśeyātām** in vidhiliṅ. In the latter, the augment's y survives before the ending vowel; it is not an arbitrary inserted glide. Loṭ A first singular uses ending iṭ→e→ai with ending āṭ, giving **viśai**. Vidhiliṅ uses ending iṭ→a and retained augment y, giving **viśeya**. Neither ending iṭ is an optional root-facing augment.

## H07. Āśīrliṅ and lṛṅ have their own recipients

P blessing uses PG20's full yāsuṭ/suṭ constructor with these pre-yāsuṭ factors:

| Root | Factor | New 3sg |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gad | gad | praṇigadyāt |
| pac | pac | praṇipacyāt / pranipacyāt |

Yāsuṭ begins with y outside val, so no ordinary 7.2.35 iṭ is added even to seṭ gad/khād. Third plural retains the relevant yāsuṭ s, giving **kriyāsuḥ**, not vidhiliṅ **kuryuḥ**. At dual/plural t/th recipients retain PG20's suṭ ownership and its local 8.2.29 schedule; 7.2.79 is inapplicable here.

For viś A, start from viś + sīyuṭ + the selected A ending. H03's aniṭ/1.2.11 kit guard preserves root i. Under 8.2.36/41, root ś→ṣ→k before s; 8.3.59 makes the first sīyuṭ s into ṣ. PG20's A constructor on **Z=vikṣ, δ=dh** then gives all nine cells. In **vikṣīṣṭa**, the first ṣ belongs to sīyuṭ, the second to t's suṭ; the root's earlier ś is now k. Dual **vikṣīyāstām/vikṣīyāsthām** keeps dental st/sth after ā. No passive O/C choice is introduced.

Lṛṅ transports PG71's actual future sya/iṭ/root interfaces, then adds aṭ at the verbal aṅga boundary and uses PG31's P/A secondary endings. Thus **praṇyagadiṣyat**, **praṇyapakṣyat/pranyapakṣyat**, **praṇyavekṣyata/pranyavekṣyata**, **pranyakariṣyat**, **pranyakṣeṣyat**, **pranyapekṣyat** and **pranyakhādiṣyat** are the seven third singulars. Kṛ keeps sya-specific iṭ; kṣi remains aniṭ. This is lṛṅ morphology on H01's completed context, not a new permissive counterfactual rule.

This is a finite planning complement, not a global Sanskrit grammar remainder or completion percentage.
