# PG72 — Pra + ni modals, conditionals and remaining perfects

Monty · 7 November 2026

Use the exact seven lexical profiles, prefix/action admission and padas of PG71. Loṭ is ordinary vidhi, vidhiliṅ is ordinary vidhi, āśīrliṅ expresses an unattained-event blessing, and lṛṅ uses H01's qualified future counterfactual context. The four P8 perfects use the inherited non-Mantra parokṣa context. All words are conditional constructions, not individually attested quotations.

Ni alternatives list ṇ before n. Pac perfect 2sg groups iṭ before no-iṭ; its 1sg groups short before long grade. Piṣ loṭ 2sg retains both allowed stop-collapse readings. Use the inherited non-doubling, unaccented, ordinary-pause spelling profile.
