# PG41 — 8.2.73–108: final alternants, vowel length and pluti

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Read with [Domain](DOMAIN.md), [Construction](CONSTRUCTION.md) and [Source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md). A pluti record owns its vowel, duration convention, accent and speech-act evidence. Historical ritual categories are source annotations, not inferred user attributes.
