# PG17 — Future continuation of the owned ṇic formation

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## C1. Input at the derived-dhātu boundary

Start on N1's native carrier with an exact PG14 causal frame: a causer,
a distinct causee/base agent, the admitted base event and its karmans,
the existing source-qualified causee-role branch and the principal-result
pada evidence. Use PG13's root operations and PG14/PG15's retained
pre-outer-affix derived dhātu. Its shape is

\[
 [C]_{root\ constituent}\;|\;[i]_{ṇic}.
\]

C can contain a root substitute and an owned root-final puk/yuk. These
owners remain distinct from ṇic. The group has dhātu status under 3.1.32.
It is not a present word with -ati/-ate removed, and it is not the bare
lexical root treated as if causativization had never happened.

Extend the base event's interpreted temporal frame by the completed
PG16 future vivakṣā. Keep the causal role decisions and use the causer
as agreement controller. No inference of causativity from a printed
string or a changed tiṅ ending is admitted.

## C2. Pada and role decisions survive the tense extension

PG14 V5–V7 supplies the causal role relation and the decision using
E (surviving 1.3.86–89 exception), F (principal result to the causer)
and U (the stated upapada condition). None of those cited rules imposes
a laṭ-only condition in the admitted causal frames. Therefore the same
source decisions compose with either future lakāra here:

| Established condition | Pada |
|---|---|
| E true | P |
| E false, F false | P |
| E false, F true, U false | A |
| E false, F true, U true | A or P, with the 1.3.77 branch witness |

In the inherited selected frames E is true for bhū, sthā and budh.
It is false for kṛ, nī, hṛ, pac, gam, dā, pā, vid and kṛṣ. Thus
there are twelve P and nine A **profile possibilities across completed
frames**, not unrestricted P/A variation for every root in one frame.
Unknown E/F/U retains PG14's Open and stable-branch treatment.

Causee kāraka and case choices are preserved too; hṛ/kṛ's optional
causee-role witnesses do not disappear because their future word can
be equal. They are not counted again as distinct morphological readings
in the numerical profile totals. Nested causatives, coincident participants,
other senses/prefixes and inanimate exceptions remain outside the inherited
domain. PG13's exact-word evidence qualifications, including kṛṣ's,
are not upgraded by this continuation.

## C3. iṭ protects ṇic from lopa

Insert sya/tās under 3.1.33 outside the **derived** dhātu. Both are
val-initial ārdhadhātuka affixes. Rule 7.2.35 supplies an owned iṭ
before them. The aniṭ classification of a bare base root such as kṛ
does not automatically classify the entire newly formed C|i dhātu:
7.2.10's original-root conditions are not satisfied merely by pointing
to that nested component. All twelve admitted C|i inputs have the
two-vowel derived shape and no selected overriding aniṭ condition.

Kāśikā **6.4.51** gives **kārayitā/hārayitā** as its explicit
negative controls: ṇic-lopa applies before an aniṭ ārdhadhātuka,
and this following affix begins with iṭ. Its lopa guard is false.
This is the source reason to keep ṇic, not an inference made solely
because a familiar future spelling contains -ay-.

Contrast the inherited causal passive: C|i_ṇic|ya_yak has no iṭ
from 7.2.35, because y is outside val, so PG15 applies ṇic-lopa.
The future and passive branches start at the same typed derived-dhātu
boundary but continue with different following-affix evidence.

## C4. Two i occurrences with different operations

After iṭ placement, the immediate owned sequence is

\[
 C\;|\;i_{ṇic}\;|\;i_{augment}\;|\;A_{future}\;|\;\tau,
 \qquad A_{future}\in\{sya,tās\}.
\]

The aṅga-final **ṇic-i** takes guṇa under 7.3.84, giving e. Rule
6.1.78 then gives ay before the augment's vowel. The **augment-i**
is protected against guṇa/vṛddhi by 1.1.6. Hence

\[
 C|i_{ṇic}|i_{augment}|A
 \to C|e_{ṇic}|i_{augment}|A
 \to C|ay_{ṇic}|i_{augment}|A.
\]

Before sya, 8.3.59 later gives **Cayiṣya**. Before tās, the luṭ
readout factor is **H=Cayit**. The guṇa, ayādi, augment protection
and ṣatva are four different addressed operations. A rule “protect
every i” blocks the needed ṇic change; a rule “guṇa every i” corrupts
the augment; “delete every causative i” confuses this future with yak.

The future sya/tās is not a kit/ṅit affix licensing a new 6.4.98
root-upadhā deletion. Its initial augment vowel alone is insufficient
for that rule. The old gam mit shortening remains the inherited
6.4.92 formation action; ciṇ/ṇamul-specific 6.4.93 does not create
an optional long gām future in this domain.

## C6. Complete worked continuations

**kṛ, causer first singular, principal result to causer, U false.**
PG14 gives A. FutureGeneral gives lṛṭ, whose first A ending iṭ
is separate from the augment:

\[
 kār|i_{ṇic}|i_{augment}|sya|iṭ_{ending}
 \to kār|ay|i|sya|e\to kārayiṣye.
\]

The last vowel junction is PG16's a+e→e under 6.1.97, after the
ending's 3.4.79 step. For the corresponding anadyatana frame:

\[
 kār|i|i|tās|iṭ_{ending}\to kār|ay|i|tās|e
 \to kārayitāhe,
\]

with tās-s→h by 7.4.52. No disappearance of ṇic is licensed.

**nī, two addressee causers, A frame.** C= nāy and the retained
ṇic becomes ay. Lṛṭ gives **nāyayiṣyethe** by the thematic A dual
chain. Luṭ gives **nāyayitāsāthe**: āthām→āthe remains after
the s-final tās interface, so 7.2.81's preceding-short-a guard fails.

**pā, several addressee causers, A frame.** The inherited 1.3.89
exception cancels the relevant ingestion exception in PG14's exact
domain. C=pā+y_yuk. Lṛṭ gives **pāyayiṣyadhve**. Luṭ gives
**pāyayitādhve**, using the adopted 8.2.25 tāsi-s lopa before dhve.
Neither operation deletes the yuk y or the ṇic-owned ay.

**gam, one third-person causer, P frame.** C=gam after the inherited
mit shortening. Outer iṭ comes from 7.2.35 on the derived dhātu,
not the bare-gam exception 7.2.58. The two words are **gamayiṣyati**
and **gamayitā**. Bare gam's **gamiṣyati/gantā** is a different
formation with different affix evidence.

**bhū/budh, result to causer.** The inherited surviving exceptions
still prescribe P in the specified frames. The future forms therefore
include **bhāvayiṣyati/bhāvayitā** and **bodhayiṣyati/bodhayitā**;
the intended benefit alone does not license A.

## C7. Causal continuation theorem

**Theorem C7.** For every completed inherited causal role/pada branch
in C1–C2 and either PG16 future interpretation, C3–C5 constructs the
unique selected stem interface. Composing it with PG16's nine ending
paths yields all 21·2·9 = **378 morphological positions** in P3.
It preserves the causal event, causee-role evidence, constituent ownership
and exact typed zero-reading.

**Proof.** The inherited frame determines the admitted pada branch and
causer controller. The source's causal pada/role rules have no laṭ-only
guard on these inputs. C3 supplies iṭ at the following future affix and
proves the 6.4.51 guard false. C4 uniquely addresses ṇic-i and augment-i
with their different rules; C5 exhausts the twelve inherited root
constituents. Both outputs meet PG16's thematic-sya or owned-tās ending
interface. Its ending proof therefore supplies each of the nine slots.
Every arrow retains the stated owners/history; N3 proves compatibility
of the composed native arrows and their zero readings. The count follows
from twelve P plus nine A profiles and two futures. □

The count does not quotient away the semantic role witnesses in the
native module. It only avoids counting unchanged semantic multiplicity
as additional morphological coverage. The theorem inherits the exact
source qualifications of PG13/PG14; it does not independently attest
every new full word in a classical corpus.
