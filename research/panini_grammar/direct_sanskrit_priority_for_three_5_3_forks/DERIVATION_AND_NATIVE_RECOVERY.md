# PG815 derivation and native recovery

## Positive source decision

PG814 retained three Kāśikā/Vasu forks as Open. Two already pinned direct
Sanskrit fields now decide the default channel without deleting the secondary
record:

| Rule | direct Sanskrit Kāśikā | Vasu English | decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.3.75 | `pūrṇakaḥ` | `cūrṇakaḥ` | `pūrṇaka` is canonical default; `cūrṇaka` remains an Accepted Vasu-English alternative |
| 5.3.76 | `bubhukṣitakaḥ` | `bubhukṣikaḥ` | `bubhukṣitaka` is canonical default; the shorter Vasu reading remains Accepted/Open internally |
| 5.3.76 | `śvasitaki` | `viśvasitaki` | `śvasitaki` is canonical default; the Vasu reading remains Accepted/Open internally |

This is an evidence-priority decision, not a claim that a secondary editor or
translator invented a form. The exact transmission history stays Open until an
independent print or manuscript witness is pinned.

## Ownership

- `pūrṇaka/cūrṇaka` remain name readings under censure and `kan`; the lexical
  base differs by source and is not merged.
- `bubhukṣitaka/bubhukṣika` remain compassion readings. Their internal
  desiderative/participial analysis is not reconstructed here.
- Direct `śvasitaki` retains root `śvas`, breathing sense, no preverb, active
  present third singular input and finite `akac` ownership already frozen by
  PG533/PG661/PG665. `viśvasitaki` is not assigned the same root or a `vi-`
  preverb merely from its English-source spelling; that analysis stays Open.
- The shared `svapitaki` reading is unaffected.

## PG14 carrier and typed zero specialization

Use the full PG14 carrier

`X=(rule,input,root,sense,preverb,voice,finite_cell,name_status,affix,source,edition,reading,status,history,outcome)`.

The source-priority extension `P_D` changes only the canonical-default status
when direct Sanskrit and an English-source field disagree. It preserves both
records and every root/sense/preverb/voice/affix owner. Its typed zero is
`P_0(X)=X`: with no positive priority evidence, the old Open fork passes
unchanged. Thus PG815 specializes the full operator/history carrier rather
than replacing it with a surface-string choice.
