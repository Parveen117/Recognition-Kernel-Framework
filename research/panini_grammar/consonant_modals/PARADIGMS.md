## P4. Exact finite count

**Theorem P4.** D2 has **324 positions /336 written readings**.

| Mood | Positions | Base readings | Extra weak-vowel reading | Extra tāt readings | Total readings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I | 108 | 108 | 1 | 0 | 109 |
| O | 108 | 108 | 0 | 0 | 108 |
| B | 108 | 108 | 1 | 10 | 119 |
| **Total** | **324** | **324** | **2** | **10** | **336** |

**Proof.** Twelve profiles have nine slots per mood. M3 adds one
reading at mṛj's ordinary 3pl in each I/B; M4 excludes this option
from O and tāt. Five P profiles each admit two B tāt targets;
their added word differs from the ordinary singular word. S6 excludes
any further sound branch. Hence 3·108=324 and 324+2+10=336.
N3 recovers this same source/choice-labelled relation at zero. □

The ordinary B words overlap I but the meaning profiles differ.
The laṅ query control in D4/N4 is not counted. This is neither a
distinct-string count nor a new runtime/whole-grammar completion claim.
