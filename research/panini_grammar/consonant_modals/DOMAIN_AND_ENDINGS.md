## D4. The īḍ/īś host is a directed source relation

Kāśikā 7.2.77 directly gives īśiṣva. Kāśikā and Kaumudī 7.2.78
give īḍiṣva/īḍidhvam and extend the dhve prescription to īś.
Kāśikā expressly admits loṭ's partially changed ending but excludes
laṅ's original dhvam; Bālamanoramā explains the direction of that
admission. A prescription naming the transformed dhve does not thereby
name every original dhvam from which such an ending might be formed.

Define a local, source-qualified host relation Host_77/78:
the admitted se/dhve occurrence and its specified loṭ descendant
sva/dhvam count for this augment query. It is not the symmetric
equivalence relation “has the same current ending letters.”
It does not reopen past aṭ, future iṭ or every operation of laṭ.

M1 applies iṭ before sound readout. N4 uses the expressly excluded
laṅ dhvam as a **query control only**. That control is not another
new paradigm or an extra counted PG24 position.
