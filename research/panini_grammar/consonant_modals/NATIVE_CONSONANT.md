## N5. A sharp three-coordinate linear repair bound

Consider at zero any C_Σ-linear repair r:C_Σ^6→C_Σ^k, appended to
q as q′=(q,r). Require that **each** L_i descend through q′: there
must be an operator on im(q′) intertwining q′ and L_i.

**Theorem N5.** Such a repair requires k≥3. Three coordinates suffice.

**Proof.** K=ker(q)=span(v_1,v_2,v_3) has dimension three. Suppose
v=Σ_i a_i v_i lies in K∩ker(r). Since q′v=0, descent implies
q′L_i v=0, and in particular qL_i v=0. But qL_i v=±a_i e_wi,
so every a_i=0. Thus r restricted to K is injective, forcing k≥3.

For sufficiency let r record the eligible coordinate in each block:
the coefficient of h_1+, of h_2− and of h_3+. The q coordinates give
the sum of the two coefficients in each pair. Subtraction recovers
the other coefficient, so q′ is injective on this six-dimensional
block. Every L_i therefore descends, by transport along this inverse. □

The same sum/selected-coordinate formulas define a B_24-linear native
repair, with 0/1 matrices and compatible zero map. A proposed compatible
native repair whose zero specialization belongs to the stated class
cannot use fewer than three output coordinates. No universal minimum
for nonlinear encodings, arbitrary observers or the entire grammar is
claimed. “Three” counts independent coefficient coordinates, not bits
or a universal number of program fields.
