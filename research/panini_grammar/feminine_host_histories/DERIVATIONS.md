# PG52 — Principal and subordinate feminine-host histories

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

The input X is the qualified **कारीषगन्ध्या** of PG51: its ष्यङ्
owner Y comes from the admitted 4.1.78 descent/feminine preparation, and
its चाप् owner A from 4.1.74. Earlier lexical formation, descent and
accent are supplied, not reconstructed here. The readable suffix residue
is य् followed by the feminine vowel; the original affix identities survive
their marker deletion. A supplied word ending in त्या or ध्या is not
automatically such an X.

Each history has its own compound nodes, referents, internal सुप owners,
gender request and source policy. The quoted विग्रह phrases supply the
case relations; this packet does not add a full case paradigm. The stage
tables record ordered dependencies, not physical time or a universal scheduler.

## H01 — Principal परम host and the two outer citations

Supply **परमा चासौ कारीषगन्ध्या च**: the adjective and X refer to
the same female bearer, with परम expressing the praise required by 2.1.61.
Kāśikā 6.1.13 directly witnesses the resulting पुत्र and पति forms;
the Nyāsa field explains the inner prescription and पुंवद्भाव.

|Stage|Construction and retained evidence|
|---|---|
|P1|Admit the supplied co-reference, praise sense and समर्थता under 2.1.1; retain X, Y and A.|
|P2|Supply the two related सुबन्त inputs and their distinct internal case owners at the inner node I.|
|P3|Form the 2.1.61 तत्पुरुष under 2.1.22. परम is the designated उपसर्जन under 1.2.43 and is first under 2.2.30; X supplies the principal bearer within I.|
|P4|Give the compound प्रातिपदिक status by 1.2.46 and remove its internal सुप affixes by 2.4.71. Deletion retains each case relation and owner in history.|
|P5|Apply 6.3.34 to the admitted भाषितपुंस्क, non-ऊङ्, co-referential परमा before this qualifying feminine उत्तरपद: परमा has the masculine-like shape परम. This operation targets the adjective, not X's final vowel.|
|P6|Obtain परमकारीषगन्ध्या. Y's female bearer is principal inside this expanded first member. The subordinate-feminine ending condition of 1.2.48 is absent there.|

The feminine meaning of the inner bearer is not changed to masculine by
P5. The masculine final citation describes the supplied पुत्र or पति.
These two citation words are not credited as two complete paradigms.

## H02 — Subordinate अति host with a masculine referent

Supply **कारीषगन्ध्यामतिक्रान्तः** and his son. The Bālamanoramā-labelled
material appended within the edition's Nyāsa 6.1.13 field explicitly
gives **अतिकारीषगन्ध्यः** and **अतिकारीषगन्ध्यपुत्रः**. Its masculine
input is retained; it is not substituted for Kāśikā's feminine input.

|Stage|Construction and retained evidence|
|---|---|
|M1|Admit X with owners Y/A, the supplied masculine referent who has exceeded X, and the accusative object relation.|
|M2|Use 2.2.18 with the cited अत्यादयः क्रान्ताद्यर्थे द्वितीयया prescription. Form the प्रादि तत्पुरुष [अति,X]; the supplied surpassing referent is principal, while X is subordinate. Do not infer this sense from the prefix alone.|
|M3|The object remains accusative when the principal referent's case varies. Retain the 1.2.44 एकविभक्ति उपसर्जन designation for X, with its अपूर्वनिपात limitation. It does not move X ahead of अति.|
|M4|Assign compound प्रातिपदिक status by 1.2.46 and apply 2.4.71 to its internal सुप owners. The surviving first-member stem is still a compound with X as its subordinate final constituent.|
|M5|Apply 1.2.48 to this compound ending in the subordinate स्त्रीप्रत्ययान्त X. With the inherited vowel/प्रातिपदिक scope of 1.2.47 and final-target rule 1.1.52, the old final आ becomes अ: अतिकारीषगन्ध्य. Preserve the shortening event and A's provenance.|
|M6|The whole referent here is masculine, so 4.1.3 does not license a fresh feminine suffix. Its optional standalone citation, using the ending route below, is अतिकारीषगन्ध्यः.|
|M7|For the supplied तस्य पुत्रः relation, form a new 2.2.8 outer compound with right member पुत्र; separately remove the new internal case owners.|
|M8|Query 6.1.13 at the outer node and original Y. Y's bearer is subordinate inside the expanded अति host, so the feminine exception does not remove the तदादि restriction. The entire first member is not Y's original prescribed host. BLOCKED: no य्→इ and no ensuing संप्रसारण-based lengthening.|
|M9|Supply the final external nominative singular of the son. At pause the cited result is अतिकारीषगन्ध्यपुत्रः, with short अ in the first member.|

The result does not authorize every ष्यङ्-bearing compound with अति:
the particular sense, accusative relation, gender and inner scope are inputs.

## H03 — Subordinate अति host with a fresh feminine suffix

Kāśikā 6.1.13 instead supplies **अतिक्रान्ता कारीषगन्ध्याम्**, then
**अतिकारीषगन्ध्या**, **अतिकारीषगन्ध्यापुत्रः** and
**अतिकारीषगन्ध्यापतिः**. These feminine readings are literal evidence.
The intermediate fresh-टाप् route below is an **explicit rule-based
reconstruction for that supplied female referent**. The selected 6.1.13
field does not itself spell out every intermediate event; this is not a
claim to have recovered an author's uniquely intended derivation.

|Stage|Construction and retained evidence|
|---|---|
|F1|Supply the female surpassing referent, the same accusative object X and its original owners Y/A. The principal female referent and X's female bearer have distinct role addresses.|
|F2|Form the admitted 2.2.18 प्रादि compound using अत्यादयः क्रान्ताद्यर्थे द्वितीयया; X remains the subordinate object.|
|F3|Assign X its 1.2.44 designation without reversing constituent order; retain compound status and the internal 2.4.71 case deletions.|
|F4|Apply 1.2.48 to the old subordinate feminine ending, producing the अ-final compound stem अतिकारीषगन्ध्य. The intended female gender of the whole does not cancel this internal shortening.|
|F5|For this newly formed अ-final compound used of the supplied female principal, admit 4.1.4 under 4.1.3 and introduce a fresh टाप् owner T. T belongs to the whole compound, not to the old object X. The later अनुपसर्जनात् heading at 4.1.14 is prospective; it is not an earlier prohibition on 4.1.4.|
|F6|Give initial ट् and final प् their इत् status by 1.3.7/3, and remove those markers by 1.3.9. The new suffix's pronounced residue is आ; the identity T remains.|
|F7|Apply 6.1.101 to the current अ+आ: one long आ results, yielding अतिकारीषगन्ध्या. The old shortened ending and new T are separate contributors in history. This is not reversal of F4 or permission to forget it.|
|F8|The new suffix T describes the principal female referent. Y still describes the subordinate X. The new outer feminine formation does not make Y principal, nor does it reopen the completed inner shortening on the new principal suffix.|
|F9|For each supplied तस्याः पुत्रः / पतिः relation, form the outer 2.2.8 compound with the exact immediate right member and fresh internal case owners, then apply their 2.4.71 deletion.|
|F10|Query 6.1.13 at the outer node and original Y. The expanded host fails the same अनुपसर्जन condition as in M8. T's principal status cannot be borrowed for Y's ष्यङ् identity. BLOCKED, despite the visible final आ.|
|F11|Supply the external masculine nominative singular for the son/husband and follow the ending route below: अतिकारीषगन्ध्यापुत्रः / अतिकारीषगन्ध्यापतिः at pause.|

This construction accounts for the Kāśikā feminine branch without changing
its literal bytes or treating the masculine short branch as erroneous.
Padamañjarī's mixed masculine विग्रह/long-आ wording remains a separate
unresolved textual-policy question; H03 does not silently emend that field.

## Shared external-ending route and credit boundary

These citations inherit the qualified external ending procedure of
[PG51](../shyan_compound_histories/DERIVATIONS.md). Under 4.1.1/2 the
specified masculine nominative singular request introduces सुँ after the
completed compound. Its nasalized marker is deleted by 1.3.2/9, giving
final स्. The सुबन्त is a पद under 1.4.14; 8.2.66 supplies रुँ, whose
marker is likewise removed, and 8.3.15 gives visarga at pause. The external
ending is not one of the internal सुप owners already deleted by 2.4.71.
The पति-final examples keep their stem-final इ; P11 lengthens only the
first member's specified vowel.

There are **three histories**, with P1–P12, M1–M9 and F1–F11 respectively,
plus nine controls. Shared citations and intermediate forms do not increase
catalogue-ID, complete-position or paradigm-reading counts. Accent, the
earlier lexical/descent preparation and general source-policy arbitration
remain open.
