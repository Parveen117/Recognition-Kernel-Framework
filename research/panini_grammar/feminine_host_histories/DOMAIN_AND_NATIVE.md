## D2. Why separate existential witnesses do not suffice

|Owner|Prescribed ष्यङ् identity|Principal bearer in the expanded host|

|Fresh T|No: टाप्|Yes: the whole female surpassing referent|

Consequently both statements “some owner is ष्यङ्” and “some owner has
a principal female bearer” are true, but no owner in this exhibited pair

**Proof.** For Y the second predicate is false, while for T the first is

must retain the bound owner and role scope. □
