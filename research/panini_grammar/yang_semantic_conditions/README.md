# PG160 · Repetition, crooked motion and censure in the intensive

Monty · 11 November 2026

Three adjacent rules resolve different properties of the same yaṅ formation. The ordinary rule refers to repetition or intensity and a consonant-initial, one-vowel root. Seven named exceptions relax the stated formal shape. The motion rule concerns the path of motion. The censure rule concerns the event described by one of its eight named roots. This packet uses sourced meanings on the retained-yaṅ, A branch.
