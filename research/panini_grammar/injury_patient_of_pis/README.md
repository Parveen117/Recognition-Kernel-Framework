# PG967 · Who controls agreement in *te piṣyante*?

Mārkaṇḍeyapurāṇa 14.72 gives the unprefixed verb *piṣyante*. The *ye … te* construction resumes a group of men. They undergo the action, and the finite ending agrees with them in the third person plural. The preceding *prayacchanti* has its own actor and object. In the second clause *śilāpeṣaiḥ* supplies an instrumental plural, realized as *śilāpeṣair* before y. Vāmanapurāṇa 12.28 also has *te piṣyante*, with singular locative *śilāpeṣe*. The singular oblique cannot control the plural ending. Both witnesses support the same finite position. Each supplies a different inflected position of the stone-grinding noun. Their common wording leaves literary dependence open.
