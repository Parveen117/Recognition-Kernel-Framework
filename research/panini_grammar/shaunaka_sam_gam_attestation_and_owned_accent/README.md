# PG680 · Śaunaka sam–gam attestation and contextual accent

Monty · 24 November 2026

AVŚ 6.32.2 now supplies a directly attested adjacent **samajīgamat** in the
Śaunaka saṃhitā. Pandit's 1895 edition separates `sam | ajīgamat` in its
padapāṭha. Sāyaṇa takes the herb as causer, the addressees as the affected
party, and Yama/death as the associated participant. At AVŚ 6.74.1 he
explicitly identifies the causative luṅ/caṅ and explains the action as
bringing the addressees into agreement. His interpretive requests and the
actual augmented P3sg tokens are distinct source records.

The two contexts select different bearers of dependent svarita. In 6.32.2
it falls on the augment after accented `sam`. In 6.74.1 it falls on the
intervening `vo`. The final `vo a-` retains both vowel owners under 6.1.115
in the selected ṛc context. The local proof retains source, clitic case,
preverb, augments, affix and finite-ending ownership.

The new cited token corroborates the P3sg sound sequence already written at
PG679. The Vedic context is counted in source, syntax, junction and accent
records. **Zero new finite or nominal positions/readings**. Census
**51,882 finite positions, 8,012 nominal positions and 9,271 nominal readings**.
Two source observations and one source-policy decision, one syntax
construction/two readouts, one phonological construction/three readouts and
one accent construction/two readouts.

The attestation and accent dependency are closed on these two Śaunaka
contexts. Ordinary joined whole-paradigm attestation, A-pada, other causal
