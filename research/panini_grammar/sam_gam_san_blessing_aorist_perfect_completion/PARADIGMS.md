## Liṭ

Prefix *jigamiṣā* with either **saṃ-** or **sañ-**.
Then add the ām nasal and one auxiliary from the table.
For kṛ append **ṃ** or **ñ** and the profile's kṛ entry.
For bhū append **ṃ** or **m** and bhū P. For as append
**m** and as P. Word division is still retained in the derivation.
