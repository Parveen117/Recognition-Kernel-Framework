# PG5 — Remaining grammar work

Author: Monty. 1 November 2026.

PG5 completes the stated thematic laṭ paths, not all Sanskrit grammar. The
next morphological development is to finish the **non-thematic strong/weak
stems and endings** already selected by this stage.

| Priority | Required development | Present boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Śnu/u/śnā stems: strong/weak guṇa, vowel junctions, u-loss/options, root-specific changes and A endings | Selection exists for su, tan, kṛ, krī; their complete words are refused |
| 2 | Śnam as a mit infix, its weak-form lopa, root/affix ownership and subsequent phonology | Rudh's prescription and placement obligation exist; no complete class 7 paradigm |
| 3 | Adādi alternations and juhotyādi reduplication, with usable luk/ślu visibility | Deletion kinds and the local 1.1.63 guard exist; ad/hu remain incomplete |
| 4 | Execute the coupled substitutions of 3.1.74/80 and expand the optional lexical domains of 3.1.70/82 | Only selection and required-action obligations exist for these entries |
| 5 | Prefix-sensitive sense/pada licensing and full prefixed derivations | The yas selection contrast exists; prefixes still prevent a complete-word result |
| 6 | Additional lakāras, sanādi/causative semantics and optional curādi ṇic | Only unaccented classical present kartari laṭ; only one cur entry receives the complete ṇic path |

The broader PG4 obligations remain: full nominal classes, kṛt/taddhita,
compounds, kāraka, accents/Vedic usage, general 1.1.56–59/whole-lopa visibility,
the complete priority calculus, semantic review of all inherited scopes,
gaṇapāṭha/Uṇādi integration and an independently reviewed derivation corpus.

The [eight root-headword disagreements and two unresolved teaching forms](../scoped_derivations/REMAINING.md)
also remain. PG5 does not rerun or inflate the 2,219/2,229 root diagnostic into
a grammar accuracy score. No percentage of the approximately four-thousand-
record sūtrapāṭha is inferred from 39 records, 162 cells or 198 paths.

Additional source review: bhvādi versus divādi bhram and the śamādi lengthening
domain; all other members named in 3.1.70/82; contextual pada facts and
curādi directives; an edition-independent comparison of the lexical metadata.

The operator-family lift and its zero-specialization compatibility remain
required before any claimed native operator or quantum-calculator application.
This linguistic stage establishes no physical/theological identification.
CI and main merging remain deferred.
