# PG941 · When the offering controls agreement with √hu

For the simple, unprefixed juhotyādi √hu (1030001), Mādhavīya 732 reports two analyses of ritual offering. On the account attributed to *eke*, the offering already belongs to the lexical meaning, as breath belongs to the meaning of living. These authorities treat the verb as intransitive. Mādhava's own account takes casting as the verbal meaning. The ritual act requires a prescribed receptacle and sacrificial material, but the material can supply an independent object. His example is *yavāgūṃ juhoti*, “one offers rice gruel.” His report of the Bhāṣya belongs to his argument. This stage does not independently establish the wording of a Bhāṣya edition. [Mādhavīya 732](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/scl/dhaatupaatha/files/mA732.html)
