## Domain and two different association contracts

Begin on PG14's full native cut-field/history carrier, retaining derived
iota/dagger, typed grammar states, pairing, source evidence and complementary
cuts. The input is a supplied lexical/semantic association graph plus the
case/position/discourse evidence of PG362. It is not an unparsed word string.
An association edge means the addressed source's yoga relation; not every
kāraka edge, nearby token or arbitrary graph path is such an edge.

For pronoun node p define D_C(p) to ask whether a directly associated item
belongs to C={ca,vā,ha,aha,eva}. The answer has true/false/unknown values,
with evidence. Define I_C(p) for an expressly supplied source-qualified
mediated association, without replacing it by unrestricted reachability.
Kāśikā 8.1.24 makes its block predicate B24=D_C. The explicit source says
yuktagrahaṇa selects direct association and yoga through another item does
not establish this block. Thus mediated-only evidence cannot be promoted
to D_C just because a particle occurs somewhere nearby.

Separately let D_N(p) and I_N(p) ask for direct/source-licensed mediated
association with a paśyārtha predicate in **nonocular cognition** sense.
On the addressed domain 8.1.25 gives B25=D_N∨I_N. The source explicitly
extends its prohibition to yukta-yukta and excludes the ocular case through
anālocane. The sense label is required input: a printed paśyati or a generic
seeing label alone does not establish nonocular cognition. Unreviewed
longer paths remain Open; no universal transitive-closure grammar is proposed.

Known true B24/B25 excludes the **local clitic replacement edge**, not every
analysis or full-pronoun utterance. Known false merely removes that blocker;
it does not establish case, preceding-word, pāda, discourse or other guards.
Unknown association/sense stays Open. The final replacement relation is the
intersection of all independently satisfied positive guards and absence of
known blocking conditions; a missing required guard is never automatic truth.

poem examples or corpus credits. At 8.1.24 the full-pronoun ca example shows

**Local proposition.** Neither a blanket “any yoga blocks” replacement of
both contracts nor a blanket “only direct yoga blocks” replacement agrees
with the four source controls on this declared domain.

required block. Also, forgetting the ocular/nonocular sense removes the
explicit reason the last source control is not blocked. These are distinct
failed identifications; they do not assert global confluence or unrestricted
surface disambiguation. A source-qualified graph/sense query retains the
needed distinctions. □
