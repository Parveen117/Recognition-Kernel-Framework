## C4. What the T142 kernel establishes, and the adapter still needed

The capsule reports **225 distinct sūtra IDs as rows** (90 nominal,
151 verbal, sixteen shared), plus seven named guard clauses. These are
different counts from 94 nominal and 157 verbal implementation rows.
Its code/ledger and evidence consistency is useful; it is not a proof
that each guard covers its complete source domain. The capsule explicitly
leaves that stronger claim open.

Its prose identifies a surface-form projection as zero-specialization
and calls root/ending attachment a pairing. PG23 N uses a different,
typed construction: coefficient specialization retains history, the
native pairing takes values in B, and spelling is a further readout.
No theorem in the inspected K1–K4 checks equates these maps or pairings.
A future integration must supply that adapter and its compatibility
proof rather than treating the names as an already established identity.
This does not diminish the finite coherence result or create another
canonical operator engine.
