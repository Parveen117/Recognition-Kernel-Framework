## P4. Exact count

**Theorem P4.** The new domain has **270 positions and 286 readings**.

| Profile | Positions | Ordinary readings | Added tāt readings | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| **Total** | **270** | **270** | **16** | **286** |

**Proof.** Ten profiles have nine slots each. M/E supply one ordinary
reading per slot and exclude other sound branches on D. Eight P profiles
each have two eligible B singulars; P3's tāt word differs from its
ordinary word in each position, so 8·2=16 readings are added. Thus
3·90=270 positions and 90+90+106=286 readings. N3 recovers the same
source/choice-labelled relation at zero. □

Ordinary B spellings overlap I, while their meaning records differ.
Positions factor out repeated contextual witnesses with the same declared
morphological profile. This is not a distinct-string, corpus-attestation,
runtime-test or whole-grammar completion count.
