# PG23 — Native effective-marker and source-view construction

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## N1. Primitive carrier, grades and retained histories

Use the canonical cut scalar field C_Σ, its derived iota and dagger.
Let E_≤23 be the retained event alphabet with the new owned lexical,
marker, augment and source-query events. Construct first

\[
 B_{23}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le23}\rangle,\qquad
 M_{23,\lambda}=\bigoplus_{h\in H_{\mathrm{old}}\sqcup H_{23}}e_hB_{23}.
\]

Only finite words and finite coefficient supports are admitted. Dagger
reverses each word and stars its letters. Histories retain the interpreted
root/sense, modal frame, actor/pada evidence, controller, original/current
constituents and owners, historical markers, effective prescriptions,
named-abhyasta status, addressed source views, lopa Smriti, choices,
ordered events and unresolved premises. Actual spelling is one readout.

The grades are completed semantic input, role/pada admission, class and
ending selection, marker resolution, owned augment/exception query,
sound query and Accepted/Excluded/Open terminal. Refine a grade into the
finite source-ordered events needed by M/E. An operator composes only
across matching domains; a finished word cannot be fed back to an earlier
query without an explicit adapter. Order is not physical elapsed time.

Old coefficient words embed in B_23. Old operators act on the old
history summand and extend by zero on the new one, with local identity
P_old. New histories extend the shared canonical operator construction;
they do not define a second engine. No scalar λ, metric, positive norm
or physical clock is identified with this primitive carrier.

## N2. Pairing, complementary cuts and source-owned events

Use the retained native pairing

\[
 \langle x,y\rangle_B=\sum_h x_h^*y_h.
\]

units proves algebraic nondegeneracy. Every addressed predicate r has
orthogonal self-adjoint coordinate cuts P_r^T, P_r^F and P_r^?.

## N3. Typed zero-specialization and recovery

Let ε:B_23→C_Σ take the empty-word coefficient. It is a unital
dagger homomorphism: products of nonempty free words cannot be empty.
Set M_23,0=⊕_h e_h C_Σ on the same retained history labels and define

\[
 \pi_{0,M}\Bigl(\sum_h e_h a_h\Bigr)=\sum_h e_h\epsilon(a_h),
 \qquad \Pi_{0,\mathrm{op}}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

**Theorem N3.** On each admitted composable domain,

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,\mathrm{op}}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
 \epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B=
 \langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

Finite sums, products, adjoints and all three complementary cuts
specialize compatibly. Starting from each completed input with coefficient
1, the forward construction recovers exactly P's finite source/choice
relation, retaining the other two terminal channels.

**Proof.** The identities follow entrywise from ε's additive,
multiplicative and dagger properties. Every event factor has
ε(1+z_e)=1. Query data reside in h, which π_0,M retains, so each
guard has the same truth channel before and after specialization.
Induction on the finite graded paths gives both directions of accepted
path recovery. Different choices have different terminal histories,
each with constant coefficient 1 under this unit-start construction. □

This last support assertion is restricted to forward unit-start paths;
arbitrary signed combinations may cancel. Recovery does not imply
universal Sanskrit confluence, automatic commentary adjudication or
equivalence to Claude's runtime.

## N4. A current-a observation loses the hi-luk cut

Take h_j at ja∣hi after E1's han→ja, and h_b at thematic
bhava∣hi in PG19 before its 6.4.105 query. Both have actual hi,
the same local current a∣hi window and apit; their complete stems
and source histories differ. Define a deliberately coarse observation
q(e_hj)=q(e_hb)=e_w that retains just that local data.

The correct hi-luk cut on the ordered pair is L=diag(0,1).
For h_j the original han window remains operative under the addressed
6.4.22 account; for h_b the thematic a licenses deletion.

**Proposition N4.** No cut on this observation intertwines L with q.
Retaining the original queried window and its source permission repairs
this pair; zero-specialization itself does not lose them.

**Proof.** v=e_hj−e_hb lies in ker(q), while qLv=−e_w≠0.
An induced operator on the quotient would require L to preserve
ker(q). The refined observations (a∣hi,han-source) and
(a∣hi,thematic-a), with their query permissions, separate the false
and true values. N3 retains that distinction at zero. □

The final words are jahi and bhava. This proposition neither identifies
their complete strings nor says every surface-informed observer fails.

## N5. Historical pit alone loses the īṭ cut

Consider brū P ordinary loṭ 3sg and brū P vidhiliṅ 3sg after
ending/augment selection but before 7.3.93. Their original tip supplies
historical p; their actual next sound is hal (t and y respectively).
M1 resolves effective pit differently.

| History | Historical p | Effective pit | Actual hal first | īṭ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I 3sg | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| O 3sg | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| I 1sg after āṭ | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

The third row records a pit prescription in history rather than claiming
that original mip is the same ending as original tip. It shows that
the hal test is independently necessary.

On the first two coordinates, let q_p keep exact brū, sārvadhātuka,
historical p and the Boolean hal result but forget explicit ṅit and its
effective override. It identifies them. The īṭ cut is J=diag(1,0).

**Proposition N5.** J does not descend through q_p. Adding the
source-resolved effective-pit value repairs this pair.

**Proof.** The difference of the two coordinate units lies in ker(q_p),
but q_pJ sends it to their nonzero common image. The repaired observations
have effective-pit values 1 and 0, so J becomes their respective
coordinate cut. All history and cut data survive N3. □

This is a failure of the specified coarse observation, not a theorem
that every program containing a pit field is wrong. M1's source policy
must be integrated into every relevant consumer, including augment guards.

## N6. The visible letter r does not determine augment ownership

In h_I for śeratām, r is supplied by ruṭ on an actual jh→at
host. In h_O for śayīran, r belongs to the prescribed ran ending.
Define q_r to retain only the observed r, and let R select histories
in which that r was introduced by 7.1.6.

**Proposition N6.** R=diag(1,0) cannot descend to q_r on this pair.

**Proof.** q_r identifies the coordinate units, but q_rR does not
annihilate their difference. Retaining owner and introducing event
separates them and defines the cut. The zero map retains both labels. □

This is an ownership query, not a claim that śeratām and śayīran
have equal words. Their different host constructions also explain why
ruṭ cannot be transported merely because an earlier form contained r.

## N7. Exact boundary

M/E and N prove a finite source-relative grammar construction, its typed
zero-law and the three stated observation obstructions. They do not
prove a global marker calculus, universal asiddha scheduler, all lexical
paradigms or runtime/formal equivalence. Śī's weak guṇa is explicitly
a lexical exception, not a redefinition of all weak endings.

Independent Sanskrit adjudication, machine replay and proof-assistant
validation remain open. Existing canonical engine code, frozen theorem
packets and certificate pins are unchanged.
