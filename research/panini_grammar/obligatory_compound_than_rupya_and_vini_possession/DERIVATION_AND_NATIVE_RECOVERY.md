# PG802 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 5.2.118 makes `ṭhañ` obligatory on eligible short-a stems in eka-/go-initial compounds, blocking alternate matup. `aikagavika` uses the samāsānta base; `gauśakaṭika` uses the short-a synonym under Kāśikā's policy. The source's Patanjali disagreement and the `ekaviṃśati` boundary remain Open rather than being erased. Rule 119 restricts the hundred/thousand value forms to immediate `niṣka`; the longer gold-niṣka compound is an explicit control.

Rule 120 distinguishes stamped form from praised form: three objects and one person share `rūpya` but not the same relation. Ordinary `rūpavān` is the general-possession control; `himya` and `guṇya` are separately supplemented `yap` outputs. Rule 121 assigns `vini` to as-final stems and to `māyā/medhā/sraj`; `māyī/māyika` retain their separate vṛīhyādi owners from 5.2.116.
