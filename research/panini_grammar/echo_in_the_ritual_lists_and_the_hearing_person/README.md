# PG952 · The echo in two ritual lists and the hearing person

The Sanskrit word *pratiśrutkā* denotes the returned sound. Its dative answers which recipient is named in the list. Its locative supplies the relation from which Śaṅkara explains the adjective qualifying the hearing person. Neither use makes *prati* a freely replaceable preverb in this nominal paradigm. The input here is the attested feminine stem as a whole. Its inner root and suffix history remain open beyond that lexical input.

**Two ritual addresses, one dative.** Böhtlingk and Roth's entry 48623 identifies VS 24.32 and 30.19. At 24.32 *pratiśrutkāyai cakravākaḥ* assigns the cakravāka bird to Echo. At 30.19 the next item is accusative *artanam*. Mahīdhara explicitly separates the dative and glosses the accusative as a person in distress. The running text has *-kāyā* before the vowel in *artanam*. The isolated reading for the named recipient is *-kāyai* in both passages. In a PG14 path through the ordinary external-junction rules, 6.1.78 maps final ai to āy before a vowel, then the Śākalya edge at 8.3.19 can delete final y after ā. That accounts for the segmental difference under that junction policy. It does not identify the transmission of a particular reciter's avagraha or authorize a second dative ending. **Locative in the gloss.** At Bṛhadāraṇyaka 2.5.6 Śaṅkara glosses *prātiśrutkaḥ* with *pratiśrutkāyāṃ … bhavaḥ*, explaining the occasion of hearing the sound return. The locative singular before a consonant has anusvāra for isolated final m. The masculine derivative agrees with *puruṣaḥ*. Its repeated occurrence and PG951's already written companion adjective receive zero credit here. The locative gloss supports the 4.3.53 *tatra bhavaḥ* analysis on this commentary policy. On aṇ under 4.1.83, first-vowel vṛddhi falls on the a of **prati**; 6.4.148 removes base-final ā before the suffix. Hence *prātiśrutka-*. The new final a is affixal. This inference is specific to the companion adjective. It leaves PG951's allocation for *śrautra-* and its variant ancestry Open. **Ordinary unaccented feminine inflection.** The two directly witnessed requests anchor the panel. For an ā-stem treated as āp-final, 7.3.113 introduces yāṭ on the marked singular obliques. This distinguishes the dative ai, genitive/ablative āḥ and locative ām after y. The instrumental singular has *-ayā*. With consonant-initial dual bh the stem's long ā remains. In the genitive plural nuṭ gives dental n: the intervening dental t after r prevents retroflexion across the stem. The 24 case/number requests have one reading apiece under this policy.
