# PG687 · Corrected unprefixed and new joined readings

The table gives the corrected null-preverb words for the old
PG671 keys. For the new joined keys prepend **saṃ-** or
**saṅ-** to each displayed word. The dual prefix and the
A 2pl dental/retroflex paths are independent.

| Slot | P | A |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3sg | gamyāt | gamayiṣīṣṭa |
| 3du | gamyāstām | gamayiṣīyāstām |
| 3pl | gamyāsuḥ | gamayiṣīran |
| 2sg | gamyāḥ | gamayiṣīṣṭhāḥ |
| 2du | gamyāstam | gamayiṣīyāsthām |
| 2pl | gamyāsta | gamayiṣīdhvam / gamayiṣīḍhvam |
| 1sg | gamyāsam | gamayiṣīya |
| 1du | gamyāsva | gamayiṣīvahi |
| 1pl | gamyāsma | gamayiṣīmahi |

For example the joined P3sg is *saṃgamyāt/saṅgamyāt*.
Joined A3sg is *saṃgamayiṣīṣṭa/saṅgamayiṣīṣṭa*.
There are **18 new positions/38 readings**. The unprefixed
19 replacements carry no new position, reading or
construction credit.
