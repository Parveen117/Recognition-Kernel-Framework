# PG978 · Trust in Vidura’s counsel and reassurance in Laṅkā

## Two Mādhavīya prints

PG977 left a textual question about Hyderabad entry 719. The Benares impression, p. 264, gives **अश्वसत्** immediately before the iṣ-aorist dual, second singular and first singular. Dwarikadas Shastri’s print, pp. 375–376, has the same sequence. Both also give the two imperfect singulars earlier. The digital XML naming the 1934 edition agrees with the earlier print at the post-blessing locus. This establishes a printed antecedent for the digital reading. It supplies neither a manuscript ancestor nor grounds to assign that spelling to Rāma’s verse. [Benares, p. 264](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.369319/page/n282/mode/1up) · [Shastri, p. 375](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/disp/index.php?page=429) · [p. 376](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/disp/index.php?page=430) · [XML, entry 719](https://github.com/samsaadhanii/scl/blob/1ec51dc4cbfecf271e4f3cb0bba41f25b5921572/dhaatupaatha/files/madhaviya_XAwuvqwwi_new.xml) · [PG977](../sighing_in_two_past_categories/README.md)

One word earlier, immediately before the separate blessing heading, the Benares print and XML give **अश्वस्यात्** with initial short a. The later print gives **आश्वस्यात्**, with the long ā of the preverb, as another ordinary potential. This position in the text distinguishes it from the unprefixed blessing in the next sentence. The short initial in the earlier print has no past-augment entitlement from an ordinary vidhiliṅ request. It is retained as an unresolved reading under its own edition. The long reading has its own positive witness.

In the trust examples Mādhavīya retains śap because it treats the operation of the class as inconstant here. This gives the thematic potential in -et. The same discussion quotes the thematic plural for the night-roamers. Jayamaṅgalā reads the poem’s primary plural with the ordinary adādi disappearance of śap under 2.4.72. It connects the conditional clause addressed to Vibhīṣaṇa with the resulting protection of the people and the inhabitants’ renewed confidence under 3.3.156. The honorific bhavān selects a singular verb, while niśācarāḥ selects the plural. Conditional meaning is compatible with the written vidhiliṅ inflection.

Jayamaṅgalā explicitly calls the thematic-looking plural a variant and offers a second analysis of that spelling. It gives the comparison with āśvāsa, derives the inner noun with pacādi ac, and invokes denominal kvip for conduct like the comparison term. Mādhavīya’s account instead retains verbal śap at the class boundary. The two analyses have different inner hosts. The cited gloss leaves the detailed vowel history from the nominal base to the variant surface unresolved here. The register credits the plural spelling once per distinct reading. It does not transfer the denominal explanation to Vidura’s trust verbs. [Prior nominal-agent and conduct controls](../agent_noun_and_outer_middle/SOURCES.json)
