# PG282 · Han substitutions retain lexical membership, not every sound property

Monty · 13 November 2026

This packet supplies an explicit **selected inheritance reading** for the earlier *ni + ja/vadha* question. The Nyāsa on 1.1.56 distinguishes an operation depending merely on a sound from one depending on a community (*samudāya*). In 8.4.17 the qualifying item is the named *han* lexical community; the target is the already present n of the **prefix ni**. The replacement is therefore allowed to retain that lexical membership under 1.1.56, while its actual sound inventory is recomputed. The two-prefix words below are written applications of that policy, **not newly located textual attestations**.

The positive membership argument does **not** restore every old property. Kāśikā 1.1.56 itself illustrates han-conditioned pada in *āhata/āvadhiṣṭa*. But 7.2.10 uses *avadhīt* as its **ekāc exclusion**: vadha is taught with final a, so the substituted root does not inherit han's original one-vowel aniṭ outcome. Likewise a missing root n is not recreated for 8.4.22/23, and the old h is not restored inside ja. The retained lexical source and the new phonemes have different jobs.

For *jahi*, 6.4.36 supplies ja before hi, while the same-host view of 6.4.22 prevents the later hi-luk rule from using that new a. The Kāśikā directly lists *jahi* in explaining this limit. The ordinary optative still has *hanyāt*, as reviewed in PG279. The A-pada option of 2.4.44 is outside these selected P requests.

The *-tāt* alternatives in third and second singular have four explicit āśis guards under 7.1.35. They remain unavailable as free alternatives for an ordinary command without that premise. No Vedic yaṅ-luk profile is added; the tiP wording of 8.4.17 retains its inherited exclusion.
