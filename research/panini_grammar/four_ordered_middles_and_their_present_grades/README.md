# PG271 · Four middle presents, the smearing consonants and optional u

Monty · 12 November 2026

All four lexical entries permit both padas. For the ordinary kartari middle, 1.3.72 places the result of the action with the agent. Kṣīrasvāmin quotes *praṇivahate* and prints *praṇivapate* in brackets. Maitreya gives *cinute* beside *cinoti*. For *dih*, the second-class bare archive and the consonant rules distinguish *digdhe* from active *degdhi*.

| Root | Ordered present, 3sg | Ordered imperfect, 3sg | Ordinary optative, 3sg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *vap* | praṇivapate | praṇyavapata | praṇivapeta |
| *vah* | praṇivahate | praṇyavahata | praṇivaheta |
| *ci* | praṇicinute | praṇyacinuta | praṇicinvīta |
| *dih* | praṇidigdhe | praṇyadigdha | praṇidihīta |

In the present and imperfect of *ci*, loss of *u* before *v* or *m* again gives two first duals and plurals: *praṇicinvahe / praṇicinuvahe* and *praṇicinmahe / praṇicinumahe*. The imperative and ordinary optative have their own bases (*cinavai*, *cinvīya* in first singular). In *dih*, compare middle second singular *dhikṣe* with active *dhekṣi*. The table uses the lexical *ni* rule across both *pra* and *pari*. The direct single-prefix condition on *vah* stays attached to its own environment.
