## H02. Whole ya and final a are different targets

The a-final denominative under 3.1.32 takes external ārdhadhātuka
material. Rule 6.4.48 deletes the real final a of derivational ya.
Kāśikā 6.4.49 discusses the meaningful ya unit and its preceding
hal. The related optional operation of 6.4.50 also needs the actual
consonant before ky. PG122's y is preceded in every case by **ā or
ī**. No optional y deletion arises from either rule. Kaumudī and
Bālamanoramā 6.4.50 additionally restrict the consonant-host ky
possibilities in their analysis. Retain both the preceding sound
and affix class; a string search for the letters ya is insufficient.
The source examples samidhyitā/samidhitā illustrate the contrasting
consonant-host construction and carry no position credit here.

## H03. Outer iṭ and the consonant that precedes it

The derived, multi-vowel dhātu takes required short iṭ before
val-initial sya, tāsi, A blessing sīyuṭ and sic. The P blessing
yāsuṭ and the perfect's vowel-initial ām have no such outer iṭ.
There is no inner lexical-san iṭ condition on these denominatives.
After 6.4.48 the y before outer iṭ is an iṇ consonant. For the
specified A 2pl blessing and luṅ words 8.3.79 therefore admits a
dental or a retroflex dh/ḍh on the inherited origin-sensitive
reading. The two are readings of one request. Lṛṭ, luṭ and lṛṅ
do not have a matching dh/ḍh choice in this packet.
