# PG32 — Complete authored word register

Author: Monty. 3 November 2026.

Every row is a qualified D input, with nine slots in the displayed order.
L− requires the addressed māṅ context; J− additionally requires sma.
The table displays the finite word before external sentence sandhi, not
a claim that the bare unaugmented word has ordinary past admission.
The slash in P06/P16 is 8.3.79's single-slot grammatical option under S.
All other entries have one reading in the adopted source policy.
