A key distinction is that lopped sic can still be relevant to 109/110's
historical-affix query while failing the adopted extant-sic īṭ guard.
Another is that māṅ suppresses the initial augment, while the separately
licensed sic-iṭ, ending-īṭ and bhū-vuk survive. Vowel-initial forms
therefore require a source-aware construction, not final-word surgery.

The selected root-qualified dh/ḍh policy is explicit. Rival commentary
readings are retained separately, not combined into unqualified options.
The present **731/3,983 bounded-rule IDs** (about 18.35%) combine written
construction and companion operational labels; this is not a percentage
of whole-grammar completion. Runtime integration, other luṅ families,
passive/bhāva continuations and broader grammar remain open.
