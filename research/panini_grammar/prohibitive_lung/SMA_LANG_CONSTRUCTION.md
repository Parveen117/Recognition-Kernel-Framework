# PG32 — Māṅ with sma: complete additional laṅ profiles

Author: Monty. 3 November 2026.

## J1. Shared context, different stem construction

Under PG30's 3.3.176 reading, associated sma adds laṅ while preserving
luṅ. It does not turn either request into an assertion of past action.
Both lack initial aṭ/āṭ by 6.4.74. Only laṅ uses the present-system
vikaraṇa here. No sic, root-facing sic-iṭ, sic-specific īṭ or bhū-vuk
is imported from L.

Bhū, edh, vand, mud, īkṣ, īh and hṛ select śap under 3.1.68.
Bhū becomes bhav by guṇa/ayādi; mud becomes mod by upadhā-guṇa;
hṛ becomes har with 1.1.51's r accompaniment. The remaining four
roots keep their admitted lexical shapes. Vand retains its num history;
late anusvāra/parasavarṇa restores the dental nasal before d. The
finite register selects the inherited non-doubling reading.

For an a-stem base B∈{edh,vand,mod,īkṣ,īh,har}, A endings give

\[
J_A(B)=(Bata,Betām,Banta,Bathāḥ,Bethām,Badhvam,Be,Bāvahi,Bāmahi).
\]

The duals use 7.2.81 ā→iy, 6.1.66 y-lopa and 6.1.87 a+i→e.
Jha has ant under 7.1.3; its a joins the stem's a by 6.1.97.
First singular ending i independently gives e by 6.1.87. Rule 7.3.101
lengthens a before vahi/mahi. None of these e vowels is licensed by
the ṭit rule 3.4.79. The supplied short-a stem, not the root spelling
alone, decides the dual and jha substitutions.

For bhū P the inherited P secondary endings instead give

\[
(bhavat,bhavatām,bhavan,bhavaḥ,bhavatam,bhavata,
 bhavam,bhavāva,bhavāma).
\]

Retain final m by 1.3.4, final n by 8.4.37, and the inherited pause
readout. The bhavāva ending-v and luṅ bhūvam's vuk have different owners.

## J2. Kṛ A: weak u, not the sic root kṛ

Reuse the exact PG6/PG19 kṛ stem contract with u under 3.1.79. Every
admitted A ending is apit sārvadhātuka and receives 1.2.4's ṅit-like
condition. Rule 6.4.110 supplies kur|u; 1.1.5 prevents incompatible
strengthening. No P-only strong ending is imported into this profile.

| Ending group | Owned operation | Readout |
|---|---|---|
|ta, thās, dhvam|u retained|kuruta, kuruthāḥ, kurudhvam|
|ātām, āthām|u→v before ā, 6.1.77|kurvātām, kurvāthām|
|jha|7.1.5 at, then u→v before a|kurvata|
|iṭ ending|marker removal, u→v before i|kurvi|
|vahi, mahi|6.4.108 deletes the affix-owned u|kurvahi, kurmahi|

The weak-stem derivation and 1.1.58/8.2.79 query contract are inherited
from PG6; deleting u does not create an illicit kūr. The iṭ **ending**
is not the val-initial ārdhadhātuka augment from L. Rule 6.4.109's
y environment is absent, so there is no blanket deletion of u.

The nine-slot result is

\[
(kuruta,kurvātām,kurvata,kuruthāḥ,kurvāthām,kurudhvam,
 kurvi,kurvahi,kurmahi).
\]

Kṛ/hṛ P laṅ remains PG30's completed work. No new positions are assigned
to those two already-authored profiles.

## J3. Exhaustion and restricted transport

L and J partition the new morphology by actual lakāra, pada, inner
affix and source-policy branch. Seven thematic J− profiles and one
owned-u J− profile exhaust D's eight new J− inputs. Their nine ending
cases are explicit above. Together with L's ten L+ and eight new L−
profiles this exhausts P's 234 new positions /236 adopted readings.

Only the context/actor/pada and targeted initial-augment suppression
contract transports across L− and J−. Root strength, sic identity,
iṭ/īṭ/vuk, jha substitution, dh/ḍh and ā→iy must be reconstructed
from their actual local inputs. Equal person/number is insufficient
to transport those operations.
