## Why goḍhā and gūhitā belong to one future request

The other changed file is the Siddhāntakaumudī aggregate at key 64089. Its only changed entry replaces *gauḍhā* with *goḍhā*. The preceding *gūhitā* remains. The Sanskrit Wikisource transcription of the Bālamanoramā edition, scan page 149, independently gives the *gūhitā–goḍhā* pair. Dhātupāṭha 01.1043 has *guhū̃*, concealing, bhvādi, both padas, optional iṭ. Use the unprefixed agent construction, at a non-today future time (3.3.15). In the middle branch the fruit of the action accrues to the agent under 1.3.72. The active branch takes the residual actor entitlement. [Edition transcription, permanent revision](https://sa.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=321158)

* **With iṭ.** The ū marker licenses the option under 7.2.44. Before iṭ + tās the vowel-initial environment and the otherwise guṇa-licensing suffix admit 6.4.89. The root has ū and retains h. Thus *gūh-i-tā*. The long ū in the root and the short i of the augment occupy different sites.
* **Without iṭ.** The consonant-initial tās does not trigger the special ū substitution. The light penultimate vowel takes guṇa under 7.3.86, giving *goh*. Before t, h gives ḍh; the dental becomes dh, then retroflex ḍh, and a single ḍh survives the cluster under 8.3.13 (8.2.31, 8.2.40, 8.4.41). The preceding o is already long. Thus *goḍh-tā* is not an additional stem for suffixation; the contracted result in the singular is *goḍhā*. The historical *gauḍhā* is a source spelling, with no vṛddhi licence here.
* **Competing explanations.** Mādhavīya entry 566 in the pinned XML explicitly discusses guṇa before the later ḍh operation while displaying *gāḍhā*. Preserve its displayed vowel as transmitted. A modern explanation of 8.3.13 works from short u and ends its luṭ illustration at *gūḍhā*. That is a distinct witness and conflicts with the corrected Kaumudī. Neither additional editorial spelling enters the selected two-reading future panel. Both are reserved for their own source histories. [Modern 8.3.13 explanation](https://ashtadhyayi-lite.github.io/sutra/8.3.13.html)
