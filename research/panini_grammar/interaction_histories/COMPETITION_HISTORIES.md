# PG47 — Qualified competition and fresh continuation histories

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

These seven histories instantiate the packet's common addressed-state
interface. They are written derivations and decision controls, not executions.
Their evidence and policy choices are explained in [C source notes](SOURCE_NOTES_C.md).
No new catalogue IDs, agreement positions or counted paradigm readings arise.

## Local competition record

For a supplied history (h), retain a record

\[
K=(h,q,p,r,s,A_r,A_s,W_r,W_s,V,\rho,\kappa),
\]

where (q) is the consumer query, (p) its source policy, (r,s) are the
two candidate rules, (A) records their admission evidence and read addresses,
(W) their proposed writes, (V) their permitted views, (\rho) the
source-supported strength relation, and (\kappa) the continuation policy.
The record identifies one current opportunity, not every future use of either
rule. A conflict can involve writes to the same sound, or a write that removes
the other operation's trigger at a different address.

An invocation of **1.4.2** requires affirmative evidence for both candidates,
simultaneous incompatibility, mutual visibility and equal strength in the
source's domain. The independent domains illustrated in C01 are retained as
evidence of this particular comparison. A named exception, नित्य relation
or admitted visibility restriction is resolved by its own source clause;
it is not converted into an invented numeric score. Unknown decisive evidence
returns **OPEN**. Proven failure of a required condition returns **BLOCKED**
for that invocation, without prohibiting an independently admitted operation.

After any accepted write, discard the old candidate's current applicability
claim and build a fresh record. Keep the original record as history. Retained
provenance is available only to consumers authorized to use it; it is not
permission to reuse a stale predicate. The descriptions below supply these
records explicitly instead of asking a global largest-ID scheduler to decide.

## C01 — वृक्षेभ्यः: a simultaneous equal-strength collision

**Sources:** 1.4.2; 7.3.101–103; 1.1.52; 1.4.14; 8.2.66; 8.3.15.

**Given frame.** An admitted nominal history has stem token (a=) वृक्ष
and following सुप् token (b=) भ्यस्, with a plural dative or ablative
request already supplied. The stem's current final sound (a.f) is अ.
The भ् at (b)'s beginning belongs to both यञ् and झल्. The selected
reading ends at an utterance boundary; accent is outside this history.

1. At (h_0), 7.3.102 proposes (a.f:) अ→आ, inheriting the relevant
   lengthening instruction from 101. Its सुप्/यञ् and अ-final predicates
   are T. It writes no sound of (b).
2. At that same (h_0), 7.3.103 proposes (a.f:) अ→ए. Its plural,
   सुप्, झल्-initial and अ-final predicates are T. These are incompatible
   replacements of one addressed vowel.
3. Kāśikā 1.4.2 supplies this exact collision. It also supplies separate
   domains: वृक्षाभ्याम् for 102 and वृक्षेषु for 103. Its equal-strength
   judgment admits the tie-break; this judgment is evidence, not an inference
   from close rule numbers. Select the later **7.3.103**.
4. Commit only अ→ए at (a.f). The new state is वृक्षे + भ्यस्. Record
   the displaced 102 proposal and the adopted continuation policy; do not
   append an applied lengthening event. C02 examines any later 102 request.
5. On the supplied सुप्-ending पद view, 8.2.66 changes the final स् of
   (b) to the operative र् of रु, retaining its marker record. At the
   supplied pause, 8.3.15 gives ः. The unaccented readout is **वृक्षेभ्यः**.

**Result:** **APPLIED**, scoped to the stated collision and its unaccented
pause ending. The rejected simultaneous आ replacement is **BLOCKED**.
The initial case/number licensing, lexical admission, accent and nonpause
external sandhi remain supplied or downstream obligations. This single
history is not a newly counted full paradigm cell.

## C02 — Reapplication policy does not reactivate a stale proposal

**Sources:** Nyāsa 1.4.2; Kāśikā 1.1.56 and 7.3.102–103.

**Given frame.** Begin with C01 immediately after अ→ए, before final-स्
processing. Preserve both the original अ and the current ए at (a.f), the
successful 103 event, and the displaced 102 record. The consumer asks whether
that earlier lengthening now applies.

|Declared policy|Fresh continuation decision|
|---|---|
|Nyāsa's जाति / पुनःप्रसङ्ग account|An earlier rule may act if it is newly applicable after the later one; admission must be reconsidered.|
|Nyāsa's द्रव्य account|The earlier operation defeated in this conflict remains defeated; the retained defeat record is decisive here.|

On the first policy, inspect the **current** stem-final sound for 102's
अ-specific lengthening. It is ए, so this local admission is F. Keeping the
old अ in history does not make this sound-conditioned consumer eligible;
1.1.56's transfer excludes the pertinent original-sound-dependent operation.
No source-specific भूतपूर्व permission for this lengthening has been supplied.
Thus the earlier proposal is **BLOCKED**, with no second vowel mutation.

On the second policy the defeat record already blocks that proposal. The
current spelling remains वृक्षे under both policies, but the decision reasons
remain different. These are two declared interpretations of continuation,
not two optional Sanskrit forms. Nyāsa states the general policy distinction;
this witness does **not** establish a concrete case in which its two policies
produce different surface outputs.

**Result:** **BLOCKED** for the requested 102 continuation. The preservation
of each policy and the fresh-check obligation are closed for this history.
Finding and fully deriving a source-attested divergence case remains open.

## C03 — वृक्षाभ्याम्: only one candidate is admitted

**Sources:** Kāśikā 1.4.2 and 7.3.102–103.

**Given frame.** Stem token (a=) वृक्ष is followed by the admitted dual
सुप् token (b=) भ्याम्. Its यञ्-initial and झल्-initial predicates are T,
but **plural is F**. This is the separate-domain witness in 1.4.2.

1. Admit 102's lengthening at (a.f): अ-final stem and यञ्-initial सुप्
   are present.
2. Evaluate 103 from its own conditions. The dual affix fails the plural
   gate. Its later rule number cannot supply the missing condition.
3. The 1.4.2 request is **BLOCKED** because there are not two admitted
   simultaneous candidates. This failure does not stop the admitted 102 map.
4. Apply अ→आ at the stem-final address, preserving (b). The supplied
   unaccented suffix spelling gives the source witness **वृक्षाभ्याम्**.

**Result:** **APPLIED** for lengthening; **BLOCKED** for the proposed
tie-break. No ए branch is licensed. Initial semantic/case selection and
accent remain outside this bounded history.

## C04 — ईश्वरः: an earlier specific prohibition defeats a later default

**Sources:** Nyāsa 1.4.2 and 7.2.8; Kāśikā 3.2.175, 7.2.8 and 7.2.35.

**Given frame.** The supplied root is ईश् in the stated तच्छीलादि agent
meaning, with its independently admitted **वरच्** affix token (b) from
3.2.175. The affix is कृत् and आर्धधातुक; its initial व् belongs to वल्
and to the more restricted वश् class. Exact affix identity and lexical
admission are present, not guessed from a finished ईश्वर spelling.

1. The general 7.2.35 इट् request passes its आर्धधातुक/वल् predicates.
   Its proposed augmentation belongs to affix (b), not to the root.
2. Independently admit **7.2.8**: this is a वश्-initial कृत्. Its action
   prohibits that इट् augmentation. Nyāsa 1.4.2 explicitly uses this
   general/specific pair to exclude a later-number tie-break.
3. Store the directed exception relation (7.2.8 \succ 7.2.35) for this
   request. Equal strength is F. A request to invoke 1.4.2 is **BLOCKED**.
4. Keep (b) without an इट् token. Append the scoped prohibition decision;
   do not insert an augment and then erase it. The resulting affix history
   supports the source witness **ईश्वरः**.

**Result:** **BLOCKED** for इट् and for the proposed later-rule override.
The prohibition decision is resolved; marker removal and nominal realization
of the cited full word remain downstream from this affix-selection history.
Kāśikā contrasts ईशिता/ईशितुम् with ईश्वरः, so the result is not a ban on
every इट् after this root.

## C05 — वृक्षैः: नित्य precedence uses an expressly authorized history

**Sources:** Kāśikā/Nyāsa 1.4.2; Kāśikā/Kaumudī 7.1.9;
Kāśikā 7.3.103, 1.1.55, 6.1.88, 8.2.66 and 8.3.15.

**Given frame.** (a=) वृक्ष, (b=) instrumental-plural भिस्, with the
current अ-final stem and the affix's सुप्/झल् features admitted. An utterance
boundary is supplied for the eventual unaccented readout.

1. 7.1.9 proposes **whole-affix** (b:) भिस्→ऐस्. Kaumudī explicitly
   connects this whole replacement with अनेकाल् under 1.1.55.
2. 7.3.103 proposes stem-final अ→ए. Its write address differs from (b),
   but the candidates are not independent: replacing (b) by vowel-initial
   ऐस् removes the झल् trigger read by 103.
3. The source explains that 7.1.9 is available both before and after the
   proposed ए operation, using **भूतपूर्व** अ-final provenance. Conversely,
   103 loses its trigger after ऐस्. This is the supplied नित्य/अनित्य
   relation, not an executed trial of two alternative grammar runs.
4. Select **7.1.9**, despite its earlier number. Commit भिस्→ऐस् only;
   retain the entire original भिस् token and its instrument/plural history.
   Re-evaluate 103: its झल्-initial condition is now F. It is **BLOCKED**.
5. The vowel junction at the two owned tokens follows 6.1.88: अ + ऐ gives
   the single ऐ. Retain both source addresses in that coalescence, obtaining
   वृक्षैस्. The पद-final रु operation and pause visarga under 8.2.66/8.3.15
   yield **वृक्षैः**.

**Result:** **APPLIED** for the whole-affix replacement and stated unaccented
completion. The later ए operation and its 1.4.2 override request are
**BLOCKED**. This expressly authorized historical predicate does not turn
every current-sound query into a query about any sound once present. Semantic
case licensing, accent and other external environments remain outside scope.

## C06 — विस्फोर्यम्: a visibility gate precedes the numerical tie-break

**Sources:** Kāśikā/Nyāsa 8.2.1; Kāśikā 3.1.124, 7.3.86 and 8.2.77.

**Given frame.** The supplied history contains prefix वि, root स्फुर् and
the independently admitted ण्यत् token of 3.1.124, with its operative य
and आर्धधातुक evidence retained. The addressed उ is the root's उपधा.
The consumer is the sapādasaptādhyāyī guṇa instruction **7.3.86**.

1. In its admitted view, 7.3.86 proposes उपधा उ→ओ, using the stated
   लघूपध relation. A proposed later 8.2.77 lengthening would instead
   produce ऊ from that उ before the consonantal affix material.
2. Resolve the visibility relation **before** a 1.4.2 comparison. Under
   the adopted 8.2.1 account, the later Tripādī instruction is असिद्ध
   relative to this earlier consumer. It cannot be installed as an equally
   available competitor merely because its number is larger.
3. The 1.4.2 request is **BLOCKED**. Commit the permitted guṇa to the
   root-owned vowel: वि + स्फोर् + य. Retain the 8.2.77 proposal and the
   exact consumer-relative reason it was ineligible to defeat guṇa.
4. A later current-value query does not replay the obsolete उ→ऊ proposal:
   its addressed vowel is now ओ, not the required इक् उपधा. Kāśikā's
   word witness is **विस्फोर्यम्**; अवगोर्यम् is its companion witness.

**Result:** **APPLIED** for the guṇa decision; **BLOCKED** for the attempted
invisible-rule override. The cited nominal ending and full accent realization
remain downstream. This history does not assert a global ban on Tripādī
effects or on later exceptions: 8.2.1 itself separately preserves the stated
exception-to-general-rule treatment illustrated by दोग्धा/दोग्धुम्.

## C07 — Withheld strength evidence leaves the choice Open

**Sources:** the admission conditions of Kāśikā/Nyāsa 1.4.2, instantiated
as an interface control derived from C01.

**Given frame.** Supply C01's actual stem, affix, addresses and two candidate
writes, but omit the source-policy witness establishing their relative
strength. Token predicates and the incompatible writes are known; the
strength coordinate is **?**, not “equal.” This artificial evidence omission
is explicitly a control, not a new example attributed to the commentary.

1. Retain both candidate records without applying either vowel replacement.
2. The conjunction required by the 1.4.2 invocation is not resolved T.
   A larger ID cannot convert the unknown strength relation into equality.
3. Return **OPEN**, with the named obligation: restore the source-supported
   comparison for 7.3.102/103 in this exact nominal domain, or supply another
   independently justified precedence relation. Neither an आ word nor an ए
   word is certified by this incomplete decision record.
4. Restoring C01's evidence permits its decision; this is refinement of the
   supplied history, not proof that a future parser can infer that evidence.

**Result:** **OPEN** for selection. No token mutation is committed. Unknown
strength, source policy, or current visibility must be resolved before the
corresponding decisive choice. These histories establish neither global
termination/confluence nor universal semantic truth or hallucination freedom.
