# PG47 — Native lift of revision-bound grammar histories

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Read [PG46](../independent_written_closure/NATIVE_CONTINUATION.md) and the
complete [PG37 N1–N5](../possession_adverbials/NATIVE_CONTINUATION.md).
The primitive remains the full operator/history carrier with cuts, cut field

it adds no positivity, physical-clock or universal-normalization assumption.

## N1. Conservative history extension

For the admitted finite events of [INTERFACE.md](INTERFACE.md), use

\[
B_{47}=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le47}\rangle,
\qquad
M_{47,\lambda}=(M_{46,\lambda}\otimes_{B_{46}}B_{47})
\oplus\bigoplus_{h\in H_{47}}e_hB_{47}.
\]

The coefficient algebra is the inherited nonnegative free-word construction.
Sending the new letters to zero retracts its inclusion. The scalar-extension
map on the old carrier therefore splits. Extend the old pairing by scalars,
make the new history summand orthogonal to it, and set
\(\langle x,y\rangle=\sum_hx_h^*y_h\) on finite new coordinates.
Coordinate vectors give nondegeneracy there; no Hilbert positivity is needed.

The coordinate h retains source edition/policy, annotated state revision,
constituent and sound-version addresses, designation rule/clause/policy origins, deletion causes,
current query view, dependency footprint, competition decision and unresolved
obligations. Optional branches, commentary alternatives and rejected requests
have distinct records. Current surface equality does not identify histories.
The label λ names the full family; it is not the primitive scalar parameter
from which cuts, iota or dimension are presumed to arise.

## N2. Evidence cuts and admitted events

On each declared finite domain, use mutually orthogonal coordinate cuts
\(P_g^T,P_g^F,P_g^?\) whose sum is I. They read the supplied evidence of
one history/revision and consumer. A different revision has different query
records, even when its visible word is unchanged. A historical view is
available only to its source-qualified consumer.

For a legal event \(e:h\to h'\), including a pure decision event, set

\[
U_e(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a.
\]

For a grammatical mutation, legality includes the interface's address, view,
necessary-gate and decisive-exclusion checks, with competition resolved where
required. A decision-only event instead obeys the interface's decision rule:
an OPEN/BLOCKED record retains missing evidence or a decisive denial without
performing that grammatical mutation. The map is supported on its respective
legal source coordinate and extended linearly. Every appended decision gives
a new history node/revision. No event factor creates missing linguistic evidence.

Admission cuts depend on retained annotations, not on the coefficient being
specialized. This is the inherited finite history-domain assumption. A future
coefficient-sensitive selector needs a separate compatibility argument; it
is not covered merely by using the same notation.

## N3. Typed zero-reading and ordered composition

Let \(\epsilon:B_{47}\to C_\Sigma\) read the empty-word coefficient and
let typed \(\pi_0\) apply it without erasing history coordinates. Nonnegative
word degree makes ε a unital dagger homomorphism. For each fixed finite
family of admitted paths and dependent alternatives, the written map satisfies

\[
\pi_{0,\mathrm{out}}S_{47,\lambda}
=S_{47,0}\pi_{0,\mathrm{in}},
\qquad
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_{B_{47}}
=\langle\pi_0x,\pi_0y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

**Proof.** Each evidence/address/policy cut is coordinate-defined and hence
commutes with coefficient reading. Each legal event factor has ε-value one.
Multiplicativity gives compatibility for ordered composition; additivity
gives it for the finite dependent branch sum. Dagger compatibility gives the
pairing equality. Each unit-start path therefore recovers its declared
history and obligations. Arbitrary signed superpositions can cancel, so
universal support preservation is not asserted. □

Reading coefficients does not conflate a whole-target substitution with a
final-sound operation, a 1.2.43 designation with the main nonordering clause
of 1.2.44, or an inner affix
with its outer feminine suffix. Their addresses and causes are in the
retained coordinates. Likewise an Open query remains Open in this reading.

Even if two permitted operations happen to yield the same final surface in
opposite orders, their retained histories and free event words need not agree.
This construction supplies no general commutation, confluence or termination
theorem. Evolution is ordered composition; revision number is not elapsed
physical time. Recovery concerns the bounded written interaction relation.
Canonical execution remains future work in `operator_foundation/` on main;
no new runtime, physical quantum result or universal truth guarantee is added.
