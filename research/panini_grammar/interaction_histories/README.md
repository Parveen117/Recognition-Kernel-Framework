# PG47 — Addressed rule interactions and worked histories

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

After [PG46](../independent_written_closure/README.md) closes both tracked
catalogue-ID sets, PG47 constructs **29 written history units** across four
interaction domains. Each records supplied admission, actual target, source
clause/policy, ordered decisions and remaining realization obligations.
Both ID sets remain **3,983/3,983**, and finite positions remain **11,552**.
New IDs, agreement positions and counted paradigm readings are all zero.

## Read the construction

The [history register](HISTORY_REGISTER.json) maps all 29 units to document
lines, sources and outcome scopes. One unit can include several applied,
blocked or Open decisions. These counts are not executed test cases, new
sūtras or complete paradigm cells.

The records distinguish a **rule's clause and policy**, not only its number;
1.2.44 itself provides different consumer permissions. They also reassess
admission after each recorded change. A previous predicate is not reused just
because an output spelling looks familiar. Concrete unaccented histories
include मनावी, मनायी, वाराह्या and औडुलोम्या; their accent and declined
paradigms remain explicitly outside the completed local stem scope.
