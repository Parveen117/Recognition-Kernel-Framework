## The coupled मनु branches

Kāśikā, Kaumudī and Nyāsa 4.1.38 agree on **औ with ङीप्, ऐ with ङीप्,
or neither**. Kaumudī explicitly calls ङीप् सन्नियोगशिष्ट; Nyāsa denies
ङीप् when neither vowel replacement occurs. Kāśikā 4.1.36 places these
three rules in the पुंयोग domain and supplies the own-sense exclusion;
37 retains that distinction. The domain is carried into all three traces.

The base is **u-final मनु**. The final-target interpretation and the matching
6.1.78 action give मनावी and मनायी after the admitted ङीप्. The no-change
branch leaves stem मनु. Its source word मनुः is separately labelled because
the displayed trace does not construct a nominative ending and visarga.
There is no fourth licensed input to a junction rule with unchanged मनु
plus independently chosen ङीप्.

All three sources retain accent information. The new traces record the

Kāśikā's literal Vedic accent displays and Nyāsa's Uṇādi explanation of the
