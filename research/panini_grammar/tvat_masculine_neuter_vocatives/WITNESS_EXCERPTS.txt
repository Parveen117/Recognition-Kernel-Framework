The pronoun inventory includes tvat “other”. Entry 123 prints masculine
tvat/tvatau/tvataḥ and sends the remainder to marut. The marut address row is
marut/marutau/marutaḥ. The neuter tvat entry sends its panel to jagat, whose
address row is jagat/jagatī/jaganti.

The feminine tvat line calls the stem ī-final but also assigns it to sarit;
sarit is itself described as following masculine marut. This internal tension
is recorded as unresolved source evidence and supplies no feminine word.
