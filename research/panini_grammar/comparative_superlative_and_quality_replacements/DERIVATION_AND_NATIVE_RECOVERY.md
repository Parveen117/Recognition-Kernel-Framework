# PG811 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 55 distinguishes general `tamap` from quality `iṣṭhan` and positively permits another degree suffix on an already superlative base. Rule 56 separately licenses only `tamap` after finite verbs; no `iṣṭhan` is inferred there. Rule 57 changes the comparison domain to two entities or a distinguished comparison set and admits `tarap/īyasun` without yathāsaṃkhya. Its singular and plural outputs therefore retain their own comparison structures.

Rule 58 restricts vowel-initial `iṣṭhan/īyasun` to quality words but does not ban `tamap/tarap` from them: `paṭutara/paṭutama` remain Accepted, while nonquality `pācaka` takes only `tara/tama`. Rule 59 is the stated Vedic extension to tṛn/tṛc bases. Rules 60–62 keep `praśasya→śr`, `praśasya→jy` and `vṛddha→jy/varṣ` as distinct histories even where `jyeṣṭha/jyāyān` coincide.

Rule 63 maps `antika/bāḍha` to `ned/sādh`; the suffix is not paired by yathāsaṃkhya. Rule 64 gives `yuvan/alpa→kan` optionally, so `kaniṣṭha/kanīyān` each carry two lexical histories beside the unreplaced young and small outputs. Rule 65 deletes `vin/mat` before the vowel-initial degree suffix while preserving the stem-specific `sraj/tvac` readouts.
