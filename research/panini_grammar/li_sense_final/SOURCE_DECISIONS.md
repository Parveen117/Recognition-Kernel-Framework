## H04. Light root and light copy produce lī

Both lap and lay have light a before one following consonant.
Copy la receives i by san-like 7.4.93/7.4.79. The copy vowel
is itself light before the single original root l, so 7.4.94
lengthens it. The factors are **lī|lap** and **lī|lay**.
Neither root consonant needs the bh→b or root-s→ṣ operations
used in PG99; their source-specific guards are absent here.

The actual affix is still caṅ, not san. No independent
copy/root option is introduced and no automatic return to the
earlier root ā decision is performed. The copy's long ī and
the root's historical original ī are different occurrences.

## H05. Prefix contact is evaluated at the addressed boundary

ullāpayām/ullāyayām through 8.4.60. In aorist, aṭ lies
