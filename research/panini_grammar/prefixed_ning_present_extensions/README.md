### Placement of the augment

Bālamanoramā on 3.1.20 explicitly separates the prefix-like first member for the purpose of inserting aṭ, comparing bhāṇḍa with the reduplicated puccha past. The same constituent address in the ordinary laṅ context of 6.4.71 yields prefix + a + nominal-derived constituent. Compare 6.1.77 at vi/pari before a, and 8.3.23 at sam before a consonant. The inserted vowel leaves final m intact. Ud before the vowel retains d. In loṭ and vidhi-liṅ there is no aṭ and the PG150 initial junctions apply. The middle endings are inherited at the thematic boundary from [the earlier ending derivation](../causative_role_identity/DERIVATIONS.md).
