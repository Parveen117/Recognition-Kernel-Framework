### A documented transfer for the Uṇādi text (S5 and S6)

In Siddhāntakaumudī issue 7 the editor considered three online sources. His dated follow-up explicitly selects the **third, AVG-Sanskrit**, because it includes the Siddhāntakaumudī commentary, and names commit 14056c5 as the incorporation. This is direct provenance for the 2017 compilation. Its input file entered at 980bb61 on 10 March. Both the source line and the first XML record put *petvam amṛtam* and *bhṛśam* under 4.105. The adjacent 4.104 supplies the preceding fused list and the examples beginning with the parents. At the later pre-import revision 8f53c7f the rule and commentary strings at **both** 4.104 and 4.105 match the corresponding 2020-lineage digital text exactly. The published entry has a separate comma-delimited *pratyay* column. The older XML has a sūtra line and its commentary and supplies no such nine-way segmentation at 4.105.

The editor's first candidate, a publicly readable comparison workbook, independently locates these entries on its Uṇādi tab in rows 559–560. Its two populated columns give the joined rule and prose commentary. Listing it among candidates does not make it the source he selected. The same workbook at row 750 and the 2017 input at 5-56 both explicitly leave the purpose of kit in kīnāśa to be investigated. This establishes the transmission of an **explicit open question** before 2020. It does not establish a k-dependent operation.

A separate project plan cited in issue 7 discusses recording affixes both with and without their markers. Its linked sample workbook for Śvetavanavāsin required sign-in when consulted. The plan establishes proposed columns; it neither establishes completed word assignments nor access to the working notes. In particular 2017 does not supply the missing extension examples for **sya, ḍha, ḍaṭ or long āṭac**. PG197's short-aṭac evidence has its own affix and spelling. The relation AVG → Patel 2017 is documented, the text agreement Patel → later publisher is demonstrated, and the actual intermediary and author of the separate affix column remain open. The direct field count stays 99 of 103.
