# Domain and specialization

## N1. Input types

Take an agent, an incorporated nominal and a relation chosen from rumination, ascetic practice, emission, production and own experience. For experience include an owner and require that its ID equals the experiencer. Register the attachment of a source extension separately from the lexical ID.

## N2. Composition

Resolve the semantic guard first, then source-specific pada, then the nominal and outer finite formation. The marker is retained even under the tapas override. Inverse lookup of tapasyati needs the lexical override; its P ending by itself would not recover the suffix kyaṅ.

## N3. Specialization

On each listed nominal/role/slot request the composite of these maps has one normalized ordinary word. Projecting the guarded full history gives that same word while retaining nominal and source in the complementary channel. The exclusions are local controls on this finite domain. No universal inventory of human emotions or actions follows from the selected source list.
