# Written derivations

## H6. Form the 16 stems

For the selected short-a-final inputs the vowel before kyaṅ becomes ā. Tapas yields tapasya and retains s; this rule is distinct from 3.1.11 behavioral s-lopa. The selected stems include romanthāya, ūṣmāya, śabdāya, abhrāya, sudināya, sukhāya and duḥkhāya. Each nominal retains its own source, including the explicitly marked vārttikas.

## H7. Four finite series with explicitly owned endings

## H8. Two vowel-initial augments

For laṅ use āṭ with vowel-initial ūṣma and abhra, with 6.1.90 giving au and ā respectively: auṣmāyata and ābhrāyata. For bāṣpa the same 3sg request has abāṣpāyata with short aṭ. The initial change is separate from the pre-y lengthening in each stem.

## H9. Source class of the extensions

For the ejection series phena is in the stated vārttika. For the production series sudina, durdina and nīhāra are in the separate extension. They are not claims that any emitted material or any weather term accepts kyaṅ. The remaining named sound-perception extensions in 3.1.17 are outside this selected packet.
