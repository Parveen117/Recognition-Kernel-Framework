# PG37 — Selection, addressed operations and compound ownership

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

## C1. Written constructor

For an input from [Domain](DOMAIN.md), first establish the intended relation,
source policy and evidence. Determine its current scope, then evaluate
the relevant positive selectors, restrictions and explicit priority clauses.
A surviving clause produces an original-affix token, or a designation /
owned operation on an already supplied token. Apply only that clause's
coupled operations, recording every source and address. Return its history
with unresolved realization work. If a decisive condition is ?, return
Open at that decision; absence of a proven exception does not prove default
admission.

This is a source-relative written construction. Its current natural-language
predicates require an implementation and independent validation. A sorted
sūtra list alone cannot implement the source's protected alternatives,
scope resets, local obligatoriness and witnessed lexical restrictions.

surface forms coincide. Replacements preserve the old token as provenance;
deletions change its active status, not the record that it was selected.

1.2.51 controls explain different downstream obligations, without adding
new construction credit or implementing all their consequences here.

## C3. Branch fibres, not a Cartesian product

For admitted q let J(q) be the finite set of source-authorized clauses.
Construct the disjoint union of `(q, clause, ordered operations)` over
J(q). At 5.2.76 J has exactly the two compatible base/affix pairs; it
does not authorize swapped affixes. At 5.3.51 the sixth-of-measure clause
selects कन्, while the eighth-of-animal-part clause deletes its already
selected ञ/अन्. These are not interchangeable operations.

At 5.2.138 each of two bases admits all seven suffixes: fourteen clauses
are intentional there. At 5.3.22 there are fourteen different temporal
stipulations with day/year distinctions. At 5.4.77 twenty-five compound
stipulations require their own classes. The number of clauses is neither
the number of sūtra IDs nor a count of completed surface words.

At 5.2.105 the source licenses a deleted possessive history without
unambiguously fixing its original affix in this packet's adopted interface.
The exact-token branch stays Open until a policy/witness supplies that token;
it is not filled by choosing the visually convenient suffix.

## C4. Meaning, scope and explicit restrictions

The 5.3.58 restriction is on इष्ठन्/ईयसुन्: quality bases may still
take तरप्/तमप्. Later prescribed replacements admit their explicitly
named bases. At 5.3.60 प्रशस्य→श्र and at 61 प्रशस्य→ज्य are alternative
histories; 62 retains the separately prescribed वर्ष path for वृद्ध.

A transformation at 5.4.50 requires an earlier non-state, a resulting
सम्पद्यकर्ता and the correct verb. At 52 the **whole material** changes;
at 53 **all members** may change only in part. सम्-पद् newly admits
साति, while च्वि remains restricted to कृ/भू/अस्. At 54 the relation
resets to ownership and at 55 adds gift intent. Sharing a final सात् does
not collapse these distinct semantic histories.

5.3.106 requires two comparison witnesses: the compound's first likeness
and the new suffix's second likeness. A matching surface compound supplies
neither witness automatically. Similarly, the transfer at 5.4.22 requires
a previously admitted collective-selection witness for the relevant base;
5.3.8 requires an actual 5.4.44/45 तसि token.

## C5. Compound-ending construction

5.4.68 makes the selected ending a final constituent of the compound.
The original tree and its class remain available for later rules. At 69,
the source's सु/अति restriction ends before the specified later बहुव्रीहि
family; it is not a block on all praised compounds. At 71, negative
तत्पुरुष is distinct from negative बहुव्रीहि; at 72 the पथिन् subtype
has optional nonapplication. These gates require the compound tree before
affix selection.

For 77 use its finite clause table. स्त्रीपुंस in द्वन्द्व does not
license the GEN तत्पुरुष स्त्रीपुमान्. The collective द्विगु द्व्यायुष
does not license GEN द्व्यायुः. कर्मधारय जातोक्ष does not automatically
license the matching बहुव्रीहि. A tree-erasing word observer cannot
distinguish these inputs; the retained construction can.

This packet ends before 5.4.86. Heading reach and cited downstream examples
do not claim the remaining compound-ending family is constructed.
