# PG37 — Source policy, adopted readings and remaining obligations

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

## S1. Primary edition and reading method

Primary data is
[`ashtadhyayi-com/data`](https://github.com/ashtadhyayi-com/data/tree/5744762f010d677cfb43f347a42d02796cf615d6),
commit `5744762f010d677cfb43f347a42d02796cf615d6`. The 3,983-entry catalogue
provides IDs/text; the pinned Kāśikā fields provide the ordinary adopted
exposition. All 321 operative Kāśikā fields were read for the authored
contracts. Vasu is an archived historical translation/control, with targeted
comparison where marker or meaning questions arose; its entire prose is
not asserted to be free of errors. Nyāsa at 5.3.5,34,95 and 5.4.51 supplies
targeted refinements. It is not silently made the policy for every rule.

The [literal archive](SOURCE_EXCERPTS.json) contains **328 sūtra fields**
and **660 commentary fields**: both main commentaries for 321 operative
IDs plus seven controls, and four Nyāsa fields. All **988 fields** are
compared exactly to their source keys. Original spellings, markup, accents,
cross-references and damaged readings are preserved; the authored tables
record the adopted construction separately.

The seven controls are **1.1.22, 1.2.49, 1.2.51, 2.4.62, 4.1.76,
4.1.82 and 8.4.45**. Their archival presence earns **no new construction
credit**. Other cited dependencies remain obligations unless already built
in earlier packets. The structured register records primary source URLs
for every operative ID.

## S3. Open work retained

The present policy is a reviewable adopted construction, not a critical
edition settling every commentary. Remaining work includes complete
source-qualified gaṇa normalization; unreviewed lexical/priority intersections;
the unresolved alternatives above; complete accent, feminine, phonology and
inflection; runtime predicates; and independent verification. Vārttikas
archived with a core field do not become fully constructed automatically.

The [predecessor review](PREDECESSOR_REVIEW.md) records the 4.4.20 sandhi
clarification separately from a grammar correction. Previous unresolved
4.4.117/132 and 5.1.66/121 issues remain visible in their frozen packet.
