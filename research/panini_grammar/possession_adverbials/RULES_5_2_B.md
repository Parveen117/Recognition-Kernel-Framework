# PG37 — 5.2.94–140: possession and qualified lexical alternatives

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Read with [Domain](DOMAIN.md) and [Construction](CONSTRUCTION.md). The
possessive relation supports GEN or LOC as specified; it is not a universal
English “has” operator. An optional suffix and an unresolved source reading
are different kinds of choice. Witnesses do not add paradigm positions.
