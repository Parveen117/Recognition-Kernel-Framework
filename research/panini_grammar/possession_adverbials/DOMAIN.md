## D2. Evidence-bearing input

Continue the previous input with an explicit derivation tree and operation
addresses:

\[
q=(x,\rho,\kappa,d,r,p,e,\mu,\tau,\gamma,\alpha).
\]

Here x is lexical identity with retained history; ρ intended relation;
κ supporting semantic case; d target denotation; r register; p selected
source policy; e evidence; μ quantity structure; τ time structure;
γ compound/group structure; and α affix tokens and their addresses.

|Component|Information that cannot be discarded|
|---|---|
|Quantity|Measure versus amount, constituent count versus ordinal completion, equal-part price ratio versus repetition, unit identity, same-agent action occurrence|
|Possession|GEN/LOC target, named/ordinary use, quality versus literal object, animal/body-part or place restriction, member-specific gaṇa condition|
|Time and direction|Day versus year, today versus other time, actual direction word, near/far relative to a reference, supporting case|
|Degree/evaluation|Property licensing the noun, two-item/class comparison, praise versus censure, compassion versus smallness, one or two distinct likeness relations|
|Change of state|Prior non-state, resulting bearer as सम्पद्यकर्ता, whole material versus all items, licensed verb, ownership versus gift|
|Compound|Actual class, members before sandhi, internal relation, prefix identity, number of vowels, retained inner affixes and final constituent boundary|
|Affix operation|Original upadeśa, marker ownership, selecting rule, replacement source, target segment/token, लुक् versus लुप्, designation|

बह्वच् at 5.3.78 means **more than two vowels**, as opposed to the two-vowel
दत्त/गुप्त controls. “Polysyllabic” alone is too weak an implementation
test. Truncation at 83/84 counts the source-qualified vowel sequence, not
UTF-8 characters or a post-sandhi string guessed to be a name.

Every decisive predicate takes T, F or ?. **? produces Open**, not the
residual/default suffix. F rejects only that rule/analysis. Input evidence
is supplied; the contracts do not infer ancient membership, diagnosis,
ethical judgment or speaker intent from spelling alone. Traditional
explanations at 5.2.92/93 are source-labelled analyses, not empirical claims.

## D4. Three kinds of alternatives

1. **Grammatical choices after admission:** e.g. द्विधा/द्वैधम्/द्वेधा
   have three authorized replacements of one owned धा token.
2. **Dependent clauses:** e.g. 5.2.76 pairs अयःशूल with ठक् and
   दण्डाजिन with ठञ्; 5.3.51 pairs two bases, target meanings and actions.
3. **Source policies or unresolved evidence:** e.g. अश्/अन् at 5.3.5,
   register limitation at 5.2.123, and the exact deleted token at 5.2.105.

These have different types. A branch choice cannot repair missing evidence
or silently resolve a textual disagreement. Literal archives preserve both
damaged and variant readings; adopted operations are in
[Source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md).

## D5. Outputs and limits

The output is a retained **selection history** with original affix, clause,
scope, evidence, designations, replacement/deletion addresses and pending
realization obligations. Status is `BOUNDED_WRITTEN_CONTRACT_NOT_EXECUTED`.
The JSON register transcribes natural-language specifications; it is not
an executable predicate compiler. Source-only controls earn no new credit.

The denominator is the pinned catalogue's 3,983 entries. This accounting
does not settle edition-count questions, make the gaṇas exhaustive, or
establish whole-grammar completion. Tests, generators and replay remain
deferred as requested. The [predecessor review](PREDECESSOR_REVIEW.md)
separates checked integrity from semantic completeness.
