# PG275 · The future iṭ of striking and the long vowel of cutting

Monty · 12 November 2026

Rule 7.2.70 explicitly gives *haniṣyati*. Mādhava writes *hantā, haniṣyati* together and Kṣīrasvāmin independently identifies the *iṭ* before *sya*. The two future series can therefore be distinguished even in the third dual: *pariṇihantārau* in the periphrastic future and *pariṇihaniṣyataḥ* in the simple future.

| Root | *pari + ni*, periphrastic future 3sg | Simple future 3sg | Conditional 3sg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *han* | pariṇihantā | pariṇihaniṣyati | pariṇyahaniṣyat |
| *ṣo* | pariṇiṣātā | pariṇiṣāsyati | pariṇyaṣāsyat |

The cutting root has *syati* with the class-four present marker, and *sāsyati* in the bare simple future. In the latter word, only the root-initial *s* changes to *ṣ* after *ni*. Its second *s* follows *ā*. In the conditional the augment separates the inner preverb from the root and 8.3.63 preserves the conditioning. These cells select the third singular, dual and plural under each prefix order. They add **36 positions** to the preceding packet’s 42, bringing this pair to **78 distinct positions**. The ordinary nonthird-person schedules and the *han → vadh* substitution remain for further development.
