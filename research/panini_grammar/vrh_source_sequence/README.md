# PG124 — Where vṛh stands in the dyutādi sequence

Monty · 11 November 2026

**Decision.** Exclude the non-idit bhvādi vṛh ‘grow’ from the
dyutādi luṅ entitlement of 1.3.91 in the two compared textual
sequences. PG116 held this membership open because the modern composite
lexical list inserts vṛh between luṭh and śubh. Direct source order
now resolves that question for the compared witnesses. No new
finite position or reading is credited. Cumulative count: **28,697**.

The non-idit vṛh of growth is XML token no. 716 inside Mādhavīya
entry 446. The neighbouring **idit vṛhi** and the following **sound**
entry 447 have different teaching forms or senses. In particular a
printed aorist in entry 447 cannot license the growth entry as a
dyutādi member. The XML token number, entry ID, commentary's running
number and modern archive ID are different numbering systems. The
ordering argument compares positions within one witness at a time.

The explicit heading at Mādhavīya 449 extends the specified group
through kṛpū; the concluding line after entry 465 records its closure.
No bṛh/vṛh growth token occurs in that interval. The separate
Kāśikā 1.3.92 enumeration already excludes this root from the
five-root san subgroup. This finding addresses 1.3.91 on these two
sequences. Ordinary P morphology of growth vṛh and other recensions
remain separate research questions.
