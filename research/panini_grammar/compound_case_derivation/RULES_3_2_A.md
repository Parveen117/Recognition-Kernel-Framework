# PG43 — 3.2.1–24: dependent nominal formations

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Twenty-four bounded contracts. An उपपद is prescribed by the rule's relation,
not simply the closest preceding word. Unattested lexical extension stays Open.

|ID|Frame|Admission|Owned action|Precedence and boundary|Source witness or control|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|3.2.1|Object-dependent agent formation|Admitted object relation to root: produced, changed or reached entity|Select अण्|Lexical expression gate retained; not every possible object+verb phrase yields a conventional compound|K: कुम्भकारः, काण्डलावः, वेदाध्यायः; ग्रामं गच्छति not freely nominalized here|
|3.2.2|ह्वा/वा/मा with object|ह्वेञ्/वेञ्/माङ् identity and object उपपद|Select अण्|Specific exception to following आ-final क; not reverse priority by rule number alone|K: स्वर्गह्वायः, तन्तुवायः, धान्यमायः|
|3.2.3|Unprefixed आ-final root|Object उपपद and no उपसर्ग|Select क instead of अण्|Same visible suffix does not make affix markers interchangeable; 2 remains exceptional|K: गोदः, कम्बलदः; गोसंदायः control|
|3.2.4|स्था with nominal relation|सुबन्त उपपद semantically related to स्था|Select क, including source's qualified भाव clause|योगविभाग also admits specified आ-final/non-object cases; later सकर्मक uses require कर्म while other uses retain सुप् relation|K: समस्थः, विषमस्थः; द्विपः/पादपः under extended split|
|3.2.5|Two paired lexical actions|तुन्द + परि-मृज् in laziness, or शोक + अप-नुद् as bringer of happiness|Select क|Objects, roots and special meanings form two paired clauses; ordinary rubbing/removal retains other affix|K: तुन्दपरिमृजः, शोकापनुदः; तुन्दपरिमार्जः/शोकापनोदः controls|

|3.2.7|सम् + ख्या|Object उपपद with specified prefix/root|Select क|Retain source root history including चक्षिङ् substitution where applicable|K: गोसंख्यः|
|3.2.8|Unprefixed singing/drinking|गै with object, or drinking पा with सुरा/शीधु object|Select टक्|Drinking distinguished from guarding पा; restricted object list and Vedic variation retained; ट् matters for feminine|K: सामगः/सामगी; सुरापः/सुरापी; क्षीरपा and guarding सुरापा controls|
|3.2.9|हृ without lifting|Object-dependent taking that is not upward lifting|Select अच्|Lifting uses its own route; listed ग्रह्-object and सूत्र-holding extensions are separately admitted|K: अंशहरः, रिक्थहरः; भारहारः control|
|3.2.10|Lifting indicates age-stage|Actual or potential lifting conveys bodily age/development|Select अच् from हृ|Restores the excluded lifting frame only with age implication, not all carrying|K: अस्थिहरः श्वा; कवचहरः कुमारः|
|3.2.11|आ-हृ habitual disposition|Object-related bringing is a natural, result-independent disposition|Select अच्|One act or mere frequency without ताच्छील्य evidence insufficient|K: पुष्पाहरः, फलाहरः; भाराहारः control|
|3.2.12|अर्ह् with object|Object उपपद in admitted worthiness relation|Select अच्|Affix distinction has feminine consequences even if masculine readout resembles अण्|K: पूजार्हा, मालार्हा|
|3.2.13|स्तम्ब-रम् or कर्ण-जप्|Locative nominal relation; respective elephant or informer referent|Select अच्|Not object-case relation forced onto intransitive रम्; paired root/referent restrictions retained|K: स्तम्बेरमो हस्ती; कर्णेजपः सूचकः; mosquito जपिता outside|
|3.2.14|शम्-dependent name|शम् उपपद with admitted root and conventional-name use|Select अच्|Explicit धातु scope protects even कृ against हेत्वादि ट; feminine formation follows अच् history|K: शंकरः, शंभवः; शंकरा named woman|
|3.2.15|शी with locus|सुबन्त locus of lying|Select अच्|Body-part/instrument, दिग्धसह and actor-state extensions require their own clauses; Vedic गिरि ड separately retained|K: खशयः, गर्तशयः; पार्श्वशयः/उत्तानशयः extensions|
|3.2.16|चर् with locus|Nominal उपपद denotes locus of moving|Select ट|ट् origin governs feminine, not merely visible अ|K: कुरुचरः/कुरुचरी|
|3.2.17|चर् special dependents|भिक्षा/सेना/आदाय उपपद in specified relation|Select ट|Restores non-locative cases; no general erasure of 16's location condition|K: भिक्षाचरः, सेनाचरः, आदायचरः|
|3.2.18|सृ with forward-position expression|पुरस्/अग्रतस्/अग्रे उपपद|Select ट|Exact relation words, not unrestricted synonyms|K: पुरःसरः, अग्रतःसरः, अग्रेसरः|
|3.2.19|पूर्व actor with सृ|पूर्व denotes the actor, not traversed object/place|Select ट|Same written पूर्व with object relation selects another route|K: पूर्वसरः; पूर्वसारः for पूर्वं देशं सरति|
|3.2.20|कृ with special agent meaning|Object उपपद; source's decisive cause, habitual disposition or compliance sense|Select ट|Three semantic clauses retained; plain manufacturing without these meanings does not qualify|K: शोककरी, श्राद्धकरः, वचनकरः; कुम्भकारः control|

|3.2.22|Paid work|Exact कर्मन् word is object of कृ and wage/remuneration intended|Select ट|कर्मणि here names the lexical word, not every object; ordinary work without pay not included|K: कर्मकरः; कर्मकारः control|
