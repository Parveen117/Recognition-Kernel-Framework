## D1. Primitive and admitted evidence

The primitive remains the full native operator/history carrier, with typed
cuts, cut field, derived iota, dagger, tensor type, pairing, complementary
channels and ordered histories. A grammatical request supplies addressed
annotations on that carrier. A surface string, scalar λ or clock does not
replace it. [Native continuation](NATIVE_CONTINUATION.md) specializes the
existing PG42/PG37 adapter under unchanged assumptions.

|Input component|Distinctions required by this packet|
|---|---|
|Edition and source policy|Pinned catalogue, operative commentary, teacher/geographic distinction, variant and unresolved field|
|Lexical identity|Root class, original markers, homonyms, nominal versus verbal entry, gaṇa membership, conventional-name sense|
|Action frame|Action identity, actor/object/instrument/locus, original versus current actor, transitivity and intended result|
|Case state|Participant role, already-expressed relation, case consumer, original सुप् identity and current exponent|
|Compound tree|Class, member addresses, semantic fitness, internal/external referent, original case relations and member order|
|Number and gender|Referent multiplicity, collective/individual construal, grammatical gender, constituent plurality versus number of written members|
|Affix chain|Root-relative order, intended affix class, original/active token, marker identity, deletion cause, auxiliary's separate finite ending|
|Discourse|True anaphoric continuation, same referent, contempt, praise, blessing, habitual disposition or first-origination intent|
|Register|Prose/Vedic/Brāhmaṇa context, attested बहुल clause and any consumer-specific designation|
|Query|Current operation, target token, visibility view, exact exception relation and downstream obligations|

Use T/F/? cuts on decisive evidence. Unknown root sense, source policy, referent
identity, naming status or lexical membership stays Open. It does not authorize
a residual suffix, a compound or an optional case. Historical source social
and biological classes are lexical annotations in a supplied text, not inferred
classifications of living people or scientific taxonomies.
