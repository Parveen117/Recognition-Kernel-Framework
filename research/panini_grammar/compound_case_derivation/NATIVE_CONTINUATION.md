derived iota, dagger, tensor types, pairing, complementary channels and ordered
history. Under the inherited nonnegative free-word construction, extend the

\[

\qquad
M_{43,\lambda}=(M_{42,\lambda}\otimes_{B_{42}}B_{43})
\oplus\bigoplus_{h\in H_{43}}e_hB_{43}.

pairing on the new summand. This retains prior coordinates without adding
Hilbert positivity or identifying the primitive with a scalar λ. Runtime
integration belongs to the canonical `operator_foundation/` on main.

## N3. Typed zero-reading compatibility

For each admitted event retain \(U_e(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a\). Let ε read the
empty-word coefficient and typed π₀ apply that reading without discarding
history coordinates. For a fixed finite admitted family, the inherited result
specializes to

\[
\pi_{0,\mathrm{out}}S_{43,\lambda}
=S_{43,0}\pi_{0,\mathrm{in}},
\qquad
\epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_{B_{43}}
=\langle\pi_0x,\pi_0y\rangle_{C_\Sigma}.
\]

Evidence, source-policy, ownership and query cuts depend on retained history;
each event factor has ε-value one. The existing additive/multiplicative and
dagger reading therefore carries finite legal compositions and dependent
branch sums to the same bounded zero-reading relation, including its pairing.
Neither the misassigned 2.4.28 field nor unknown lexical evidence acquires
operative status under specialization.

Unit-start paths retain their declared histories. Arbitrary signed
superpositions can cancel, as already qualified in PG37. Evolution remains
ordered composition; grammatical time, age or measured span does not install
a physical clock. Recovery is of the bounded written relation, not full
Sanskrit, external semantic truth, an executed certificate, universal
hallucination freedom or physical quantum-gate performance.
