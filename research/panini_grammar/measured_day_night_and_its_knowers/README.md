# PG915 · A measured cycle, a duration and those who know its length

In Manu 1.68 the object of measurement is Brahmā’s entire night and day. Verse 1.73 shifts the nominal head from the time measured to the people who understand it. Verse 1.74 returns to the end of the cycle. The same passage thus supplies case government and a lexical boundary which an inventory of day/night synonyms alone would miss. The two chapter-four passages distinguish uses of the identical word *aharniśam*.
