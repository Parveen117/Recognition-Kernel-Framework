# PG140 — Which participant determines the verb in a coordinated clause?

Monty · 11 November 2026

There are two separate decisions: which entity or group controls agreement, and which grammatical person and number that controller supplies. For a group containing a speaker and one other participant the collective forms have first-person dual. Panchal and Kulkarni also document singular agreement with a nearby individual in specified orders. Both readings have a place in [AGREEMENT.json](AGREEMENT.json).
