This fixes the scope of the bodily reading. The dictionary also lists a neuter collection of ears under bhikṣādi. The pinned Vasu field at 4.2.38 lists *śrotra* as member 15 and gives the governing collection sense. These two witnesses allow two requests with the same base, suffix and isolated nominative.
