Monty · 10 November 2026

## H06. Present-system and future interfaces

Laṭ/laṅ/loṭ/vidhiliṅ use kār+ṇic ay and outer śap,
with the pinned complete thematic endings. Ordinary vidhiliṅ
s-lopa and loṭ P2sg hi-luk retain their own scopes. Laṅ
aṭ precedes the whole stem, giving akāray-.

Lṛṭ/lṛṅ sya and luṭ finite tāsi receive ordinary short
iṭ after ṇic, which remains ay: kārayiṣyate/kārayiṣyati,
kārayitā, akārayiṣyata/akārayiṣyat. The basic aniṭ tag
is not copied across the current derived-stem interface. Finite
luṭ endings keep tiṅ provenance, including shared third-person
spellings across padas. No ciṇvat option is admitted.

## H07. Blessing and two different dental-n boundaries

P blessing has non-val yāsut and 6.4.51 ṇic-lopa, giving
kāryāt and its full paradigm. A blessing retains ṇic through
iṭ: kārayiṣīṣṭa. A2pl has the pinned pre-iṭ ay-based
dh/ḍh option, giving kārayiṣīdhvam/kārayiṣīḍhvam.

Loṭ P1sg **kārayāṇi** has 8.4.1/2 ṇatva: root r and
ending n lie in the same word, with allowed vowel/y interveners.
This applies in both P profiles. Conversely, in kārayanti and
other nt forms, earlier 8.3.24 makes n an anusvāra before t;
8.4.58 later restores dental n. Its later output is not fed
back to earlier ṇatva under 8.2.1. Kāśikā 8.4.58 explicitly
explains this order through kurvanti/kṛṣanti.

Final n, as in acīkaran and kārayeran, is separately protected
by 8.4.37. The register distinguishes two ending-ṇ keys,
twelve nonfinal nt keys, and eight final-n keys. They are not
one undifferentiated “nasal exception”.

## H08. Perfect has primary and auxiliary kṛ occurrences

Primary kārayām uses 3.1.35 and 6.4.55 ay, without primary
copy/aṭ/iṭ. Primary liṭ-luk and 1.1.63 have their pinned
scope. Auxiliary 3.1.40 kṛ/bhū/as takes its own perfect.
Kṛ alone transfers primary pada by 1.3.63; bhū/as remain P.

## H09. Caṅ is morphological in all three semantic frames

Actual ṇic selects caṅ in luṅ; 6.4.51 removes ṇic.
The cīkar factor takes the inherited thematic secondary tuples

\[
P=(at,atām,an,aḥ,atam,ata,am,āva,āma),\quad
A=(ata,etām,anta,athāḥ,ethām,adhvam,e,āvahi,āmahi).
\]

Aṭ precedes the copied constituent. Thus A acīkarata and
P acīkarat occur under their different semantic certificates.
All 27 aorist keys are singleton, with no outer iṭ. Thematic
a prevents direct-abhyasta plural substitutions; A2pl dh is
dental. Copy c and original k retain separate addresses.
