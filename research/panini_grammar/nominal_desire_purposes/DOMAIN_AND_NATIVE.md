# Domain and specialization

## N1. Typed intentions

The base request contains a nominal, an agent/wisher and a self-related beneficiary. The sense certificate carries a positive purpose or the identified ordinary-sense condition. Unknown purpose is a partial input with two possible analyses, not a negative assertion. The outer request carries lakāra and agreement.

## N2. Native operator

Extend the inherited carrier with typed sense-choice, stem and vowel-initial augment arrows. A semantic choice retains its source and complementary ordinary/special possibility. The udan substitution preserves the fact that the source nominal is udaka; its surface n has a specific exception to ordinary nominal n-lopa.

## N3. Projection and partial information

On a complete sense input precisely the named member of H06 survives both in the full construction and its ordinary specialization. On a partial purpose input take the union over compatible completions. Forgetting an unknown example within the ordinary frame preserves its one grammatical output. Incidence converse on finitely supported bases supplies the matching relation without assigning probabilities or a physical clock.
