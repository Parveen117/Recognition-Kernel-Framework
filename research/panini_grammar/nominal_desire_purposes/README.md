# PG130 — Why someone wants water changes the verb

Monty · 11 November 2026

The source distinguishes wanting water to drink from wanting it for a bath or irrigation. A role model that records only “person wants water” misses the distinction. This packet stores the purpose separately, along with attested contrasts for food and wealth.
