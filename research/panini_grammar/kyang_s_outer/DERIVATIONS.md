# Written derivations

## H6. Three payas futures

Payāya → payāy-iṣyate; payasya with y retained → payasy-iṣyate; the same s-retaining inner stem with y dropped → payas-iṣyate. The written words are payāyiṣyate, payasyiṣyate and payasiṣyate. Luṭ 3sg gives payāyitā, payasyitā and payasitā. Each is one alternative at the same position.

## H7. Source filtering for ojas and internal s

At lṛṭ 3sg the Kāśikā stem gives ojāyiṣyate. The separately attributed Bhāṣya policy gives ojasyiṣyate or ojasiṣyate after its inner ojasya decision. No policy recorded here gives all three as internally free. In haṃsāyiṣyate and sārasāyiṣyate the internal s survives, with the following ā precluding the outer option.

## H8. Five distinct A 2pl readings on a three-path profile

In luṅ payas gives apayāyidhvam/apayāyiḍhvam, apayasyidhvam/apayasyiḍhvam, and apayasidhvam. In āśīrliṅ the same 2+2+1 split occurs with the blessing suffix. The augmented ojas and apsaras paths use āṭ with initial vṛddhi; the surviving outer conditioning is independent of this initial change.

## H9. Separate the perfect members

For payas the primary words are payāyām, payasyām and payasām. Each takes kṛ-A, bhū-P or as-P with the inherited seam spellings. Payasāñcakre identifies a retained-s/deleted-y primary and A-kṛ. The same string does not establish the meaning or recitation policy outside the declared nominal and source domain.

## H10. Arithmetic with ordered alternatives

The eleven present-series stems become fifteen complete outer factors. Each supplies 54 positions across six labels before surface alternatives; those paths share 378 morphological position keys. The A perfect gives 45 readings for each factor instead of nine. The eleven y-retaining factors each add one retroflex reading in two A 2pl slots. Hence 15×(54+36)+11×2 = 1,372. With PG141 the seven complete ten-lakāra families total 630 positions and 1,768 readings.
