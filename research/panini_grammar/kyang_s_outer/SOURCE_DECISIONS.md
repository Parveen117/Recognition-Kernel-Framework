# Source decisions

## H1. Retain the source-qualified inner decision

Use the PG141 paths and commentary IDs. Under the shared payas reading both s schedules exist. For ojas, s retention and loss are available under different named analyses. A formation selected under the Bhāṣya alternate account cannot silently switch to the Kāśikā s-lopa path during outer inflection.

## H2. The y decision is conditioned on the earlier s decision

The genuine s-final inputs ojas, payas, yaśas and vidvas have a retained-s branch. There 6.4.50 applies to the initial y of kyaṅ before the selected ārdhadhātuka. In a lost-s stem such as payāya, or an internal-s stem such as haṃsāya, there is a vowel immediately before y. Its deletion is excluded.

## H3. S and y have different addresses

6.4.48 removes final denominative a at the outer junction. 6.4.50 addresses the ky-initial y. The lexical s-removal in 3.1.11 has already determined what precedes y. Treating these as independent binary flags could generate a spurious lost-s/lost-y stem.

## H4. The optional A 2pl sees a third local environment

8.3.79 looks at what precedes outer iṭ in the specified A-ending environments. A retained y is iṇ. If the outer y was dropped after s, s itself precedes iṭ and is outside iṇ. Only the retained-y factors get the optional retroflex. Retain dental on the other factor.

## H5. The role survives two-word expression

Liṭ adds ām, followed by the established three auxiliaries. Kṛ takes the A entitlement of these seven kyaṅ profiles, while the ordinary bhū/as auxiliaries retain their P maps. No inner lexical branch should be chosen a second time on the auxiliary.
