# PG142 — Apply y-loss only after the decision about s

Monty · 11 November 2026

The outer ārdhadhātuka form cannot be computed by making s-loss and y-loss independent switches. On the seven families of PG141, losing the original s exposes a vowel before y and blocks 6.4.50. Keeping original final s leaves a consonant that licenses optional y-loss. Payas consequently has three stem paths, with the applicable source policy retained. Six lakāras add 378 positions and 1,372 readings.
