# PG270 · Ninety-nine ramparts and one Vedic accusative plural

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Zürich corpus assigns both occurrences of *dehī́-* ‘mound, rampart’ to one position, **feminine accusative plural**. The annotated word is *dehyàḥ* in both. In the continuous pāda the same two tokens appear as *dehyò* before the following voiced sound.

| Ṛgvedic pāda | Annotated word | Continuous-text neighbour | Context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6.47.2d | dehyàḥ | dehyò hán | The verse counts ninety and nine ramparts |
| 7.6.5a | dehyàḥ | dehyò ánamayat | The ramparts are the objects of bending or bringing low |

Monier-Williams gives the accented feminine headword and its senses including ‘rampart’. Its citation shape resembles the masculine nominative singular *dehī* ‘one having a body’ of the preceding packet. Their accusative plurals differ: observed Vedic *dehyàḥ* for the ramparts, ordinary *dehinaḥ* for the embodied beings. Neither is the longer lexical *dehanī* of the smearing root. These two hymn passages add **one** nominal position and **two** source observations. The verse table preserves the pāda, word number and word accents.
