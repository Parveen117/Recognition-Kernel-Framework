# PG279 · Ordinary optatives retain han and the present-system cutting stem

Monty · 13 November 2026

For cutting, the present-system *sy-* and the sārvadhātuka yā-to-iy prescription of 7.2.80 give *syet*. This is not the root-grade history behind the blessing *seyāt*. Rule 7.2.79 deletes the nonfinal liṅ *s* in its own sārvadhātuka domain; it does not delete a final person-ending *s* or apply without qualification to blessing-liṅ.
