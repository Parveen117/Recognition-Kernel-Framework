# PG277 · Present and imperfect persons: the inner preverb and the root nasal

Monty · 13 November 2026

Mādhava independently contrasts *hanti, hataḥ, ghnanti* and gives the present *haṃsi, hathaḥ, hatha, hanmi, hanvaḥ, hanmaḥ*. His imperfect list separately contains *ahan, ahatam, ahata, ahanam, ahanva*. The new tables retain the two ordered preverbs through the different root grades.

| Selected cell | Striking, pra + ni | Cutting, pra + ni |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present 2sg | praṇihaṃsi | praṇiṣyasi |
| Present 1du | praṇihanvaḥ | praṇiṣyāvaḥ |
| Imperfect 2sg | praṇyahan | praṇyaṣyaḥ |
| Imperfect 1sg | praṇyahanam | praṇyaṣyam |

The two nasal histories must be kept separate. Rule 8.4.17 changes the inner *ni* to *ṇi*. The resulting intervening *ṇ* is outside the interveners admitted by 8.4.2, so the single-prefix *prahaṇvaḥ/prahanvaḥ* alternatives of 8.4.23 do not supply two options here. The selected ordered word is *praṇihanvaḥ*, with dental root *n*. Anusvāra in *praṇihaṃsi* comes from 8.3.24, not from that nasal change.

The weak-root vowel deletion is 6.4.98, *gamahanajanakhanaghasāṃ lopaḥ kṅity anaṅi*; final-nasal deletion before a qualifying consonantal ending has the separate 6.4.37 condition. These should not be collapsed into one “weak form” operation. The already written third-person root-initial *gh* remains governed by 7.3.54.

The second and third imperfect singular of striking coincide in *praṇyahan*. They remain different grammatical positions. With PG274 these 48 new cells complete all eight present/imperfect panels. The pair reaches **198/360** positions. The whole-root *ja* and *vadh* substitutions remain for their own source review; neither is silently licensed by this packet.
