# PG836 · Maimata and the missing feminine in Jñānendra’s argument

In two printings the upper Siddhāntakaumudī and the lower Tattvabodhinī answer different questions at 4.1.150. Bhaṭṭoji identifies the allocation of two affixes to two bases under two readings. Jñānendra asks whether the correct **input** exists for the alleged feminine effect of ṇit. The difference affects when the PG833 case panel may be used.
