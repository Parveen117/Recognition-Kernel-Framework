# PG267 · Four future bases and the vowel-initial blessings of sowing and carrying

Monty · 12 November 2026

The two futures separate the four roots even when their meanings overlap. The first column below has the periphrastic future, the second the simple future. All entries select third singular under *pari + ni*.

| Root | *luṭ* | *lṛṭ* | Benediction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *ci* | pariṇicetā | pariṇiceṣyati | pariṇicīyāt |
| *dih* | pariṇidegdhā | pariṇidhekṣyati | pariṇidihyāt |
| *vap* | pariṇivaptā | pariṇivapsyati | pariṇyupyāt |
| *vah* | pariṇivoḍhā | pariṇivakṣyati | pariṇyuhyāt |

Kāśikā 6.3.112 cites *voḍhā*, with *o* upon the stipulated loss of *ḍh*. The Mādhavīya quotes the sequence *voḍhā, vakṣyati* and later *uhyāt*. Both *vap* and *vah* have a vowel-initial weak root in the active benedictive (*up-, uh-*). Therefore *ni* ends in *y* in these two blessings even though the construction has no past augment. In the conditional the same boundary occurs before the augment in all four roots: *pariṇyaceṣyat, pariṇyadhekṣyat, pariṇyavapsyat, pariṇyavakṣyat*.

The frame of *vah* matters to pada choice. Rule 1.3.81 gives active after *pra* and cites *āvahate* to illustrate the limitation. The Mādhavīya attributes the extension to direct *pari + vah* to some commentators. For *pra + ni + vah*, Kṣīrasvāmin separately supplies both *praṇivahate* and *praṇivahati*. The Mādhavīya also cites *praṇyavahad* before *iti*; the pausal imperfect in the preceding packet ends in *-t*.
