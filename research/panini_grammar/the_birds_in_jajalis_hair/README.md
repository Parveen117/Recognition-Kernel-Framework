# PG979 · The birds in Jājali’s hair

In Mahābhārata 12.253.20–22 two birds build a nest in Jājali’s matted hair. He stands still. The two birds then dwell there **pariviśvastau**, fully confident. In 12.253.26 the same pair keep returning to his head and are **āśvāsitau**, reassured. The two participles agree with the two birds, while the adjacent finite verbs carry their own histories. The second verse leaves the agent of reassurance unexpressed. Book 14 supplies a control for that missing relation. Yudhiṣṭhira says **āśvāsito ’ham** to the sages and expressly assigns the reassurance to them with instrumental plurals (14.14.6–7). The text directly licenses a masculine singular and dual for the causal participle. The masculine dual for the mixed bird pair is a grammatical agreement reading. ## Complete masculine words

Use the ordinary masculine a-stem endings in [PG971’s case register](../four_negated_properties_in_the_gita/STATE.json). The four exact bases before the endings are pariviśvast-, āśvāsit-, āśvast- and āśvasit-. All take dental n in the instrumental singular and genitive plural. In pariviśvasta the intervening ś and t separate the earlier r from the suffix n. For example, the two bird instrumentals with a dual controller would be pariviśvastābhyām and āśvāsitābhyām. The simple masculine singular instrumentals are āśvastena and āśvasitena. The ablative singular retains the two final voicing options under the existing pause convention. A vocative request requires address, and singular and plural requests have their own number conditions. [Written words with individual credit and evidence](FORMS.tsv)

## Typed recovery

On the PG14 paired right modules keep the ordered prefix tuple, root, affix host, actor or causee, iṭ policy, ordered ṇic history, agreement features, source and boundary on the basis. In the causal grade, the event that supplies affix-owned i precedes the event deleting ṇic. In the simple grade two separate cuts admit the attributed niṣṭhā histories. An agreement edge consumes a gender/case/number request only after the stem and referent have been selected. On each admitted h → k the right-linear operator sends e_h b to e_k(1+z)b. Output augmentation gives e_k ε(b), identical to the classical event after input augmentation. On the complementary input both are zero. Multiplicativity gives typed recovery for the composites, and compatibility with dagger recovers the paired adjoints.
