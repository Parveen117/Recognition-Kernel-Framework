# PG147 — Mocking speech changes two agreement assignments

Monty · 11 November 2026

In Kāśikā's matched rice examples, the ordinary pair **manyase / bhokṣye** becomes **manye / bhokṣyase** under the specific mocking construction of 1.4.106. The initial imperative ehi is outside the two targeted assignments. This is a direct source example in which grammatical person cannot, by itself, identify the real-world thinker.

| Context | Thinking verb | Other verb | Source situation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mock, rice | manye, 1sg | bhokṣyase, 2sg | The rice has already been eaten in the commentary’s example |
| Mock, chariot | manye, 1sg | yāsyasi, 2sg | The vehicle has already gone in the commentary’s example |
| Ordinary comparison, rice | manyase, 2sg | bhokṣye, 1sg | Regular assignments in the paired source control |
