# PG88 — San blessings, perfects and aorists

Monty · 9 November 2026

These are manually written rule inferences on PG87's eight unchanged
source-qualified profiles. The actor of desire controls agreement. The
blessing, parokṣa and past certificates concern the desire event, not
automatic accomplishment of the desired action. Read the [source
decisions](SOURCE_DECISIONS.md) and [derivations](DERIVATIONS.md).

Each perfect row is **one request position**. Its three auxiliary columns
list internal alternatives, with original request pada kept distinct from
auxiliary pada. Slashes list the selected within-position words; the
ṃ/ñ and ṃ/m alternatives concern the boundary after ām. The inherited
unaccented, non-doubling, pausal IAST convention is unchanged.
