## H01. Three contexts on the same desire event

For āśīrliṅ choose the liṅ member of 3.3.173: a wish for the unattained
desire event. For liṭ require 3.2.115's non-today parokṣa context,
including an appropriate indirect-knowledge account for first-person
requests. For luṅ use the ordinary augmented past domain of 3.2.110.
The primary event remains desire, controlled by its actor. A past desire
does not assert completion of its intended action; wishing for the desire
is not identical to wishing for that action's accomplishment.

PG87's transitive anu+jñā P and intransitive knowledge-guided engagement
A are different certified frames, not optional padas of an unqualified
input. The latter retains inner agent ⊗ instrument and absence of karman;
all other profiles retain agent ⊗ object. Those inner roles coexist with
the desired action being the object of the outer desire. Missing context,
actor-identity or licensing-cause evidence remains HOLD.

sic's pronounced s separately from its enunciative i and marker c.
