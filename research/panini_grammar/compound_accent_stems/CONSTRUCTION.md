# PG39 — Owned constructions and dependent choices

Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Read the [domain](DOMAIN.md) and the seven rule tables before using these
worked specifications. These are written constructions and source-grounded
decision cases, not executed Sanskrit tests or newly completed paradigms.

## C1. Event record and selection

Retain the full input, policy and compound tree. For an admitted clause,
record its rule, trigger token, target address, pre-event representation,
result, branch choice and remaining obligations. Substitution keeps its
original token; deletion marks it inactive; augmentation states its owner
and boundary. Preserve rejected and Open candidates as evidence.

1. Establish original lexical/suffix identity, compound admission and meaning.
2. Resolve the rule's scope and query-specific view, including original case.
3. Evaluate local exceptions and priority under the chosen source policy.
4. Select only admitted dependent clauses, retaining the choice history.
5. Perform the addressed action; leave subsequent phonology and inflection
   to their independently admitted rules.

This order is a specification of dependencies, not a universal runtime
schedule. Some later queries use earlier views while actual results remain
in history. A source witness never fills an otherwise unconstructed cell.

## C2. Accent targets and dependent alternatives

|Action|Owned information retained|
|---|---|
|Preserve first/last member|The constituent's original accent structure, not a guessed initial accent|
|Set initial/final/penultimate accent|The admitted vowel address in the specified constituent|
|Preserve both members|Both structures simultaneously; इन्द्राबृहस्पती can contain three udāttas|
|Accent before कप्|The pre-suffix member boundary and original कप् token|
|Block a placement|The rejected candidate and still-eligible earlier accent route|

At **6.2.110/167/169**, prefixed निष्ठा + body-part मुख first selects
169's optional last-final placement. If declined, 110 can give first-member
final accent; declining that leaves the original गति accent. These are
three conditioned histories. They do not license three freely chosen
positions on every मुख compound. The 168 exclusion still precedes the
comparison option for गो/मुष्टि/वत्स.

At **6.2.148**, retain both restrictions rather than replacing them with
one blanket blessing requirement:

|Case|Result of the name-accent decision|
|---|---|
|कारक + दत्त/श्रुत, blessing name|Final accent admitted: देवदत्त, विष्णुश्रुत|
|कारक + दत्त/श्रुत, nonblessing name|148 restricts final accent; देवदत्त conch keeps first-member route|
|कारक + another क्त, blessing name|देवपालित does not receive 146's name-final accent|
|कारक + another क्त, nonblessing name|146 remains available, as in देवखाता|
|Nonकारक गति + दत्त/श्रुत|संश्रुत/विश्रुत not excluded by the कारक restriction|

At **6.2.172–175**, distinguish the final vowel of the whole compound
from the vowel before कप् and the penultimate vowel of its short-ending
last member. Under K/Nyāsa's 174 reading, one-vowel ज्ञ lacks that
penultimate target. 173 is barred there, and 172 accents the final vowel
of the कप्-ending compound. Vasu's prose assigns that fallback to 173;
the archive preserves it, but it is not the adopted route. बहु transfers
the applicable negative-compound rule only with उत्तरपदार्थबहुत्व.

At **6.2.198**, अक्रान्त excludes a first member ending in the sequence
**क्र**, not separate क्-ending and र्-ending classes. चक्रसक्थ keeps the
final accent supplied by षच्; गौरसक्थ admits the stated option.

## C3. Sup retention, morphology and local meaning

अलुक् takes an existing सुप् token and prevents its deletion under
2.4.71. It does not add a new ending to a bare word and does not itself
license a compound. A later accent query can consult original तृतीया or
सप्तमी even if ordinary deletion has occurred.

The **6.3.24** स्वसृ branches are dependent: retained genitive allows
the separately optional षत्व, while deletion selects the mandatory
8.3.84 route. Thus मातुःष्वसा, मातुःस्वसा and मातृष्वसा carry different
case/phonetic histories. पुत्र inheritance at **25** jumps over 24;
it does not turn the पुत्र case into another optional aluk at 24.

For **6.3.34–45**, record the original feminine and its corresponding
masculine use in the same sense. पुंवद्भाव does not assert that the referent
has changed sex. The क्-upadhā prohibition is limited, under the adopted
vārttika policy, to तद्धित/वु-derived क्. The later कर्मधारय/जातीय/देशीय
restoration retains the original भाषितपुंस्क and no-ऊङ् gates.

श्रेयसितरा and श्रेयसीतरा are the shortening/retention branches of
**45**; श्रेयस्तरा uses the separately stated masculine-treatment or
gender-unexpressed analysis. They must not share a fabricated single
shortening history.

At **6.3.99**, the non-GEN/non-INSTR condition belongs to the seven
आशिस्-family nouns. कारक and छ retain दुक् even in those case relations.
At **100**, अर्थ has its own optional augmentation. The archive's displaced
Nyāsa text at key 63100 is not evidence for this construction.

would erase the information needed by later clauses.

## C5. 6.4.22 is a rule-relative view

For an operation event and a query, retain their rule addresses, their
आभीय membership, their triggering आश्रय tokens, and any stated visibility
exception. In the adopted K/Kaumudī reading, the domain runs through
6.4.175, including the भ authority starting at 129. This packet authors
22's contract, not every later numbered rule in that region.

|Source case|Required view|
|---|---|
|एधि/शाधि|Earlier consonant-based धि admission survives the relevant replacement through the prescribed earlier view|
|आगहि/जहि|Do not let the new अ-ending appearance license 6.4.105's हि deletion|
|अभाजि/राग|7.2.116 is outside this query domain; it sees the nasal deletion for वृद्धि|
|पपुषः/चिच्युषः/लुलुवुषः|Suffix-triggered operations and विभक्ति-triggered वस् सम्प्रसारण have different आश्रय; no blanket suppression|
|बभूव and उपदिदीये families|Explicit वुक्/युट् visibility exceptions remain available to उवङ्/यण्|

The query reads an allowed view of a history. It does not erase the actual
event, restore every deleted letter to active status, or prevent all
priority within the region. **6.4.42** explicitly lets सन्'s later आ
replacement defeat 37's nasal deletion in its admitted environment.

## C6. Nasal and suffix branches

**6.4.17/18/42:** an इट् branch can remove the झलादि environment.
तितनिषति, क्रमित्वा and सिसनिषति cannot inherit an action that required
the unaugmented consonant-initial suffix. Actual ल्यप् also preempts the
क्त्वा lengthening route.

**6.4.38:** the retained म् roots have optional loss, while other admitted
nasal-final roots have obligatory loss under the source's व्यवस्थितविभाषा.
**39** distinguishes क्तिच् from क्तिन् and blocks both nasal loss and
competing lengthening. **45** adds the specific सन्/क्तिच् branches
सातिः, सतिः and सन्तिः. These source constructions are reused specifications;
they add no newly completed finite positions or new numbered IDs beyond 45.
