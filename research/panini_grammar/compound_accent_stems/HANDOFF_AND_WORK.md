## W4. Required implementation properties

1. Retain source policy, compound tree, original case token, root identity,
   markers and accent structure. Missing decisive evidence stays Open.
2. Keep preserved accent, new initial/final accent and simultaneous accent
   structures distinct. A nested last member can contain multiple accents.
3. Represent 148's two restrictions and 110/169's dependent choices directly;
   never replace them with a single unrestricted optional flag.
4. Preserve कप् ownership and the 174 one-vowel fallback policy; parse 198's
   excluded ending as क्र, not a pair of individual consonant classes.
5. Treat अलुक् as retention of an existing सुप् token. Compound admission and
   case-sensitive later queries remain independent obligations.
6. Keep masculine treatment, shortening and unexpressed-gender analyses as
   distinct histories, including the third form at 6.3.45.
7. Distinguish suffix identity from visible suffix residue: घञ्/अच् बन्ध,
   क्तिन्/क्तिच्, तृन्/तृच्, खित्, यक्/श्यन् and ङी/ऊङ् are not interchangeable.
8. Put मुम्, अम्, नुट् and आदुक् on their stated owners. Keep both origins
   of replacements such as सतुक्क छ्; permit root-specific segment order.
9. Evaluate 6.4.22 using event/query rule addresses, same-आश्रय evidence and
   explicit exceptions. Do not globally roll back the current derivation.
10. Resolve इट् before clauses whose current consonant-initial suffix gate
    it changes. Keep optional loss distinct from optional prohibition.
11. Require attestation for बहुलम्, आकृतिगण and दृश्यते extensions; no
    arbitrary fallback may certify an otherwise unsupported word.
12. Preserve diagnostic wrong-key source fields without treating them as
    evidence for their labelled rule. Runtime compilation and independent
    validation remain future work.
