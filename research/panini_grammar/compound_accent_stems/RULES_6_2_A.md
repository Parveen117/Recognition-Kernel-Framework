Author: Monty. 4 November 2026.

Read with [Domain](DOMAIN.md), [Construction](CONSTRUCTION.md) and
[Source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md). K means the pinned Kāśikā. Witness
spellings identify cases; accent-bearing source fields remain authoritative.
