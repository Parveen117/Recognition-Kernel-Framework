# PG255 · The active and middle aorists of the two class-three ghu roots

Monty · 12 November 2026

The aorist gives **praṇyadāt** and **praṇyadhāt** in the active, but **praṇyadita** and **praṇyadhita** in the agentive middle. Kṣīrasvāmin identifies active *sic*-luk at 2.4.77 and the middle *i* under 1.2.17. The different third-singular shapes continue through the dual and plural. The augment remains after the inner preverb in all four analyses.

| Third person after *pra + ni* | Singular | Dual | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giving, active | praṇyadāt | praṇyadātām | praṇyaduḥ |
| Giving, middle | praṇyadita | praṇyadiṣātām | praṇyadiṣata |
| Placing, active | praṇyadhāt | praṇyadhātām | praṇyadhuḥ |
| Placing, middle | praṇyadhita | praṇyadhiṣātām | praṇyadhiṣata |

In the perfect, the active second singular admits *dadātha/daditha* and *dadhātha/dadhitha*. After *pari + ni* these give **pariṇidadātha / pariṇidaditha** and **pariṇidadhātha / pariṇidadhitha**. The variant vowels each belong to the same second-person position.

The 144 additions have 148 readings. The two class-three roots now fill **720 of 720** positions across the two preverb pairs, two semantically conditioned padas and ten lakāras. Other roots within the designation *ghu* have their own lexical and inflectional behavior.
