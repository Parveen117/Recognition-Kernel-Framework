# PG168 · Five outer inflections of the named nominal actions

Monty · 11 November 2026

The named source actions from PG167 now have five further written continuations. A context-dependent ṇic history can retain -ay- before iṭ, lose ṇi before the selected an-iṭ P blessing, or select ām and a separately inflected auxiliary. This produces, from one satya input, satyāpayitā in the periphrastic future, satyāpyāt in the P blessing, and satyāpayiṣīṣṭa in the A blessing. The two blessings have different licensed result conditions.

In Raghuvaṃśa 8.33 Nārada goes to sing with a vīṇā. The literary infinitive upavīṇayituṃ has its own interval in the edition. It independently supports the -ay-i- continuation of the instrument-derived base. The table's upavīṇayitā uses finite tāsi with the same interior boundary. Its inflection is a deduction, so the infinitive has its own evidence type.

The army conditional retains retroflex ṣ and ṇ across the inner augment, as in the directly witnessed imperfect in PG167. For sam + varman, the alternate nasalized v spelling is possible only when the prefix directly meets v. All auxiliary readings and optional 2pl spellings are represented as separate history paths within their original profile and ending. The 30 selected profiles now have 2,430 of their 2,700 ten-lakāra positions. The remaining 270 require the separate caṅ reduplication of the aorist.
