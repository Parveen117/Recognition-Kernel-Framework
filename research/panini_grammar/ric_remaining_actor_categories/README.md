## Construction and ownership

**Imperfect.** Laṅ contributes aṭ rather than a preverb. Class-7 śnam follows the root vowel under 3.1.78. Strong P singular cells retain its vowel and yield *ariṇak/ariṇag* at 3sg and 2sg; their two terminal stops are boundary readings of the same key. Weak cells expose the nasal before root-final c. The successor depends on the ending: compare *ariṅktām*, *ariñcan* and *ariñcva*. A has only weak cells, but ending ownership still distinguishes *ariṅkta*, *ariñcātām* and *ariṅgdhvam*. These c/k/g/j outputs are not replaceable by the dental history used for √chid.

**Future families.** The root is aniṭ on the cited path. Guṇa gives re, and the root-final c plus sya has the kṣ readout in *rekṣyati/rekṣyate*. Lṛṅ adds aṭ and secondary endings to that future base; P 3sg alone has *arekṣyat/arekṣyad*. Luṭ meets the t-initial future-agent suffix on the k readout and yields *rektā-*. The identical P/A third-person luṭ words retain different ending schedules and actor-result premises.

**Perfect.** Reduplication gives *ri-ric*. Strong P singular cells use e in *rireca/rirecitha*; the other P cells and all A cells retain i in *riric-*. Ending-owned i in *riricire* and augment i in *riricidhve* are independent. The same P or A spelling at first and third singular remains two person keys.

**Aorist.** The lexical ir marker admits the inherited P aṅ alternative: *aricat/aricad, aricatām, arican*. P sic strengthens i to ai and exposes kṣ: *araikṣīt/araikṣīd*, with the remaining junctions recorded separately. Every P key has both branches and 3sg has two boundary readings on each, giving 20 readings over nine positions. A does not inherit the P aṅ guard. Its sic-kit path retains i and begins *arikta, arikṣātām, arikṣata*. Patient/passive ciṇ, causative and prefixed aorists require other typed inputs.

**Blessing.** P yāsuṭ gives *ricyāt/ricyād* and one reading in each other cell. A follows its distinct sīyuṭ route, beginning *rikṣīṣṭa*. Neither is PG22’s ordinary optative *riñcyāt/riñcīta*: the blessing path has no present-system śnam and retains different marker ownership.
