Raghuvaṃśa has a textual apparatus and additional suffixed or starred verses. Only its unique, plain numbered locations enter the comparison. Kirātārjunīya gives an additional analysis in a note alongside each verse. Excluding those notes prevents the analysis from supplying evidence for its own main text. In Śiśupālavadha the verse address comes from the printed MSpv label at the end of the last line, and both labels numbered 11.2 are held apart. Raghuvaṃśa's duplicated 9.65 is also excluded. A decorated Kumārasaṃbhava address is kept outside the plain-number projection. The edition register gives the affected counts.

The term “whole” reports visible boundaries only. Cross-word phonology, variant readings, meter and interpretation need linguistic evidence. Multiple sūtras can legitimately cite a single token. No grammatical decision is attached solely on the strength of a string match.
