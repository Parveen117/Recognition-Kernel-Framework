# PG837 · The iron covering in Bhāravi’s seventeenth canto

Bhāravi’s *Kirātārjunīya* 17.49 uses **tiraskāra** for a protective covering in Mallinātha’s interpretation. The comparison is between Arjuna’s own anger and an iron coat of armour. His limbs have lost their sense of pain and he disregards the mass of arrows. The anger protects him like the coat. The masculine nominative **lauhaḥ** qualifies the covering. The verse’s grammatical subject is **ātmamanyuḥ**, glossed as Arjuna’s own anger.
