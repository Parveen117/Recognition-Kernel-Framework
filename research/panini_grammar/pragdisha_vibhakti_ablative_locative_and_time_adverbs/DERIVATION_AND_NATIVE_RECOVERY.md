# PG807 · Derivation and native recovery

Rule 5.3.1 names the suffix span before 5.3.27 `vibhakti`; 5.3.2 restricts its bases to `kim`, eligible pronouns and numerical `bahu`, excluding `dvi` onward and ordinary nouns. Kāśikā and Vasu explicitly license optional case-expression in this early span (`kutaḥ/kasmāt`, `kutra/kasmin`). PG807 accepts that local statement but does not undo PG4's quarantine on automatically importing 4.1.82 `vā` across later svārthika rules.

Rules 3–6 retain whole-base substitution owners: `idam→iś`, its r/th exceptions, source-labelled `etad→aś/an`, and optional `sarva→s` before d-initial prāgdiśa suffixes. Rules 7–8 give the same four ablative surfaces two histories, direct `tasil` and replacement of later `tasi`. Rule 9 admits `pari/abhi` only in the stated all-around/both-sides senses. Rules 10–13 keep `tral`, `h` and `at` distinct, including the cited optional `kutra` and Vedic `kuha`; the conflicting Rigvedic locators remain Open.

Rule 14 is lexical, not productive for every case: the fourteen credited positions preserve the seven case contexts of displayed `bhavat` under `tatas/tatra`. Rule 15 assigns `dā` only in time; `sarvatra deśe` is a retained place control. The wider `dīrghāyus/āyuṣmat/devānāṃ priya` realization is unconstructed rather than excluded.

Reuse PG14's full carrier
\[B=C_\Sigma\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E\rangle,\quad M_\lambda=\bigoplus_{h\in\mathcal H}e_hB,\quad M_0=\bigoplus_{h\in\mathcal H}e_hC_\Sigma.\]
Type base and inner case `I`, ablative/locative/time/place relation `R`, semantic and lexical guard `Q`, suffix/substitution/accent owner `H`, case/context and ordered derivation history `h`, source `S`, output `W`, and outcome `O`. Every admitted edge obeys
\[T_{e,\lambda}(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a,\qquad \pi_{0,M}T_{e,\lambda}=T_{e,0}\pi_{0,M}.\]
Thus zero specialization preserves direct versus replacement suffix history, the source-labelled substitutions, case context, Vedic register and time/place guard instead of identifying equal surface strings.
