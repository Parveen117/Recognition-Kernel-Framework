# PG683 · Seven written lakāras

The word is the indicated prefix followed by the printed cell.
For example A laṭ 3sg gives *saṃjigaṃsate* and *sañjigaṃsate*.
P laṅ 3sg gives *samajigamiṣat*. The two laṭ A 3sg spellings
are direct grammatical examples, and the other forms are
written rule compositions. They use an accent-free display.

There are 18 request keys in each block. The five consonant-contact
blocks give 180 readings over 90 keys. The two vowel-contact blocks
give 36 readings over 36 keys. **126 keys and 216 readings**.
The source controls in older rule registers were evidence for rule
scope, with no matching finite panel credit. No bare gam, iṅ-origin,
PG682 or caṅ key is counted here.
