## The two iṭ queries and the two nasals

Kāśikā 7.2.58 excludes the special obligatory iṭ with same-word taṅ.
The witnessed intransitive motion profile gives the aniṭ route. Its
s-initial san meets the consonantal condition at 6.4.16, but lexical

*adhijigāṃsate* has the iṅ-origin history and its own long vowel. Some
grammarians also explain other specified vowel-final substitution

On the P destination request 7.2.58 gives short iṭ to san. It
changes the actual immediate environment and causes the affix s→ṣ
by 8.3.57/59. Root m is before a vowel and stays m. In the A request
root m is before san-s and gives ṃ by 8.3.24. That ṃ stays before s.

The san n is a marker under 1.3.3/9 and its a remains part of the
stem: **jigaṃsa** for A, **jigamiṣa** for P. The copy's i differs
from inner iṭ. The Kāśikā separately reports a Pādaśeṣa reading
with a different range for 7.2.58. Its named nonfinite forms and
range remain separate. It supplies no additional finite choice on
these two requests.

## The reported Vedic comparison

Kāśikā 6.4.16 cites *samajigāṃsat* with a transitive heavenly-world
destination and long root ā. Its explanation invokes the
*anyeṣām api dṛśyate* clause (canonical 6.3.137). Padamañjarī
also reports an augmentless form and attributes P to the transitive
construction. Its account treats the absent iṭ and absent aṭ as
Vedic. Preserve the difference in reported copy spelling and the
augmented report. This is a separately addressed comparison. The
Classical P panel has short a, inner iṭ and temporal aṭ where admitted.
The exact Vedic source locus and distribution of these readings are
still Open, and the grammatical quotation receives no word credit.
