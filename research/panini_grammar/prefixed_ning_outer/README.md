### Interface eligibility

The Kāśikā on 3.1.20 makes the ṇ marker available for the general ṇi rules. In the four non-perfect requests, the ordinary 7.2.35 iṭ before a val-initial outer formation keeps 6.4.51 from deleting ṇi. Under 7.3.84 and 6.1.78 its i appears as ay before that vowel. The lexical ṇiṅ history remains the marker holder. The former-object karmavat intervention studied at PG94 requires a different event frame. These five requests express their source-licensed actors. A middle entitlement alone cannot supply the missing karmavat premise.

For the perfect, the derived stem takes ām under 3.1.35. Rule 3.1.40 supplies the selected auxiliaries. The 1.3.63 treatment applies to kṛ under this primary A entitlement. The junction after ām has its own two options before kṛ and bhū. It is independent of the optional nasal at the earlier sam prefix. For example *sambhāṇḍayāmbabhūva* uses the m realization at both boundaries. The ten readings of sam + bhāṇḍa in the 3sg perfect consist of two prefix junctions times five auxiliary realizations.

### Native readout

Pair each action and prefix with the ordered choices for the primary stem, optional initial augment, and final realization. Keep both nasal sites separately addressable. Under the zero reading the factor concatenation gives each written word. Conversely each written word has the indicated prefix and ending history and at least one licensed action. Contracting a prefix junction and an auxiliary junction to one shared switch would falsely remove readings. Lṛṅ has no prefix nasal option before the vowel augment. The 2pl blessing can have dh or ḍh at its ending independently of the prefix.
