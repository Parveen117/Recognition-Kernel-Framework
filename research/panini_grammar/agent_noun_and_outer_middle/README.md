### When the outer middle is assigned

The Bhāṣya on 7.2.36 gives *prasnavitrīyate*, ‘behaves like one who flows forth’. It calls the outer middle external to the inner decision. Padamañjarī 7.2.58 applies that comparison to *jigamiṣitrīyate*. Tattvabodhinī 7.2.59 explicitly says that the subsequent taṅ leaves the earlier prohibition in force in *vivṛtsitrīyate*. The noun of comparison is the ṛ-final agent stem. Rule 7.4.27 gives rī before the y-initial formation, as in the Kāśikā’s *pitrīyate*. This accounts for the *trī* sequence without requiring a feminine agent. Rule 3.1.11 licenses kyaṅ for behaviour resembling the agent, and its ṅ supplies the outer middle.
