# Domain, source policy and proof

## Typed input

A term beginning with $ is a variable scoped to a single analysis. Reusing $x expresses identity between agent and beneficiary. Other strings are lemma constants; an incorporated standard is a lexical type, without an assumed individual referent. A case-marked participant may bind a variable once its syntactic role is supplied. Unbound variables intentionally survive. The framed relation can be read in either direction; an OUTSIDE_PACKET result makes no judgment on unattested Sanskrit generally.

## Source and normalization

Read Kāśikā 3.1.8–11 and 7.4.34 for the lexical items, and PG130 for the two independent ordinary water-purpose witnesses. Distinguish clauses printed together from separately normalized phrases: the crow example supplies the actor in an analytic comparison, and the inflected attachment is shown here in normalized order. In kumudaṃ puṣkarāyate the continuous form reflects the word boundary; the nominal lemma and separated nominative kumudam survive independently. The locative comparison is explicitly the commentary extension at 3.1.10. General accusative 2.3.2 and locative 2.3.36 are under the 2.3.1 anabhihita domain. The supplied finite event is kartari. A bare -am string alone cannot select a patient.

## Native extension and specialization

Let F be the finite analysis basis with its lexical, source, affix, intended relation and binding history. Let R be the ordinary typed predicate relation, W the normalized word relation. Add incidence arrows F→R and F→W to the inherited full operator carrier, keeping their source, complementary evidence and converse. A clause analysis is a fiber restricted by the supplied word, role bindings and compatible sense conditions. On each fact, binding the declared variables and then projecting gives the same argument tuple as restricting the projected relation by the same bindings. This follows coordinate by coordinate because the map retains each variable's identity and role. Erasing the role before applying the guard can change that result, as the pupil and water examples show.

The converse reports all retained facts, and recognizes the two different ways in which the finite map fails to be one-to-one. Support union and the finite incidence pairing are defined. No probability distribution, general parser, unrestricted word-order analysis or physical clock is constructed here.
