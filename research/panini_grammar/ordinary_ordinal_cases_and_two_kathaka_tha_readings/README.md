**Agreement on a supplied completing member.** The [ending table](ENDINGS.tsv) completes the remaining twelve ordinary PG791 profiles across seven cases, three numbers and three genders. The literal left pieces below concatenate with every entry in its gender/number row. A vertical bar separates complete alternative endings. At O20 apply both left pieces to the same address. For example, O13 F DATsg is trayodaśyai, O20 N NOMdu is viṃśe or viṃśatitame, and TG M ACCpl is gaṇatithān. A plural adjective refers to several qualifying bearers, for example the fifth members of several ordered groups. It does not make several individuals the unique fifth member of a single series.
