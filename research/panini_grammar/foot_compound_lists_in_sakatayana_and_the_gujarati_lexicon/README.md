# PG1000 · Three spellings of the foot-compound head and the shorter supadī

Monty · 27 November 2026

Two printed lists bear directly on the open K139 variants. Tripāṭhī's Śākaṭāyana-vyākaraṇam (Bhāratīya Jñānapīṭha, 1971), appendix p. 474, gives a *kumbhapadyādi* list keyed to **1.3.10** of that school. The Sanskrit–Gujarati Śabdaratnamahodadhi, part I, p. 622, gives its own inventory under *kumbhapadyādi*. The Gujarati prose identifies it as a word group used in Pāṇinian grammar. This is evidence for what each edition prints. The tables preserve an exact form before any comparison of grammatical operations or lexical meanings. The Gujarati page gives a separate lexical entry for *kumbhapadī*. It explicitly glosses a woman affected by *ślīpada*, a disease characterized there by enlargement of the foot, and prints an analysis using ṅīp. PG628 already owns the bounded pathological meaning. This positive gloss is specific to the entry immediately above the list. The list's entries receive no such group gloss. The source evidence for *kumbhapadī* does not interpret *munipadī*, *sūcīpadī* or any of the other open heads.
