# PG838 · Halāyudha’s seven words for disrespect

Halāyudha places **atyākāra, paribhava, nikāra, parābhava, anādara, abhibhava, tiraskāra** together in a single verse. Aufrecht’s 1861 edition numbers it **book IV, verse 19**, printed p. 75. The Vārāṇasī edition reproduced by Cologne numbers it **704**, p. 81. This closes the primary work and verse for the Halāyudha attribution at Vācaspatyam L22789. The words *parābhava* and *tiraskāra* also give a lexical parallel for the disputed Mādhavīya example. The verse cites no policy for gati or 8.3.42.
