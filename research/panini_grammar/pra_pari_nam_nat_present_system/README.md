# PG219 · Pra and pari distinguish the teaching initials of nam and naṭ

Monty · 12 November 2026

**Praṇaṭati** and **pranāṭayati** begin alike in the unprefixed spelling *naṭ*, yet the prefix exposes different histories. In its discussion under 7.4.68 the Tattvabodhinī gives three explicit forms. Its *praṇaṭati* is the basic verb, *praṇaṭayati* is the causative of the short-vowel entry and *pranāṭayati* has dental *n* in the long-vowel curādi construction. This last entry is explicitly excepted in the discussion of 6.1.65. The separate class-one causative is retained here as a source comparison.

| Dhātupāṭha input | *pra*, present P | *pari*, present P | *pra*, imperfect P | *pari*, imperfect P |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *ṇam*, bow | praṇamati | pariṇamati | prāṇamat | paryaṇamat |
| *ṇaṭ*, two class-one addresses | praṇaṭati | pariṇaṭati | prāṇaṭat | paryaṇaṭat |
| *naṭ*, curādi performance | pranāṭayati | parināṭayati | prānāṭayat | paryanāṭayat |

The Kāśikā on 8.4.14 directly supplies *praṇamati* and *pariṇamati*. It also distinguishes *praṇāyakaḥ* ‘a leader’ from *pranāyako deśaḥ* ‘a country whose leaders have gone away’. For the country, *pra* belongs to the understood action of going. Mere adjacency to *nāyaka* gives the wrong result. For the imperfects the augment comes between the prefix and root, giving *prā* or *parya*.

The four lexical addresses give five inherited pada-profiles per prefix. All four present-system lakāras yield **360 positions**. The lexical entries and five source-to-cell links are recorded separately. In Bhāgavata 11.29.16, running *praṇamed* supplies the P optative *praṇamet*. In 10.64.42 the same text has **ahaṃ praṇame viprān**, an active worshipper using the middle ending. Its own source-conditioned reading falls outside the 360 positions and is retained for the next voice comparison.
