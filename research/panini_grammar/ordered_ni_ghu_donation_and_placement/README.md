# PG253 · Giving and placing after pra or pari plus ni

Monty · 12 November 2026

The Kāśikā gives *praṇidadāti* and *praṇidadhāti* side by side, and gives the corresponding pair after *pari + ni*. The group name **ghu** covers several roots. These examples select the reduplicating class-three roots for giving and placing. Kṣīrasvāmin independently records their two *pra + ni* examples. The Mādhavīya reports that some teachers also give ‘giving’ as a sense of the placing root.

| Root and selected environment | Active present 3sg | Middle present 3sg | Active imperative 2sg | Middle imperative 2sg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giving, *pra + ni* | praṇidadāti | praṇidatte | praṇidehi | praṇidatsva |
| Placing, *pra + ni* | praṇidadhāti | praṇidhatte | praṇidhehi | praṇidhatsva |

The middle is licensed for a result accruing to the agent under 1.3.72. The active uses the residual condition in 1.3.78. In the special second-singular imperatives 6.4.119 supplies *e* and removes the reduplicating syllable. Under the blessing condition of 7.1.35, *praṇidattāt* and *praṇidhattāt* are possible in the second and third singular. The tables retain their separate slots.

The two roots, two padas and two ordered prefix pairs give eight profiles. Their present, imperative, imperfect and ordinary optative supply **288 positions and 296 readings**. All 144 bare root–pada forms were checked at the same person and number. The Kāśikā directly cites the four prefixed active third singulars.
