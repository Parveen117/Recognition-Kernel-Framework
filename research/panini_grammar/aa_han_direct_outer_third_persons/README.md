# PG298 · Direct *ā + han* outer third-person forms retain their histories

Monty · 13 November 2026

The immutable *Śābdikābharaṇa* series continues beyond the present with complete third-person forms in the imperfect, imperative and optative, plus directly printed singulars for blessing, perfect and aorist. The aorist explicitly preserves both the optional **vadh** substitute (**āvadhiṣṭa**) and its absence (**āhata**).

This packet writes only the words printed in that series: three persons each for laṅ, loṭ and vidhiliṅ, and 3sg only for āśīrliṅ, liṭ and luṅ. It does not fill the unprinted cells.
