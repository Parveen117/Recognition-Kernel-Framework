### Resolve a printed p by checking the word and its meaning

At 2.123 the primary Sanskrit field writes **निपद्वरः** in sandhi and glosses the word as mire. Its sūtra gives *ni* with *sad*. Uṇādikośa 2.122 has **निषद्वरः**, explains the same mire sense, and gives the feminine **निषद्वरी** for night. Monier-Williams L110689 and L110691 and Apte L19280 and L19280.002 separately record the retroflex spelling and those senses. We select **niṣadvara** on that evidence. The literal primary *p* remains available for edition comparison. It supplies no alternative productive sound rule.
