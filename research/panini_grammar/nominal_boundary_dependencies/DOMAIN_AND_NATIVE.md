# Domain and specialization

## N1. Carrier records a dependency

Extend the inherited full carrier by a source-labelled dependency edge from suffix y to the v substitution, or by a padatva → n-lopa edge for rājan. Rai has neither edge. The later request also records role and outer continuation. Printed consonants alone do not determine the edge type.

## N2. Run each operation against its proper input

The morphological y-loss query sees the supplier of v. The later sound-reduction query sees adjacent y after the visible v. For rājan, the preceding vowel operation sees n-lopa via its local 8.2.2 condition. These three observations use different projections of a shared ordered path.
