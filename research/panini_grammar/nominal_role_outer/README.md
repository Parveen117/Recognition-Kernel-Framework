# PG129 — Kāmyac and the six outer continuations

Monty · 11 November 2026

The P blessing putrakāmyāt retains the source affix kāmyac in its morphology. Its y can coincide with that of the blessing in the sound readout. Preserving the owner of that y separates this case from the optional morphological ky loss on samidh. The eight profiles of [PG128](../nominal_role_relations/README.md) now have all ten selected lakāras.
