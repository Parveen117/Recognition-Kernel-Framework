sārvadhātuka ending. Its ś/p markers remain owned after lopa. Root short

is introduced. The interface is **car|a**, with the latter a thematic.

\[

B is the qualified car interface; C also includes initial aṭ. Prefix
contact is applied as H08 specifies. Laṭ 1sg uses ending-i→e and 6.1.97;

**uccareyātām**, distinct from loṭ uccaretām. First dual/plural ā is
the thematic/ending seam, not root vowel lengthening.

## H07. Blessing, contracted perfect and seṭ sic

Blessing is ārdhadhātuka. A sīyuṭ takes short iṭ; its actual vowel-initial
interface does not meet 1.2.11–13's jhal requirements. With Z=cariṣ,
reuse PG20's owned A map

\[
B_A(Z,\delta)=(Zīṣṭa,Zīyāstām,Zīran,Zīṣṭhāḥ,Zīyāsthām,
Zī\delta vam,Zīya,Zīvahi,Zīmahi).
\]

Sīyuṭ ī is not long iṭ. Singular suṭ follows ī and gives ṣṭ/ṣṭh;
dual suṭ follows ā and remains st/sth. The first-person a is the ending
substitute under 3.4.106, not initial aṭ. No present śap remains.

For **liṭ**, all A endings are apit; car is not saṃyogānta, so 1.2.5
makes them kit. Copy the root under 6.1.8 and retain **ca|car** after
7.4.60. Initial c is already palatal: there is no g→j or other changed
copy initial. Internal a lies between single c/r, so **6.4.120** gives
root a→e and separately deletes the abhyāsa: **cer**, not *cacer*.
Kāśikā's anādeśādi and same-form discussion supports this condition;
this e is prescribed by 6.4.120, not ordinary guṇa of root a.

The full perfect tuple is

\[
(cere,cerāte,cerire,ceriṣe,cerāthe,ceri\delta ve,cere,cerivahe,cerimahe).
\]

Root-facing iṭ occurs only at 2sg/2pl/1du/1pl. Irec's 3pl i/r belongs
to the ending, and equal 3sg/1sg e retains eś versus original-i→e
provenance. The copy-deletion history survives; no extra cacar branch
is added to these kit A endings.

For **luṅ**, cli→sic under 3.1.43–44 uses the inherited enunciative-i,
c-marker policy. No named alternative cli-substitution premise is present
for this basic unmarked root. All positions take iṭ; 1.2.11–13 does not
make this interface kit. P-only 7.2.2 vṛddhi is false, so the root remains
**car**, not cār. PG32 L2 gives the unaugmented tuple

\[
(cariṣṭa,cariṣātām,cariṣata,cariṣṭhāḥ,cariṣāthām,
cari\delta vam,cariṣi,cariṣvahi,cariṣmahi).
\]

There is no A ending īṭ. Before ta/thās, affix-owned i is not a
short-vowel-final root satisfying 8.2.27; 8.2.26/28 likewise do not delete
sic-s. At 2pl only, 8.2.25 removes s before dhvam. Third plural has
jha→ata; final 1sg i is its original ending.

At **2pl in these three labels**, iṭ directly follows root r, an iṇ
sound. **8.3.79** gives δ={dh,ḍh}; there is no aniṭ path to receive
8.3.78 alone. Each of these paradigms therefore has ten readings on nine
positions. All seven H05/H06 labels retain dental dh: the three-label
restriction of 8.3.78–79 cannot be extended merely because r or iṭ occurs.

## H08. Ud+c contact, augment intervention and source-key mismatch

Retain the UD prefix identity across the source's ut citation and the
voiced ud composition stage; 8.2.39 supplies the voiced dental padānta
stage, which stays d before initial aṭ. At actual **ud|car/cer**
contact, **8.4.40** gives d→j before c and **8.4.55** gives j→c:
the result starts **ucc-**. Both c occurrences retain separate owners.
Kāśikā supplies the contact rule; Bālamanoramā 8.4.40's saccit account
explicitly shows this ordered dental→palatal→unvoiced route.

Laṅ/lṛṅ/luṅ instead have **ud|aṭ|car**, so d and c are not adjacent.
The start is **udacar-**, not *uccar-* with a past ending attached.
Reuse Bālamanoramā 1.3.17's aṭ-intervention adapter for the contextual
prefix licence. Sam likewise gives selected **saṃcar-/saṃcer-** without
augmentation, but **samacar-** when m precedes initial aṭ. Perfect's
abhyāsa lopa precedes the final prefix/cer contact; its history is not erased.

The literal **Bālamanoramā key 84055** is headed **शरोऽचि**, the text
of 8.4.49, and continues into nominal examples. It is not commentary on
khari ca merely because the electronic key is 84055. Preserve that field
as a source-key mismatch, with zero authority for the present devoicing
step. Use the independently matching sūtrapāṭha, Kāśikā/Kaumudī 8.4.55
and Bālamanoramā 8.4.40. No silent archive repair is performed.

Retain inherited pausal visarga, final-n protection of ran and the ordered
ant→anusvāra→dental-n route before stops. Root r therefore does not trigger
a second earlier ṇatva pass on late n. Accent, optional sound doubling and
alternative nasal spellings are outside the selected spelling policy.
