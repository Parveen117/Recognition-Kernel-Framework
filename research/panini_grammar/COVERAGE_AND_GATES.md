## Completion means closing obligations, not loading text

Use distinct evidence states for each rule or family:

1. **Catalogued:** exact text and identifier exist in a pinned witness.
2. **Interpreted:** grammatical reading, anuvṛtti, adhikāra and commentary
   choices are explicit; disputed readings are retained as such.
3. **Domain closed:** lexical/semantic premises, all relevant exclusions,
   interacting scopes and accepted alternatives are identified for a stated
   language domain. A Classical scope must not be presented as all Vedic usage.
4. **Derivationally reviewed:** licensed traces and blocked near-cases have
   been checked against primary commentary or identified attestation.
5. **Implemented:** a later program realizes that reviewed relation and
   returns explicit unsupported/ambiguous outcomes outside it.
6. **Validated:** output forms, derivation paths and source provenance have
   been compared with independent evidence on a declared test domain.

PG1 completes the catalogue and a foundational specification, with selected
interpreted examples. It does not assign stages 3–6 to the entire inventory.

Within each package, prepare the grammar and derivations first. Avoid a fresh
finite program whose hard-coded outputs merely conceal unclosed source
questions. Independent comparison can reveal differences; agreement with a
second program alone is not proof of historical derivational accuracy.

## Gates for a later implementation

- The domain is explicitly declared: Classical/Vedic scope, morphology versus
  analysis, available lexica and optionality policy.
- Each admitted rule has the reading contract in [GRAMMAR_SPEC.md](GRAMMAR_SPEC.md).
  No unresolved context is silently treated as false or as a wildcard.
- Provenance survives it-lopa, whole-affix lopa and substitution, while each
  rule receives only the history it is licensed to consult.
- Regression examples include **am retaining m**, **bhavanti rather than
  \*bhavānti**, the internal/external boundary distinction, optional pause
  forms and a same-surface/different-affix case.
- A correct final word and a correct derivation path are evaluated separately;
  T66's path discrepancy must not be hidden by an oracle match.
- Missing lexical entries, undecided readings, several licensed analyses and
  resource exhaustion produce different result statuses.
- The full-family/classical specialization contract is shown on the admitted
  domain before a linguistic rule is reused as a native operator application.

Only then should the quantum-calculator application be designed against that
declared contract. No claim about physics, a physical clock or the resolution
of the repository's open mathematical problems is made by this grammar work.
