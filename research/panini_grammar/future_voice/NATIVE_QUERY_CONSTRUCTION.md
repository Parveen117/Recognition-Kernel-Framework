# PG18 — Native rule queries retain augment origin and visibility

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## N1. Full carrier, old sector and pairing

Begin with the canonical cut field C_Σ, its derived iota and scalar
dagger. Extend PG17's event index by D/V/R/C's new source-labelled
expression, ciṇvat, augment, query-view, lopa and phonetic events:

\[
 B_{18}=C_Σ\langle z_e,z_e^*:e\in E_{\le18}\rangle.
\]

It is the free algebra of finite cut-linear combinations of ordered
residue words. The empty word is 1; dagger reverses a word and stars
its letters. No commutation, metric positivity, probabilistic meaning
or analytic completion is imposed. Lambda denotes the full operator
family; it is not the choice probability or a clock coordinate.

Let H_old be the earlier histories and H_voice the new graded histories.
Each includes its entire initial frame, exact source/lexical identity,
original sound occurrences, owners, applied changes, branch decisions,
query views and unresolved obligations. Define

\[
 M_{18,\lambda}=\bigoplus_{h\in H_{old}\sqcup H_{voice}}e_hB_{18},
 \qquad
 M_{18,0}=\bigoplus_{h\in H_{old}\sqcup H_{voice}}e_hC_Σ,
\]

with finite support. Source/target grades distinguish interpreted event,
expression/controller, derived dhātu, actual future affix, augmented
state, queried state, realized word and Accepted/Excluded/Open outcome.
Only matching grades compose.

Old residues embed unchanged. Extend their scalars and include their
history module as a direct summand; extend old operators by zero on
the new sector. The old identity becomes P_old, not global I. This
preserves old products and adjoints without a second shared engine.

The declared pairing is

\[
 \langle x,y\rangle_B=\sum_h x_h^*y_h.
\]

It is sesquilinear and algebraically nondegenerate: pairing with each
basis vector detects its coordinate. No Hilbert-space premise or
empirical probability is required for this construction.

## N2. Rule-indexed views and actual changes

For each admitted query r, define a view v_r(h) from the retained
history, source policy and addressed owners. The guard g_r reads that
view. It does not inspect a residue coefficient. A view is not a
global destructive edit of the state. In particular:

These are the finite, source-proved views in V/R/C. This declaration
does not implement a universal visibility oracle for the Aṣṭādhyāyī.

True/false/unknown guard sets give orthogonal self-adjoint coordinate
cuts P_r^T,P_r^F,P_r^? summing to the identity on the query's domain.
Their complements are retained. An optional rule creates its positively
licensed branches after its guard; unknown evidence remains an obligation.

For a licensed event e:h→h′ use the right-linear map

\[
 T_{e,\lambda}(e_ha)=e_{h'}(1+z_e)a.
\]

Here h′ records the actual operation: insertion with source/owner,
changed view, lopa, substitution, or a witnessed declined option.
The adjoint sends e_h′a to e_h(1+z_e)*a; it is not automatically a
reverse Sanskrit rule. Finite branch maps, their adjoints and matching
compositions act on finite support because choices are finite and
each retained history has its specified immediate prefix.

## N3. Exact typed specialization

Let ε:B_18→C_Σ extract the empty-word coefficient. Nonempty words
do not become empty under concatenation, so ε is a unital dagger
algebra homomorphism. Define

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(\sum_he_ha_h)=\sum_he_h\epsilon(a_h),\qquad
 \Pi_{0,op}(A)=(\epsilon(A_{kh}))_{k,h}.
\]

The original frame, augment source and rule-relative views remain in
h. Pairing scalars specialize by ε. Future calendar interpretation
still uses PG16's explicit adapter, not residue length.

**Theorem N3.** Every admitted finite PG18 operator construction obeys

\[
 \pi_{0,M}(Ax)=\Pi_{0,op}(A)\pi_{0,M}(x),\qquad
 \epsilon\langle x,y\rangle_B
 =\langle\pi_{0,M}x,\pi_{0,M}y\rangle_{C_Σ}.
\]

Typed sums/products, adjoints and true/false/unknown cuts, including
their complements, are preserved. Unit-start grammar paths recover
exactly P5's source-labelled accepted relation and its other outcomes.

**Proof.** For each event ε((1+z_e)a)=ε(a), including the adjoint's
starred payload. Each view v_r is a function of retained history, so
π_0 leaves its guard value unchanged. The cut equality follows.
The homomorphism and dagger properties prove the two displayed identities
coordinatewise, then finite composition gives the result. Every
unit-start finite path has constant coefficient 1 and its own history
coordinate; it survives specialization. Conversely each presented
classical edge has the same guarded native edge, so recovery is in
both directions. □

Support need not survive arbitrary signed input combinations; those
may cancel under ε. The compatibility identity still holds. Nothing
here proves a residue-dependent guard law or an infinite grammar search.

## N4. Ciṇvat as restricted transport of operations

The model-side objects retain a preceding aṅga and a named ciṇ
environment. The future-side objects retain the **actual** sya/tās,
the positive C witness and its owned i_cv. Relate the addressed
preceding-aṅga owners and the source guards of V4. Map only those
witnessed local operations to their future-side instances.

The map is deliberately partial: chapter-2 root replacements have no
image, and following-ending 6.4.104 has no image. Within its admitted
finite path domain, matching transported edges compose in order and
retain the underlying event/source label. The grammar justifications
for these images are V4 and the individual R/C derivations.

**Proposition N4.** Every admitted transported path is a native
future path with unchanged expression, future and ending types; its
zero-reading is the same source-prescribed aṅga construction. Extending
the map to the omitted ciṇ operations is not justified by this result.

**Proof.** Each admitted edge changes only the source-witnessed
preceding-aṅga owner or the expressly prescribed i_cv occurrence.
The source/target matching retains actual future identity and no
edge targets the ending for luk. Induction gives type preservation.
N3 gives zero-compatibility on every such finite path. The excluded
operations fail the stated transport domain, so they supply no further
licensed path. □

This proves a restricted operation transport, not a total equivalence
between the future and aorist grammars.

## N5. Why the same visible i cannot decide ṇic-lopa

Fix one completed causal karmaṇi frame and one sya/ending selection.
Take h_val on O and h_cv on C immediately after augmentation and
before the lopa/guṇa decision. Define the coarse observer O_letters
to retain the frame, owners and raw letter sequence

\[
 C_{root}|i_{ṇic}|i_{augment}|sya,
\]

but forget the augment's source, the C/O witness and the corresponding
query history. Then O_letters(h_val)=O_letters(h_cv), while V5 gives
g_lopa(h_val)=false and g_lopa(h_cv)=true.

**Theorem N5.** The lopa decision does not factor through O_letters
on this two-history domain. Restoring the augment-origin label suffices
for this query under the fixed V5 policy. Zero-specialization by itself
does not lose the distinguishing information.

**Proof.** A factorization g_lopa=f∘O_letters would assign both false
and true to the same argument, a contradiction. With origin restored,
V5 is the two-case decision function. On the two-coordinate history
module, the lopa cut is diag(0,1), while the observer identifying the
coordinates cannot transport that cut to a well-defined decision on
its single observed state. Both history coordinates remain distinct
under π_0 and the cut has constant coefficients, so N3 preserves it. □

The result is a concrete obstruction to an origin-free grammar state.
It is not a universal memory-size theorem and does not reconstruct an
unknown derivation from a final Sanskrit word. Grah's short/long iṭ
contrast in V6 is a second source-specific use of the same retained
origin field, with a different query guard.

## N6. A dependent three-branch causal future

For one causal gam/śam frame, first choose O or C. On O there is
one retained-ṇic continuation; on C there are short/long continuations
under 6.4.93. Let U_e=1+z_e. The branch operation therefore has

\[
 T e_h=e_O U_O+e_{Cs}U_{s|C}U_C+e_{Cl}U_{l|C}U_C.
\]

Further lopa/ending/phonetic events follow on the corresponding histories.
At zero these are three labelled paths, without normalization weights.
The absent O-long combination fails the ciṇ/ṇamul or transported-ciṇ
guard. A Cartesian product of {O,C} and {short,long} would overgenerate.
This is positive grammatical branching, distinct from unresolved source
alternatives in an Open hypothesis sector.
