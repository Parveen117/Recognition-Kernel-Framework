Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

## P1. Two nine-slot constructors and the Bhāva restriction

The order of components is (3sg,3du,3pl,2sg,2du,2pl,1sg,1du,1pl).
Using PG16 E2/E3/E5 with R7/C8 gives

\[
 L_A(B)=(Bate,Bete,Bante,Base,Bethe,Badhve,Be,Bāvahe,Bāmahe),
\]
\[
 U_A(H)=(Hā,Hārau,Hāraḥ,Hāse,Hāsāthe,Hādhve,Hāhe,Hāsvahe,Hāsmahe).
\]

B omits the final thematic a; H is the lawful final luṭ factor.
These are exact readout formulas, not a suffix table that bypasses the
owned derivation. In particular luṭ A has -āsāthe, -ādhve and
-āsvahe under the retained PG16 source policies.

For Bhāva define the **restricted** constructors L_B(B)=Bate and
U_B(H)=Hā. Only these scalar entries are licensed on the Bhāva domain.
Writing L_A/U_A for a morphological interface does not authorize its
other eight slots there. All registers retain the exact root IDs in D4/C6.

## P5. Coverage and the bounded construction theorem

| Sector | Positions before morphological options | Branch readings |
|---|---:|---:|
| Four basic Bhāva entries, two futures, 3sg only | 8 | 13 |
| Twelve basic Karma entries, two futures, nine slots | 216 | 378 |
| Thirteen causal Karma entries, two futures, nine slots | 234 | 504 |
| Total | **458** | **895** |

There are **98 nine-slot branch rows plus 13 single-slot rows**:
98·9+13=895. The basic sector uses sixteen exact entries. The causal
sector reuses twelve earlier frames and adds one śam frame. Counting
formations separately does not count the same underlying root as a new
lexical discovery. Counts are not distinct words, independent quotations,
all possible semantic histories, or new software executions.

**Theorem P5.** Relative to D's domain and the declared source/sound
policies, the register specifies every admitted morphological branch
and agreement slot, and each has the owned derivation in V/R/C. Typed
native zero-specialization recovers the same labelled relation.

**Proof.** D6 exhausts expression/controllers. V7 exhausts C/O and the
coupled augment decisions. R2/R7 exhaust the basic interfaces and their
ending extension; C6/C8 do so for causal frames, including the two
conditional mit length options. P2 lists every restricted Bhāva cell;
P3/P4 list the forty-two and fifty-six complete nine-slot rows. P1
defines every component. N3 supplies exact typed zero-recovery. Conversely
an admitted frame must enter one of these partitions and rows, so no
additional cell is left unnamed inside the selected domain. □

This is a written factorization proof, not an independently executed
895-case experiment or a percentage of all Sanskrit grammar.
