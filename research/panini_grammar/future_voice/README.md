# PG18 — Bhāva/karma futures and source-sensitive ciṇvat

Author: Monty. 2 November 2026.

PG18 constructs lṛṭ/luṭ in **bhāva and karmaṇi**, continuing the
PG16–PG17 futures and PG15's causal role selection. It includes sixteen
basic lexical entries and thirteen causal frames: twelve inherited ones
plus class-4 śam. The selected domain has **458 morphological positions**
and **895 factorized written branch readings**, specified by 98 complete
nine-slot rows and thirteen Bhāva single-slot rows. These are written
source-relative constructions, not executed cases or distinct-word counts.

The central new dependency is the **origin of iṭ**. Ordinary iṭ keeps
causative ṇic before these futures; the ciṇvat augment of 6.4.62 is
invisible to the specified 6.4.51 query, allowing ṇic-lopa while the
augment survives in the output. Grah separately distinguishes ordinary
long ī from ciṇvat short i. A grammar state that forgets that source
cannot make both decisions correctly on the constructed domain.

## Completion boundary

The domain is classical, unprefixed, unaccented and finite, with the
stated non-doubling sound profile. Bhāva does **not** get an artificial
nine-cell person/number table. Karmaṇi does not copy its agreement from
the unexpressed agent or the causer. Future morphology does not insert
yak first, and ciṇvat does not replace the future by a complete aorist
procedure.

The adopted 6.4.22/tāsi/8.2.25 source policies remain explicit. The
construction is not a universal adjudication of visibility or all ciṇvat
uses. Wider roots, prefixes, san/yaṅ, nested causatives, accent and
runtime integration remain open. No whole-grammar completion percentage
is inferred from overlapping stage counts.

Python grammar/navigation tests and certificate replay remain deferred
under the owner's mathematics-first instruction. Historical certificates,
PG1–PG17 source packets, the companion branch and the shared engine are
unchanged by this addition.
