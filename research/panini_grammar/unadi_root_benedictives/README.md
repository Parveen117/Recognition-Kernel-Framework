# PG215 · Benedictive ā and e and the four middle second plurals

Monty · 12 November 2026

The fifteen PG206 root–pada profiles gain **135 benedictive positions with 157 readings**. The paired roots *styai* and *ṣṭyai* allow **styāyāt** and **styeyāt** under 6.4.68. The difference between ā and e is a choice within each root. It does not distinguish the two root identities.

| Input, active blessing | Third singular | Governing condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The two *styai* roots | styāyāt / styeyāt | Non-śit gives root-final ā; conjunct onset, kit blessing and exclusion from the compulsory list permit optional e. |
| *sthā*, as a source control | stheyāt | Included in the preceding compulsory list. |
| *yā*, as a source control | yāyāt | Simple onset; the optional rule requires a cluster. |
| *nam* | namyāt | P *yāsuṭ* without outer iṭ. The *m* remains before *y*. |

On the four middle profiles the āśīrliṅ has outer iṭ followed by a separate long ī in the blessing ending. At second plural both dental and retroflex dh are available: *māhiṣīdhvam / māhiṣīḍhvam*, for example. For *māh* the sound before iṭ is *h*; for the three curādi profiles it is *y*. Across all 135 cells the 157 selected words occur in the comparison archive. The 13 extra archive spellings have final voiced *d* in active third singular.

A second published source, the Samsādhanī concordance, supplies full active blessings for *nam* and the retroflex citation *ṣṭyai*. Its **27 readings across 18 slots** agree, including both ā/e choices in every slot of the latter. On the fifteen-profile grid, **1,080 of 1,350** positions are now written. The perfect and aorist account for the remaining 270.
