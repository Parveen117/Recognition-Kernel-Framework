# PG842 · The boundary Indra broke and the two feminine stems

Jaiminīya Brāhmaṇa **3.111** preserves the very definition used in the two Ṛgvedic comments at PG841: *sima* designates the foremost. The prose explains the names of the Mahānāmnī verses. Indra broke the boundary (*sīmānam*), whence their name *simāḥ*. It also relates the name to creating them from the boundary/end. The change of vowel length belongs to the text’s explanation of two words. It supplies no free vowel alternation in the ordinary inflection of either noun. Sāyaṇa labels the definition Śāṭyāyanaka at RV 1.102.6 and Vājasaneyaka at RV 8.4.1. The Jaiminīya address supplies a direct textual parallel.
