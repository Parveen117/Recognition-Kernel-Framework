# PG95 — Complete causative-role perfect and aorist words

Monty · 9 November 2026

Six unchanged PG93 profiles; twelve complete paradigms, 108 request
positions and 458 within-position words/paths. Each slash separates
an admitted reading of the same qualified request. These manually
written tables are source-rule inferences, not runtime output or
individual-word attestations. Primary A/P labels do not label all
auxiliaries: kṛ transfers that pada; bhū/as remain P.

Perfect order: H then P for ruh; within each root, kṛ with ṃ/ñ
(P1sg short-a pair then long-ā pair), bhū with ṃ/m, then as.
Aorist order: H/P for ruh and R/AR for dṛś. Upasargas and initial
aṭ remain separately owned even at a coalesced ā seam.
