# PG850 · The sleep blessing in two printed editions and the two past schedules

Mādhavīya, adādi śīṅ svapne (XML entry id 682, numbered 1000), puts two second-plural words inside the blessing introduced by āśiṣi. In the electronic text the retroflex member has **śayi-** and the dental member **śāyi-**. Its named Benares 1934 edition, printed p. 237, has short **śayi-** in both, on successive lines. Dvārikādāsa Śāstrī's 1983 edition, printed p. 339, has the two short-vowel words side by side. Both go on to give the two aorist second plurals, again with the short root syllable. This establishes the two basic dental/retroflex pairs. The XML's long letter is recorded only as its text. The later discussion of patient or event predication has different inputs. It cannot transfer a long pre-suffix vowel to the basic agent blessing. Lesson 18 prints only the retroflex blessing in the address to many.
