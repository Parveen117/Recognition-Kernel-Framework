## H01. Five-rule scope and nontechnical upapada

Kāśikā/Nyāsa 1.3.77 retain the five-rule group 1.3.72–76:
svarita/ñit actor-result roots, apa+vad, ṇic, selected prefixed
yam outside grantha, and unprefixed jñā. The option is not a
universal override of every preceding A prescription. In particular,
PG98–102's special 1.3.68/70/71 licences are not that five-rule scope.

Upapada here is a word uttered nearby which communicates the
actor-directed principal result. Nyāsa explicitly rejects importing
the technical 3.1.92 designation. This is neither an upasarga nor
an affix. A bare token sva is insufficient without the supplied
referent and result-signalling relation.

## H03. Selected Kāśikā/Nyāsa policy: aprāpta-vibhāṣā

Kāśikā explains that the earlier A indication does not arise
when the actor-result meaning is already indicated by the nearby
word. Nyāsa invokes uktārthānām aprayoga and explicitly calls
this aprāpta-vibhāṣā. Under that selected policy, retain the
independent word's meaning signal, then apply 1.3.77's optional
A. The unselected-A branch takes P in the local remainder.

The interpretation is recorded as a source policy, not a newly
proved universal redundancy principle. Unknown word-reference,
principal-result or scope premises stay HOLD. An unrelated nearby
sva or a five-rule guard that fails stays outside this target.
Each admitted pada is already a separate request key; the option
is not multiplied again inside every cell.
