## Source forms and declared operations

**Tamil.** The selected written forms are `மரம்` (*maram*, tree), `மரங்கள்`
(*maraṅkaḷ*, trees), `மரத்தை` (*marattai*, singular accusative) and `மரங்களை`
(*maraṅkaḷai*, plural accusative). `PL` maps the bare singular to the bare
plural. The change at the join is `m + k → ṅk`. `ACC` maps each bare form to
its selected accusative, with the singular oblique `maratt-` and the plural
stem `maraṅkaḷ-`. Case-bearing states are terminal within this alphabet.
Ramasamy, Bojar and Žabokrtský (2012), Figure 1, gives both accusatives and a
second singular form, which lies outside the selected fixture. Shulman gives
the bare-to-plural-to-accusative sequence in *Tamil: A Biography*, “Tamil
Syntax: What It Feels Like to Live in Tamil”.
[Figure 1](https://aclanthology.org/W12-5611.pdf) · [Shulman](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674059924)

and one third-person masculine singular past verb from the root כ־ת־ב. The
declared `PLURAL` probe asks for the masculine absolute plural of a noun or
the third-person plural past of the verb. It preserves the input's lexical
analysis. The following are lexicon forms, and `erase` removes the combining
points from the displayed spellings. The benchmark uses three analyses from

## Full ordered carrier and its zero-reading

Admit exactly the Tamil transitions listed above and the Hebrew paths
`pointed input → unpointed input with retained analysis → plural with retained analysis`.
Each Hebrew path keeps its lexical ID, root, template, original pointing and
source. Each Tamil path keeps its stem, number, case, join and source. Tamil
prefix histories are represented by distinct basis labels. Hebrew has three
distinct paths even through the state whose printed label is `כתב`.

Use the [PG14 native operator lift](https://github.com/Parveen117/Recognition-Kernel-Framework/blob/7f185fadde6901097883baab507f7cbb273774ce/research/panini_grammar/grammar_construction/NATIVE_OPERATOR_LIFT.md)
with the finite ordered events from these fixtures. Let `C` be its cut-scalar
field and `B` the free unital `*`-algebra of path letters `z_e, z_e*` over `C`.
The admitted coefficients are all finite linear combinations of ordered
words. Dagger reverses words, interchanges the two letters for each event and
conjugates coefficients. A history grade `x` carries the right module `M_x`
freely generated by its history labels. Use the declared pairing
`⟨u,v⟩ = Σ u_h* v_h`. A typed edge on one history label carries the operator
coefficient `1 + z_e` by left multiplication and transports the whole history
to the target label. A guarded action is the sum of its admitted edges. The
guard retains its domain independently of the zero linear extension used for
matrix calculations. The adjoint of an edge follows from the pairing by
reversing the edge and applying `*` to its coefficient. No clock enters the
construction.

The typed zero-reading applies the augmentation `ε: B → C` to coefficients
and retains all labels and guards. A product of nonempty words has positive
length, so `ε(ab)=ε(a)ε(b)`, `ε(a*)=ε(a)*` and `ε(1+z_e)=1`. Thus each
declared edge specializes to its label transport with unit coefficient.
Entrywise application to sums and matrix products proves
`π₀(U_δ U_γ)=π₀(U_δ)π₀(U_γ)` and `π₀(U_γ*)=π₀(U_γ)*`. The pairing, the
rational cuts below and every cut corner specialize by the same calculation.
In particular, replacing full-family coefficients by their zero-reading
preserves grammar histories. The Hebrew pointing erasure is a different
operation, defined by the spelling observer. The executable calibration uses
the rational subfield of the zero-reading.

## Tamil order witness

On the bare singular, the chronological word `(PL, ACC)` reaches `மரங்களை`.
The word `(ACC, PL)` is disabled on its second step even though singular
`ACC` itself is licensed. In the full carrier the first path carries
`(1+z_ACC,pl)(1+z_PL)`, whose augmentation is `1`. The other path is absent
at both levels. In the linear extension the difference `ACC·PL − PL·ACC`
therefore has the nonzero target coordinate `மரங்களை` after specialization.
The grammatical witness is the guard asymmetry. It proves order sensitivity
on this fixture and supplies no general Tamil precedence scheme.

## Hebrew recognition, Smriti and composition residue

At the middle grade `y` the observed spelling is `כתב` for all three input
histories. Each history admits the **same** `PLURAL` probe. At the terminal
grade `z` the pointed observer yields three distinct strings. The unpointed
observer yields two because the two noun plurals coincide. MR-06 therefore
gives respectively three and two minimum retained states on the middle
spelling fiber. This follows directly too. Two histories with distinct probe
outputs require distinct records, and storing their output class is enough
for the declared one-step probe and terminal behavior. Fixed-width binary
records require two bits and one bit respectively on that fiber.

For the linear experiment, order coordinates as verb, script, reporter.
Present observation is `A = [1, 1, 1]`. The unpointed terminal observer is
`D = [[1,0,0],[0,1,1]]`, and the pointed terminal observer is `I₃`. At the
zero-reading both transports are `I₃` between their different labeled
bases. Since the row of `A` is the sum of the rows of `D`, the visible and
future ranks are `1 → 2` unpointed and `1 → 3` pointed. MR-04 and MR-05 give
minimum additional linear channels 1 and 2. An attaining repair stores the
verb coordinate for the unpointed observer and the verb and script
coordinates for the pointed observer. The present sum supplies the last
coordinate or class total. The direction `e_script − e_reporter` is blind
to both the present and unpointed future observers, and the pointed future
detects it. `e_verb − e_script` is already detected by the unpointed future.

The T24 realization uses `P_x=I₃`, `P_y=(1/3)AᵀA`, `Q_y=I₃−P_y`, and
`P_z=I₃` for the pointed target. For the unpointed target choose
`P_z=diag(1, J₂/2)`, where `J₂` is the two-by-two all-ones matrix. Its kernel
equals `ker D`, and its image represents the two unpointed output classes.
These are self-adjoint idempotents for the declared pairing. They lift with
the same rational entries to `B`. The erase arrow `γ` and the plural arrow
`δ` are diagonal in the lexical IDs with their respective full event
coefficients. In this notation `U_δ Q_y U_γ` describes the portion of the
full transport carried by the middle complementary grade before terminal
projection. The full T24 residue is `J_δ C_γ = P_z U_δ Q_y U_γ` and
specializes to `P_z Q_y`. The latter has rank 2 for pointed output and rank 1
for unpointed output. Inserting `I=P_y+Q_y` proves the exact composition law
at both levels. The rank claims concern the rational zero-reading, and the
full-family residue is nonzero in both cases by specialization. Keeping the
same input and changing only the target explains the difference in Smriti.

## Reproduce the finite checks

From the repository root, run `python3.12 -m research.cross_linguistic_seams.verify`.
The check reuses MR-06 for guarded paths and behavioral quotients, and MR-05
for exact linear repair. It verifies the pointed and unpointed forms,
distinguishing futures, minimum channel counts, and cut-residue identities.
The full-carrier construction above has a written algebraic proof. The
benchmark assumes a known lexical analysis before erasure and says how much
of it must survive for a specified later probe. Open work includes obtaining
analyses from context and extending the source inventories and rule domains.
