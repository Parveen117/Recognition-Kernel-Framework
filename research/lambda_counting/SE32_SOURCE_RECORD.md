The last two sources define boundaries, not imported premises for the
new construction. SE32 derives its completion by fixing each successive
basis response exactly. It imposes no weight inequality or primitive
Hilbert norm. The old mass/weighted completions remain valid for their
own classes; their limitations do not obstruct this differently defined
counting completion.

The order of proof is: finite counting support; arbitrary compatible
response columns; the triangular coefficient decoder; ordered product
readings and their adaptive support; the unit-memory quotient; functions
of counts; and exact inversion of a generated lowering action. Each
step is proved from preceding counting actions. No analytic or spectral
theorem is used to supply the conclusion. These elementary algebraic
mechanisms are not claimed to be unprecedented mathematical discoveries.
Earlier classical method attributions remain in the immutable SE30/31
source records; they are not hidden or relabelled.

Observer reading remains the sole certification rule. The two example
packets record the acquired columns and pass detached serialized data
to decoders that do not call the original operators. The general
infinite results are written proofs; a finite acquired bank is only
claimed to determine its stated finite input target.

The executable is a counting-module application of the canonical
engine. Its special response adapters and fixtures live with proof_lab;
it does not fork or repin the shared engine. The new source manifest
inherits SE31's pins, adds that manifest and the two consulted
completion sources, and pins this stage's six non-manifest files.
Navigation and the manifest itself are outside the self-reference cycle.

The earlier RH certificate is preserved through inherited pins and is
not optimized or recomputed. No new application result is asserted.
